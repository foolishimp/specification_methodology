# Bounded handoff and independent review

Use delegation only when required by a material boundary and authorized by the current configuration. A sufficient direct Writer task goes straight from current basis/grant to the exact effect and focused proof; no automatic coordination or review gate is added.

Before handoff retain:
- Exact outcome and subject; Product Definition and immutable method; governing requirement and design; changed producer, transport, consumer and artifact relations.
- Frame id/revision and result algebra; activation identity; operation grant and precise territory; separate semantic, evaluation, decision, acceptance and publication authorities.
- Required evidence and predicates; admissible source routes; retained failures/counterexamples; original observations and their provenance.
- Host/tool implementation and method support; actor/context/resource limits and exclusions; stop/change invalidators; return schema and named consumer.

Select only the smallest dependency-ready reference frontier that preserves those distinctions. If it does not fit the envelope, refine or sequence within existing authority; seek the actual missing capability or envelope decision when needed. Do not erase material obligations to fit.

Discover the supported `context.assemble`, `contract.preflight`, `dispatch.start`, `dispatch.observe`, `return.capture` and `return.check` contracts through the public path in [tools](tools.md), selecting only operations required by this activation. A missing supported binding stops the dependent handoff. Do not replace the selected dispatch with a native spawn just because it exists. Retain the actual input delivery, process result, stdout/stderr and parsed original closed return. Transport success and actor output availability do not establish requested effects or fitness.

Workers implement only their grant, self-assess the exact candidate and return. They do not activate the next stage. Executive consumes closed results and selects only an already authorized next action; if a record must be written, bind a Writer rather than mutating in the Executive frame.

A required Reviewer acquires governing sources and derives expected behavior before the exact candidate and proof. Use a fresh permitted reviewer and retain actual exposure, author relationship, model family/resolution and limitations. Nonauthor same-family review does not prove cross-family independence. Where a review.prepare tool is selected, actually call its supported contract. The Reviewer reports findings, severity, evidence, affected claims and uncertainty; it does not implement the adopted repair or decide promotion. After material repair, withdraw only affected old reliance and obtain the required fresh assessment.

A returned candidate_ready concerns availability. Assessments keep their own predicates and results. Owner disposition is a separate act. Contradictory results require disposition, not majority voting.

At a Product crossing or controlled-view handoff, use [composition](composition.md)
to rebind the actual actor/purpose/route and preserve eligibility, exposure and
continuation obligations. Parent permission and unchanged candidate bytes alone
do not establish child access or current evidence.
