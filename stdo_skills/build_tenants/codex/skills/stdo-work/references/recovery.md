# Resume from evidence

On restart acquire the current target, material governing sources, grant, operation records and retained return/effect evidence. Preserve their original identities and distinguish historical acquisition from fresh observation. Compare the changed inputs and apply only affected invalidators.

For identical candidate bytes with different histories, do not produce the same continuation automatically:
- A current applicable accepted assessment may support the next already authorized action.
- An unresolved counterexample that defeats that assessment withholds the dependent claim. Preserve the obligation, reacquire its deciding source, and route to the required repair or reassessment owner. Equal file hashes cannot erase it.

After a timeout or lost acknowledgment:
1. Retain the original request/attempt id and unknown-effect state. Do not resend it or infer rollback.
2. Acquire a new workspace.observe of the target and read the original operation/effect evidence with evidence.read.
3. If intended postimage and qualified completion evidence agree, consume that single effect without repeating it.
4. If only changed bytes are visible, establish their custody and intended-effect relation; unrelated matching bytes alone do not close it. If absence is established, retry only through the declared recovery contract and existing authority. Otherwise return the unreconciled effect and missing observation. Process end alone does not prove descendant/external end.

For unsupported method, execution error or missing acquisition retain the actual refusal/error. Withhold dependent success, name the precise re-entry (tool owner, basis owner or operation owner), and preserve useful partial artifacts. Continue only independent authorized work. Do not silently switch tools or repeat an uncertain effect.

Use the selected return contract. Under this native binding:
- candidate_ready: exact candidate plus transition evidence exists; not a fitness result.
- incomplete: activated construction ended without its needed output; its availability is falsified, with stop cause and residuals.
- refused: an applicable prohibition or owner-valid refusal defeats construction.
- re_entry_requested: name basis or authority cause; map materially unusable basis to invalid_basis and missing authority to out_of_frame.
- A performed assessment returns satisfied, falsified with counterexample, indeterminate on undecidable admissible evidence, invalid_basis, or out_of_frame as warranted.
- An unperformed assessment has no result and belongs in not_performed.

Close with original frame, activation, subject, method/observation basis, actor and consumer. Add exact current artifact/effect evidence without replacing the original activation basis. Retain failed attempts and unknown costs. After source/tool/host/grant/candidate changes or a counterexample, invalidate only affected reliance with its cause and preserve unrelated valid evidence. Never declare acceptance from test totals.

At a Product crossing or controlled-view handoff, use [composition](composition.md)
to rebind the actual actor/purpose/route and preserve eligibility, exposure and
continuation obligations. Parent permission and unchanged candidate bytes alone
do not establish child access or current evidence.
