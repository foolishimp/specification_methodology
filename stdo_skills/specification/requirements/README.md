# Shared requirements

Status: common construction candidate. Applies equally to Codex and Claude.
Each tenant traces these obligations to its own design, implementation and
[scenario evidence](SCENARIOS.md); no identical skill decomposition is required.
The exact method owners are indexed in [SOURCE_OWNERS.md](../SOURCE_OWNERS.md).

## SK-01: Discoverable task entry

In a declared supported host installation, ordinary relevant task requests must
make the applicable capability discoverable without requiring the user to remember
the skill name. Explicit invocation must work too. Descriptions distinguish
governed development from unrelated work. Discovery is tested separately from
whether a loaded skill completes the task. Host limits and observed failures are
reported instead of claiming guaranteed model compliance.

## SK-02: Static basis and scope

The selected Product Definition and exact installed method are resolved and
verified before relying on their contracts. Ambiguous, missing or altered bases
withhold dependent work. A moving selector, sibling source or retained summary
cannot replace the pin. Setup installs or pins only within the supplied grant;
ordinary execution never adopts a newer cut implicitly.

## SK-03: Lawful intake and proportional work

The skill identifies the required outcome, changed owner and applicable existing
work basis, then performs the smallest sufficient authorized work. A small direct
task does not automatically acquire a ticket, coordinating actor or reviewer.
Material coordination and independent assessment are activated when their actual
conditions require them. Existing sufficient authorization is reused without a
repeated permission ceremony; missing material authority remains explicit.

## SK-04: Supported tool use

Each selected operation has an exact implementation, input/output contract and
supported method binding. A declared required tool is actually called; its
observed response and effects support the resulting claim. The skill does not
replace its outcome with invented checks, a narrative of intended calls, or an
unqualified alternate path. Pure construction, assessment, authority and F_P
results retain their distinct contracts under 3.0.1.

Compatibility is established per operation. A legacy acquisition may supply
opaque source bytes under its actual contract; an unsupported lifecycle profile
cannot issue 3.0.1 admission or join results by relabeling. A tool failure preserves
partial evidence and unknown effects, withholds the dependent claim and identifies
the next supported re-entry. Independent useful work may continue within its grant.

## SK-05: Adequate bounded context

Context retains the exact outcome, subject/basis, material relations, authority,
required evidence, capability limits, return consumer and invalidators needed for
the task. It is acquired through current source routes and may use verified
derived views. Equal candidate bytes do not erase differing retained assessments,
unresolved obligations or currentness facts that require different continuations.
No general optimal-compression claim follows from a sufficient task context.

## SK-06: Construction and delegated return

Direct or delegated workers receive the governing requirement/design, exact
operation grant and territory, applicable proof predicates and bounded return.
Delegation preserves these bindings across the actual host handoff. A child
completion does not start an unrequested stage. Actor output, transport success,
candidate availability, assessment and acceptance remain distinguishable.

## SK-07: Independent review

Where required, the reviewer derives expectations from governing sources before
evaluating the exact candidate and proof. The reviewer reports actual exposure,
evidence, severity and affected claims; it does not author the adopted repair or
assume acceptance authority. A different role label alone proves no independence.
Required fresh assessment after material change cannot be replaced by a stale
review result.

## SK-08: Continuation and recovery

On resume or interrupted execution, acquire material current state and reconcile
observed effects before retry. Retain unresolved obligations and valid evidence;
invalidate only affected reliance with its reason. Distinguish no assessment,
performed indeterminate assessment and incomplete construction. A timeout does
not prove rollback, and a repeated request must not duplicate an uncertain effect.

## SK-09: Truthful status and closure

Status names what was constructed, assessed and decided, including exact subject,
basis, evidence and remaining work. Closure follows applicable predicates and
required dispositions, not test count, review occurrence or a fluent summary.
An unsupported or unperformed case remains visible, never a passing result.

## SK-10: Release and installation

Release skills bind the exact selected candidate, release conditions, publication
grant and destination, using the supported release tools. They can prepare a
reviewable release without inferring publication authority. They preserve the
method's immutable-cut semantics and keep installation/adoption decisions explicit.
Installation into one host must not silently enable the other tenant or change
unrelated user configuration. An upgrade retains the ability to identify the
previous package and deliberately replace or remove the selected installation.

## SK-11: Independent realization and maintenance

Each tenant declares its requirements-to-algorithms and algorithms-to-host
bindings, supported versions, failure behavior and qualification scope. Shared
mechanics are consumed rather than reimplemented without a supported distinction.
Tuning is driven by observed task behavior. Host changes, tool-contract changes
and method adoption identify affected tests and claims without resetting unrelated
valid evidence. Skill/source references remain resolvable in the delivered form.

## SK-12: Evidence and benefit

Qualification uses common task semantics, independent expected outcomes and the
actual public host/tool paths. Retain actual invocation, acquired inputs, outputs,
tool traces, observed effects, elapsed time and reported token/cost data; unavailable
measures remain unknown. Compare equivalent substantive information and count
retries, repair and review in total cost. One passing trial is bounded evidence,
not statistical reliability or general benefit. Keyword and formatting checks
may establish structure but not successful method application.


## SK-13: Recursive composition and eligible context

When a task consumes another Product, resolve the declared edge and selected
contract/basis using the capable manager implementation named by the activation.
Retain separate standalone/composed outcomes, material reliance and per-route
permissions; a dependency graph does not supply a work order or transitive grant.
Inspect any required trusted a_c composition binding through its public operation
before relying on that guard, and use it at source/context/delegation/recovery
boundaries. Unknown or absent binding withholds the dependent controlled-exposure
claim. Do not substitute older manager success or a narrative inspection result.

Acquire only eligible information under the actual grant, including summaries,
tool outputs and permitted derivations. An approved export needs receiving-policy
eligibility too. Authorized Reviewer inspection can be broader without granting
Worker disclosure. Preserve actual exposure, currentness, outstanding obligations
and required continuations; permission and context adequacy are separate.
Changed authority or already-delivered excluded material triggers supported
re-entry rather than claiming the actor has forgotten it.

The [a_c relation](../composition/A_C_SUPPORT.md) and exact source manifest own
this construction binding. It applies existing installed 3.0.1 laws locally and
requires no mutable v3 methodology source. It creates no general clean-room host
isolation, optimized context selector, accepted provider release or new outcome
algebra. [T-002](../../.ai-workspace/tickets/active/T-002-composition-support.md)
records the affected implementation/evidence; prior Q01–Q12 evidence stays on its
original subjects and does not automatically cover this increment.
