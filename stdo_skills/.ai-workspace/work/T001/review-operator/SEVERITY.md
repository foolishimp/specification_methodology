# T001 finding severity

Root reuses the selected S0–S4 scale from the initial review activation: S0 catastrophic irreversible harm or systemic unauthorized effects; S1 major authority/basis violation or broadly unusable core outcome; S2 reproducible material functional/semantic failure in a supported ordinary path; S3 bounded defect with a practical workaround or limited consequence; S4 minor clarity/usability issue without demonstrated material outcome failure.

Each finding binds exact file:line, violated source relation, reachable counterexample/evidence and consequence. Distinguish demonstrated cause from inference and state repair uncertainty or the smallest affected boundary without authoring a repair. Severity is evidence; Root owns priority, disposition and continuation. Missing trials remain unestablished claims, not invented failing behavior or passing results.
