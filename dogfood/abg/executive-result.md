### Required evidence

The return must contain:

- An exact base commit/tree and hashes for every governing source used.
- A matrix for every pre-binding and post-binding operation reached, naming
  immutable inputs, authority source, capability/grant derivation, public
  definition/schema/handler, output coordinate, owner, and refusal partition.
- An acyclic authority graph rooted only in external authenticated authority
  plus installed Product contract/capability truth. No edge may depend on a
  future WorkspaceBinding, Catalog, execution basis, or caller-minted grant.
- The exact transition after `workspace.bind` to ordinary workspace-scoped
  DefinitionCalls.
- A deterministic derivation of declaration-application coordinates from
  verified installed declarations, schema, Catalog/View, and contributor
  provenance. It must reuse `DeclarationApplication`, require no fixture
  manufacture or executable downstream semantics provider, and emit no runtime
  truth.
- Evidence that overlay identity and transitive membership are preserved rather
  than flattened.
- Exact installed-public routes for Program admission, GraphFunction
  membership/resolution, materialization, and non-lowering validation.
- Explicit separation of installation, binding, Catalog admission, visibility,
  declaration application, Program admission, and runtime start.
- Classification of every unresolved relation as `design_gap` or
  `Product_or_requirement_reentry_required`; no implementation proposal.
- Falsifiers for circular authority, source/ambient fallback, wrong-owner
  resolution, executable-provider substitution, rival registry/controller/IR,
  and premature runtime admission.
