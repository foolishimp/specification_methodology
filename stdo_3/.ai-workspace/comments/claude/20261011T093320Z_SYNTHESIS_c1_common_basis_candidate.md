# Common Principles

Status: accepted common principles basis after C1 (Jim, 2026-10-11). This
basis derives from the C1 working subject frozen in `C1-inputs-20261011-03`,
both tenants' repaired C1 candidates and their source-first reviews. It is
the basis C2 derives capabilities and conformance from. It is not installed
STDO law; the builder remains `stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/`.

Standing vocabulary: recovered (an rc.2 clause states it at the stated
scope), generalised (an rc.2 clause states it for a narrower subject),
derived (follows from named commitments under named premises), independent
(not supplied by the other commitments; argued by non-entailment),
hypothesis (no clause and no argument establishes it).

## P0 Bounded Construction Under A Selected Outer Contract

**Formulation.** A finite actor realises a selected outer contract through
bounded steps. Each step has a selected context that fits the actor's
capability envelope. The actor's internal path within a step is not
determined by the contract. In one sentence: interpret a scoped subject;
select a purpose and governing contract; transform within admitted authority
while conserving its material relations; evaluate the resulting claims;
continue, recover or conclude from current state under explicit uncertainty.

**Standing.** Recovered as a composite of
[RF-002](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-002-finite-attention),
[RF-003](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-003-material-sufficiency),
[AC-010](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-010-material-closure),
the [Probabilistic Work Boundary](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#probabilistic-work-boundary),
the RF engagement functions
([Select And Activate](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#select-and-activate),
[Return A Closed Result](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#return-a-closed-result),
[Reconfigure When Necessary](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#reconfigure-when-necessary))
and, in the graph profile,
[graph functions](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#graph-functions).
The composition into one generic statement, and the refinement
A → B → … → X outside the ODD profile, are generalised. P0 does not by itself
derive purpose, identity, interpretation or authority.

**Premises.** P2 (a selected outcome); actor change
([RF-013](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-013-reconstructable-activation),
[UP-020](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#agentic-construction-execution-stdo-up-020)
intake); step-produced facts are not re-acquirable from sources
([AC-014](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-014-judgment-separation));
open world (RF-003 second paragraph,
[RF-015](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-015-residual-uncertainty));
probabilistic construction (UP-020,
[Ambiguity Governance](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#ambiguity-governance-rule)).

## P1 Contextual Interpretation

**Formulation.** Distinguish a subject, its representation and an
interpretation under a declared context. An interpretation must explain what
distinctions its consumer can rely on. An algorithmic description can make
such interpretation operationally explicit.

**Standing.** Independent; recovered. Non-entailment: with purpose, identity,
evidence and authority fixed, two interpretation contracts give one
representation two meanings
([RF-008](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-008-overlap-without-identity-collapse)).
The algorithmic clause has three readings that are kept separate:
interpretation involves a process (weak, recovered); every material
interpretation can be made operationally explicit (the methodological
ambition this basis adopts); all meaning is captured by a formal language
(hypothesis, refused by AC's scope). "Algorithmic" means explicit and
structurally checkable
([AC-018](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-018-structural-and-semantic-separation));
a semantic decision is lawful only under an exact decision contract
([AC-019](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-019-valid-model)),
and structural success alone never establishes fidelity.

**Premises/dependencies.** A selected subject, context, interpretation
contract and adequacy relation.

**Recovery.** [Interpretation and models](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#interpretation-and-models), [AC-017 fidelity](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-017-interpretation-fidelity), [AC-018 separation](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-018-structural-and-semantic-separation), [AC-008 latitude](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-008-explicit-latitude), [bounded-context semantic resolution](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#bounded-context-semantic-resolution).

**Falsifier.** Structurally identical representations with materially
different interpretations are admitted by this basis; a claim that structure
decides meaning falsifies a realisation, not the principle.

## P2 Purpose And Adequacy

**Formulation.** A selected outcome U defines adequacy for a designed
transformation. Its interfaces inherit material obligations from the
transformation they serve; the outcome's owner fixes reserved value choices.

**Standing.** Independent; recovered. Non-entailment: one finite machine can
serve opposite purposes with identical capacity; nothing in P0, P1, P3 or P6
selects an outcome. Interface teleology is derived from UP-013 with UP-014:
an interface obligation with no route to the outcome is unjustified
([UP-005](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#boundary-and-governance-cost-stdo-up-005)).

**Premises/dependencies.** A selected purpose, observable completion
condition and governing constraints; interpretation explains what an
observation means.

**Recovery.** [Product outcome conservation](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#product-outcome-conservation-stdo-up-013), [reconstruction sufficiency](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#reconstruction-litmus), [constitutional chain](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#constitutional-chain).

**Falsifier.** An interface adds an obligation without a material outcome or
authority relation; a local green check substitutes for the selected outcome.

## P3 Identity, State And Representation

**Formulation.** An object O has a declared identity, boundary and material
relations. Its identity can survive admissible state or encoding changes.
Current state, historical observations and representation identity remain
separate.

**Standing.** Independent; recovered. Non-entailment: two finite stores can
key by persistent identity or by byte digest within the same capacity. A
boundary is a claim with a proof obligation
([Entity Cut Construction Law](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#entity-cut-construction-law)).
Decision-relative state sufficiency, statistical Markov properties and
Markov-category structure are separate claims; the statistical construct is
candidate status
([epistemic status](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#epistemic-status-of-the-construct)).

**Premises/dependencies.** Declared type/context, scope, owner, basis and
admissible change relation.

**Recovery.** [Scoped identity](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/IDENTITY_METHOD.md#1-scoped-identity), [AC-002](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-002-scoped-identity), [AC-014](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-014-judgment-separation), [recursive Product taxonomy](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#recursive-product-taxonomy), [UP-015](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/RELEASE_METHOD.md#successor-baseline-conservation-stdo-up-015), [Markov object](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#markov-object).

**Falsifier.** Equal content taken as identity; an unchanged identity taken
as current content.

## P4 Typed Composition And Conservation

This principle has two parts of different standing.

**P4a Typed composition.** Two transformations compose when their typed
contracts, standing and preconditions are compatible. Independent; recovered
at [AC-016](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-016-typed-composition)
and [RF-010](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-010-composition-on-demand).
Non-entailment: shape-compatible joins with different units pass every other
commitment and still compose nothing.

**P4b Conservation of step-produced facts.** A step's judgments, residuals,
observations and authority decisions that the outer contract needs are
conserved by the step's output; sources and evidence a later step can
re-acquire are governed by sufficiency, not conservation. Derived from P0
with P2 and the step-produced-facts premise; stated as law at
[AC-009](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-009-residual-conservation),
[AC-013](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-013-transformation),
[AC-005](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-005-authority-conservation),
[UP-017](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#bounded-frame-conservation-stdo-up-017),
[UP-021](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#complete-enclosing-relation-admission-stdo-up-021)
and the baseline [work result](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#work-result).
A known failure A and an uncovered required obligation B survive any join.
Conservation concerns a recoverable material relation, not a copied body.
Shared machinery supplies instances without merging responsibilities
([UP-019](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#computational-realization-projection-stdo-up-019),
[UP-023](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#capability-and-foundation-selection-stdo-up-023)).

**Falsifier.** A locally valid join erases A or B; a reference becomes
inaccessible and is counted as success.

## P5 Finite Capacity And Graceful Total Handling

**Formulation.** Finite capacity is P0. This principle is totality: a
selected control model has explicit dispositions for unknown or inadequate
conditions (refine, recover, yield, re-enter, terminate) and can continue
within the current contract when supported.

**Standing.** Derived from P0 with the open-world premise and P6: out-of-context
conditions occur and cannot be excluded by design; a step that met one cannot
report success, so its result algebra carries indeterminate, out_of_frame and
invalid_basis as first-class outcomes and its control model names the next
move. Recovered at RF-012, RF-014, AC-007 and Reconfigure When Necessary.
Restart is re-entry over current persistent work state under UP-020's
checkpoint and continuation clause; it does not imply idempotence of external
effects. No universal budget, timeout, retry count or actor count follows;
cost declaration is selected where capability fit or a cost claim is the
governed question.

**Premises/dependencies.** A selected actor/capability envelope, sufficient
material relations, permitted actions and observable outcome distinctions.
Complete world knowledge and guaranteed termination are not assumptions.

**Recovery.** [RF-002](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-002-finite-attention), [RF-003](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-003-material-sufficiency), [RF-012](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-012-closed-results), [RF-014](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-014-capability-fit), [RF-017](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-017-proportional-compression), [AC-007](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-007-constraint-and-refusal), [AC-011](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-011-projection), [Totality Rule](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#8-totality-rule), [UP-020](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#agentic-construction-execution-stdo-up-020).

**Falsifier.** Insufficient context causes silent omission or an unhandled
state; a required coupled context that cannot fit produces anything other
than refinement, another actor or a truthful stop.

## P6 Justified Claims And Separate Authority

**Formulation.** Definitions, assumptions, observations, candidate judgments
and accepted claims have distinct standing. Evidence supports a selected
claim on a basis; authority permits operations or disposes acceptance within
declared scope.

**Standing.** Independent; recovered. Non-entailment: a finite actor
satisfying P0 and P1–P5 can still accept a green label as acceptance or treat
capability as permission; UP-022, RF-006 and UP-004 are the separate
commitments that forbid it. Justification and authority are distinct premise
relations inside this principle.

**Premises/dependencies.** Exact subject, claim, evidence, evaluator,
applicable independence and actual owner authority.

**Recovery.** [UP-001](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#proof-target-identity-and-adequacy-stdo-up-001), [UP-008](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#semantic-evidence-and-projection-separation-stdo-up-008), [UP-022](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#assurance-boundary-congruence-and-method-qualification-stdo-up-022), [UP-004](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/IDENTITY_METHOD.md#authority-identity-and-conservation-stdo-up-004), [UP-007](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/TICKET_METHOD.md#execution-and-verification-authority-separation-stdo-up-007), [RF-006](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-006-authority-conservation), [RF-007](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-007-semantic-evidence-and-verdict-separation), [AC-014](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-014-judgment-separation), [judgment continuity](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/TICKET_METHOD.md#recorded-judgment-and-owner-ruling-continuity).

**Falsifier.** A successful parse, valid map or historical judgment acquires
unsupported semantic acceptance or authority.

## Formalisation And Open Claims

Retain U (purpose), O (object), Σ (common WHAT), 𝒜 (algorithmic
descriptions), ℐ (informational interpretations) and 𝒞_H (computational
realisations over architecture H; H names an architecture, never a runtime).

Maps S:𝒜→ℐ and R_H:𝒜→𝒞_H. A tenant realisation a_j conforms when its
interpreted behaviour satisfies Σ under a P1 interpretation contract and a P6
judgment. Conformance, semantic equivalence and computational equivalence are
different claims; costs depend on input, state and architecture.

Established at C1: for the fragment where 𝒜 is the free category on typed
primitives, ℐ is the selected carrier sets with total functions and 𝒞_H
pairs functions with additive costs, S and R_H are functors and S is not
faithful; that S is therefore not an equivalence. Other reformulations remain
open. To claim any further category or functor, define objects, arrows,
identities and composition and prove preservation
([functor kinds](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#traversals-and-functor-kinds)).

Hypotheses: categorical equivalence of 𝒜 and ℐ under some reformulation;
universal algorithmic capture of meaning; statistical Markov closure; runtime
traversal composition laws (any runtime's, none is named in this basis).
