# Qualification Method

Standing: Codex documentary candidate. Installed STDO 2.5.1 RC2 governs its
construction. This owner imports [claim and judgment](EVIDENCE_METHOD.md) and
[work and effects](WORK_METHOD.md); it does not create another judgment family.

## K-01 Select The Claim Before The Proof

Bind the outcome, audience, selected subject and dependency identities,
operating conditions, sufficient evidence, applicable independence and actual
acceptance owner. Define a discriminating counterexample before inspecting the
candidate. Qualifications have a scope and interval; broad names such as
"complete", "installed" or "production ready" do not enlarge the supported
claim. An unselected audience or unresolved material obligation stays open.

Construction, structural checking, independent source fidelity, actual use,
owner acceptance, installation, release publication and adoption are different
claims. Applicable evidence can join them; no one claim supplies all the others.
Keep their deciding owners visible without requiring a separate document for
every word or operation.

## K-02 Demonstrate The Producer To Consumer Relation

Use the actual selected material and path. Establish an adequate positive
baseline before a genuine defect exercise. Introduce the bounded actual defect,
freeze it, and let an author-independent assessor recover source expectations
before the subject. Preserve the negative result and original citations;
repair the causal owner inside its grant and reassess affected obligations on
the successor. Supplied negative answers exercise result handling only.

A fixture is an external test subject. Its particulars cannot become generic
method features. A local check can support its selected predicate while the
outer outcome fails. Exercise material joins, unavailable context and lawful
stops alongside success. Recovery evidence concerns current persistent work,
fresh applicability and unresolved effects; it does not require every history
body to become future input.

If a trial fails, triangulate the governing contract, selected design,
implementation or projection, fixture, join identity/authority/effects and proof.
Identify supported causal owners and the affected repair before retry. A
diagnosis may be uncertain or plural. A failed intended discriminator remains
failed even when a different useful distinction was observed.

## K-03 Qualify Publication By Use

A source index can establish discovery and exact recovery. It does not prove
that an unfamiliar capable actor can select useful work. Give a fresh actor a
selected outcome and the published entry, without private author instructions
or a peer realisation. Observe recovery of relevant owners, material relations,
the permitted first action and truthful stops, then an actual useful result.

Test an inadequate guide separately from broken source resolution. A correct
index with poor task guidance receives structural credit and an unresolved or
failed usability claim. A responsibility view may omit immaterial detail while
preserving coupled invariants and sufficient access to governing source.
Published views route meaning; they do not create rival law.

## K-04 Account For Complete Material Cost

For a selected operational claim, state workload, actor/capability envelope,
architecture and observable measures. Compare adequate results on the same
relevant task and basis. Count material preparation, body acquisition and
copying, repeated reads, external actor work, assessment and human intervention
where observable. Critical-path elapsed, aggregate effort, bytes, tokens and
peak memory measure different things. Unavailable counters remain unavailable.

A short prompt, small index or one cheap stage cannot prove whole-path
improvement. State displaced work and measurement limits. No universal payload,
RSS, token, retry, time or reviewer quota follows from this rule. A selected
activation can have its own operating bound and lawful stop.

## K-05 Verify The Selected Dependency Before Use

Bind a directed consumer-to-owner relation: selected purpose, subject, contract,
identity, material membership, availability and change consequences. Verify
the required target through the selected Install before claiming installed
use. A tag, source locator, digest or matching field shape alone is insufficient.
Resolve material changed dependencies at their owning boundary; unaffected
supported relations retain their scoped applicability.

For a_c, recover the [exact common dependency contract](../../../specification/requirements/A_C_DEPENDENCY.md).
Keep the selected program, signature, maps, bindings and source-to-model
dispositions bound to their frozen subject. Mechanical validation, independent
interpretation fidelity and neutral finite-fragment behavior have separate
results. A valid carrier cannot prove truth, complete logical validation,
method effectiveness or owner acceptance. The tool is separately owned;
representation and method repairs cannot silently patch it.

## K-06 Dispose The Successor And Release Claims

Once an owner selects the exact predecessor and migration relation, every
applicable predecessor claim receives supported conserved, superseded,
intentionally removed or not-applicable disposition. An undisposed affected
claim leaves successor qualification incomplete. Selection of RC2 as the
builder does not itself select the STDO 2-to-3 predecessor or release lineage.

Bind a release claim to the unchanged accepted candidate, dependencies,
publication membership, installed material and selected owner acceptance.
Changed bytes create a successor and reopen affected support. A candidate
ready for review is useful work; it is not acceptance, publication or adoption.
Only the owning authority may select those further operations.
