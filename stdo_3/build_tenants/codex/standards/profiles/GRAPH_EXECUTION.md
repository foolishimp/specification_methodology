# Graph Execution Profile

Standing: optional Codex candidate. Selection follows
[PR-01](README.md#pr-01-select-a-profile). This profile recovers graph-native ODD
meaning; it imports [Spec](../SPEC_METHOD.md), [Work](../WORK_METHOD.md) and
[Evidence](../EVIDENCE_METHOD.md). GTL, ABG, HoG and other engines remain
separately owned realizations with their own selected contracts.

## GE-01 Bind Typed Graph Meaning

A node names a bounded truth state or asset. A vector declares a lawful
transition through required inputs, target/output contract, evaluators and
gates. A graph function realizes a vector directly, refines it into inner work
or supplies reusable composition. The outer vector retains its governing
contract; local asset and function names remain Product bindings.

Derive the outcome traversal over typed assets before selecting its carrier and
necessary transport/persistence adapters. An adapter cannot invent semantic
movement, next-action selection, lineage or closure. The selected Product and
graph contracts supply those meanings and authorities.

Recovery: [RC2 graph model](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#5-graph-model),
[local binding](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#8-responsibility-layers-and-local-binding)
and [outcome-first realization](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#112a-outcome-first-realization-order).

## GE-02 Separate Static Admission From Live Binding

An admitted fixed graph can reuse established validation for the same graph,
predicate, basis and validity conditions. That evidence does not establish a
new invocation's input identity, capability, authority, dynamic bindings or
current dependency state. Admit those relations at their actual owner before
effects; refresh only support affected by a material invalidator.

Each admission owner retains responsibility for its complete material relation,
participants, joins and current basis. Reuse neither transfers that responsibility
nor supplies a new permission.

A function or role boundary alone does not require repeating unchanged static
facts. Conversely, a cached success cannot waive a changed basis, missing live
input or required independent judgment. The selected engine owns how it binds
and persists these facts; this profile specifies no cache or execution API.

Recovery: [RC2 enclosing admission and reuse](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#complete-enclosing-relation-admission-stdo-up-021)
and [accepted C1 mechanism account](../../design/PRINCIPLES.md#candidate-spec-method-mechanisms).

## GE-03 Keep Traversal And Worker Responsibilities Distinct

A probabilistic traversal binds its external contract, context, capability,
provenance, required evaluation and lawful completion/continuation/stop outcomes.
Its internal solution strategy remains worker HOW. The ODD F_P, F_D and F_H
classification describes scoped probabilistic work, deterministic evaluation
and human adjudication; it supplies neither mathematical functor laws nor
ambient human or worker authority.

Apply Work's execution and current-state recovery to the bound traversal.
Observation, prior-action assessment and next-action selection retain their
different responsibilities. Yield preserves actual progress, residuals and
possible effects. Edge closure supports its declared claim; Product acceptance
and authority to choose another outcome remain with their actual owner.

Recovery: [RC2 probabilistic compute](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#probabilistic-compute)
and [constructive evaluation/yield](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#115d-constructive-evaluation-and-yield-loop).

## GE-04 Preserve The Selected Computational Scope

Graph representation, operational runtime and method meaning have distinct
identities and qualification claims. A changed runtime or binding can reopen
affected evidence while leaving an unchanged static relation applicable. Graph
shape or local green checks alone do not establish whole-path adequacy.

Restart uses current persistent workspace and supported observations under
[W-06](../WORK_METHOD.md#w-06-re-enter-current-persistent-work). A selected
event-sourced engine may own state through history and projections; that
selection does not require every history body in each actor's working context.
HoG monad laws and broader categorical identifications remain scoped hypotheses
under [F-07](../FOUNDATIONS.md#f-07-orthogonal-accounts-and-proof-limits).
