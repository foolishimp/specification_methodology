# Work Records Profile

Standing: optional Codex candidate. Selection follows
[PR-01](README.md#pr-01-select-a-profile). This profile owns durable work identity,
carrier lifetime and state projections. [Work](../WORK_METHOD.md) owns the work
relation; [Evidence](../EVIDENCE_METHOD.md) owns judgment and support.

## WR-01 Choose A Carrier For The Actual Lifetime

Use the smallest lawful carrier that preserves the work's required identity,
authority, current state, obligations, evidence and lifetime. A durable ticket
can own work across sessions. A sprint can own a bounded batch and its local
entries. A run-scoped contract can bind a direct action when no independent
durable or batch identity is needed. Their names and storage formats are HOW.

Selecting this optional successor profile does not waive ticket or execution
admission duties in the currently operative builder law. An existing adequate
carrier need not be supplemented with another registry, ticket per judgment or
serialized control pack. Bind work through
[W-01](../WORK_METHOD.md#w-01-bind-the-selected-work).

Recovery: [RC2 work-carrier model](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/TICKET_METHOD.md#core-model)
and [carrier-to-execution rule](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/TICKET_METHOD.md#work-carrier-to-execution-rule).

## WR-02 Give Current State One Owner

Declare the owner of each work state and its relation to the selected outcome.
Subordinate milestones retain their exact claims and conditions. A partial
milestone does not close the wider item. Comments, dashboards, indexes and
summaries project that authority; recency or repetition cannot replace it.

Record judgments and owner rulings in the existing owning carrier with enough
subject/basis, evidence, scope, uncertainty and invalidation information for
their consumers. Preserve the original ruling separately from interpretation.
Resumed work reuses valid applicable results; a new session or role alone does
not invalidate them or supply new authority. Contradictory owning state or
required support withholds dependent closure.

Recovery: [RC2 milestone truth](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/TICKET_METHOD.md#milestone-and-phase-scoped-truth-stdo-up-006),
[judgment/ruling continuity](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/TICKET_METHOD.md#recorded-judgment-and-owner-ruling-continuity)
and [state projection](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/TICKET_METHOD.md#one-truth-state-projection-and-drift-refusal-stdo-up-009).

## WR-03 Re-enter The Persistent Workspace

Apply [W-06](../WORK_METHOD.md#w-06-re-enter-current-persistent-work) to current
workspace and dependencies. The state owner can use files, a selected event
stream or another admitted carrier. Recover retained progress, unresolved work,
applicable support and uncertain effects rather than replacing them with private
conversational memory.

History supplies audit and may be needed evidence for a particular recovery
claim. It is not a universal obligation to load or replay all past bodies into
RAM at every iteration. A summary or reference is sufficient only under
[F-04](../FOUNDATIONS.md#f-04-conservation-through-change). If the selected
carrier derives state from events, its projection must remain faithful to that
event authority; this profile adds no generic event store or history protocol.

## WR-04 Close Conditions And Preserve Residuals

Closure depends on applicable satisfied obligations, sufficient exact evidence,
required judgments and actual owner decisions. A review event or active status
does not close the claim. Unknown applicability, an unfinished required judgment
or a material contradiction remains visible and withholds dependent closure.

Preserve an original failed or superseded subject and its actual history.
Reprice only affected obligations at their owner; retained, superseded, deferred
or withdrawn work keeps its evidence and authority relation. Suspension states a
current condition and resumption route. It does not assert completion or erase
in-flight effects. Completion consumes only the selected work instance's grant
under [S-08](../SPEC_METHOD.md#s-08-preserve-outcome-scope-and-authority).

Recovery: [RC2 condition-based closure](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/TICKET_METHOD.md#condition-based-closure-and-reuse).
