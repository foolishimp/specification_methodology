# Software Profile

Standing: optional Codex candidate. Selection and source basis follow
[PR-01](README.md#pr-01-select-a-profile). This profile owns software design
obligations; it imports [Spec](../SPEC_METHOD.md), [Work](../WORK_METHOD.md) and
[Evidence](../EVIDENCE_METHOD.md).

## SW-01 Derive The Semantic Design

Derive a new or materially changed semantic boundary from common WHAT. Its
accepted semantic model owns entities, scoped identities, relationships,
invariants, lifecycle, authority, functions, composition and effects. Structural
design chooses topology, interfaces, carriers and algorithms under that model.
Code and tests cannot self-ratify a missing design decision.

Make material relations jointly coherent and recoverable for the affected
boundary. Establish adequate domain, sequence, state and computational coverage
of that one design. Declare admitted variation and resolve contradictory
projections or materially different semantic alternatives before promotion.
The presence of views alone proves no consistency. Existing design surfaces can
carry the relation without a second pack or universal document layout.

Recovery: [RC2 ontology-first design](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#4b-ontology-first-design-rule),
[decision completeness](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#decision-complete-symbolic-design)
and [invariant reconstruction](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#invariant-reconstruction-sufficiency-stdo-up-018).

## SW-02 Close Admission And Composition

A consequential transition has an explicit enclosing admission relation: entity
identity, predecessor/current basis, participants, candidate domain, authority,
predicate, commit/effect boundary, durable result and supersession. Dispose
alternative paths relative to that relation. Shared functions, replicas or
independent implementations remain lawful only with their actual roles and
bindings preserved. A projection cannot admit itself from reconstructed truth.

Validate every material participant and equality join before transition effects.
Preserve predecessor/currentness through admission or re-enter without effect.
Multi-owner use binds an equal or declared coherent composite basis. Admission
establishes the complete semantic transition or none, including supported nested
forms; partial publication cannot count as admitted truth.

Derive interfaces from [S-04](../SPEC_METHOD.md#s-04-derive-interfaces-from-outer-outcomes).
Define parameterized function families for genuine common machinery and explicit
composition laws where higher-order behavior is material. Variation in payload
or kind does not by itself create another semantic owner. Different identity,
effect, authority or failure laws remain distinct even when they share helpers.

Recovery: [RC2 transformation admission](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#transformation-admission-completeness-stdo-up-016),
[complete enclosing relation](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#complete-enclosing-relation-admission-stdo-up-021)
and [function families](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#atomic-functions-and-higher-order-composition).

## SW-03 Expose Computation And Effects

Project the material design into data structures, algorithms, keys, mutations,
effect edges and implementation roles. Establish that these accounts describe
the same selected system. Prefer semantic transformations over admitted inputs,
explicit typed non-success outcomes and effects applied at declared edges.
Adapters transport or translate supported meaning; they cannot establish a new
semantic center through defaults or hidden orchestration.

An operational claim binds its workload, architecture, input growth, material
costs and owned acceptance limits. Similar computation motivates a reuse
evaluation; it does not erase domain responsibility. A product can select
independent implementation for supported operational constraints. Neither a
language mandate nor a universal complexity or budget follows.

Recovery: [RC2 computational projection](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#computational-realization-projection-stdo-up-019),
[totality](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#8-totality-rule),
[effect edge](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#9-effect-edge-rule)
and [interface non-bleed](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#12-interface-bleed-prohibition).

## SW-04 Change Only The Affected Design

A change wholly inside an accepted boundary can reuse its applicable design and
evidence while demonstrating the relevant preservation claim. A changed
material semantic or structural relation returns to its owner before dependent
promotion. [S-07](../SPEC_METHOD.md#s-07-diagnose-before-repair) owns causal
triage; Work and Evidence own execution and reassessment. A deployment, UI or
test fixture is a separate realization or evidence subject, not a new generic
software obligation by precedent.
