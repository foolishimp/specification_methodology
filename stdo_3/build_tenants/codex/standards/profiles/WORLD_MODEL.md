# World Model Profile

Standing: optional Codex candidate. Selection follows
[PR-01](README.md#pr-01-select-a-profile). This profile owns recovered semantic
entities, treatments and publication. It imports [Foundations](../FOUNDATIONS.md),
[Spec](../SPEC_METHOD.md), [Work](../WORK_METHOD.md) and
[Evidence](../EVIDENCE_METHOD.md). Source domains retain their operational truth.

## WM-01 Recover The Domain's Meaning

A world model is a derived semantic account of an operating domain. Recover its
entities and functions from authoritative source surfaces; a convenient schema
or attribute cluster does not invent entity identity. Resolve each material
claim through its source owner, exact basis, context and assurance.

Data supplies evidence interpreted under that context. An API, schema, query or
table can expose a projection of the semantic account while retaining its own
delivery contract. It cannot become the highest semantic authority merely by
being executable. Probabilistic ranking or correspondence proposals retain
overlay standing distinct from accepted treatments and published semantic truth.

Recovery: [RC2 source sovereignty](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#source-systems-remain-sovereign),
[evidence/context](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#data-is-evidence-not-meaning-by-itself)
and [projection law](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#projection-law).

## WM-02 Publish A Discriminated Entity Cut

An entity cut is a bounded, identity-bearing, evidence-backed publication of a
recovered entity. Make identity, boundary, authority, state, evidence, treatment,
covariance and ambiguity explicit and traceable. A stated absence or justified
non-applicability is explicit; do not manufacture a treatment or comparison
population to fill the account.

Discriminate the boundary from plausible alternatives at an evidence altitude
adequate for the claim. State the reinterpretations under which identity
survives and the basis of that conclusion. Interventions, round trips or
cross-context comparisons may strengthen a claim where available and authorized;
they are not mandatory universal experiments. Sparse or inaccessible evidence
limits the claim rather than silently strengthening it.

A published cut is immutable. Revising identity or boundary produces a successor
with its own support. Storage shape and schemas are realization choices.

Recovery: [RC2 entity cut](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#entity-cut)
and [entity-cut construction](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#entity-cut-construction-law).

## WM-03 Compose Through Declared Treatments

A cross-domain mapping is a semantic treatment. Declare its source/destination
interpretations, preserved structure, changed meaning, loss, surplus and
ambiguity. An interpret-back contract can discriminate identity preservation;
matching field names or a successful structural round trip alone cannot.

Compose published domain artifacts with their identity, version, local authority
and treatment semantics intact. Preserve declared loss and unresolved ambiguity
under [F-03](../FOUNDATIONS.md#f-03-typed-composition) and
[F-04](../FOUNDATIONS.md#f-04-conservation-through-change). A changing value list
or classification retains its temporal source/basis rather than becoming an
unqualified constant. Concrete mesh topology and query mechanics remain selected
Product HOW.

Recovery: [RC2 semantic treatments](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#mappings-are-semantic-treatments),
[temporal reference meaning](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#value-lists-are-domain-artifacts-too)
and [composition](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#composition-law).

## WM-04 Keep Stronger Models At Their Actual Standing

A lawful entity cut requires no geometric identity direction or statistical
Markov proof. An optional Markov-object overlay needs a declared representation
space, construction contract and evidence; its absence does not weaken an entity
cut. The RC2 source records candidate standing for its stronger construct and an
unmet conditional-independence promotion predicate. That is recovered source
standing, not a fresh experimental result here.

Decision-relative state sufficiency, statistical conditional independence, a
temporal Markov property and mathematical Markov-category structure are distinct
claims. Each needs its own domain, assumptions, predicate and support. Neither
a bounded object nor an API boundary establishes those stronger laws. Use
[F-07](../FOUNDATIONS.md#f-07-orthogonal-accounts-and-proof-limits) and the
[accepted C1 distinctions](../../design/PRINCIPLES.md#world-model-state-sufficiency-and-markov-claims)
without promoting scoped hypotheses into general Spec Method obligations.

Recovery: [RC2 overlay](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#markov-object-overlay)
and [construct standing](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#epistemic-status-of-the-construct).
