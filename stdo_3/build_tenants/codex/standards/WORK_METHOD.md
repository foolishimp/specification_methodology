# Work Method

Standing: Codex first-thread implementation under the selected C2 contract.
Construction remains governed by installed STDO 2.5.1 RC2. This document owns
work binding, context acquisition, execution and next-action disposition. It
imports [foundations](FOUNDATIONS.md) and [judgment](EVIDENCE_METHOD.md) instead
of duplicating their definitions.

## W-01 Bind The Selected Work

Recover the current outcome and the exact bounded action selected for this
activation. Bind its subject/preimage, governing basis, material relations,
responsibility, capability/access, permitted effects, completion condition and
truthful stops. Original owner decisions retain their original scope. These
facts can use an existing work carrier; their field names and serialization are
HOW, not a universal template.

Outer Product context constrains the selected action. It cannot replace that
action with a broader application demand. A handoff preserves the actual chosen
bounded job, producer coordinates and required source rather than reconstructing
another job from the surrounding context.

If purpose, subject, authority or a deciding relation is ambiguous, the dependent
action is unready. Acquire or return the missing relation to its owner. Evidence
can constrain an existing grant; it cannot enlarge or renew exhausted authority.

## W-02 Acquire Sufficient Context

Begin with the selected responsibility and its material seed relations. Recover
their deciding sources and the dependencies needed for that decision. A source
route or compression index routes attention; it is not replacement authority.
Resolve a needed reference under F-04 before relying on its body or claim.

Within an admitted actor/envelope, the context operation is:

1. Recover the exact governing and current input identities.
2. Identify the material relations and coupled invariants for this responsibility.
3. Obtain their sufficient source, or a justified sufficient projection.
4. Check that the selected context and task fit the actor's declared capability.
5. Proceed only with adequate context; otherwise reconfigure or return the
   actual insufficiency and the unresolved obligation.

This expresses one library relation, not a mandatory recursive closure engine.
A material-closure computation needs an exact dependency family and supported
termination/fit. An unbounded or unknown closure cannot be replaced by guessed
defaults. An absent source is not evidence that the obligation is absent.

## W-03 Refine Without Weakening The Contract

If sufficient joint context does not fit, retain the outer obligations and
identify a supported change: a smaller responsibility with complete applicable
source, another actor/capability, a different sufficient projection, or governing
re-entry. Subtasks compose under F-03; their parent still accounts for whole-task
coverage and material seams.

Independent responsibilities may be handled separately. A coupled invariant is
kept together in a sufficient local or joining evaluation. If no admitted
configuration can resolve it, return out-of-frame pressure with the unresolved
relation and resumption condition. It cannot be split until its decisive facts
disappear. More actors or receipts do not establish sufficiency.

## W-04 Transform And Observe

Perform only the bound work and permitted effects. The worker owns its internal
strategy inside that boundary. Deterministic checks can supply local evidence;
they do not promote the worker's domain strategy into framework law.

Observe actual output and effects against the current workspace and declared
external state. Preserve producer, current subject/basis and unresolved effects.
A reported leader exit, successful transport or file existence alone proves only
its own predicate. If an effect may have occurred before acknowledgment failed,
preserve that uncertainty and obtain sufficient state before repetition.

Author checks return observations and limits. Required independent assessment
consumes the actual frozen material under the [Evidence Method](EVIDENCE_METHOD.md#e-03-assess-the-exact-material).
Authored output does not prove that assessment occurred.

## W-05 Select The Next Authorized Action

Evaluation and action are different types. Consume the applicable judgment and
current work state, diagnose the pressure, then choose only an action admitted
by the still-live governing contract. Several pressures may coexist; every
material constraint continues to apply.

| Current pressure | Lawful response within the selected contract |
|---|---|
| Adequate result; supported work remains | Continue the next authorized bounded action. |
| Actual defect; causal owner and local repair supported | Repair at that owner and reassess affected claims. |
| Missing or indeterminate admissible evidence | Acquire the deciding evidence, or yield with a truthful residual. |
| Insufficient context/capability | Use supported refinement/reconfiguration, or return a truthful limit. |
| Invalid subject/basis or incompatible join | Reacquire/rebind the affected relation; withhold dependent promotion. |
| Possible unresolved effect | Observe/reconcile sufficient state, or withhold repetition and preserve uncertainty. |
| Governing meaning must change | Return to that owner for an accepted successor before dependent work. |
| Exhausted, withdrawn or out-of-scope authority | Conclude or return to the owner; an active label cannot renew it. |
| Another material condition is not represented adequately | Return explicit unknown/out-of-frame pressure and a lawful stop/re-entry condition. |

This boundary account is total over its admitted represented observations,
including unknown pressure. It makes no guarantee that all external conditions
will be recognized or external work will terminate. A hanging dependency tests
the selected supervision assumptions as well as the control account.

## W-06 Re-enter Current Persistent Work

Restart is another application of the same observation, assessment and
next-action relation to current work. An actor occurrence can change while the
Product identity, authored progress and applicable support remain.

Read current workspace state and material dependencies. Retain unchanged valid
work and original producer/judgment coordinates. Refresh only affected
applicability when content, basis, access or another material premise changed.
An unfinished judgment stays unfinished. A historical failure stays historical
when a changed candidate needs new current assessment.

Private conversational memory is not the authoritative workspace. An event log
can supply audit and applicable recovery evidence; every historical body need
not be loaded or replayed. Files, an admitted event stream or another state owner
may realize the required relation. Restart does not establish universal
idempotence: a possibly completed external effect needs sufficient reconciliation.

Erasing retained work or reconstructing the whole output is a separate decision.
It needs a supported reason why repair/continuation is insufficient, including
which obligations, valid support and causal assumptions change.

## W-07 Progress, Churn And Conclusion

Judge an iteration against the same selected unresolved outcome and interval.
Material advance, prerequisite readiness, preservation, regression and churn
have different standing. Only material advance projects outcome progress.

A repeated unchanged rejected action is churn. Diagnose its material cause before
retry: candidate, design, producer/consumer join, identity/basis, authority,
effect observation and evidence may have different repair owners. Uncertain or
plural causes remain explicit. Arbitrary retries cannot substitute for repair.

Suspend with a truthful current state and resumption condition. Withdrawal does
not imply completion, deletion or resolution of in-flight effects. Complete only
the selected outcome instance and interval under its actual acceptance authority.
Retained useful output does not revive its exhausted growth grant.

## Recovery Owners

W-01/W-04 recover the probabilistic work boundary and UP-020 execution relation;
W-02/W-03 recover RF material sufficiency, selective acquisition and
reconfiguration; W-05/W-06/W-07 recover closed-result applicability and
checkpoint/continuation/progress/churn. Exact routes are in the
[accepted source recovery](../design/PRINCIPLES.md#exact-source-recovery).
Per-task limits and state carriers are realizations of these duties, not new
universal quotas, workflow stages or actor counts.
