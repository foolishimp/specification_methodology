and stops under the [Work owner](../../../../../standards/WORK_METHOD.md#w-01-bind-the-selected-work).

meaning or permission.

For the supplied comparison task, produce an auditable documentary operator
note from the selected quotes, interval, units and exclusions. State the
assumption owner and the allowed first documentary action. Preserve the owner's
separate offering/deployment choice. Source uncertainty or a changed quote
reopens affected work; an unsupported effect remains unready.

A passing cost review supports only its exact cost predicate and subject.
Before deployment, payment, contact, common-source mutation or owner acceptance,
recover the separate current operation/owner grant under
[Evidence E-05](../../../../../standards/EVIDENCE_METHOD.md#e-05-evidence-does-not-grant-authority).
This selected grant authorizes documentary work only; stop before any dependent
ungranted effect. Preserve useful assessed work within that original scope.

qualification example, not a new generic method owner or release.
