# Native STDO 3.0.1 consumer binding

The owner selected STDO Skills as a separate Product pinned to released STDO
3.0.1, with Codex and Claude skill tenants, and instructed the Executive to use
current tooling to construct it. This bounded a_c increment supplies that
consumer binding. The a_c builder and its RC9 historical evidence remain as
selected; the method used by the new consumer is a separate exact input.

- N301-01: Accept only explicitly supported immutable method/profile identities.
  Native 3.0.1 use requires its exact released URI, manifest and source routes;
  stale or differently selected Product basis refuses.
- N301-02: Preserve the native method's packet, role, admission, result,
  currentness and authority relations through the public operation path.
  Carrier validation establishes its declared checked properties, not semantic
  sufficiency, independent assessment, acceptance or effect completion.
- N301-03: Preserve the separate predicates of operation payload availability,
  task construction, performed assessment and physical effects. Exhaustion or
  an unperformed assessment creates no universal result mapping.
- N301-04: Keep profile identity in provenance and dependent execution/replay
  checks. Context reassembly cannot erase activation history. Native selection
  does not relabel old evidence, admit a grant, or change a symbolic model's basis.
- N301-05: Make the supported fragment discoverable and usable by ordinary
  operators. Unqualified native operators must refuse explicitly. Setup examples
  and test fixtures grant no real Product work authority.
- N301-06: Demonstrate actual public CLI acquisition, context, preflight,
  process completion, original return capture/check and adverse controls, plus
  retained-profile regression. Controlled processes establish transport facts;
  real actor use and semantic assessment have separate qualification claims.

STDO owns method meaning. A tool's named domain operation is not automatically
an F_D application under 3.0.1. Any new kind claim must establish that edition's
closed dependencies, outcome and totality obligations; authority remains in the
operation contract and actual grant.
