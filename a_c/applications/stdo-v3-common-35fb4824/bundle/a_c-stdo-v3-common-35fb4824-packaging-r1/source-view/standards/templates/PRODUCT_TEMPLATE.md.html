# Project Product

State the current product realization for this project in present tense.

Resolve and verify the exact installed STDO basis declared by the applicable
Product Definition while you fill this document out.

## Product Position

Describe what kind of product this is and what domain it provides.

## Product Terms

Declare each bounded context, its stable identity, owning authority, applicable
basis, and governed scope. Define the key Product concepts that downstream
requirements and design will use.

Treat term spelling as a label rather than a context-free concept identity.
Where another context uses the same term, either keep the meanings isolated or
declare the exact authorized import, translation, equivalence, or
disambiguation relation. Define Product meanings such as user, tenant, account,
actor, owner, frame, and product explicitly when they are material; do not rely
on a shared glossary default.

## Goal Model

State how goals focus one bounded body of work and how those goals feed intent
and downstream product change.

## Product End State

Describe the intended end-state product shape in concrete terms.

## Current Product Definition

State the current product realization in terms that can be decomposed into
requirements.
