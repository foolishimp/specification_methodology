ordinary generation and actual Worker-context/preflight/dispatch integration,
independent source/proof/actual-path assessment, and measured qualification

not replace a consumer methodology's result algebra, applicability, work grant

establish whole-calculus validity. Owner acceptance and release remain reserved.
