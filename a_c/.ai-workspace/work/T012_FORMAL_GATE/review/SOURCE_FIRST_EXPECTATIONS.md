## Deciding expectations

1. **Unseen supported inputs, unchanged grammar obligations.** A new lawful
   model/carrier can be selected without a fixture identity being its admission
   condition. Exact calculus, subject, signature, model and carrier identities
   remain separate and are checked against supplied bytes. Generalising away
   from T-011's two signature digests must not drop closed fields, reference
   domains/cardinality, unique local/external resolution, fixed core links or
   dependency-role declarations. In particular, transformation traversal,
   constraint latitude and transformation residual targets retain their required
   families. Unknown/unsupported grammar refuses explicitly. Sources:
   FR-001/002/006; Graphify design lines 8–49 and 71–94; AC-001–006.

2. **Prove the exact actual projection.** The public path must acquire the
   selected subject, use the actual Graphify projection operation, and bind that
   exact result to checked Lean data and the applicable generic theorems.
   Exact seeds, directed required relation and record budget participate.
   Missing reachable members, unrelated extras, altered retained content,
   relabelled authority/basis and erased residual/boundary data cannot satisfy
   the gate. Equal closure counts are insufficient. Preserve the distinction
   between required edges and provenance/discovery; no relation reversal may
   arise from carrier connectivity. Sources: FR-003–005; Graphify design
   lines 103–126; AC-009–011.

3. **Kernel claim, bridge and runtime stay distinguishable.** Evidence identifies
   the actual theorem contracts, discharged concrete premises, permitted axiom
   dependencies, exact imported data and observed runtime comparison. Reuse
   unchanged T-011 generic proof assessment; assess changed definitions and the
   new data/consumer bridge. A copied fixture proof or generic theorem name does
   not establish this new subject's result. No native/compiler-based decision,
   assumed desired closure or JSON success value substitutes for the selected
   kernel obligation. Opaque record preservation is not predicate truth or prose
   fidelity. Sources: FR-001/006/007 and T-011 `design/BOUNDARIES.md`.

4. **Portable evidence binds the whole selected claim.** A consumer must be able
   to verify the exact model/carrier, signature/dependency mapping, seeds, budget,
   actual projection, formal input, proof/runner and toolchain/library/adapter
   identities through the declared artifact path. Subject and judgment identities
   remain distinct. Relocation must not silently replace unavailable content
   with an author's working directory or a similarly named fixture. A changed
   field, missing deciding artifact or unsupported claim cannot be concealed by
   a matching summary digest or `status: passed`. Sources: FR-006/007;
   FD-003/008/009/010; AC-006/014/018/019.

5. **Cache is verified support, not authority.** Cache reuse must establish both
   the exact performed check it reuses and applicability to the current selected
   inputs. Tampered/missing proof content, different source/schema/adapter/
   toolchain, changed seeds/budget/projection or unsupported evidence cannot pass
   from stored JSON status alone. Recheck material current bytes at the dependent
   operation; HEAD and a prior success are not sufficient. Valid unchanged support
   need not be recomputed merely because a session changed. Sources: FD-002/008/
   012; Graphify design lines 117–126; support design lines 2315–2344,
   2356–2366 and 3511–3523.

6. **Optional gate selection preserves generation standing.** Source-first
   generation remains F_P proposal construction. The optional gate needs an
   explicit selected contract and reports only the checked structural property.
   Unsupported, timed-out, failed, partial or unperformed checking cannot become
   formal success or semantic acceptance; old successful evidence cannot mask a
   newer failed or unfinished attempt. Failed/partial proposal artifacts and
   original source dispositions remain inspectable. Absence of gate selection
   preserves the existing ungated contract without manufacturing a gate pass.
   Sources: generation design lines 101–138 and 140–173; AC-R-015;
   Product proposal/admission boundary; FD-008/010.

7. **Selected Worker gate reaches the actual effect boundary.** Follow the
   ordinary public Context -> preflight -> dispatch -> fixed supervisor ->
   registered Worker path. Bind the checked model/projection and portable
   evidence to the exact retained Context and actual Worker input bytes, not
   merely a Context summary or an adjacent artifact. Requester/target roles and
   existing grants remain distinct. Drift or mismatched evidence must prevent
   the dependent launch; a formal pass cannot grant work authority. Successful
   preparation is not observed delivery. Sources: FD-005–007; support design
   lines 426–459, 543–563, 605–618, 630–687, 2325–2344, 2415–2432,
   3543–3573, 3586–3612 and 3665–3688; native 3.0.1/composition refinements.

8. **Failures and cost retain their scope.** Distinguish a found structural
   counterexample or projection mismatch from unsupported grammar, unavailable
   tooling, timeout, interrupted/not-performed proof and uncertain delivery.
   Use the selected existing operation/result contracts; none creates a Worker
   semantic verdict or proves absence of effects. Report measured time, size,
   cache/recheck use and failed-attempt costs with units. Faster checking does
   not prove improved semantic quality or lower total accepted-result cost.
   Sources: AC-007; FD-007/008/010/013; FDV-001/002; support design lines
   679–687; native 3.0.1 operation-availability mapping.
