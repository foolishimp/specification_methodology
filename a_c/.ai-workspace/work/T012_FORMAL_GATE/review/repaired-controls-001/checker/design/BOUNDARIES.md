| Layer | Checked / proved here | Assumed, opaque or unformalised |
|---|---|---|
| Normative source | Exact installed calculus and builder manifest coordinates; clause-to-theorem map | The calculus is source-owned. This work does not prove its global consistency, completeness or whole-calculus validity. |
| Subject | Exact frozen raw/canonical digests; complete field reconstruction; primary carrier recovery | Authenticity of upstream publication/custody, F_P interpretation fidelity, semantic admission and owner acceptance are external. The 3.0.2 subject does not replace the 2.5.1-rc.2 builder. |
| JSON/schema importer | Strict two-signature parsing/shape/reference/coverage checks, source paths, dependency extraction and adversarial tests | Python, parser, SHA-256 library and schema-to-edge mapping are trusted bridge components. The generator and parser are not Lean-proved. A wrong-but-consistent source relation is not corrected by a closure proof. |
| Formal records | Eight-family distinction, whole canonical source data, finite key/identity/endpoint checks; exact retained-record equality | Prose predicates, uncertainty statements, qualifications, evidence/authority claims and other payloads remain opaque strings. Their preservation is not their truth or legitimacy. External resolutions are retained coordinates, not authenticated targets. |
| Declared closure | Generic seed inclusion, closure, exact finite reachability, leastness, fixed-point equation, certificate uniqueness | Required-edge semantics and selection are inputs. No universally optimal context, token/byte/cost minimum, semantic sufficiency or observation equivalence is proved. |
| Projection | Actual success links to the checked closure and full retained records; parent/authority/basis/boundary/residual conservation; exact record-budget refusal | No new semantic owner/model or residual disposition. The full Python boundary adds crossing-relation metadata; the Lean projection conserves omitted local-reference and excluded-record boundaries but does not separately interpret a crossing-relation predicate. | Lean and Mathlib | Pinned stable 4.35.0; exact Mathlib commit and transitive manifest; concrete `decide +kernel`; explicit fixed theorem contracts; axiom audit; fresh kernel replay | The Lean logical kernel, standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, compiler/toolchain and host are trust components. | Runtime / serialization | Actual `project` execution for all 46 selectors, full emitted field comparison, 46 underbudget refusals; false certificate kernel rejection | Compiled/interpreted execution and JSON output serialization are tested, not compiler-correctness proofs. | Unsupported operations | Importer rejects unsupported signatures/contracts; the exposed formal operation is finite closure/projection | AC-013 transformations, overlays, arbitrary predicate execution, functor/category semantics, F_P generation, F_H adjudication, actor orchestration, semantic/carrier admission, Product acceptance/release and claimed efficiency improvements are unimplemented/unclaimed. |

The generic soundness theorem's substantive premise is an executable finite
certificate check returning true, with a finite input relation and exact seed
set. Its concrete instances discharge that premise in the kernel. No premise
already asserts desired reachability, leastness or preservation. The theorem
about leastness quantifies over candidate seed-containing closed sets, as a
leastness statement must; it does not assume its output is least.

`AC.Audit` pins expected proposition types and audits the deciding proof graph.
`check.py` refuses additional axioms and scans tenant proof sources for admission,
unchecked native proof or skipped-kernel constructs. The kernel rejection probe
attempts to prove a truncated real-fixture certificate successful and must fail.
These checks are author-produced construction evidence. Independent review and
owner disposition remain separate.
