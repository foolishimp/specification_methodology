## Expected declared fragment

1. **Exact, independent tenant.** Register Lean as its own computational HOW.
   Bind exact calculus, model/signature, subject fixture, importer/profile,
   toolchain and dependency identities. The released 32-record STDO302
   composition supplement is a subject fixture, not a constitution or mutable
   v3 runtime dependency. AC-R-001/013 and Product lines 9–13, 96–102 separate
   these roles.

2. **Eight-family model without identity collapse.** The formal domain must
   distinguish all eight record families. State the admitted finite universe,
   unique local identities, local/external separation and supported reference
   typing. Preserve record content, sequence order, multiplicity, directed and
   parallel relation identities. Unknown or unsupported shapes refuse where
   checked. Do not claim arbitrary predicate semantics from opaque payloads.
   Source: core signature lines 140–238, AC-001–004, AC-R-002–006.

3. **Actual closure theorem.** For exact seeds and declared governing
   dependencies, establish seed inclusion, closure under required dependencies,
   leastness among closed supersets, and a terminating finite computation (or
   explicitly delimit any unproved link to executable computation). Check the
   edge direction and fixture-to-relation mapping. Cycles must terminate;
   discovery/provenance relations do not become governing merely by existing.
   Leastness is relative to the declared relation, not proof that all material
   source meaning was selected or that prose/tokens/cost are minimal.
   Source: AC-010 lines 431–444; AC-R-008/009.

4. **Projection conservation.** Successful output includes exactly the
   declared material closure, unchanged selected records, exact parent and
   selection, and inspectable excluded/external/unresolved boundaries. Bind any
   translated and invalidation-sensitive seams required by the supported
   contract, or explicitly refuse/limit unsupported seam claims. The result is
   a view, not a complete replacement model. Source: AC-011 lines 446–457;
   AC-R-009.

5. **Source, basis, authority and residuals remain attached.** Model/projection
   content must conserve the declared exact metadata and source re-entry.
   Preserving an authority string does not verify its legitimacy or transfer
   authority. Omitted residuals remain explicitly accounted for; omission is
   not resolution. A theorem about retained bytes must not be promoted into
   proof of prose fidelity, source materiality or lawful owner acceptance.
   Source: AC-002/005/006/009, residual lines 340–366, AC-017/018,
   AC-R-001/003/004/011.

6. **Budget refusal.** The executable interface either supplies the full
   supported closure within its declared capacity or refuses. It must not
   return a truncated successful view, silently choose different seeds or drop
   constraints/residuals. A refused operation supplies no success theorem for a
   partial result. Source: AC-011 lines 454–457; AC-R-008/012.

7. **Bounded judgments.** Retaining traversal, transformation and judgment
   records is not executing them. Distinguish successful Lean checking, the
   specific structural predicate, semantic acceptance, carrier admission,
   Product acceptance and release. Neither source fixture acceptance nor
   arbitrary F_D/F_P/F_H authority follows from the proof. Source:
   AC-013–020, especially lines 774–778, 790–816 and 838–859;
   AC-R-007/011/012 and Product lines 37–44, 119–125, 135–148.

## Proof and trust-boundary review

- Inspect theorem statements and assumptions before proof counts. Reject an
  advertised closure result whose premise simply assumes the desired closure,
  an impossible well-formedness premise, a fixture-only equality presented as a
  general theorem, or a proved abstraction disconnected from the actual
  executable output. A deliberately bounded assumption is permissible when its
  origin and discharge are explicit.
- Inspect axiom dependencies of deciding theorems and concrete instances.
  Identify `sorryAx`, extra project axioms, classical assumptions and any
  compiler/native-evaluation trust. State exactly what is checked by Lean's
  kernel and what the compiler, parser, generator or runner is trusted to do.
- Trace the complete path: immutable raw artifact bytes -> parser/importer ->
  complete model/signature and dependency representation -> Lean declarations
  -> theorem/check instance -> public command -> exact verdict/evidence.
  Check hashes alone cannot launder a mismatched payload, ignored field,
  changed dependency, reused generated file or success from an unrelated model.
- Distinguish preservation of uninterpreted content from formal semantics of
  that content. Every reported claim must identify which side it occupies.
- Require meaningful satisfiable positive instances including the actual
  32-record fixture and an application-neutral model. Use a bounded set of
  independent adverse probes for duplicate/unknown records or references,
  dependency direction/cycles, excluded residual accounting, budget refusal,
  source/generated mismatch and stale proof/verdict reuse as applicable.
  The review does not demand a whole-calculus or prose-fidelity formalisation.
