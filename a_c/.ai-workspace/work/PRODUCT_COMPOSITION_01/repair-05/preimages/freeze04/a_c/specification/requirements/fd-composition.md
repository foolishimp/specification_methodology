# Governed Product composition support

The owner selected implementation of the reviewed recursive Product-composition
proposal on 2026-10-26. This increment supports finite, operator-selected
information and operation bindings for existing development actors. The a_c
builder remains 2.5.1 RC2; the native consumer profile remains released 3.0.1 RC1.
It does not infer grants from arbitrary Product prose or change either calculus.

- FC-01: Bind a consumer, exact Product edges/contracts, purpose, route and actor
  to an existing operator-authorized declaration. A caller cannot expand that
  declaration. Mechanical identity/resolution does not establish semantic
  compatibility, lawful grants or sufficient context.
- FC-02: Apply the same declaration to ordinary source/model acquisition,
  requester and activated-target contexts, late reads, retained-content reads,
  review and returned actor content. Refuse unsupported disclosure routes instead
  of allowing them to bypass the selected boundary.
- FC-03: Preserve route-specific permission. Traversability and a shared leaf
  create neither implicit transitive grants nor a union of separate permissions.
- FC-04: Check declared provenance closure and information exclusions. An export
  requires both disclosure authority and explicit receiving-policy eligibility.
  Unknown provenance cannot satisfy a selected completeness condition. The tool
  checks declarations and observations; it cannot discover concealed provenance
  by inspecting arbitrary prose.
- FC-05: Bind exact acquired bytes and material currentness, including dirty
  source bytes. Preserve delivered exposure across policy changes and principal
  relabeling under stable actor identity. Revocation does not erase exposure;
  dependent clean-room use requires lawful re-entry. Cleanup/observation of an
  existing effect must remain possible through a redacted supported path.
- FC-06: Preserve existing policies without the optional composition binding.
  Keep native `results.conjoin` unsupported. Distinguish mediated acquisition
  enforcement from OS/host confinement; refuse a requested confinement claim
  outside the implementation's supported boundary.
- FC-07: Exercise the actual CLI path with positive/negative composition,
  requester-versus-target, provenance, currentness and historical-exposure cases.
  Controlled subprocess tests establish their declared operational predicates,
  not LLM semantic adequacy, owner acceptance or release qualification.

The Graphify tenant's [computational design](../../build_tenants/graphify/design/fd-composition.md)
owns the finite declaration and its unsupported cases. STDO owns the methodology;
Product owners own grants and compatibility. No sibling implementation is a
runtime dependency.
