the initial view. The record, visible neighbourhood and exact link identities,

reading trail persist across switches. The page contains one representation
payload. The exact [artifact comparison](artifact.json) confirms that it equals
the previous 2D/3D and layered pages byte for byte.

The page is still standalone and works from disk without a service or network.

and reuses its instance on return. The reader's label loop also stops while that
renderer is hidden. If WebGL cannot initialize, the same selected information
remains in Layered and the shared reader, with an explicit availability message.

Explicit `--layout force` and `--layout layered` remain supported for separate
exports. Public `ac_project.py build` emits one `explorer.html`; its ordinary
inventory and readback still bind the generated bytes. Earlier runs retain their
original machinery identity and are not rewritten. The Skills provider manifest
has [22 matching pins](provider-pin-refresh.json) for the current implementation.
