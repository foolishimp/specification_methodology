## 2. Expectation delta by named gap

### A. Authority, binding and exact basis

**A1. Sources for the v1 authority expectations (preserved, now sourced to RC9).**
- FOUNDATIONS §2 Authority kinds supplies the six distinct kinds.
- Its "What creates no authority" list now directly sources v1's FD-006, FD-009 and FD-011 discriminators: role name, label, path, ticket, passing check, payload shape, reachability, and drafting beyond admission.
- **Further supported:**
  - The list covers composition, adapters, wrappers and registries, which "never widen". This sources v1 §4.5: CLI/MCP must be thin and non-proxy.
  - "A read-only evaluation declares no operation authority." Read operations (FD-001/003/004/011-projection) should therefore declare none.
  - Effectful operations should inherit an exact owner grant.

**A2. Basis (FOUNDATIONS §2 Basis, Coordinate; RF-005).**
- **Narrowed:**
  - Each FD result binds every coordinate whose change can alter it.
  - A moving basis needs a declared advancement detection and reevaluation condition.
  - Mixed bases need an explicit composite relation.
- *Discriminator:* the builder, method and subject bases may be combined only through a declared composite relation, and are otherwise refused. "Equal names, values, shapes, locations" create no coordinate equality.

**A3. Builder and method separation (FOUNDATIONS Development product; PRODUCT_DEFINITION §4, §11, §12).**
- The rc.2 install is a development product. It transfers no meaning or authority.
- `constitution.stdo.basis.{uri,manifest_sha256}` is the sole authored STDO selection.
- No cache, registry, source checkout or alias may replace it.
- Mutable method source is not operative consumer law before release. A project-local copy is lawful only when explicitly bound and verified.
- **Correction/narrowing of v1 §3:** RC9 is a candidate source activated by work contract (`mode: owner-selected work-scoped candidate dogfood`). It is not the Product's constitution basis.
- *Discriminators:*
  - A design that writes RC9 into `constitution.stdo.basis` fails.
  - A design that lets the tool discover RC9 by path proximity fails.
  - A design that presents RC9 as the operative or installed method fails.
  - `stdo_a_c.json` is inside the write territory, but "builder basis … changes" is an excluded boundary. Any edit to it must leave the basis bytes unchanged.

**A4. Profile availability vs adoption vs activation (PRODUCT_DEFINITION §7; EXEC §2, §16).**
- The admitted contract lists `selected_profiles: [EXECUTIVE_BASELINE.md, DESIGN_MODULE.md]` for activation. Only Product authority adopts a profile, through an accepted Project Reference-Frame Basis.
- *Discriminator:* the design must not claim EXEC adoption, or write an adoption record, unless an admitting Product authority is cited. `REFERENCE_FRAME_BASIS.md` is in the territory, so this matters.
- EXEC §19 adoption content (severity and priority scales, hard stops, the twelve families) is absent. That absence is consistent with "no severity scale". The design must not fabricate it.

**A5. Tenant binding content (SPEC_METHOD §11, rules 1–7).** This now supplies the concrete design obligation for the "declared supported RC9 owner binding." The calculus and execution binding must be declared in the tenant design surfaces, with:
- per operation: inputs, outputs, effects and outcome values, each value mapping to exactly one core value, and effects recorded per ACTIVATION §3 Effects;
- composition laws and permitted transformations;
- resource measures with unit and boundary (this further sources v1 FD-013);
- operational assumptions about actors, tools, availability and failure.

Additional rules:
- Rule 2: no environment choice removes a required human decision or independent assessment.
- Rule 4: merging required independent assessments, or substituting automation for a required human decision, makes that obligation `falsified`.
- Rule 6: structural validation of an a_c model is not execution conformance.

**A6. Derived-surface fidelity (PRODUCT_DEFINITION §11).** `fd-method-claude-rc9.json` is a derived surface.
- **Expectation:** each binding row carries the deciding RC9 path and digest. It preserves the owning clause's applicability, obligations, permissions, exclusions, decision owners and invalidators. It requires neither a stricter nor a weaker relation by compression.
- *Discriminators:*

### B. RF results, joins, coverage and reuse

**B1. Refusal causes (RF §5 refinement table).** These now resolve v1 gap 3, "activation refusal causes":
- `activation_refusal(capability|authority)` maps to `out_of_frame`; `(basis)` maps to `invalid_basis`, topology mismatch included.
- `conjoin`: `conflict` maps to `falsified`, and the disagreement is preserved for the decision owner.
- `translate`: `declared_loss` maps to `satisfied`, and the loss travels as a residual. `refusal(condition|authority|basis)` maps to `falsified`, `out_of_frame` and `invalid_basis` respectively.
- *Discriminators (FD-010):*
  - mandatory satisfied/falsified disagreement yields `conflict`, not a merge;
  - an unperformed assessment or uncovered coverage is not a `conjoin` outcome;
  - `decision(holds)` is not acceptance.
- EXEC §3 (Work result) confirms that an `activation_refusal` is never a synthetic Worker result.

**B2. Coverage (RF §8 ledger, basis tuple, operational full coverage).**
- FD-004 coverage output uses the seven statuses with the stated core mapping, or else returns classification facts only (B3).
- *Discriminators:*
  - `excluded_by_authority` maps to `satisfied` only with a cited authority, and never reads as performed;
  - an `uncovered` row enters a conjunction as required-coverage-gap, not as a `falsified` component;
  - a stale or ambiguous coverage tuple maps to `invalid_basis` with no status assigned;
  - authority coverage requires every effectful path to name its operation authority.

**B3. Computed classification (SPEC_METHOD Computed classification).**
- **Correction/narrowing to v1 FD-004 and FD-012:**
  - changed/unchanged/unknown facts, added/removed/changed facts, and covered/missing/stale/ambiguous/outside-scope facts map to **no core value**;
  - an evaluation that consumes them returns its own value, and unknown stays unknown;
  - a missing candidate is an input state.
- **Further support:**
  - authority traversal includes root, authority and grant changes even when bytes are equal;
  - a rename is removal plus addition unless an identity-preserving relation is supplied;
  - a filename or digest alone establishes no accepted coverage.
- *Discriminator:* step 4 "rule-determined treatment" may appear only from declared rules with recorded owners and sufficient premises. It must never come from a label or filename. Reserved O stays with its owner.
- **Preserved from v1:** the tool selects no next action. If the design computes treatment, it must declare this as C under named rules and not as a selection.

**B4. Reuse and revision.**
- C invalidators are enumerated by name: request, candidate, owner or root relation, projection, design trace or acceptance, rule, configuration, evidence, basis, and revising observation.
- A new role, conversation or resumption reuses C after an applicability check.

**C2. Totality and refinement (ACTIVATION §4).**
- Every condition class has a declared next move, an explicit unknown class included.
- A hanging call, an unsatisfiable demand and a lost post-effect response remain three distinct cases. This sources v1 FD-007.
- Hard stops: an absent authority surface and a missing capability.
- Approximate steps (for example FD-005 compression or projection) need an adequacy relation and an end-to-end bound. A per-step tolerance does not compose into one.

**C3. AC-007 operation outcomes (correction to v1 FD-005/FD-008 wording).**
- **Outcome mapping:**
  - `hold` maps to `indeterminate`;
  - stop `gap` maps to `invalid_basis`, as a closed result;
  - `refusal(constraint)` maps to `falsified`;
  - `refusal(basis)` maps to `invalid_basis`, including a closure fixed point that is not finite and unique.
- **What this corrects:** v1's `closure_unresolved` must be declared as a refinement mapping to exactly one core value consistent with this table.
- *Discriminators:*

  - a demonstrated violation alongside a basis condition stays `refusal(constraint)`, with the basis result reported beside it.

**C4. Constraint surface (AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS §1281+).**
