## 3. Source-bound findings

### Finding A — `relations.query` declaration content and coverage semantics remain open, while the parallel acquired-body carriers are closed

**Locators in `fd-support.md`.**
- **L259 (inventory).** `relations.query` takes `declarations` as a SourceRef and returns "Paths/closure/coverage from explicitly authored typed declarations, supplied applicability separated, incomplete-coverage boundaries."
- **L414–422.** The carrier keys are `id, subject, basis, relations (id/source/target/kind), governing_kinds, coverage (obligations, mapped support and explicit unknowns)` and `applicability (source-bound supplied judgments or registered predicates)`. The operation "performs deterministic sorted-identity traversal and set operations only."
- **L752–767 ("Closed acquired content contracts").** Exactly five SourceRef bodies are designated as actual closed JSON bodies "not merely manifests or arbitrary dictionaries". `tools.describe` returns their `content_schemas`, and "No private builder or unstated implementation default supplies semantics." The five are:
  - `results.conjoin.plan`;
  - `refresh.prepare.declarations`;
  - `refresh.prepare.invalidators`;
  - `resources.aggregate.accounting`;
  - `return.capture.return_contract`.

  `relations.query.declarations` is not in this set.
- **L968–1019.** The `$defs` catalog contains no `RelationDeclarations` definition. The root `oneOf` has seven bodies, and none of them is this carrier.
- **L1536–1542.** The core Worker owns the public schema inventory. If an implementation would change "a material semantic boundary, it returns the exact design gap" rather than choosing.

**Entry and state.** A core Worker implementing FD-004 `relations.query` with `query: "coverage"` and `seeds`, under the accepted design, without hidden context (ACTIVATION §1 Intake; DESIGN_MODULE UP-018).

**Causal path.**
1. The FD-004 WHAT requires "included and missing declared obligations/dependencies, predicate results only for the supported supplied decidable contract". It also requires computed facts to be shown separately from supplied F_P judgments.
2. The design names three ingredients only in prose and fixes neither their types nor their relations:
   - "mapped support", with no stated target domain (relation ids, node ids or evidence refs);
   - how `seeds` scope a coverage query (coverage of seed obligations, or coverage of obligations whose support meets the seed closure);
   - whether a supplied applicability judgment removes an obligation from the reported missing coverage, or is only displayed beside it.
3. The shape of a "source-bound supplied judgment" is open. The repair-004 JudgmentRef rule (L781–792) says caller-authored JSON of the same shape cannot satisfy a judgment reference, but that rule is stated only for the five closed carriers. Whether it governs this carrier is not stated.
4. The admissible implementations therefore differ:
   - one excludes a supplied false-applicability obligation from "missing coverage", and the other lists it;
   - one computes coverage over the seed closure, and the other over seed obligations.

   For the same declaration bytes, they return different missing-coverage sets to the F_P Executive, Worker or Reviewer.
5. Under ACTIVATION §1 Applicability, an exclusion requires a falsified applicability result under cited authority, and unresolved applicability stays in required coverage. An implementation that let unauthenticated supplied text drop obligations would differ materially from one that did not. Neither is excluded by the text.

**Affected actor and relation.**
- The core Worker; FD-004 and SC-AC-FD-004/005.
- ASSURANCE §13 contract sufficiency for F_P actors who author declarations. They cannot determine the exact body from the published contract, because `tools.describe` publishes no content schema for it.
- DESIGN_MODULE §5 uniqueness.

**Uncertainty.**
- A reader may take "supplied applicability separated" (L259) together with the F1 row "computed facts, never RF audit_coverage" (L2177) to mean that supplied applicability never alters computed coverage. That reading narrows the third choice in step 2.
- It does not settle the meaning of "mapped support" or the seed scoping of a coverage query.
- A prior independent excerpt (PRIOR_FINDINGS_DATA, L126) called this carrier "fully closed". I do not inherit that judgment. My reading is that the closure the design itself requires for the sibling carriers is absent here.
- If the Executive judges these choices to be semantically equivalent layout, the value would differ. I do not find support for that equivalence in the text.

**Material consequence.** Under the design's own L1536–1542 rule, the core Worker must either invent FD-004 coverage semantics or return a design gap. Either way the design does not yet settle a material relation, so `decision_complete(B)` is not established.

**Smallest bounded correction locus (evidence only, not a repair).** The closed content catalog and the relations.query paragraph.
