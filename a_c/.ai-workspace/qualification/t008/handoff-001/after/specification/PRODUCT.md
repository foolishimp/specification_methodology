# Axiomatic Calculus Implementations

## Product boundary

This source project constructs implementations of the **axiomatic calculus**,
`a_c`, identified by `urn:stdo:concept:axiomatic-calculus:a-c`. Its bounded context
is `urn:a-c-implementations:bounded-context:realization`.

The exact selected published STDO `AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md` owns calculus meaning.
The shared requirements here own implementation obligations. A tenant's design
owns its computational calculus, representation, operations, concrete stack and
demonstrated fragment. Neither an implementation nor its successful use amends
the calculus or accepts a subject interpretation.

## Capability

A realization consumes an explicitly authored symbolic model and its exact
signature and basis. It checks a declared structural fragment, preserves the
model through its representation, and supports source-reenterable inspection
and bounded acquisition. It keeps constraints, latitude, residuals, traversals,
transformations and judgments distinguishable from ordinary objects and edges.

The initial Graphify realization carries all eight fundamental record families
and their exact content. Its mechanical checks and acquisition operations have
an explicitly bounded contract. It does not execute arbitrary predicates,
transform models, select applicable frames, infer logical truth or supply
semantic acceptance. Unsupported operations refuse rather than silently pass.

A tenant may also facilitate creation of that model from an exact source
corpus through a declared F_P authoring workflow. Graphify extraction and
discovery may supply authoring context inside this workflow; an existing
native interpretation is not a prerequisite. The workflow retains exact source
grounding, authoring provenance, coverage dispositions and unresolved meaning.
Authoring, mechanical validation, carrier generation and external semantic
assessment remain separate claims. A generated model starts as a proposal.

An unaccepted model may have a clearly identified **proposal view**. That view
is not `a_c.X.C` admission. An encoding represented as the calculus's accepted-
interpretation carrier relation requires a separate semantic judgment over the
exact unchanged model. Tooling checks the supplied coordinates; decision
authority and semantic fitness remain external judgments. Carrier admission is
a separate judgment over unchanged carrier bytes; creating bytes or passing a
partial check does not itself issue that judgment. Its declared assessment may
be mechanical, as in `F_D[v_carrier_admission]`.

## F_D support for F_P development actors

The Product additionally provides deterministic support tools for F_P
Executives, Workers and Reviewers building in a Specification Methodology
workspace. STDO3.0 owns the methodology and consequently the contracts these
tools support: lifecycle, role duties, triage/re-entry, frame/activation,
applicability, admission, results/conjunction, review/independence and acceptance.
The tools consume those contracts through an exact caller-selected method/profile
binding. a_c owns their supplied inputs, outputs, checked properties, public use
and qualification; it does not create a second lifecycle or coordinate F_P work.

| Deterministic support | Use by F_P actors |
|---|---|
| Discover tool contracts; bind/observe exact Product, method and current workspace | Executive, Worker and Reviewer identify supported operations, input identities, current material bytes and explicit unsupported contracts. |
| Resolve records/raw source/grounding; query authored dependencies, impact and coverage | All three acquire deciding content and declared relationships. Executive uses them for its selections; Workers inspect the affected implementation/proof; Reviewers acquire source and evidence independently. |
| Assemble/check caller-selected reference-frame context; preflight execution contracts | Render the method-owned activation from supplied selections and exact content; check the declared closure, envelope, bindings and Product-bound admission evidence without inventing authority. |
| Dispatch through a selected executor; retain observations and notifications | Supply exact context/settings and the existing grant, and expose acknowledgment, output, observed effects, partial evidence and exceptions to the declared consumer. |
| Capture/check closed returns; prepare review comparisons; execute declared checks and joins | Preserve actor outputs and their provenance; inspect exact candidate/source/proof, detect binding errors, and evaluate only supported supplied predicates or method-owned operators. |
| Project/record existing work and evidence; prepare affected refresh/handoff; retain usage | Read existing owned carriers and apply already-authorized records through named Writer interfaces; preserve actual support, unresolved impact/effects and measured attempt costs across sessions. |

These are tools available at the points where the selected method and F_P actor
need them, not an a_c stage sequence, task-state store or agent topology.
Public interfaces must support direct Worker and Reviewer source/context/proof
access as well as Executive use. Commands, MCP/protocols, storage, executor,
provider and concrete schemas remain tenant HOW. Existing work/evidence owners
remain authoritative; a tool view or recorded recommendation is not a decision.

In the target calculus, `F_D[v]` is deterministic evaluation/proof over declared
properties and returns a judgment. Source acquisition, context rendering,
serialization, dispatch and authorized recording are named domain operations
with their own contracts; the `F_D` label does not merge their identities or
confer effects. The deterministic wrapper may invoke an F_P actor, but its
semantic output remains F_P. Qualified deterministic evaluation of an already-
declared predicate or method-owned conjunction is supported only within its
explicit checked fragment; F_P supplies semantic selection and unresolved
judgment. Existing source-to-model F_P authoring remains a separate capability,
not one of these new deterministic evaluations.
