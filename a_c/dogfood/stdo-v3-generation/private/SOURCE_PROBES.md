## Grading contract

Required distinctions must be recoverable as authored semantic roles: for example governing constraints, predicate/value meaning, conditional relations, traversal contracts, residuals or their declared combinations. A preserved source quote or link alone is grounding, not substantive authored preservation. Raw extraction fragments or source-graph edges count only insofar as the actual authored interpretation carries the required semantic roles, conditions and authority.

Do not require exact words, one record per source section, one record per criterion, a specific family for a concept, one layout, or duplicate bodies when sufficient lawful references carry the meaning. Recover roles across all applicable authored records before judging omission.

For each criterion cite exact generated record identity and field/content location plus its semantic role and applicable source locus; distinguish present meaning, explicit unresolved disposition, omission and contrary settled assertion. Preserve conflicts and failed/refused/incomplete work.

Contradicted requires a material positive conflict and takes precedence over partial preservation. Otherwise preserved requires every criterion; partial requires some substantive criterion support with a remaining omission/ambiguity; missing means no substantive support. Report per-criterion evidence rather than averaging away a critical conflict.

A missing record is not automatically a contradiction. An explicit unfinished coverage disposition remains visible as unfinished; it does not preserve the missing meaning. There is no numeric aggregate grade in this oracle.

Codex RC5 records performance separately from verdict and defines conjunction precedence on an interpretable composite. Claude RC8 gives an unperformed evaluation no core/closed result and leaves non-satisfied ordering to the applying owner. Apply the specific probe below, without importing one cut's rule into the other.

## Mechanical checks are separate

- **M-ORACLE — mechanical custody and coordinates:** Verify twelve unique probes, six per candidate, source-file digests, manifest membership, heading/line bounds, and exact supporting quotation substrings. A correct citation or matching digest proves custody/coordinates, not that required meaning was authored.
- **M-COVERAGE — mechanical processing accounting:** Independently compare dispatched source chunks, returned fragments and coverage/disposition records to the frozen inventory; retain omitted/refused/partial work. Source processing coverage and populated dispositions are not interpretation completeness or semantic preservation.
- **M-STRUCTURE — declared structural fragment:** Run the selected a_c validation contract for exact identity, reference resolution, permitted families/kinds and fields, and explicit empty populations. Eight-family presence, linked nodes and legal types establish only the checked structure; they do not decide these semantic probes or authority legitimacy.
- **M-RECOVERY — carrier and recovery mechanics:** Check deterministic content identity, exact proposal encode/decode/recovery and pinned upstream graph boundary where selected. Round-trip equality preserves the authored payload, including any mistake; it is not interpretation fidelity or accepted-interpretation admission.
- **M-CLOSURE — algorithmic implementation:** On exact authored governing roles, separately check finite closure/termination, required-member retention and refusal rather than silent budget truncation. A graph algorithm cannot establish that the chosen dependency roles are semantically complete or that an inferred edge is normative. Those require source-grounded judgment.
- **M-PROVENANCE — execution provenance and resource evidence:** Independently bind prompts, source chunks, graph neighborhoods, model/settings, invocations, actual CLI usage, timing and failures to each run. Execution records, extraction confidence and resource counters establish neither a semantic verdict nor lower cost of an accepted result.
