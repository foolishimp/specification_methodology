## Changed behavior and evidence

The new explicit conversion contract preserves one provider-checked a_c
source-semantic model as the existing Axiom program shape. It retains every
record identity, value, reference, qualification, source ground, residual and
model metadata. Restoration regenerates and compares the whole program,
detecting visible-row tampering as well as payload damage. Populated operational
families refuse until an explicit conversion contract is selected.

Actual qualification used the current STDO 3.0.5 model, with 1,885 records.
It restored the exact model value, reproduced the map bytes, matched five
a_c closures in both projection modes, refused visible-statement tamper,
dangling-reference and stale-map controls, and preserved exact pure joining.
The 38-case suite passes under normal and optimized Python. Native skill
shape checks pass. Detailed actual receipts are retained in the cohort work
record and Representation's `mechanical-qualification.json`.

Structural conversion, source resolution and projection checks do not establish
source fidelity, semantic completeness, native LLM usability, Lean execution,
Product acceptance, publication or consumer adoption. Representation owns
STDO-specific content and its separate semantic/native qualification. Historical
cuts and evidence retain their original scope.
