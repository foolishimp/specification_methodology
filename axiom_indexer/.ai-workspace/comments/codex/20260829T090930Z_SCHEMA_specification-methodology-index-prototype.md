The main proposed relations are:

```text
source-project --constructs--> release-cut --releases--> product
```

The main proposed constraints are:

1. `F_D` cannot perform the construction it judges.
2. `FunctorKind(v) = F_P` grants no authority; only
   `F_P[v_semantic_compile]` is proposal-only here.
3. `F_H` has only the authority carried by its exact activation and grant.
4. A judgment points to an unchanged subject.
5. Projection preserves mandatory least closure or returns a hold/refusal.
6. A reference-frame label is not an activation or capability grant.
7. Mutable subject evolution and immutable occurrence/event history are distinct.
8. GTL encoding and carrier admission cannot select or repair semantics.

Two residuals prevent promotion:

- the current `a_c` source has no released publication basis; and
- the occurrence profile has unresolved calculus/publication bases and remains
  active work under T-017.

## Query Prototype

The LLM proposed each natural-language question and seed set. Navigation from
the seed set through declared relations is deterministic. The answers remain a
preview over `Q_STDO*`; they are not Context Packets over an accepted index.

### Which operation extracts semantics?

Seed closure: `stdo:F_P`, `stdo:F_D`, `stdo:F_H`, judgment separation.

Result: semantic extraction is an exact `F_P[v_compile]` traversal. `F_D`
checks declared structure after construction. `F_H` may accept or reject the
unchanged proposal under a separate grant. Neither structural success nor
encoding can synthesize semantic acceptance.

### What does iteration over a mutable workspace mean?

Seed closure: occurrence profile, occurrence, mutable subject, observation,
effect, causation.

Result: each iteration is a fresh immutable traversal occurrence over the
current terrain `W_n`. A separately authorized effect operation may produce
`W_n+1`. Immutable observations, judgments, events, and typed causal links
record what happened. The next occurrence covers the evolved terrain; it does
not mutate or replay the prior occurrence.

### Where does GTL enter?

Seed closure: accepted model, carrier boundary, GTL module, carrier admission.

Result:

```text
Q_STDO* --J_STDO.accepted--> a_c.STDO
a_c.STDO + exact GTL profile --Encode_GTL--> a_c.STDO.GTL bytes
a_c.STDO.GTL --F_D[v_carrier_admission]--> admitted | refuse
```

`Encode_GTL` is the domain construction operation, not `F_D`. GTL makes the
accepted model compact and machine-addressable. It does not perform semantic
extraction or acceptance. A downstream query layer then resolves identities,
walks typed edges, finds applicable constraints, computes declared least
closure, and re-enters exact source locations.
