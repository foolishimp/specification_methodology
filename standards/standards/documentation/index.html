The work loop

Bind the claim and current admitted carrier, acquire sufficient context, work and check, diagnose discrepancies, re-enter the first changed owner, verify affected claims, then continue or close within the grant. Preserve durable results and the next lawful action for a fresh continuation.

Incremental QA, ordinary repair closure, Product acceptance and release are separate claims. Tests, independent assessment and explicit owner decisions apply where their governing conditions require them.

Evidence boundaries

Frames and indexes route source acquisition. A matching hash proves identity; a context judgment supports its exact query; a test supports its tested claim. None supplies a broader semantic verdict or effect grant. Historical combined tooling and proof payloads remain on their original immutable release.
