# STDO Representation Goals

## GOAL-001 — Establish the carrier-neutral F_P semantic-index project

Status: active

Establish the constitutional WHAT, exact source basis, independent build-tenant
boundaries, and acceptance gates for programmatic semantic indexes of the STDO
Symbolic Axiomatic Program used by probabilistic LLM consumers.

### Current selection

- Use `../stdo_representation.json` as the Product Definition overlay.
- Govern the source project with exact STDO cut `v2.5.0-rc.1`, installed
  manifest `3cd24c3196d8334fd9e87fe353e0c8039dbce9f15305cfc8474c7fd71d79d338`,
  and 51-member aggregate
  `87dca989f2200e91406524b6b2a3e85b230bf201581425614b57a7e0469be1e5`.
- Interpret that exact subject as a candidate `a_c.STDO` model with the complete
  `M = (b, I, O, E, C, L, X, V, T, J)` population law.
- Bind the exact generic `a_c` `F_D`, `F_P`, and `F_H` identities as the fundamental
  deterministic, probabilistic, and human functor kinds around the
  representation;
  one declared `F_P[v_compile]` LLM traversal proposes the semantic compilation,
  and later `F_P[v_reason]` consumers reason over the admitted index and a separately supplied
  workspace.
- Keep deterministic work limited to exact acquisition, construction,
  structural carrier validation, canonical identity, and measurement.
- Require an exact `F_P[v_compile]` proposal, deterministic unchanged-payload
  candidate construction, an external `F_H[v_select]` Semantic Selection
  Ledger, and external accepted `J_B` before carrier construction.
- Define an Executive Context Assignment and least-closure projection contract
  for proportionate Executive, Worker, and Reviewer context packets.
- Register GTL and JSON Schema as independent HOW tenants.
- Keep tenant profiles, concrete indexes, and releases unselected until their
  exact designs are separately reviewed and accepted.
- Keep deterministic workspace assessment and embedded runtime execution
  outside the Product payload. A consuming host may separately specialize a
  generic traversal into ODD under its own exact authority.

### Completion conditions

- The Product Definition validates and verifies against its exact installed
  STDO basis.
- The project binds a conforming accepted reference-frame basis.
- Intent, Product, and requirements consistently define Source STDO as a
  symbolic axiomatic program and the reusable Product as its programmatic
  semantic index, with surrounding `F_D/F_H` authority boundaries and an
  exact `F_P[v_compile]` semantic-compilation contract plus distinct downstream
  `F_P[v_reason]`
  consumption contracts rather than a carrier assessment bundle.
- The algebra is a closed `a_c.STDO` model with all eight core record families,
  a total population relation, exact external resolutions, and no open identity
  domain.
- Index Product identity is content-first and cannot cycle through later evidence,
  acceptance, or invocation records.
- The `F_P[v_compile]` semantic-compilation proposal and subsequent
  `F_H[v_select]` selection are
  durably distinguished; neither semantic acceptance nor truth is inferred
  from successful GTL structural validation.
- Executive context setting binds exact frame activations, actor capability,
  authority, role, evidence, stop states, source re-entry, and token budget;
  mandatory closure is never silently removed to fit context.
- Requirement categories and active ticket metadata conform to the selected
  methods.
- The GTL carrier authority remains confined to the GTL tenant.
- The JSON Schema tenant fails closed until an exact dialect is selected.
- No shared serialized intermediate graph, deterministic semantic assessor, or
  execution surface is introduced.

## GOAL-002 — Realize STDO.gtl

Status: active

Define, accept, construct, and measure the first canonical GTL realization of
the STDO Programmatic Semantic Index against the exact frozen GTL basis.

### Current selection

- Publish the canonical consumption artifact as `stdo.gtl`.
- Support exact carrier-native projections of `stdo.gtl` for authorized
  Executive, Worker, and Reviewer context assignments without changing the
  parent Product.
- Map the complete accepted `M = (b, I, O, E, C, L, X, V, T, J)` model, its
  exact external resolutions, and its total external record-provenance relation
  `P_B` reversibly into one compact indexed GTL Rule configuration. Reverse
  lookup from every represented record to its exact Source STDO member route is
  total. Frozen GTL Nodes remain confined to callable GraphFunctions and
  therefore do not enter this passive Product.
- Target direct generic `a_c` `F_P[v_reason]` LLM traversal; use an `F_D[v]`
  traversal only for declared structural properties and never as a semantic
  evaluator.
- Use the exact GTL profile as the reliable typed tenant encoding of the
  unchanged carrier-neutral algebra selected by `F_H[v_select]` and accepted by
  external `J_B`, without treating GTL admission as semantic proof or allowing
  GTL to shape `F_P[v_compile]`.
- Do not reinterpret arbitrary semantic relations as GTL traversal edges.
- Keep GraphFunction, Job, HoG, ABG, execution, events, continuation, and runtime
  truth outside the payload; the external host binds the `F_P[v_reason]`
  traversal.
- Admit construction only after the exact GTL representation-profile bytes and
  digest are explicitly accepted.

### Completion conditions

- The GTL profile maps `I`, `O`, `E`, `C`, `L`, `X`, `V`, `T`, `J`, and the
  exact external-resolution relation directly into frozen GTL.
- The accepted Semantic Selection Ledger binds the exact Source STDO population
  and every retained, omitted, or uncertain selection before serialization.
- `stdo.gtl` is reproducible, canonical, structurally valid, content-addressed,
  and traceable to exact Source STDO.
- A frozen role-bound assignment reproduces the same least declared projection,
  inclusion/omission identities, carrier digest, and token measurement or
  returns an explicit hold.
- Exact byte and token measurements demonstrate the achieved context reduction.
- Frozen `F_P[v_reason]` observations exercise representative and adversarial workspace
  reasoning without claiming deterministic semantic closure.
- The Product payload contains only the symbolic index, constraints, minimal basis
  coordinates, and source routes required by the LLM consumer.
