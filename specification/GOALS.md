## Governed Outcome

The successor must establish two bounded changes.

### Product-Testing Reference Frames

The optional `STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md` profile assigns distinct
claims through a non-hierarchical fan-out:

```text
Product and accepted design
  |- user-outcome claims -> user-acceptance frames
  |- runnable causal-path claims -> end-to-end frames
  |- composition-boundary claims -> integration frames
  `- module-owned laws -> unit frames

bound results -> declared conjunction -> Product or release disposition
```

The accepted review corrections are mandatory:

1. unit evidence proves module-owned laws for every coded module; internal
   combinatorial complexity controls evidence size and strategy, not eligibility;
2. the four testing frames fan out from Product and accepted design rather than
   forming a linear hierarchy;
3. technical-debt erasure separates exact runnable-Product production closure
   from assurance/source closure, preserving lawful isolated test-only seams;
4. UAT and E2E both use the canonical deployed, installed, or otherwise
   runnable Product subject;
5. assurance acquires owner-issued runtime truth through exact owner surfaces
   and evaluates it with a separately derived oracle rather than a rival
   proof-owned semantic interpreter; and
6. counterexamples return a causally minimal supported frontier represented as
   one unique locus, a jointly established incomparable set, unresolved
   alternative frontiers, or `indeterminate` evidence.

The profile remains derived. It does not acquire Product, design, testing,
review, disposition, or release authority independently of its cited owners.

### Capability And Foundation Selection

`DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md` gains one federal owner relation,
`STDO-UP-023`, requiring a proportional preconstruction comparison for a
material generic capability, common mechanic, algorithm, or dependency.

The relation must:

- state the capability without Product, domain, module, vendor, file, or
  incumbent labels;
- distinguish irreducible semantic or authority-bearing relations and their
  declared owner, role kind, subject, scope, basis, lifecycle, and refusal
  invariants from generic mechanics and foundation capabilities;
- compare credible project, native or standard, predecessor-lineage,
  maintained external, local, and mixed foundation compositions;
- bound discovery through searched sources or categories, cutoff and version
  basis, applicability criteria, exclusions, predecessor or incumbent status,
  discovered candidates, residual unknowns, material-gap refusal, and later-
  candidate invalidation;
- operate inside the Product boundary and any applicable migration strategy
  already selected by their existing owners without replacing SPEC's strategy
  decision;
- consider the decision-relevant total-lifecycle dimensions and hard
  eliminators without fabricated scores or favorable treatment of unknowns;
- refuse a dominated candidate, including a local generic rebuild, only after
  every material dimension is comparable or evidenced as non-discriminating
  and no material unknown could reverse the dominance relation;
- select from the undominated frontier only through priorities and risk
  tolerances declared by the existing tradeoff owner for the exact Product and
  basis, retaining a gap when they do not resolve it;
- permit a foundation to realize an explicitly assigned authority-bearing role
  only while preserving the declared relation's owner, role kind, subject,
  scope, basis, lifecycle, and refusal invariants; ownership follows that
  relation rather than source authorship;
- retain decisive evidence or a falsifier for rejected material candidates;
  and
- recursively re-enter when another material generic subproblem appears.

Existing finite-frame, Prime, admission, exact-assurance, recurrence,
construction, review, and release laws remain their own owners and are cited,
not restated.
