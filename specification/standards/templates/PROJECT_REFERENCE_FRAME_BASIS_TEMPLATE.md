## Activation And Return Topology

```text
existing authority
  -> Executive basis and outcome lock
  -> exact Worker, Reviewer, or specialist activation
  -> closed result
  -> Executive or declared conjunction
  -> authorized disposition and next activation | stop | re-entry
```

No child result, tool output, model response, shared actor, or lateral handoff
implicitly activates another frame or authorizes continuation.

## Drift Locks, Invalidation, And Revision

Revalidate before activation and disposition:

- outcome lock: Goal, Product outcome, boundary, and re-entry class;
- basis lock: subject, checkpoint, authority, dependency, configuration,
  topology, evidence, and time;
- Product-role lock: Source Project, Product Definition, Development Product,
  Release Cut, Product, Install, Artifact, and dependency roles;
- authority lock: semantic, evaluation, operation, decision, acceptance, and
  publication owners and grants; and
- coverage-and-return lock: material evaluations, interactions, residuals, and
  declared result routes.

List every method, Product, outcome, subject, checkpoint, candidate,
dependency, composition contract, actor, capability, evidence, authority,
workspace, or topology change that invalidates this basis or its derived
activations, results, conjunctions, and coverage claims.

Exact review checkpoints and released Products remain immutable under their
own lifecycle law. Their immutability does not make the Source Project building
a successor immutable. A lawful mutation creates a new checkpoint and
invalidates every affected prior result.
