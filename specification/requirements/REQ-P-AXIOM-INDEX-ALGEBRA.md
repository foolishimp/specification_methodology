# REQ-P-AXIOM-INDEX-ALGEBRA — Carrier-Neutral Program Algebra

Family: `REQ-P-ALGEBRA-*`
Status: Active
Category: Constraint / Guarantee
Design ownership: common algebra and closure law owned by WHAT; compatible
target extensions require separately selected target-profile authority

Derives from: `../PRODUCT.md#core-relation`,
`../PRODUCT.md#extension-boundary`

## Purpose

Define the minimum closed index envelope around a model governed by an exact
external axiomatic calculus, without redefining that calculus, creating a
serialized intermediate carrier, or importing target vocabulary.

## Common model

```text
P_X = (B, A, T, M_X, R_X)
```

`B` is the Corpus Basis, `A` the exact calculus basis, `T` the Target Profile,
`M_X` the accepted finite model conforming to `A` and `T`, and `R_X` the
duplicate-free source-binding relation from model identities to exact corpus
routes.

## Requirements

### REQ-P-ALGEBRA-001

The accepted model shall be finite and closed for one
exact subject under the complete selected calculus signature and Target
Profile. Unknown kinds require a calculus-conforming compatible extension or
shall refuse.

### REQ-P-ALGEBRA-002

Every model record shall satisfy the selected calculus's
exact record and coordinate law. Axiom Indexer shall not create same-spelled
local alternatives to calculus-owned kinds or relations.

### REQ-P-ALGEBRA-003

Every model reference shall resolve uniquely under the
selected calculus. Every accepted model identity shall also have one or more
exact source bindings declaring corpus member, semantic address, selection
disposition, and source-reentry route.

### REQ-P-ALGEBRA-004

Record-family, relation, constraint, latitude, residual,
judgment, traversal, and transformation semantics belong to the selected
calculus and its compatible target extension. Index construction shall preserve
them without adding implicit semantics.

### REQ-P-ALGEBRA-005

Distinctions required by the selected calculus shall
remain distinct in the index. Failed or indeterminate evaluation shall not
become latitude; omitted or invalidated material shall not disappear without a
calculus-conforming residual and source route.

### REQ-P-ALGEBRA-006

A target profile may extend the common signature only by
an exact compatible-extension declaration binding the added kinds, domains,
codomains, context, owner, scope, basis, and refusal law.

### REQ-P-ALGEBRA-007

Derived indexes and adjacency views shall be reproducible
from the canonical records and shall not become rival semantic authority or a
mandatory shared serialized representation.

### REQ-P-ALGEBRA-008

The accepted parent program shall be complete relative to its exact
semantic-selection ledger and accepting semantic-selection judgment. The total
typed conservation relation from candidate population to accepted program,
ledger, residuals, and replacement lineage shall close before acceptance. A
carrier or projection shall not silently change that accepted population.

### REQ-P-ALGEBRA-009

The common algebra shall contain no target workspace,
prompt, model configuration, model response, execution operation, runtime
event, continuation, or downstream closure truth.

### REQ-P-ALGEBRA-010

An external consumer may reason over the index and
separately supplied evidence, but its output shall not mutate the immutable
parent index or acquire authority from index visibility.
