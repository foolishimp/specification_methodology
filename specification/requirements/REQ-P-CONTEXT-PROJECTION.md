# REQ-P-CONTEXT-PROJECTION — Bounded Axiom-Index Context

Family: `REQ-P-CONTEXT-*`
Status: Active
Category: Capability
Design ownership: common least-closure and packet boundary owned by WHAT;
carrier serialization and downstream invocation remain external HOW

Derives from: `../PRODUCT.md#projection-relation`,
`../PRODUCT.md#authority`

## Purpose

Derive exact, proportionate reasoning contexts from an accepted Axiomatic
Program, then encode and structurally admit each projection without treating
visibility as authority or budget pressure as permission to remove governing
closure.

## Requirements

### REQ-P-CONTEXT-001

Every Projection Assignment shall bind the exact accepted
Axiomatic Program, accepted selection ledger, accepting semantic-selection
judgment, selected carrier basis and profile, purpose, intent, subject and
basis, target actor or capability, selected frames, authority and grant
references, required evidence, explicit seed identities, tokenizer coordinate,
context budget, and stop states. The three accepted-subject identities shall
resolve to one exact `(P_X, S_X, J_X)` relation before projection.

### REQ-P-CONTEXT-002

Projection shall compute the unique least fixed-point semantic closure of the
assignment seeds under the declared projection relation and shall construct a
separate projection envelope containing that closure, the exact applicable
rows from the unchanged parent selection ledger, the accepting `J_X` identity,
and exact judgment, evidence and source-reacquisition relations.

### REQ-P-CONTEXT-003

The projection envelope shall preserve every applicable identity,
authority, scope, basis, dependency, constraint, latitude, residual, evidence,
exclusion, refusal, invalidation, selection-ledger,
semantic-selection-judgment, and source-reentry relation.

### REQ-P-CONTEXT-004

Projection shall expose exact included and omitted
identity sets and digests for both semantic members and applicable ledger rows.
Omitted material shall retain source-reentry routes and shall not be treated as
supplied or inapplicable outside the assignment. The projected ledger rows
shall remain exact views of `S_X` rather than replacement judgments.

### REQ-P-CONTEXT-005

After semantic closure, the selected carrier tenant shall invoke a distinct
projection-encoding operation with the exact immutable projection-envelope
identity. The operation shall verify that envelope, encode only its bound
unchanged semantic closure, and expose those bytes to Carrier Admission. It
shall not accept an unbound substitute closure. Carrier Admission shall judge
the unchanged encoded bytes. Token measurement shall occur only over admitted
carrier-native projection bytes under the assignment's exact tokenizer
coordinate.

### REQ-P-CONTEXT-006

When mandatory closure exceeds capability or budget,
projection shall return capability mismatch or budget exceeded. It shall not
trim mandatory members to force admission.

### REQ-P-CONTEXT-007

An admitted projection manifest shall bind parent accepted
program identity, parent selection-ledger identity, parent accepting
semantic-selection-judgment identity, assignment identity, selected carrier
basis and profile, projected-ledger-row identity set and digest, included and
omitted semantic sets, semantic-closure identity and digest, projection-
envelope identity and digest, acceptance-evidence and reacquisition relation
digest, encoded projection digest, Carrier Admission judgment, token count,
residuals, source-reentry routes, and disposition.

### REQ-P-CONTEXT-008

A Context Packet shall bundle the assignment, projection envelope, selected
frame declarations or exact reacquisition routes, carrier-native projection,
manifest, required external evidence, stop states, residuals, and re-entry
routes.

### REQ-P-CONTEXT-009

A Context Packet is invocation input, not a new Product,
constitution, grant, accepted semantic subject, or runtime fact.

### REQ-P-CONTEXT-010

The common Product shall not prescribe named roles,
prompt tactics, model strategies, execution engines, or downstream workflows.
Target or consuming Products may bind those through their own exact profiles
and authority.
