# REQ-P-BASIS-AND-IDENTITY — Exact Bases And Content Identity

Family: `REQ-P-BASIS-*`
Status: Active
Category: Constraint / Guarantee
Design ownership: common basis and identity law owned by WHAT; target profiles
and carrier tenants own only their declared coordinates

Derives from: `../PRODUCT.md#identity`, `../PRODUCT.md#authority`

## Purpose

Bind every semantic and carrier claim to exact immutable inputs without cycling
acceptance, admission, release, or invocation evidence into subject identity.

## Requirements

### REQ-P-BASIS-001

Every indexing subject shall bind one exact finite Corpus
Basis containing a corpus identity, duplicate-free ordered member inventory,
exact member content digests, source authorities, and acquisition boundary.

### REQ-P-BASIS-002

Every compilation shall bind one exact Axiomatic Calculus
Basis and one exact Target Profile identity and digest. Missing, mutable,
unresolved, or mismatched bases shall refuse before semantic compilation.

### REQ-P-BASIS-003

The Target Profile shall declare its corpus-population law,
semantic-address grammar, required source routes, compatible vocabulary
extensions, mandatory closure, and refusal conditions. It shall not name or
select a carrier tenant.

### REQ-P-BASIS-004

Corpus Basis, calculus basis, target profile, compilation
candidate, structural inspection judgment, accepted program content, selection
ledger, semantic-selection judgment, carrier basis, carrier profile, carrier
bytes, assignment, semantic projection closure, projection envelope, and
projection manifest shall have distinct immutable identities.

### REQ-P-BASIS-005

Subject identity shall be content-first. Acceptance,
admission, release, invocation, and observation records shall point to exact
subjects and shall not enter or change the identity preimage of those subjects.
In particular, the semantic-selection judgment shall point to the accepted
program and ledger identities and remain outside both identity preimages.

### REQ-P-BASIS-006

Canonical identity inputs shall bind one exact selected canonicalization,
Unicode, ordering, and digest law owned by the subject envelope or selected
carrier. The common Product shall not require JSON or another carrier syntax.
Invalid scalar values, duplicate keys or identities, non-finite values,
ambiguous ordering, and undeclared extensions shall refuse.

### REQ-P-BASIS-007

Equal spelling shall not imply equal identity across
different kinds, bounded contexts, owners, scopes, or bases. Every reference
shall resolve uniquely under its declared coordinates and expected record kind.

### REQ-P-BASIS-008

Source re-entry shall resolve each represented or residual
claim to an exact corpus member and semantic address, or expose an explicit
unresolved residual. A generated summary shall not substitute for source bytes.

### REQ-P-BASIS-009

A carrier shall not embed its final content identity into
the bytes from which that identity is derived. Manifests and release records may
bind the completed identity externally.

### REQ-P-BASIS-010

Changing any basis, target profile, accepted program,
carrier profile, canonical bytes, or projection assignment shall create a new
subject identity. Prior evidence remains bound to its original subject.
