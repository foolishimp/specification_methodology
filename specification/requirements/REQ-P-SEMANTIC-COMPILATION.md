# REQ-P-SEMANTIC-COMPILATION — Candidate Proposal

Family: `REQ-P-COMPILE-*`
Status: Active
Category: Capability
Design ownership: common compilation and structural-inspection boundary owned
by WHAT; compiler realization remains external HOW

Derives from: `../INTENT.md#product-relation`,
`../PRODUCT.md#core-relation`, `../PRODUCT.md#authority`

## Purpose

Permit probabilistic semantic extraction while keeping its result an exact,
reviewable proposal.

## Requirements

### REQ-P-COMPILE-001

Semantic compilation shall bind the complete Corpus
Basis and bytes, exact calculus basis, exact Target Profile, compilation intent,
selected frames, capability and context budget, common output algebra,
provenance obligations, and lawful stop states.

### REQ-P-COMPILE-002

Semantic compilation shall return one immutable
candidate containing proposed program records, a disposition row for every
source member or declared population unit, and explicit residual uncertainty;
or it shall return hold, gap, or refusal.

### REQ-P-COMPILE-003

Every candidate row shall distinguish retained, omitted,
uncertain, conflicting, and unrepresentable source material and shall preserve
its rationale and source route. Silence shall not mean omission or acceptance.

### REQ-P-COMPILE-004

A semantic compiler is a proposer. It shall not claim
semantic selection, acceptance, completeness, structural admission, Product
authority, or downstream decision authority.

### REQ-P-COMPILE-005

Structural inspection may validate declared candidate
shape, bases, kinds, identities, references, and closure. It shall return a
separately identified judgment and shall not add, remove, reinterpret, or
accept semantic content. Eligibility is evidence supplied to semantic
selection; it is not semantic acceptance.

### REQ-P-COMPILE-006

Carrier profiles and carrier constraints shall not be
inputs to semantic compilation. A compiler shall not trim a proposal because
one carrier cannot represent it.

### REQ-P-COMPILE-007

Compilation shall retain ambiguity, latitude,
contradiction, omission, and unresolved source material as typed records or
residuals rather than silently completing the source.

### REQ-P-COMPILE-008

Recompilation against changed corpus bytes, calculus,
profile, intent, frames, capability, or output contract shall create a new
candidate. It shall not mutate or overwrite prior candidates or judgments.
