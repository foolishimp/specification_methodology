# STDO Representation Product

Status: active source definition; no released STDO Representation Product
exists.

## Product Terms

Within `urn:stdo-representation:bounded-context:product`:

- **STDO Representation Source Project** is this mutable workspace while it
  defines and builds candidate Products.
- **Source STDO** is the exact immutable STDO release whose constitutional
  meanings and relations are represented.
- **Axiomatic Calculus Basis** is the exact external
  `stdo.axiomatic-calculus-basis` record for the `a_c` edition published in
  Source STDO. It is distinct from both the STDO subject basis and this
  Product's interpreted model.
- **STDO Symbolic Axiomatic Program** is the exact Source STDO constitutional
  corpus understood as one symbolic system of named declarations, identities,
  typed relations, constraints, authority boundaries, and lawful latitude. In
  an LLM context those typed constitutional declarations form a declarative
  reasoning program: they constrain the meanings, dependencies, authorities,
  lawful choices, refusals, and re-entry routes available to the traversal.
  `Axiomatic` names their constitutional character; it does not collapse
  obligations, prohibitions, refusals, latitude, or authority relations into
  one untyped rule class. The complete selected Source STDO documents remain
  the Program's authoritative notation.
- **Candidate a_c.STDO Model** is one content-first interpretation of exact
  Source STDO under the exact `a_c` basis, closed `Sigma_STDO` signature, and
  interpretation contract. Its complete model population is
  `M = (b, I, O, E, C, L, X, V, T, J)`.
- **Accepted a_c.STDO** is the unchanged candidate model and total external
  record-provenance relation together with an external accepted semantic
  judgment over their exact interpreted identity and model-content digest.
- **STDO Programmatic Semantic Index Product** is one immutable carrier-native,
  machine-addressable, basis-bound, and source-addressed encoding of accepted
  `a_c.STDO`. It is designed to
  preserve the governing semantics material to declared LLM reasoning while
  reducing the context required to supply them, and is bound to one exact
  Source STDO basis, one build tenant, one exact carrier basis, one
  representation profile, and one canonical content digest.
- **Programmatic Semantic Index** is the Product's identity-addressed graph and
  passive-constraint navigation surface. Software can resolve, validate,
  traverse, and project it without inferring governing relations from textual
  similarity.
- **STDO Representation Algebra** is the carrier-independent `a_c.STDO` model
  contract defined by this Product's requirements. It imports all eight `a_c`
  record families unchanged and specializes them through `Sigma_STDO`.
- **Semantic Compilation** is, for this Product, one bounded
  `F_P[v_compile]` traversal in
  which an LLM probabilistically extracts and composes meaning-bearing
  declarations from an exact prose corpus into a candidate closed,
  source-addressed representation algebra. It must expose ambiguity, omission,
  or insufficiency and cannot grant its candidate semantic or acceptance
  authority.
- **Semantic Compilation Candidate** is the complete proposed `a_c.STDO` model,
  total record-provenance relation, selection rows, source routes, compilation
  residual uncertainty, and exact invocation/provenance envelope
  deterministically constructed around the unchanged `F_P[v_compile]`
  proposal. It becomes eligible for semantic
  selection only after `F_D[v_candidate_structure] = eligible`; carrier
  construction additionally requires an exact `F_H[v_select]` ledger and
  external `J_B.decision = accepted` over the unchanged interpreted model.
- **Build Tenant** is one independent HOW realization of that algebra.
- **Representation Profile** is a tenant-owned, versioned mapping from the
  common graph and constraints into one exact carrier basis.
- **STDO.gtl** is the GTL build tenant's canonical `a_c.STDO.GTL` encoding. It
  is a passive frozen-GTL `ModulePublication`, not a frozen-GTL `GtlProgram`.
- **F_D**, **F_P**, and **F_H** are the exact generic `a_c`
  traversal-functor-kind identities for deterministic evaluation/proof,
  probabilistic computation, and explicit human adjudication respectively.
  They classify how one declared vector or edge traversal is mapped from its
  upstream contract to its result; they are not names for domain operations.
- **F_P Consumer** is a probabilistic LLM bound to the exact generic `a_c` `F_P`
  functor-kind identity for one declared external traversal contract. It
  receives a
  programmatic semantic index and separately supplied workspace input, intent,
  frame, and capability budget.
- **F_P Semantic Compiler** is a probabilistic LLM bound to the same exact
  generic `a_c` `F_P` functor-kind identity under the distinct `v_compile`
  traversal contract. It
  receives exact prose, compilation intent, frames, capability budget, common
  algebra law, and proposal output contract and returns only a carrier-neutral
  proposal or lawful stop. Deterministic domain HOW may bind that unchanged
  proposal to its exact invocation and provenance; it cannot alter meaning or
  judge the resulting candidate.
- **Workspace Input** is consumer-supplied evidence about the workspace being
  reasoned over. It is not embedded in or owned by the index.
- **Reasoning Invocation** is one external use of a programmatic semantic index
  by an `F_P` consumer under one host-owned declared traversal
  identity. Its model, workspace, prompt, output, and cost coordinates are not
  Product identity coordinates.
- **Executive Context Assignment** is an external immutable binding of one
  parent index Product, workspace basis, outcome, intent, target actor, Source
  STDO
  Executive, Worker, or Reviewer engagement role, activated frames, authority,
  capability, evidence, stop states, and context budget.
- **Projection** is the least declared subgraph fixed point over an exact
  assignment's index seeds, with every required constraint and interpretation
  relation for its bounded purpose. That exact role-, intent-, and frame-bound
  closure is the compact reasoning program supplied to its LLM consumer.
- **Context Packet** is an invocation-input bundle containing the exact
  assignment, selected frame declarations or routes, carrier-native projection,
  projection manifest, required evidence, and stop, residual, and source
  re-entry routes. It is not a new Product or authority surface.
- **Structural Admission** is deterministic validation of exact bases,
  canonical carrier bytes, closed references, and carrier law. It does not
  judge the semantic truth of an LLM response.
- **Semantic Selection Ledger** is external `F_H[v_select]` evidence binding
  the exact candidate `a_c.STDO` identity and digest, evaluated Source STDO
  population, and every retained, omitted, or uncertain selection to
  its rationale, source route, and authoring authority. The ledger is itself
  the exact `F_H[v_select]` decision; its content identity, subject digest,
  actor, authority, grant, evidence, and basis make that decision durable.
  It has no second acceptance record.
- **Authority Acceptance Record** is an external immutable `F_H` decision over
  one exact subject identity and digest under one exact actor, authority, grant,
  basis, time, and evidence boundary.
- **F_P Observation** is empirical evidence about probabilistic usefulness or
  failure. Repetition may characterize behavior but does not turn it into
  deterministic semantic proof.

The Representation Algebra is constitutional WHAT. It is not a physical common
model, shared serialized intermediate representation, deterministic evaluator,
workspace assessor, executable plan, vector database, similarity-retrieval
authority, or runtime-truth system.

## Symbolic program and index relation

Source STDO prose does not merely describe a later program: at constitutional
level, its typed rules, definitions, authorities, relations, and latitude are
the STDO Symbolic Axiomatic Program in human-readable notation. When supplied
in an LLM context they already constrain probabilistic reasoning as a program,
but their complete prose form imposes avoidable context, token, and cost load.

Subscript `B` binds a value to the exact Source STDO basis, `C` denotes the
semantic-compilation coordinate, and `T` denotes one selected build tenant. A
trailing `*` marks a proposed semantic value that has not yet received the
acceptance needed by the next relation. `Y` names the raw semantic proposal,
`Q` names its deterministic invocation/provenance-bound candidate, `D_Q` its
deterministic structural result, `G`
the immutable tenant carrier, `D_{G,T}` its separate deterministic admission
judgment, `R` a reference-frame configuration, and `K` a capability and
context-budget coordinate.

`Encode_T` constructs `G_{B,T}` before admission. The carrier's canonical bytes,
content identity, and digest do not encode or imply its admission disposition.
`F_D[v_carrier_admission]` evaluates declared properties of that unchanged
carrier and returns only `D_{G,T}`. An `admitted` judgment authorizes the exact
carrier-and-judgment pair to cross the declared structural gate; it does not
transform, rewrite, reissue, or rename `G_{B,T}`. A `refuse` judgment blocks
downstream use while leaving the evaluated carrier bytes and identity
unchanged.

This Product instantiates `{Source STDO prose} -> F_P[v_compile] ->
carrier-neutral representation algebra -> tenant encoding`. The common
compiler stops at `Y_B*`; domain HOW may only bind its unchanged content into
`Q_B*`. No tenant profile or carrier basis may influence
which Source STDO meaning it proposes. A tenant that cannot represent accepted
`a_c.STDO` returns a profile gap or refusal rather than asking the compiler to omit
meaning.

For tenant `T = GTL`, `G_{B,T}` is GTL's typed, closed, canonical encoding of
accepted `a_c.STDO`; it is `a_c.STDO.GTL`. The triple
`(G_{B,T}, J_B, D_{G,T})` is an admitted reliable carrier relation
only when `D_{G,T} = admitted`. GTL does not own or redefine the
carrier-independent algebra. Reliability here is structural: GTL makes the
carrier addressable, reproducible, and rejectable under an exact carrier law.
It does not make the `F_P` extraction deterministic or semantically true. Any
claim that this compilation pattern governs another Product belongs to that
Product or an owning shared method, not to this Product by generalization.

Every accepted record resolves through its unchanged `P_B` row to the owning
authority, bounded context, governed scope, selected subject basis, exact model
basis, and exact Source STDO member. The
`F_P[v_compile]` semantic compiler proposes the relation between `S_B`,
candidate `M_B*`, and total record provenance `P_B*`. Every model residual is
retained in `M_B*.X`; `U_B*` records
compilation uncertainty about the proposal. The Semantic Selection Ledger
records the exact `F_H[v_select]` disposition of every proposal and residual
against `S_B`; `J_B` separately records `F_H[v_accept_interpretation]`
acceptance of the unchanged interpreted-model identity and model-content
digest. Carrier construction only serializes that accepted `a_c.STDO` relation.

The representation claim is bounded and reviewable. It claims preservation of
the governing semantics material to the declared reasoning purposes and
accepted semantic selection, not equivalence to every conceivable reading of
natural-language prose. Omission, ambiguity, or insufficient representation is
retained as explicit uncertainty or a Source STDO re-entry route rather than
silently completed by the carrier or consumer.

The index is **programmatic** because software can validate its closed identity
universe, follow declared edges, compute declared least closures, create
role-bound projections, and return from every represented record to its exact
Source STDO member. It is **not executable**: it contains no GTL callable
program, workflow,
model invocation, hidden reasoning strategy, event loop, or runtime truth.

Embedding or vector retrieval may help an external host acquire unstructured
workspace evidence. Similarity, rank, or proximity cannot establish which STDO
identity, authority, bounded context, dependency, constraint, omission, or
closure applies. Those relations come only from the exact symbolic index and
its Source STDO authority.

## Product statement

An STDO Programmatic Semantic Index Product is one canonical, basis-bound, and
source-addressed carrier encoding of accepted `a_c.STDO`. It carries the exact
model populations, relations, authorities, bounded contexts, source routes,
and residual uncertainty accepted under `F_H[v_select]` for its
declared reasoning purposes. It does not prove a complete or unique
natural-language interpretation. Its exact role-, intent-, and frame-bound
least lawful closures act as compact probabilistic reasoning programs within
an LLM context.

For one exact Source STDO basis and carrier realization, an STDO Programmatic
Semantic Index Product provides:

1. a compact representation of the complete accepted `O/E/C/L/X/V/T/J`
   populations, exact external resolutions, and total `P_B` relation;
2. passive constraints declaring what an `F_P[v_reason]` consumer must preserve or avoid;
3. the authority, bounded-context, basis, scope, semantic-address, and
   provenance coordinates required to interpret those records;
4. role-bound projections for authorized Executive, Worker, and Reviewer frame
   assignments and capability budgets;
5. exact Source STDO member re-entry routes when the compressed index is
   insufficient;
6. reproducible byte, token, and cost measurements against the exact source;
   and
7. external, digest-bound semantic-selection evidence sufficient for `F_H`
   authority to review what compression retained, omitted, or left uncertain.

The Product is consumed only through a complete declared invocation contract.
That contract supplies the admitted complete index or one lawful projection,
workspace input, reasoning intent, selected frame, capability and context
budget, traversal identity, output contract, gates, provenance, and stop
states. A role-bound invocation additionally supplies an admitted Executive
Context Assignment, Context Projection Manifest, and Context Packet. Merely
placing index and workspace bytes beside a prompt is exploratory probabilistic
processing, not a claimed `a_c` `F_P[v_reason]` traversal or role activation. The LLM acts
as a bounded probabilistic interpreter of the represented symbolic axiomatic
program. It does not execute the GTL carrier, prescribe a deterministic answer,
or make the response authoritative.

The canonical parent index is complete relative to its accepted semantic
selection; it is not claimed to be one globally smallest expression of STDO.
Minimality belongs to a frozen consumption coordinate:

```text
Index_A = project(Index_B, assignment_seeds(A), L_context)
Index_A = (M_A, P_A)
P_A = P_B restricted to Local_{M_A}
```

For one frozen assignment, seed set, parent index, and closure law, `M_A` is the
unique least lawful record closure and `P_A` is its exact provenance
restriction. Its token count is measured only after the
selected tenant serializes it. If that measured payload exceeds `K_A`,
contextualization returns `budget_exceeded`; the budget never trims `Index_A`.
Removing any member of the governing closure is semantic loss, not compression.

## Fundamental traversal-functor binding

This Product uses the three exact generic `a_c` concepts unchanged under
`b_ac`:

```text
F_D = urn:stdo:concept:axiomatic-calculus:f-d
F_P = urn:stdo:concept:axiomatic-calculus:f-p
F_H = urn:stdo:concept:axiomatic-calculus:f-h
```

They remain owned by `AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md` in
`urn:stdo:bounded-context:axiomatic-calculus`. Their use here does not mint
local meanings or transfer their authority:

```text
F_K[v](upstream_v) -> result_v
```

`F_K` is one of the imported functor kinds `F_D`, `F_P`, or `F_H`; `v` is one
exact declared traversal identity. Bracket application classifies
that traversal under the functor kind. `SemanticCompile`, semantic selection,
carrier admission, and workspace reasoning are domain operations realized by
different `v` values; none is an alias for, or redefinition of, `F_K`.

`v_compile`, `v_candidate_structure`, `v_selection_structure`, `v_select`,
`v_accept_interpretation`,
`v_carrier_admission`, `v_context_admission`, `v_frame_assignment`,
`v_accept_frame_basis`, and `v_reason` are role-labelled traversal variables in
this constitution. A variable name is not an identity. Each realized invocation
or decision must bind it to one non-empty absolute `traversal_ref` in the exact
applicable candidate, evaluation receipt, ledger, admission receipt, assignment,
acceptance, or host invocation record. Two records sharing a role label do not
thereby share a traversal identity.

- domain HOW performs deterministic acquisition, construction,
  canonicalization, serialization, digesting, and measurement, while an
  `F_D[v]` traversal evaluates or proves declared properties of those results;
- `F_P` applies both to one bounded semantic-compilation traversal from exact
  prose into a candidate `a_c.STDO` model and to later bounded LLM traversals over an
  admitted semantic index; each use has its own complete traversal contract;
  and
- `F_H[v_select]` reviews and selects the exact semantic-compilation candidate
  and adjudicates its material ambiguity; separately identified `F_H[v]`
  traversals own frame-basis, profile, interpreted-model, Product, and release
  acceptance decisions under their exact human or bounded-proxy grants.

The programmatic-index payload is optimized for `F_P`. The source project and
external qualification records still preserve the surrounding `F_D` and `F_H`
boundaries.

The graph-native ODD identities with equal shorthand are distinct concepts.
This Product declares no identity, equivalence, or implicit import between
`urn:stdo:concept:axiomatic-calculus:f-*` and
`urn:stdo:concept:graph-native-odd:f-*`. A consuming ODD Product may establish
its own directional specialization under an exact context, basis, scope,
preservation, loss, refusal, and invalidation relation.

## Programmatic index boundary

The consumer relation is:

```text
F_P[v_reason](Index_B, W, I, R, K) -> J_reason
```

where `Index_B = (M_B, P_B)` is the unchanged accepted `a_c.STDO` model and
total record-provenance relation represented by the programmatic index, `W` is
a separately supplied
workspace input, `I` is the reasoning intent, `R` is the selected
reference-frame view, `K` is the consumer capability/context budget, and `J_reason`
is probabilistic reasoning output or an explicit hold, gap, or refusal.

That compact relation is not a second definition of `F_P`. A host claiming an
`a_c` `F_P[v]` invocation binds a complete traversal contract:

```text
T_P = (traversal_ref, upstream, target, context, role, gates,
       provenance, stop_states)
upstream = (Index_B, W, I, R, K)
target   = output_contract(J_reason)
```

`traversal_ref` identifies one host-owned declared traversal.
The contract declares required upstream assets, target/output contract,
required context, role or capability expectation, governing evaluators and
gates, provenance obligation, and lawful stop, hold, gap, continuation, and
completion states. The external host owns that contract and any HoG or ABG
realization. `Index_B` remains a passive immutable index and contains no runtime.

Every `F_D`, `F_P`, or `F_H` claim in this project is qualified by such a
declared traversal contract. Supporting prose may name the functor allocation
without repeating the packet, but an execution or decision record cannot omit
its `traversal_ref` and still claim the exact `a_c` functor-kind identity.

`W`, `I`, `R`, `K`, and `J` may be bound by an external invocation record. They
do not mutate `Index_B` or enter its immutable Product identity. The host consuming
the Product owns prompt assembly, model invocation, workspace acquisition, and
response handling under its own authority.

Before a role-bound invocation, an authorized Executive applies Source STDO
frame declaration and activation law to create an exact assignment `A`. This
Product then defines the carrier-neutral context relation:

```text
Z(A)    = mandatory frame refs + exact role refs + explicit model seed refs
Index_A = project(Index_B, Z(A), L_context) = (M_A, P_A)
P_A     = P_B restricted to Local_{M_A}
K_A     = A.context_budget
contextualize(Index_B, A) -> ContextPacket(Index_A, K_A) | hold
```

`L_context` preserves the exact semantic-address, bounded-context, authority,
scope, basis, dependency, constraint, evidence, exclusion, refusal,
invalidation, and re-entry relations required by the assignment. The full
index is lawful when selected and within capability; otherwise no mandatory
closure member may be removed merely to meet `K`.

An Executive may target itself, a Worker, or a Reviewer. Self-targeting creates
no self-grant or independent-review relation. Worker context carries only its
inherited bounded operation grants and returns a closed result to Executive.
Reviewer context binds exact-subject acquisition and independence conditions,
prohibits repair while retaining the Reviewer claim, and also returns to
Executive. The complete common contract is
`requirements/REQ-P-EXECUTIVE-CONTEXT-PROJECTION.md`.

## Product identity

Using paths relative to `specification/`, the Product WHAT member set is ordered
as `INTENT.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, then active `requirements/REQ-P-*.md` members by
ascending path. Its identity is:

```text
what_member_set_identity = sha256(
  for each member: utf8(path) + NUL + lowercase_sha256(member_bytes) + LF
)
```

The candidate model content is addressed before semantic selection:

```text
model_content_identity = "sha256:" + sha256(JCS(M_B*))
```

After the Semantic Selection Ledger exists, the interpreted-model identity is:


Its canonical bytes are exact RFC 8785 JCS bytes with no framing bytes, and its
identity is the `AuthorityAcceptanceIdentity` prefix followed by
`sha256(JCS(AuthorityAcceptanceRecord))`. The record is created only after the
accepting actor is presented the exact subject identity and digest.
`subject_sha256` addresses the exact file or canonical record bytes selected by
`subject_kind`. `basis_refs`, non-null `admitting_authority_refs`, and
`evidence_refs` are duplicate-free and sorted by ascending unsigned UTF-16 code
units before JCS. For `subject_kind = "reference_frame_basis"`,
`admitting_authority_refs` is non-empty and equals exactly the applicable
Product Definition's `reference_frame_bases[*].authority` string set after
sorting, without URI rewriting. For every other subject kind it is `null`.
`grant_identity` locates the durable authority grant and `grant_scope` states the
bounded permission exercised for this subject. The record points inward to that
unchanged subject; the subject never embeds the later acceptance-record
identity. Repository ownership, a Git author string, a chat instruction,
possession of a digest, or successful `F_D` validation does not substitute for
the declared actor identity and grant.

`tenant-label` is the unique stable label assigned to a build-tenant identity by
