# STDO Representation Product

Status: active source definition; no released STDO Representation Product
exists.

## Product Terms

Within `urn:stdo-representation:bounded-context:product`:

- **STDO Representation Source Project** is this mutable workspace while it
  defines and builds candidate Products.
- **STDO Representation Product** is one immutable carrier assessment bundle
  bound to one exact Source STDO basis, one build tenant, one exact carrier
  basis, and one representation profile.
- **Source STDO** is the exact immutable STDO release whose constitutional
  meanings and relations are represented.
- **STDO Representation Algebra** is the carrier-independent set of semantic
  domains, relations, operations, laws, and acceptance predicates defined by
  this Product's requirements.
- **Build Tenant** is one independent HOW realization of the common algebra.
- **Representation Profile** is a tenant-owned, versioned mapping from every
  applicable algebraic domain, relation, operation, and law into its selected
  carrier.
- **Semantic Address** is an exact, basis-qualified locator for a meaning or
  relation owned by Source STDO.
- **Projection** is a bounded representation selected for an explicit intent,
  frame, corpus, and capability budget.
- **Residual** is an explicit record of material excluded from or unresolved by
  a projection or tenant representation.
- **Coverage Finding** classifies one required semantic subject or relation as
  `represented`, `limited`, `unresolved`, or `unrepresentable`.
- **Assessment Disposition** is `complete`, `limited`, or `blocked` under the
  acceptance law in the requirements.

The Representation Algebra is constitutional WHAT. It is not a physical graph,
schema, syntax, file layout, intermediate representation, or executable plan.

## Product statement

An STDO Representation Product answers, for one exact tenant realization:

1. what Source STDO corpus was assessed;
2. how every required semantic subject and relation maps to the common algebra;
3. how the tenant realizes that algebra in its selected carrier;
4. what compact projections are available under declared context budgets;
5. what is limited, unresolved, or unrepresentable; and
6. whether the artifacts and findings can be regenerated and independently
   checked.

An honest carrier boundary is a valid assessment result. A `limited` assessment
Product cannot claim to be a complete STDO representation. A `blocked` result
is evidence, not an admitted Product.

## Product identity

An immutable Product identity binds:

1. Source STDO release identity and installed-manifest digest;
2. this Product WHAT identity and requirements-member-set digest;
3. build-tenant identity;
4. exact carrier basis identity;
5. representation-profile identity and digest;
6. canonical tenant-artifact root and member-set digests;
7. semantic-address map, coverage matrix, projection, and measurement digests;
8. conformance and regeneration-record identities; and
9. assessment disposition.

Changing any coordinate creates a different candidate Product. A mutable
version selector is discovery input and never part of immutable identity.

## Product Authority

- Source STDO owns every represented constitutional meaning, semantic address,
  authority relation, bounded context, dependency, and admission rule.
- STDO Representation WHAT owns the abstract representation algebra,
  projection rules, coverage states, comparison rules, and acceptance
  predicates.
- Each build tenant owns its selected carrier basis, representation profile,
  canonicalization, concrete artifacts, and tenant-local evidence.
- A carrier's external authority owns that carrier's syntax and semantics; it
  does not amend Source STDO or the common algebra.
- Human acceptance under this Product's governing STDO basis admits immutable
  STDO Representation Products.

No tenant mapping, schema validation, graph edge, generated artifact, or size
improvement mints semantic authority.

## Product contents

An admitted `complete` or `limited` Product contains or content-addresses:

- exact Source STDO, Product WHAT, tenant, carrier, and profile identities;
- a complete Source STDO member and semantic-address inventory;
- the tenant's direct realization of the Representation Algebra;
- a total semantic-address and relation coverage matrix;
- explicit residual and carrier-boundary findings;
- bounded projections with their intents, frames, budgets, included closures,
  and residuals;
- reproducible source, representation, and projection measurements;
- positive, negative, mutation, and applicable cross-tenant comparison
  evidence;
- independent regeneration inputs, procedure, receipts, and comparison; and
- acquisition and provenance records for every immutable input.

## Reference Frame Basis

Every assessment and projection declares its frame instance, governing intent,
authority basis, admitted corpus, carrier capability, and measurement budget.
The baseline families are:

- constitutional and semantic fidelity;
- identity, provenance, and basis binding;
- bounded-context isolation and authority preservation;
- dependency, composition, overlay, and projection law;
- carrier constructability and context bounds; and
- assurance, evidence, falsification, comparison, and regeneration.

These families organize assessment. They create no semantic or decision
authority beyond the cited owners.

## Current boundary

The source project selects STDO `v2.4.3-rc.3` as its constitutional basis. Its
operative representation algebra and acceptance obligations are the active
requirements under `specification/requirements/`.

The GTL and JSON Schema build tenants are registered independent HOW
realizations. The GTL tenant has an exact frozen carrier basis. The JSON Schema
tenant has not selected a dialect. Neither tenant has an accepted
representation profile, constructed representation, candidate, or released
Product.

Execution, HoG traversal, and ABG runtime realization are outside this Product.
