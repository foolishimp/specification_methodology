## Outcome

Publish the exact eight-member `0.1.0` candidate as immutable
`v0.1.0-rc.1`, advance the annotated `v0.1.0` selector to that same commit,
and accept only the independently reacquired exact cut.

## Release subject

`releases/v0.1.0.md` owns the exact Product member inventory, release-scoped
claims, dependency basis, genesis relation, exclusions, and evidence boundary.
The source repository carries additional specification, evidence, history, and
bookkeeping bytes that are not Product members.

## In scope

- Bind the exact accepted project frame basis into the Product Definition.
- Freeze and qualify one source candidate under the installed STDO Release
  Method.
- Commit the exact carrier.
- Publish `rc/0.1.0`, annotated immutable `v0.1.0-rc.1`, annotated mutable
  selector `v0.1.0`, and aligned `release/0.1.0` atomically where supported.
- Verify the remote tag objects, peels, branches, tree, Product members, and
  release claims.
- Reacquire the public cut into a clean temporary checkout and run the exact
  cut review without repair.
- Record Product-owner acceptance only over the immutable reviewed subject.
- Record publication-caused ticket and source-work closure on continuing
  `main` without moving the immutable RC tag.

## Out of scope

- Changing Product meaning, the eight-member inventory, or release claims
  after RC publication.
- Moving `v0.1.0-rc.1` after it is published.
- Adding GTL, GraphFunctions, ABG, automatic frame selection, or local engine
  code.
- Treating repository visibility, validation, publication, or selector
  advancement as Product acceptance.

## Acceptance

- Pre-RC qualification passes against one deterministic candidate inventory.
- The project frame basis is accepted by exact URI and digest and the Product
  Definition validates with that binding.
- The remote immutable RC tag and selector are annotated and peel to the same
  commit; both release branches resolve to that commit.
- Clean public reacquisition reproduces the repository tree, eight-member
  inventory, dependency coordinates, map, native skill, release claims, and
  declared tests.
- Independent exact-cut review reports no release-blocking finding.
- The Product owner accepts the exact RC tag object, peeled commit, tree,
  Product inventory digest, and release-record bytes.

## Non-closure

- A local commit, green source tests, or intended tag name does not publish a
  Product.
- A pushed tag without remote peel and highest-RC verification does not close
  publication.
- Any qualifying-byte repair after publication requires `v0.1.0-rc.2`; the
  existing immutable tag never moves.
- Acceptance over a mutable branch, selector name, or unreacquired checkout is
  invalid.
