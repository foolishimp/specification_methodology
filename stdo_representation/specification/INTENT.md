# STDO Representation Intent

Give an LLM a consistent, source-reenterable representation of exact current
STDO for bounded reasoning, with explicit logic and uncertainty. An LLM
authors meaning once; a_c and Axiom Indexer provide deterministic checked
carriers, indexes and views of that declared meaning. Source STDO remains
authority. Every current output binds the same frozen semantic corpus and
cohort version, while historical evidence retains its original identity.

## Deterministic boundary

Provider tools preserve declared data, references and source routes. They do
not invent semantics, accept the methodology, decide applicability or grant
operations. The native skill routes attention and source re-entry; the LLM
selects frames, judges evidence and writes any ordered text supplied to the
pure joiner. Exact preservation and fresh source-based review make compression
useful; small bytes, counts and green checks do not establish fidelity.

## Success criterion

The current model, Axiom program/map, bounded projections and selected viewers
agree on one exact representation, preserve material conditions and residuals,
and support fresh native use with reliable source recovery. Missing meaning
causes explicit residuals and owner re-entry, never a false completeness claim.
