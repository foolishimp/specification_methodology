## Governing Principle

> Think globally, act locally, and externalize the invariant relation so a
> bounded actor can prove the local action globally lawful.

STDO is federal method authority. Local law may add constraint where STDO
delegates choice or a domain requires stricter rule; it cannot weaken,
contradict, or reinterpret STDO.

```text
local authority = concrete instance decisions
                + explicitly stricter non-conflicting policy
                + nothing already stated by STDO
```

## Materiality Gate

L2, L3, and L4 apply at **material** frames only.

Materiality is **not redefined here**. A material frame is one governing a
material semantic boundary as already defined at `DMM:363` — a boundary whose
change affects identity, authority, lifecycle, public contract, externally
observable Product meaning, cross-module topology, or accepted effect or
closure law.

Admission relations and persistence boundaries are added only where they are
not already entailed by that definition; independently governed transformations
are entailed by authority and lifecycle and are not restated.

A second, narrower materiality definition would leave two definitions to keep
synchronized and would exclude frames governing only closure, an effect
boundary, cross-module topology, externally observable meaning, or lifecycle
without independent entity ownership.

Subordinate values and trivial helpers inherit their owning frame's package.
Asset depth scales with frame significance, and explicit `not_applicable` with
a stated reason remains lawful throughout.

## Proposed Normative Delta — Four Laws

### L1 — Transformation Admission Completeness

For every consequential entity-transition domain, accepted design shall contain
the complete transformation-admission relation over entity identity,
predecessor basis, authority scope, candidate family, decision cardinality,
admission predicate, resulting durable fact, and supersession law — through the
existing authority evidence or an equivalent closed representation. Multiple
actors, stages, or implementations are lawful only as constituents or disjoint
instances of that relation.

A projection may produce a candidate transformation, but projection status does
not confer admission authority; any consequential result must re-enter the
governing admission relation against its explicit predecessor basis.

Extends the Authority Model at `DMM:576`, which names an `Admitter` role but not
the relation it governs, and `DMM:609`, which prohibits projections creating
authority but not self-authentication.

**Canonical minimum evidence shape:**

| Entity transition domain | Predecessor basis | Candidate family | Admission relation | Durable result | Competing-path disposition |

Competing-path disposition does **not** imply deletion. An equivalent path may
be a lawful constituent, replica, replacement, or deliberately independent
realization — provided its relationship to the singular admission law is
explicit.

*Falsifiers.*
- Two materially non-equivalent admission relations remain reachable for the
  same governed transition domain on the same entity and predecessor basis.
  Disjoint scopes or identities are not a counterexample.
- A materially equivalent competing implementation remains reachable without
  being identified as a constituent, replica, replacement, or deliberately
  independent realization.
- A projection-triggered reaction uses its own reconstructive state as
  self-authenticating admission evidence.

### L2 — Bounded Frame Conservation

Each material design frame identifies the governing frames and accepted
relations from which it derives, the bounded affected relation set, applicable
inherited invariants, authority and composition seams crossing that set, and
subordinate frames opened by unresolved material distinctions.

The result is **evaluated against** every affected governing frame, preserves
each applicable accepted relation, and updates only affected subordinate
projections. A result that changes a governing relation re-enters at that
relation before acceptance.

A bounded local action conserves governing truth; it does not rewrite it.
Changing a governing relation is explicit design re-entry, not a side effect of
local work.

A frame may derive from more than one governing frame: a persistence component
belongs to an entity lifecycle boundary and an operational consistency
boundary; an admission function belongs to a domain transition and a
runtime-authority boundary; a shared callable may be consumed by several
sibling domains without becoming the child of one. A single governing frame
remains lawful where the structure is genuinely hierarchical; it is not
universal.

This preserves the constraint-network model already stated at `DMM:485-488`.
STDO currently has no frame vocabulary — `reference frame`, `frame altitude`,
`global-to-local` all return zero occurrences across all 41 members.

*Falsifier.* A change is locally valid inside the selected frame but violates,
duplicates, or silently reinterprets an invariant in any affected governing
frame.

### L3 — Invariant Reconstruction Sufficiency

Given the accepted frame basis and a proposed bounded action, the reconstruction
package must be sufficient to recover every governing relation in the action's
causally closed affected set and to decide whether the action preserves,
changes, duplicates, or violates those relations.

The package is a projection of one accepted model, not a peer truth authority,
and need not be separate files — the requirement is the information, not the
artifact count.

Sufficiency is bound to a declared action and its affected set. It is **not** a
claim over an unbounded space of possible future changes, and reviewer
performance is not constitutional evidence.

*Falsifier.* For a declared bounded action, the package does not expose a
governing relation in that action's causally closed affected set, or does not
permit the preserve / change / duplicate / violate decision to be reached from
the package alone.

*Trial.* Named test cases with known governing relations and expected
decisions, evaluated against the package with conversation history, commentary,
prior-worker memory, and implementation folklore withheld. The trial tests
whether the package exposes the necessary information — not whether a
particular reviewer succeeded.

### L4 — Computational Realization Projection

**Component role sets.** Every material realization component has an explicit
set of semantic and operational roles. Technology capability does not confer
authority. A component's declared role set is design authority; where a role
carries authority, composition and use preserve the applicable authority
relation under `STDO-UP-004`, which is supporting law here rather than the owner
of the role model.

Three cases must be distinguished: behavior already inside an abstract declared
role is ordinary variation; a new operational responsibility and a new semantic
or authoritative role are each design changes. Only the latter two require
re-entry.

**Computational projection.** DMM's `classDiagram` at `:1020` is a
domain-structure view and deliberately shows domain identities. For each
material frame, project the domain design additionally into abstract data
types, identity and key structures, algorithms, algebraic laws, mutation and
effect boundaries, selected implementations, and domain-to-computational
mappings. Cross-view evaluation (`DMM:1126`) extends to prove the domain views
and the computational projection describe one system.

**The projection is subordinate.** It reveals repeated implementation structure;
it cannot decide semantic identity, ownership, or authority. Computational
similarity produces a *candidate*, never an adjudication:

```text
computational similarity
  -> commonization candidate
  -> semantic and operational equivalence evaluation
  -> reuse, parameterized common relation, or justified separation
```

Two components may share a `Map` or an append-only sequence while differing
materially in identity and key law, ownership, ordering, admissible mutation,
consistency and concurrency, retention, failure semantics, access policy,
lifecycle, or authority over truth. Any of those differences justifies
separation.

**Disposition is two-part.** *Semantic disposition*: existing accepted relation,
extension, or new candidate relation. *Realization disposition*: consume,
implement locally, optimize, adapt, or deliberately duplicate under stated
non-semantic constraints — isolation, substrate, performance, security, or
dependency ownership are each lawful grounds.

The invariant is not "never rebuild equivalent code." It is:

> A local realization must not present an existing accepted semantic or
> authoritative relation as newly owned local truth.

Placed near decision completeness (`DMM:458`), Prime contraction (`DMM:679`),
and recurrence extraction. `library_usage` is untouched; `TICKET_METHOD`
requires the active plan to carry accepted dispositions but does not own their
semantics.

*Falsifiers.*
- Two domain-named components implement materially equivalent abstract data
  types or algorithms without a semantic disposition.
- A generic building block becomes a new semantic authority.
- Superficially similar operations are commonized despite different identity,
  ordering, failure, ownership, or lifecycle law.
- A local realization presents an existing accepted relation as newly owned
  local truth.

*Worked interpretation* (informative, not mandatory technology):

```text
domain frame          computational projection
Catalog        ->     Namespaced Map<CompoundKey, ImmutableValue>
Registry       ->     Namespaced Map<IdentityKey, Registration>
Ledger         ->     AppendOnlySequence<Entry>
Event Store    ->     AppendOnlySequence<Event> + Index<Coordinate, Event>
Projection     ->     PureFold<ValidatedImmutablePrefix, TypedView>
```

Four domain nouns over two computational structures raises the commonization
**question**; it does not answer it. Whether catalog, registry, ledger, and log
are one storage authority or four depends on their identity, ownership,
ordering, retention, and authority law — which only the semantic evaluation
decides. Likewise six domain-local equality helpers raise one ordered-sequence
equality candidate; whether they merge, share a parameterized relation, or stay
separate is a semantic question, and declaring the relation once may be
sufficient without merging any implementation.

## Acceptance Conditions

### A1 — Two separate sufficiency proofs

**Federal compression sufficiency.** `stdo_compressed` tells an actor which
invariant assets and reasoning operations are required. It remains a derived
read model and prompt input, never source authority; where a question cannot be
decided from it, the actor falls back to the released source member and must not
invent a local interpretation.

**Consumer reconstruction sufficiency.** The consumer's concrete design package
supplies the Product-specific invariant values.

*Trial input set.* Released STDO compression, Product authority, active-frame
invariant package, bounded ticket. No commentary, no historical dialogue.

*Trial cases.* Named, with known governing relations and expected decisions:
identify the governing frames and affected relation set; recover inherited
invariants; detect a locally valid but globally invalid proposal; map domain
entities to computational structures; identify an existing accepted relation;
reject a competing truth or authority path; state the exact local
implementation and proof boundary.

If the compressed input cannot drive these, the release is incomplete even when
the source members are correct.

### A2 — Shrink and deletion test

After consumer adoption, **every general method clause duplicated locally must
be deleted.** ABIogenesis retains only concrete Product and module frames,
actual entities and lifecycle rows, actual authority and composition seams,
selected component role sets, selected common callables, the current affected
relation set, and any explicitly stricter local policy. If no such content
remains, the local method artifact is deleted rather than preserved
ceremonially.

Method sections that cannot move upstream name exactly what L1–L4 still fail to
state, and become the next increment.

### A3 — Clause quality

Every adopted law carries a clause identifier and a decidable falsification
condition, is domain-independent, proportional under the materiality gate,
consistent with current members, independently reviewed, and useful beyond
ABIogenesis-specific names and runtime structure.
