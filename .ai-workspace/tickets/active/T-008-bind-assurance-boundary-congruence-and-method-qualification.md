## Candidate Scope

`SPEC_METHOD.md` solely owns concise `STDO-UP-022` federal law:

- assurance supporting promotion or closure binds the exact claimed subject
  and authoritative composition relation;
- per-file evidence cannot establish a composite carrier-set claim, whose
  satisfaction and conflicts are decided at its declared composite boundary;
- mechanical enforcement names an executable or reproducible predicate and a
  witness reachable through the declared ordinary assurance path; absent either,
  the claim stays open or narrows to the planned property;
- claim quantifier, population, and scope do not exceed evaluated evidence;
  generalization declares its inference relation, comparable population,
  counterexample treatment, and governing evidence;
- later counterevidence invalidates dependent verdicts until supersession,
  withdrawal, or requalification;
- generic method sufficiency explicitly consumes the Probabilistic Work
  Boundary, `STDO-UP-020`, and Reconstruction Litmus;
- a fresh competent constructor operates inside a declared capability, context,
  and configuration envelope using only the declared ordinary method and
  authority surfaces, independently authorized evaluation declares its governing
  basis and comparison predicate, and material expected/reference outcomes,
  source exemplars/incumbents, author memory, and ad hoc rescue remain withheld
  from the constructor until its result freezes where exposure would compromise
  independence;
- after freeze, the evaluator compares the frozen construction with the
  mandatory governing semantic basis; any separately held material expected/
  reference outcome, if one exists and is applicable, is optional and explicitly
  non-authoritative evidence, and equivalence to it is required only where the
  governing basis requires it;
- a lawful alternative output permitted by the basis passes, subjective
  similarity cannot prove semantic conformance/equivalence, and none of byte/
  structural identity, unique derivation, determinism, or incumbent equality is
  a generic comparison criterion; and
- post-exposure revision is a declared method constituent and qualification
  boundary or a new qualification subject, and cannot retroactively pass the
  original frozen construction.

`DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md` adds one compact IACS revision obligation: accepted
Ontology/design re-entry admits additions, removals, merges, and splits;
re-derivation and delta classification cover the complete set; Promotion and
Boundary Inflation rerun over that full set; conformance/checker subjects bind
the declared composite carrier relation; and implementation reconciles before
closure. It consumes `STDO-UP-022` without restating federal law.
