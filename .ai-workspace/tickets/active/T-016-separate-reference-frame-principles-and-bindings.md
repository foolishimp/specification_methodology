## Intake Triage

**Substantive?** Yes. The pure method currently contains a named application
profile, Product adoption guidance, and consumer discovery provenance. The
optional baseline also describes a Product binding as though the profile owned
it and contradicts its inherited Worker operation grant.

**Boundary crossed?** Reference Frame Method, optional STDO reference-frame
profile, Specification Method's existing Project Reference-Frame Basis,
Product-owned STDO source-project binding, compressions, and tests. No
occurrence theory, runtime, downstream Product, release, or another Product's
adoption is in scope.

**Smallest lawful re-entry.** Product repricing. This Product adds one required
invariant-conservation frame and adopts the optional STDO profile, then the
method, profile, and binding relations make their constitutional owners and
operation-authority conditions explicit and fail closed.

## Governed Outcome

1. `REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md` owns only substrate-neutral declaration,
   activation, evaluation, relation, result, coverage, revision, and
   qualification principles.
2. `STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md` owns only its optional Executive, Worker,
   Reviewer, specialist, and testing profile mappings and their qualification.
3. `SPEC_METHOD.md` owns the Product-binding relation. A Project
   Reference-Frame Basis binds exact method/profile basis, admitting authority,
   frames, actor-binding rules, capability and operation-grant requirements,
   scopes, result and coverage relations, triggers, and invalidation law.
4. Profile availability, Product adoption, and execution-scoped activation
   remain three distinct relations.
5. The Worker label grants no mutation authority. An effectful Worker may
   mutate only the exact subject and write territory named by its inherited
   owner grant; missing, ambiguous, or out-of-territory authority refuses.
6. This Product's bound Fundamental Invariant Conservation frame evaluates one
   named invariant against the complete finite closure of its exact owning
   authorities at declared lifecycle triggers.
7. Consumer discovery history remains commentary, not constitutional method
   law.

## Non-Closure Conditions

- the pure method defines, selects, imports, or owns an application profile,
  role mapping, Product binding, adoption, consumer, or runtime;
- the optional profile mints Product frame-set or adoption authority;
- a Product binding cannot reconstruct exact frames, actor-binding rules,
  capability and grant requirements, scopes, triggers, result relations, or
  invalidation;
- a role label or broad workspace access is treated as mutation authority;
- a test-local frame evaluator substitutes for method, profile, or
  Product-owned binding authority;
- a named fundamental invariant can be reviewed without acquiring every exact
  owning authority in its finite closure;
- compression or index projections contradict their deciding sources; or
- occurrence, runtime, release, or downstream adoption work is inferred from
  this repair.
