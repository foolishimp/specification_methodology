## Candidate Scope

`SPEC_METHOD.md` solely owns one concise `STDO-UP-021` relation:

- every admission owner independently derives the complete enclosing relation;
- derivation, validation, and admission bind one exact admission-valid basis,
  with effect-free re-entry if declared currentness advances;
- participating owners use an equal or design-declared coherent composite basis;
- every participant and equality join validates before any transition effect;
- the complete transition is one semantic commit, never a participant subset;
  and
- standing qualification covers every owner and supported boundary through
  direct, supported composite/nested, forged, stale or advanced-basis,
  ambiguous enclosing-relation, incoherent multi-owner-basis, competing same-
  scope-authority, fresh-process, exact-replay-equality, and post-validation
  atomic-publication cases.

`DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md` adds one compact declaration obligation for:

- owner and supported boundary;
- exact basis identity and continuity;
- enclosing relation, participants, and equality joins;
- refusal and effect boundaries;
- atomic unit and decomposability; every materially distinct post-validation
  partial-failure boundary, or a design-declared dominance/equivalence proof
  that named observations cover the complete surface;
- reconstruction and supported structural forms; and
- competing paths.

Qualification is instantiated per owner and boundary. Unsupported composite or
nested forms require accepted design authority and proof that no weaker or
competing admission path exists.
