## Boundary

STDO already owns normative construction algebra, necessary causal order,
authority relations, admissibility, refusal, and evidence invariants. An
agentic construction execution protocol is therefore method law when it states
the abstract relation required to drive a probabilistic coder.

It does not cross into consumer realization unless it selects or implements a
model, prompt, tool, process, worker topology, repository layout, runtime,
workflow engine, or conformance governor.

```text
normative execution relation       STDO
concrete orchestration machinery   consumer
```

The proposal must preserve the existing exclusions on executable STDO
machinery, mandatory review-round counts, candidate thresholds, automatic
ticket creation, numeric progress algorithms, and prescribed collaboration or
solution-search procedures.

## Structural Update

### 1. `SPEC_METHOD.md` owns the protocol

Add one top-level **Agentic Construction Execution** section after
`Proportional Method And Delivery (STDO-UP-014)` and before specialized
migration protocols.

That section owns the complete generic execution relation:

```text
accepted governing basis
  -> bounded affected relation set
  -> selected computational relations
  -> delegated execution and decision authority
  -> construction
  -> self-review
  -> independent live-surface assessment
  -> accept | local repair | re-enter | reject
  -> exact accepted checkpoint
  -> next bounded action
```

The section applies when a consumer delegates construction to a probabilistic
or agentic worker. It defines required relations and decisions, not a required
ceremony or artifact count.

### 2. `DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md` consumes the protocol

Amend `6A. Design To Module To (Implementation, Unit Tests) Evidence Route`
only enough to bind module realization to the `SPEC_METHOD` execution
protocol. Do not restate worker, assessor, transition, or advancement law in
DMM.

The candidate `STDO-UP-017` through `STDO-UP-019` clauses remain the design
inputs to execution:

- affected governing frames and invariants;
- transformation-admission relations;
- reconstructable invariant basis; and
- computational realization projection and dispositions.

### 3. `TICKET_METHOD.md` carries selection, not semantics

Amend Ticket Method only to require an active work item to identify the
selected execution basis when agentic construction is used. Existing ticket,
post, commit, or evidence surfaces may carry the transition record; no new
mandatory document type is introduced.

Ticket Method must not redefine the protocol or turn it into a ticket-state
machine.

### 4. Reconcile `GOALS.md` exclusions

Clarify that the exclusion of executable conformance/workflow machinery and
prescribed collaboration procedures does not exclude an abstract normative
agentic execution relation.

Do not select a 2.3 work wave or authorize implementation through this ticket.

### 5. Rebuild derived authority only after source acceptance

If authorized and accepted, rebuild the affected member compressions and
`stdo_compressed.md`, then account for the change in the 2.3 release delta.

No downstream consumer adoption belongs to this ticket.

## Required Normative Updates

### E1. Sufficient execution intake

A bounded agentic action begins from a reconstruction-sufficient authority
basis, not conversation history, commentary, worker memory, or implementation
folklore. The intake identifies:

- the selected Product outcome or prerequisite;
- the bounded affected relation set and governing invariants;
- the accepted transformation-admission relations;
- selected computational/common-library dispositions;
- the worker's construction authority;
- the assessor's decision authority; and
- the exact conditions requiring upstream re-entry.

This consumes T-005's invariant-reconstruction law rather than duplicating it.

### E2. Bounded causal construction

The unit of execution is the smallest coherent causal dependency cone that can
deliver working behavior while preserving every affected governing relation.
It is not necessarily one function, file, module, ticket field, or design
document.

Unrelated incompleteness outside the declared cone is recorded and does not
block acceptance. A far-side seam that can causally affect the action belongs
inside the cone or must be proven unable to affect it.

### E3. Construction and assessment separation

The constructor owns bounded implementation and self-review. An assessor that
supports promotion independently evaluates the exact live code, authority
paths, proof, and installed subject; it does not accept the constructor's
summary as sufficient evidence.

This defines role separation, not actor count. One actor may hold several roles
across different actions when independence for the reviewed claim is preserved
under existing independent-review law.

### E4. Delegated routine advancement

The authority relation declares whether a bounded assessor may accept and
advance routine actions. Human action is not required at every transition when
the governing relations, affected cone, decision envelope, and re-entry
conditions are already accepted.

The bounded proxy may accept preservation, require local repair, reject a
violating candidate, and advance to the next already-authorized action. It may
not change Product meaning, governing authority, or accepted design relations.
Those decisions re-enter at their owning authority.

### E5. Proportional disposition

Every assessed candidate receives one of four dispositions:

| Disposition | Condition |
|---|---|
| `accept` | the candidate closes its declared cone and preserves every affected governing relation |
| `local_repair` | implementation or proof is locally defective while the governing relation remains coherent |
| `re_enter` | Product, requirement, identity, lifecycle, authority, public contract, or accepted design must change |
| `reject` | the candidate violates a governing relation or leaves an undisposed competing truth path |

Global correctness requires conservation of affected global relations, not a
proof of the whole Product inside every bounded action.

### E6. Transition evidence

A reviewable transition identifies:

- exact candidate identity;
- affected relations and changed code paths;
- authority paths added, removed, retained, or still open;
- focused proof and any required integration proof;
- remaining seams and explicit non-changes; and
- assessor disposition.

The representation is consumer-owned. The evidence is a read model and does
not become specification, design, or admission authority.

### E7. Proof cadence

Construction uses the cheapest focused proof capable of falsifying the active
relation. Expensive whole-candidate qualification occurs at a declared
candidate boundary, not after every local edit, unless the affected risk makes
the whole proof necessary.

A durable or reconstructable authority claim cannot be established solely by
a same-process proof when loss of incidental process state is a material
counterexample. The proof must destroy or exclude the incidental authority and
compare reconstructed semantic outcomes.

### E8. Exact checkpoint and automatic continuation

An accepted action binds one exact checkpoint. The next already-authorized
bounded action may begin without renewed human ceremony. Acceptance of one
checkpoint confers no authority to widen the Product outcome or affected
relation set.

### E9. Progress and churn semantics

STDO defines categories, not a numeric algorithm:

- accepted material Product movement is progress;
- prerequisite work names the Product obligation it enables;
- preservation and repeated proof are evidence, not new progress;
- rejected or superseded construction is churn;
- discovery of previously hidden distance revises the forecast but is not
  itself negative implementation progress; and
- paperwork, code volume, test count, commit count, and review count are not
  Product progress by themselves.

Consumers may calculate their own progress and churn vectors from these
categories.

### E10. Convergence without a review state machine

Repeated rejection on one unchanged boundary requires reassessment of frame
altitude, causal-cone selection, overlooked common components, misplaced
design-versus-code questions, and authority sufficiency.

The method sets no candidate-count threshold and mandates no number of review
rounds. The obligation is to stop repeating an unchanged failed construction
relation.
