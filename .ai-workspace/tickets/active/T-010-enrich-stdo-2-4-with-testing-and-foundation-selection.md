## Candidate Scope

### Testing profile

The optional baseline must:

1. fan Product and accepted-design claims out to UAT, E2E, integration, and
   unit frames rather than depicting a linear hierarchy;
2. use the canonical deployed, installed, or otherwise runnable Product form
   for both UAT and E2E;
3. retain a module-derived unit lane for every coded module, including public
   module contracts, while using internal combinatorial complexity only to
   determine proportional evidence size and strategy;
4. keep every result bound to its own claim and compose results only through a
   declared conjunction;
5. distinguish production closure from assurance/source closure so isolated,
   declared test-only seams outside Product distribution and ordinary-path
   evidence remain lawful;
6. acquire owner-issued Product/runtime truth through exact owner surfaces and
   evaluate it through a separately law-derived oracle rather than a rival
   proof-owned semantic interpreter; and
7. return counterexamples as a causally minimal supported frontier represented
   by one unique locus, a jointly established incomparable set, unresolved
   alternative frontiers, or `indeterminate` evidence.

### Universal design law

DMM `STDO-UP-023` must:

1. state a material generic capability without Product, domain, module, vendor,
   file, or incumbent labels;
2. distinguish irreducible semantic or authority-bearing relations and their
   owner, role kind, subject, scope, basis, lifecycle, and refusal invariants
   from generic mechanics and foundation capabilities;
3. compare credible project, native/standard, immutable-lineage, maintained
   external, local, and mixed foundation compositions;
4. bound discovery through searched sources or categories, cutoff and version
   basis, applicability criteria, exclusions, predecessor or incumbent status,
   discovered candidates, residual unknowns, material-gap refusal, and later-
   candidate invalidation;
5. operate inside the Product boundary and any applicable migration strategy
   already selected by their existing owners without replacing SPEC's strategy
   decision;
6. compare only decision-relevant total-lifecycle dimensions and retain
   unknowns rather than manufacturing a score;
7. eliminate hard authority, contract, safety, security, license, and
   constructability failures;
8. refuse a dominated candidate, including a local generic rebuild, only when
   every material dimension is comparable or evidenced as non-discriminating
   and no material unknown could reverse the dominance relation;
9. select from the undominated frontier only through priorities and risk
   tolerances declared by the existing tradeoff owner for the exact Product and
   basis, retaining a gap when they do not resolve it and recording decisive
   evidence for rejected candidates;
10. permit a foundation to realize an explicitly assigned authority-bearing
    role only while preserving the declared relation's owner, role kind,
    subject, scope, basis, lifecycle, and refusal invariants; and
11. recurse when another material generic subproblem appears.

The pass is proportional and does not require a dependency survey for trivial
helpers with no material effect on authority, Product behavior, lifecycle cost,
or accepted design.
