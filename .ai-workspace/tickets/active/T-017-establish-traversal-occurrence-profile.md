## Governed Outcome

1. `TRAVERSAL_OCCURRENCE_PROFILE.md` owns one `a_c` model-family profile in its
   own bounded context.
2. The profile imports one exact `a_c` basis and instantiates one closed
   model-family signature without amending the calculus.
3. Immutable occurrence identity, admitted framework-event facts, typed
   lineage, externally mutable subject bindings, observations, effect
   invocations, and projection frontiers remain distinct.
4. Identity dependency and declared causal subgraphs are acyclic. Wider typed
   lineage is not falsely collapsed into one acyclic graph.
5. Functor kind, traversal, effect operation, executor, actor, grant, and
   invocation remain separate coordinates.
6. Repeated traversal application creates a fresh immutable occurrence over
   the then-current subject. Evidence, snapshots, results, and projections do
   not replace the mutable subject.
7. Candidate claims, judgments, admitted events, and materialized relations
   retain separate identities and authority.
8. Profile availability grants no Product adoption, runtime meaning,
   operation authority, or implementation selection.

## Non-Closure Conditions

- the profile modifies or embeds pure `a_c` or Reference Frame principles;
- consumer, runtime, engine, language, project, or role names enter the
  application-neutral profile;
- every framework event is forced to belong to one occurrence;
- ordinal, timestamp, adjacency, containment, or correlation creates cause;
- a typed-lineage loop is rejected merely because unlike relation kinds form
  an untyped cycle;
- a functor kind is treated as the effect operation or its executor;
- a result, snapshot, checkpoint, cache, or event log becomes the mutable
  subject carrier;
- construction or profile availability mints authority;
- compression, glossary, or inventory edges are stale; or
- source authoring is represented as acceptance, release, or downstream
  adoption.
