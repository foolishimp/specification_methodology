## Intake Triage

**Substantive?** Yes. The method defines a frame and lists activation fields,
but the only copyable activation packet is currently profile-specific. The
optional baseline also leaves its Executive attention frontier, iterative
evaluation loop, Product-chain distinctions, and drift-control duties more
implicit than a fresh actor should have to infer.

**Boundary crossed?** Reference Frame Method, Specification Method starter
guidance, optional STDO Reference Frame Baseline, templates, bootstraps,
compressed read models, and focused qualification. No consumer architecture,
runtime, interpreted axiomatic subject, carrier, or downstream Product enters
the method.

**Smallest lawful re-entry.** The current Goal first selects this work. Product
re-entry is required because the optional baseline distributed by the STDO
Product adds a distinct Product Composition specialist family. The source
project's separately accepted frame basis is not retargeted by availability of
that successor profile. Intent remains stable.

## Governed Outcome

1. `REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md` states plainly that a frame is an evaluation
   contract defining finite attention scope and that prompts or context packets
   are projections of an activation.
2. The pure method contains one generic activation binding and one filled,
   carrier-neutral Product-drift example sufficient for a fresh actor.
3. `SPEC_METHOD.md` remains the owner of Product frame-basis adoption, and a
   starter template projects that law without becoming authority.
4. The optional baseline states the Executive cycle as attention management,
   evaluation orchestration, and authorized action.
5. Executive coverage iterates over the smallest dependency-ready activation
   frontier that fits the actor envelope. It does not load every frame or
   synthesize their internal contexts.
6. Product-chain work preserves the identities and authorities of mutable
   source projects, immutable checkpoints and release cuts, released Products,
   installs, artifacts, and Development Products.
7. A downstream scenario, specialization, implementation, dependency, or
   builder substrate cannot silently replace or create the governed Product.
8. Mutable-workspace change invalidates affected activations and results; an
   immutable binding or checkpoint never makes the live workspace immutable.
9. Independent frames return closed results. Executive synthesizes only
   declared seams and chooses only an already-authorized next action.
10. Product Composition is a distinct specialist evaluation family from
    executable integration and is disposed for every material directed Product
    edge.

## Non-Closure Conditions

- an LLM requires `a_c`, STDO Representation, a prompt framework, or hidden
  conversation history to reconstruct a basic activation;
- the generic method imports the optional profile or one consumer topology;
- the baseline names or depends on a consumer-specific architecture;
- Executive becomes a semantic, implementation, operation, acceptance, or
  publication owner merely by role;
- all available frames are loaded or activated without materiality and
  dependency disposition;
- a summary substitutes for a closed frame result or exact source re-entry;
- Product-chain identities, authorities, or mutable/immutable subjects are
  collapsed; or
- compression, bootstrap, templates, or qualification contradict the deciding
  standards.
