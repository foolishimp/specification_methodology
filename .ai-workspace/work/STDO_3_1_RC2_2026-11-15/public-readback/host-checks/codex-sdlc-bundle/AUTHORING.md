An excluded or unavailable imported source remains an explicit gap when it may affect the action.

Emit the supplied index schema. Copy the exact `corpus_sha256`, `input_view_sha256`, `purpose_id` and `purpose_sha256`. Give rules stable local IDs and source references to original source IDs and one-based line intervals. Retain enough complete wording to act from the context, with source re-entry references available.

The `when` predicate combines all `all` clauses with, when `any` is nonempty, the disjunction of its clauses. Empty `all` and `any` mean true. Operators are typed scalar `eq`/`ne` and scalar membership `contains`/`not_contains` in a fact list. A missing, null or incompatible fact yields unknown. Boolean true is not integer 1. Use only declared purpose fact names and predicates justified by the source. Preserve richer conditions as text; record material undecidable applicability in `unknowns` rather than manufacturing facts.

Exceptions retain their complete deciding text and predicate even when currently false. A true exception qualifies its rule; the compiler preserves both without deciding an arbitrary prose outcome. Unknown exception applicability prevents a covered claim. Dependencies retain other local rules together; a false dependency remains visibly inapplicable. Precedence names its affected rule and source-warranted reason. Similar labels do not establish either relation.

Provide exactly one `source_coverage` row for every selected source member, with `rule_ids` equal to all rules citing it. `represented` means it has authored rules; `no_applicable_rules` requires a stated reason and no grounded rules; `unresolved` records incomplete interpretation. This is a processing ledger, not proof of an exhaustive obligation denominator. Use `unresolved` entries with `material: true` for questions that may change the allowed action, verification or delivered constraints. Rule `unknowns` are material when that rule is selected.

Validate the actual index file. Preserve failed authored attempts and their corrections. Structural validity and declared closure do not establish source-semantic adequacy, successful recipient use or economic value.

A separately selected assessment, when available under the governing contract, has `index_sha256`, `corpus_sha256`, `query_sha256`, `status` (`supported`, `falsified` or `unknown`), `evaluator`, and nonempty `evidence` references. The helper checks exact subject bindings; it does not authenticate the evaluator, inspect those evidence references or decide whether required independence was satisfied. Do not use author self-certification as that evidence. Without such an assessment, retain `covered: false`; existing authorized experimental use remains explicitly labelled. Source/purpose/index/query changes invalidate mismatched reliance.
