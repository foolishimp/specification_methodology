# SDLC workflow purpose and rebuilding

These requirements are admitted by the 2026-11-14 cross-Product activation.
They extend the Product's reusable context capability; they do not change its
installed 3.0.4 construction constitution or migrate an existing consumer.

| ID | Observable obligation | Deciding negative case |
|---|---|---|
| PC-S01 | Supply a separately named SDLC workflow purpose over explicit governing source closure, with actual reusable catalog, purpose, source and index assets. | Relabeling a Python refactor entry, a prose promise, or an index with missing applicable imported law is insufficient. |
| PC-S02 | Retain ordinary work/QA, causal diagnosis and lawful owner re-entry, bounded repair verification and conditional rechecks, continuation/stopping, and named acceptance/release boundaries. | Repeated failure cannot force automatic code repair or specification change; incremental QA cannot imply acceptance or publication. |
| PC-S03 | Preserve exact claims, populations, results and unknowns, and distinguish severity, priority, boundary effect and disposition. | An unperformed predicate receives no result; an adjacent finding cannot silently widen the reviewed subject or block it on severity alone. |
| PC-S04 | Retain applicable mandatory gates and Product-owned policy choices. | A size budget, review count, green suite or default projection cannot waive a hard stop, required independence or missing operation grant. |
| PC-S05 | Declare actual query facts and conditional branches with their evidence meanings. | Missing, null or ill-typed deciding facts remain unknown; fixtures cannot be copied as claims about a live task. |
| PC-S06 | Make source-to-index rebuilding reproducible: bind requirements, selected source membership/order/bytes, purpose/facts, authoring recipe and generator, then validate through the existing deterministic core. | A changed source/recipe/purpose cannot retain old generated bindings by updating a label or digest alone. |
| PC-S07 | Fail closed on changed selected source, source manifest, closure, authoring recipe, purpose, query or public bundle before dependent reliance. | Stale default projection, imported owner or candidate manifest cannot pass an old catalog binding. |
| PC-S08 | Bind an unreleased method candidate as development source with exact predecessor identity and explicit caller selection. | No fabricated release URI/cut, release qualification claim, automatic latest selection or silent consumer migration is allowed. |
| PC-S09 | Preserve all earlier Python 3.0.4/3.0.5 index and evidence bytes; identify impacted claims and reuse only demonstrably applicable evidence. | Unchanged owner meaning is not enough to replay an assessment whose exact index/corpus/query binding changed. |
| PC-S10 | Provide finite positive/negative controls for all binding boundaries, unknown applicability, conditional selection, full-body budgets and retained old paths, plus realistic source-first external assessment subjects. | Checksums, source ledgers and author self-review cannot establish semantic adequacy or actual native task behaviour. |
| PC-S11 | Retain ticket/carrier-based work continuity within the workflow purpose: bind the selected durable work record and current workspace, construct bounded context from qualified governing references, record step-produced facts/results and continuation state before return, and reconstruct after context loss while checking invalidators and reconciling uncertain effects before dependent work. | A chat summary cannot overrule the ticket or current evidence; missing private context cannot reset work, justify blind repetition or conceal an unrecorded result. The requirement creates no extra carrier, universal capacity gate, new approval or independent review beyond the selected law and work grant. |
| PC-S12 | Establish the continuity capability through finite actual-use observations from the current ticket and linked workspace without the prior conversation, with separately derived source-first assessment expectations. | A successful index build, rendered-body check, count of rules, or author-written expected response cannot establish that a fresh actor continued correctly, handled stale evidence or preserved an uncertain effect. |

The coverage predicate is retention of the selected method's applicable SDLC
workflow constraints before the requested decision. It does not assert that a
task is admitted, evidence exists, a fix works, review was independent, a policy
has been selected, or any boundary has passed. Complete conditional obligations
name the evidence and owning Product inputs the consumer must obtain. Unknown
facts deciding which constraints apply remain material gaps.

The continuation capability is part of ordinary work, QA and evidence reuse
in the existing `sdlc-workflow/1` family. Its source-owned conditions stay in
the rules; additional boolean facts must not make retaining results, observing
current evidence, or lawful restart optional. The existing purpose and fact
meanings are preserved unless an explicit semantic change establishes a new
purpose boundary.

Finite actual-use evidence for PC-S11 and PC-S12 includes these situations:

| Situation | Required observation and claim limit |
|---|---|
| A fresh actor receives an admitted ticket and its current linked work state, with no previous conversation. | It recovers applicable progress, governing inputs and the next authorized action, produces the bounded result, and retains required facts/evidence before return. No mandatory transcript or new carrier is inferred. |
| A retained summary contradicts the current ticket or workspace evidence. | Current authoritative state corrects the account; unresolved conflict stays explicit and contradictory acceptance evidence withholds its dependent closure. |
| Material evidence changes while another recorded result remains applicable. | The actor checks invalidators, reopens affected support and preserves independently surviving support with its original subject and evidence. |
| An interrupted effect may have occurred or may still be acting. | The actor reconciles sufficiently before repetition or dependent action, retaining `completion_unknown` or the unresolved end beside `not_effected`; missing history is not evidence of no effect. |
| The selected activation declares a context capacity. | The actor records the selected material's size, unit, boundary, capacity and comparison before reading it; unmeasured host context remains outside any measured fit claim. Insufficient fit follows lawful refinement, sequencing, compression, reassignment or refusal without omitting material obligations. |

These are requirement scenarios, not supplied evaluator answers or claims of
successful performance. An assessor derives its own discriminators from the
owning source before candidate exposure. Record exact task, source, query,
index, received context and actual effects for every performed observation;
keep unperformed cases and broader effectiveness or release claims open.

Rebuild on an affected requirement, selected method/source closure, purpose,
fact meaning, authored rule/ground/condition, generator or catalog contract
change. Compare old and new exact subjects and state the impact. Mechanical
rebuilding may conserve unchanged rule meaning; changed meaning needs source
re-entry. A new external assessment can cite applicable prior evidence but
must bind its own exact subject. Final release packaging reconciles to the
actual immutable cut; development-source identity is not a final release pin.
