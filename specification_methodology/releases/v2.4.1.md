## Product Delta

### Claim-Relative Product Testing Frames

The optional `STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md` profile gains four
claim-relative specialist testing frames. They fan out from Product and
accepted design rather than forming a hierarchy:

```text
Product and accepted design
  |- user-outcome claims -> user-acceptance frames
  |- runnable causal-path claims -> end-to-end frames
  |- composition-boundary claims -> integration frames
  `- module-owned laws -> unit frames

bound results -> declared conjunction -> Product or release disposition
```

The profile establishes:

- a testing frame is selected by its claim, exact subject, path, population,
  oracle, evidence, and falsification condition rather than by runner or label;
- UAT and E2E both use an exact deployed, installed, or otherwise runnable
  Product form;
- UAT is primary only for the claim that a declared user can obtain the
  Product-defined outcome through supported ordinary surfaces;
- a harnessed substitute proves only the exercised composed relation and cannot
  prove an external boundary claimed as live;
- E2E proves the complete authoritative causal path, and equal output through a
  forbidden path is a counterexample;
- integration proves one declared composition boundary and interaction
  population, while a substitute proves only its admitted narrower claim;
- unit evidence proves module-owned laws, including public module contracts,
  and every coded module retains its Design Module Method unit lane; internal
  combinatorial complexity controls evidence volume and strategy rather than
  eligibility;
- coverage retains interaction strength and residual combinations instead of
  collapsing into test count, code coverage, or aggregate green;
- path deletion distinguishes production closure from assurance/source
  closure. An isolated, declared test-only seam outside Product distribution
  and ordinary-path evidence is lawful, while an obsolete fixture or proof
  consumer that can falsely close or reintroduce the Product path is not;
- owner-issued Product and runtime truth is acquired through the exact runnable
  owner surface, then evaluated by a separately law-derived oracle; generic
  assurance may compare owner-issued evidence but cannot reconstruct a rival
  owner state from raw payloads, events, logs, labels, or implementation
  fragments; and
- counterexamples return a causally minimal supported frontier represented as
  one unique locus, a jointly established incomparable set, unresolved
  alternative frontiers, or `indeterminate` evidence.

This remains an optional application profile. It does not create another
testing, Product, design, review, disposition, or release authority.

### Capability And Foundation Selection (`STDO-UP-023`)

`DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md` gains one universal preconstruction relation for a
material generic capability, common mechanic, algorithm, or dependency.

The adopting design boundary:

1. states the required capability without Product, domain, module, vendor,
   file, or incumbent labels;
2. distinguishes irreducible semantic or authority-bearing relations and their
   owner, role kind, subject, scope, basis, lifecycle, and refusal invariants
   from generic mechanics and foundation capabilities;
3. compares credible compositions of admitted project, language/runtime/
   standard-library, immutable-lineage, maintained external, and bounded local
   foundations;
4. bounds discovery through searched sources or categories, cutoff and version
   basis, applicability criteria, exclusions, predecessor or incumbent status,
   discovered candidates, residual unknowns, material-gap refusal, and later-
   candidate invalidation;
5. operates inside the Product boundary and any applicable migration strategy
   already admitted by their existing owners and does not replace
   `SPEC_METHOD.md`'s bounded-evolution versus fundamental-re-adoption decision;
6. gives no presumptive preference to source order, familiarity, sunk cost,
   local code, or dependency count within that boundary;
7. evaluates only dimensions material to total lifecycle cost, including fit,
   authority leakage, integration and migration, deletion and future code,
   license and maintenance, security and supply chain, runtime and operations,
   determinism and recovery, reconstruction and replay, proof burden, and exit
   cost;
8. eliminates hard authority, Product-contract, safety, security, license, and
   constructability failures while retaining unknown evidence as unknown;
9. treats one lawful composition as dominating another only when every material
   dimension is comparable or evidenced as non-discriminating, it is no worse
   on each comparable dimension and strictly better on at least one, and no
   material unknown could reverse that relation;
10. selects from the undominated frontier only through priorities and risk
    tolerances declared by the existing tradeoff owner for the exact Product
    and basis, retaining an explicit gap when they do not resolve it;
11. permits a foundation to realize an explicitly assigned authority-bearing
    role only while preserving the declared relation's owner, role kind,
    subject, scope, basis, lifecycle, and refusal invariants; and
12. recursively re-enters the relation when another material generic subproblem
   appears.

Generic local code is a candidate, not the default. Reuse does not automatically
confer or transfer authority; ownership follows the declared relation rather
than local or external source authorship. The pass is proportional and does not
require a package survey for a trivial helper with no material effect on the
accepted design network.

This preconstruction law is distinct from DMM section 11C. `STDO-UP-023`
prevents avoidable reproduction before code; recurrence extraction detects and
dispositions repeated realization after patterns exist.

  -> shared-method intake

  -> consistency, qualification, review, and human acceptance
  -> one complete immutable STDO release
