## Product Delta

### Transformation Admission Completeness (`STDO-UP-016`)

Naming an admitter did not identify the complete relation deciding which
candidate transformation becomes durable entity truth. The successor requires
each consequential entity-transition domain to declare identity, predecessor
basis, authority scope, candidate family, decision cardinality, admission
predicate, durable result, supersession law, and competing-path disposition.

Singularity attaches to the admission relation rather than one actor or
callable. Constituents, replicas, replacements, quorum, multi-signature, human
step-up, delegated proxy, and disjoint jurisdiction remain lawful when their
relation is explicit. Projection state cannot authenticate its own admission.

### Bounded Frame Conservation (`STDO-UP-017`)

`Global` and `local` are made relative to a selected frame. A bounded action
declares its governing frames, affected relation set, inherited invariants,
and crossing authority and composition seams. Frames form a constraint network
rather than a mandatory tree.

Every causally relevant far-side seam is included or proven unable to
participate. A local action conserves governing truth; changing a governing
relation requires re-entry at that relation.

### Invariant Reconstruction Sufficiency (`STDO-UP-018`)

Accepted method and design must expose enough durable reasoning state to
recover every governing relation in a bounded action's causally closed affected
set and decide whether the action preserves, changes, duplicates, or violates
it. Conversation history, commentary, prior-agent memory, and implementation
folklore are excluded from that sufficiency claim.

This adds no mandatory artifact. It makes reconstructability a property of the
accepted design evidence already required.

### Computational Realization Projection (`STDO-UP-019`)

Material design now projects domain structure into abstract data types,
identity and key structures, algorithms, algebraic laws, effect boundaries,
selected implementations, and domain-to-computational mappings. Every material
component declares its semantic and operational role set; technology capability
does not confer authority.

Computational similarity raises a commonization candidate but cannot adjudicate
semantic identity or ownership. Every material algorithmic obligation receives
both a semantic disposition and a realization disposition.

### Agentic Construction Execution (`STDO-UP-020`)

Where construction is delegated to a probabilistic or agentic worker, the
successor governs the abstract execution relation:

```text
accepted governing basis
  -> bounded causal dependency cone
  -> selected computational relations
  -> delegated construction and assessment authority
  -> construction and self-review
  -> independent live-surface assessment where required
  -> accept | local repair | re-enter | reject
  -> exact checkpoint
  -> next already-authorized action
```

The protocol requires reconstruction-sufficient intake, proportional local
repair versus upstream re-entry, exact transition evidence, focused proof
during construction, whole-candidate proof at declared boundaries, and loss or
exclusion of incidental process state when same-process state is a material
counterexample to durable authority.

It defines progress as accepted material Product movement and churn as rejected
or superseded construction without prescribing a numeric progress algorithm.
It permits bounded routine proxy advancement without weakening non-delegable
human Product or release acceptance.

### ODD And Ticket Specialization

`ODD_METHOD.md` identifies ABG transformation admission as the graph-native
specialization of `STDO-UP-016` without collapsing ABG's broader execution,
retry, supervision, or projection roles into admission.

`TICKET_METHOD.md` requires an active agentic work item to identify its selected
execution basis, affected relations, delegated authority, and re-entry
conditions. Existing evidence carriers remain sufficient; no ticket state
machine, artifact type, review-round count, or approval ceremony is added.

  suite, or consumer Product;
- required model, vendor, prompt, tool, repository layout, worker count, or
  collaboration topology;
- mandatory review-round count, candidate threshold, ticket-generation rule,
  ticket state machine, or numeric progress formula;
- new standards member or mandatory artifact type;
- downstream adoption or consumer implementation change; or
- weakening of final publication review and direct-human acceptance.
