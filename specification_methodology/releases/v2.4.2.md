## Product Delta

### Method Identity

The glossary and aggregate compression define the core shorthand explicitly:

- `STDO` — Specification, Ticketing, Design, and Outcome-Driven Development;
  and
- `ODD` — Outcome-Driven Development.

The shorthand names the four key pillars but does not reduce the STDO Product
to four files. Consumer authority remains one complete immutable released cut
with its exact member inventory.

### Product Definition Overlay

Every STDO-defined product publishes one JSON Product Definition Overlay per
distinct current product `WHAT`. The conventional filename is
`stdo_<label>.json`; `stdo_default.json` is the singleton default.

The overlay supports:

- an existing project without restructuring its folder or tool topology;
- a default project using the supplied scaffold;
- a monorepo with several distinct product definitions at one root; and
- a hierarchical repository with product definitions at arbitrarily deep
  project roots.

One definition represents one distinct current `WHAT`. Several build tenants
realizing that same `WHAT` remain entries in one definition. Directory nesting
creates no implicit inheritance, ownership, composition, or constitutional
override.

The normative schema is
`schemas/product-definition.schema.json`. The single fill-in form is
`templates/PRODUCT_DEFINITION_TEMPLATE.json`. Together they standardize the
following identity, locator, and relation surface:

- product-definition identity, display name, source-project locator, and
  bounded-context declaration;
- complete constitutional authorities and derived reading entrypoints;
- local axioms, overrides, and disambiguations with lawful authority, target,
  and scope;
- one or more durable collective project reference-frame bases with their
  admitting authorities and governed scopes;
- Intent, Product, and specification `WHAT` bindings;
- common realization and one or more independent build-tenant `HOW` bindings;
- current epic/goal, durable ticket, comment, and optional sprint carrier
  bindings; and
- explicit product-composition relations.

The overlay owns only identity, location, discovery, and relation mapping.
Referenced constitutional, Product, requirement, design, ticket, comment, and
runtime carriers retain their own meaning and authority.

### Constitutional Sufficiency And Local Decisions

A product constitution is a governing document set, not five prescribed files.
Collectively, the selected set makes its axioms, ontology, epistemology,
taxonomy, and semantics recoverable for the governed scope. These are
sufficiency dimensions, not filenames, directories, or a one-document-per-
dimension partition.

`constitution.authorities` selects complete constitutional sets.
`constitution.entrypoints` provides useful navigation but cannot narrow or
replace those sets. Local axioms, overrides, and disambiguations name their
carrier, existing lawful authority, target where applicable, and exact scope.
Listing a URI does not mint or widen authority.

### Definition And Composition Identity

`product.definition_id` identifies one continuing mutable product-definition
line. It is distinct from every immutable Product and release identity produced
from that line.

Each composition edge binds:

- the current target-definition locator;
- the expected target `product.definition_id`;
- the authority defining the directed relation, source and target roles, owner,
  scope, lifecycle, and invalidation conditions; and
- at least one exact governing contract.

Resolution verifies both locator and expected identity. Moving a definition
requires a locator update without changing its identity. Replacing content at
the same locator with another identity fails closed. Mutable definition
identity cannot substitute for an immutable Product or release identity
required by the relation or contracts.

### Schema And URI Conformance

Portable Draft 2020-12 JSON Schema validation establishes structural shape.
The schema's URI formats remain annotations unless the validator implements
format assertion. Conformance therefore performs an explicit RFC 3986 URI and
URI-reference syntax check with an assertion-capable validator, followed by
separate resolution, fragment-existence, selected-release identity, cross-file
uniqueness, composition-identity, constitutional-sufficiency, and authority-
congruence checks.

### Build Tenancy And Work Carriers

Build tenancy is one constitutional `WHAT` realized by one or more independent
project-owned `HOW` tenants. One tenant is the singleton case. Multi-build-
tenancy begins when the same `WHAT` has more than one independent realization.

A tenant may own tenant-local design, tooling, implementation, proof, release,
and lifecycle state but cannot become a rival constitution. Common realization
law exists only when explicitly adopted across more than one tenant; similarity
does not promote tenant-local truth automatically.

When one upstream work item spans several tenant lifecycles, the upstream
authority remains distinct and each affected tenant uses its own independently
closable execution ticket. One tenant's green proof or closure cannot close or
hide another tenant's work.

The Product Definition Overlay may bind Goals or an equivalent current epic
carrier, ticket lanes, comments, and optional sprints to local directories,
repository resources, tracker collections, API endpoints, or product-scoped
queries. `GOALS.md`, `.ai-workspace/`, and the supplied build-tenant folders are
default scaffold paths rather than universal layout law.

Build tenancy does not itself mean hosted, customer, account, runtime, or data
multitenancy. A Product claiming those forms defines their identity, isolation,
lifecycle, and proof obligations separately.

### Collective Reference-Frame Basis And Agent Activation

`reference_frame_bases` locates the durable shared frame-basis declarations
through which finite actors collectively engage the governed Product and work.
Each binding names its admitting authority and exact governed scope.

An authorized ticket or other work instruction separately binds one agent's
temporary frame configuration, evaluation, subject, and capability envelope.
The Product Definition Overlay is not an actor registry and does not persist
temporary activations. A Product or runtime carrier named `Frame` remains
`WHAT` or `HOW` unless separately declared as a Reference Frame Method
evaluation frame.

### Generic Specialist Reference Frames

The optional STDO baseline adds eleven generic specialist-frame families:

- Product;
- Design;
- Design Component;
- Public Boundary;
- Entity;
- Operator;
- Owner;
- Effect;
- Reuse/Foundation;
- Install; and
- Proof.

They are evaluation archetypes, not required actors, stages, components,
services, files, runtime types, or new semantic owners. For one exact outcome,
each family is instantiated where material, established as non-material by
capable authority, or retained as an explicit residual.

The families remain non-hierarchical. Composite satisfaction requires a
declared conjunction that satisfies every applicable constraint. Each material
cross-frame seam preserves or lawfully translates identity, coordinate and
basis, value or evidence, lifecycle, authority, and provenance. Missing or
non-commuting seams return closed pressure rather than selecting a dominant
frame.

The baseline imports no ABIogenesis, GTL, HoG, ABG, or renamed consumer-local
architectural substitute. Consumers may declare local frames under their own
Product or accepted-design authority where material.

### Compression And Bootstrap Projection

The normative Product Definition Overlay schema is a deciding source for the
aggregate compression and carries its own digest edge. Source-specific and
aggregate compressions project the new identity, layout, build-tenancy,
work-carrier, composition, and reference-frame relations without becoming
independently selectable authority.

Codex and Claude bootstrap templates carry equal method semantics while
retaining only their agent-specific names and file-order conventions. They
discover the applicable Product Definition Overlay, constitutional set,
project frame basis, authorized activation, `WHAT`, `HOW`, and work carriers
instead of assuming one physical repository layout.
