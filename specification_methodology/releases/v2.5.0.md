## Release Delta

### Proportional Work Carriers

Ticket Method now chooses the smallest lawful execution carrier by boundary
lifetime: an existing admitted exact ticket, a durable ticket for state that
must outlive the local boundary, a sprint-local entry, or a one-run execution
contract. A one-run contract may be drafted, validated, admitted, and executed
in one invocation. Ticket-shaped data does not itself create a durable ticket.

Execution still requires a distinct admission witness. Every admitted carrier
names a Product-bound durable result or evidence surface before work starts;
closure, residual obligations, and re-entry are recorded there rather than
being lost in conversation or converted into ticket churn.

### Exact Product Basis And Fail-Atomic Setup

Every distributed workflow skill uses one shared Product-basis procedure.
Zero, multiple, or mismatched applicable Product Definitions stop before any
effect; path proximity and mutable-source fallback cannot select authority.

New-project setup preflights all sources and targets, stages both bootstrap
outputs, installs them without overwrite, and rolls back only outputs it owns
if either finalization fails. The guide detects and upgrades pre-RC3 toolchains
before exact-cut installation. A delegated setup actor may draft a frame basis
but must stop for Product-owner ratification rather than self-admit it.

## Release-Scoped Claims And Successor Disposition

- `STDO-2.5-RC3-C01`: carrier lifetime selects the smallest lawful durable,
  sprint-local, or run-scoped work contract without manufacturing tickets.
- `STDO-2.5-RC3-C02`: execution admission is distinct from drafting and binds
  one durable result/evidence surface that retains closure and residual work.
- `STDO-2.5-RC3-C03`: every distributed workflow skill fails closed unless
  exactly one applicable Product Definition and verified exact STDO basis are
  established before effects.
- `STDO-2.5-RC3-C04`: new-project setup is fail-atomic, detects and upgrades an
  old toolchain, and keeps delegated frame-basis drafts provisional pending
  Product-owner ratification.
- `STDO-2.5-RC3-C05`: one exact, aligned `2.5.0-rc.3` plugin payload exposes the
  shared five-skill workflow to Claude and Codex while keeping Claude refresh
  manual-only.
- `STDO-2.5-RC3-C06`: `stdo-toolchain 0.1.2` behavior and executable bytes are
  conserved while its proof covers the expanded auxiliary plugin payload.

claims above without silently superseding an RC2 claim.
