## Release Delta

### Axiomatic Calculus

The release adds the carrier-neutral Axiomatic Calculus `a_c`. Its closed model
kernel contains eight fundamental record families:

```text
M_b = (b, I, O, E, C, L, X, V, T, J)
```

One total population relation assigns every fundamental record to exactly one
of `O/E/C/L/X/V/T/J`. One total reference-domain relation closes every
identity-bearing field to declared local record families and sorts or an exact
external target. Transformation partitions local records separately from exact
`Resolution_M` external coordinates and requires preservation witnesses for
both. The calculus separates deterministic evaluation (`F_D`),
probabilistic proposal (`F_P`), and human adjudication (`F_H`) from actors,
operations, grants, subject meaning, carriers, and runtimes.

An interpreted `a_c.X` model and encoded `a_c.X.C` carrier remain separately
identified downstream products or cuts. Semantic acceptance and carrier
admission are external judgments over unchanged subjects.

### Reference-Frame Boundaries

The pure Reference Frame Method owns frame algebra and lifecycle law. The
optional STDO baseline owns reusable capability profiles. Each Product owns its
concrete frame-basis projection, actor-binding rules, grants, activation
triggers, coverage, result flow, and invalidation.

This source Product supplies one explicit Project Reference-Frame Basis under
`specification/REFERENCE_FRAME_BASIS.md`. It binds the immutable RC3 method
basis while evaluating the mutable successor as a candidate subject. The
Worker label grants nothing by itself; bounded mutation requires the exact
owner-issued subject and write-territory grant.

### Optional Traversal Occurrence Profile

The release adds an application-neutral optional profile over `a_c` for
reasoning about immutable traversal occurrences over externally mutable
subjects. It closes:

- occurrence seed and graph-relation congruence;
- event-kind, operation-kind, grant, invocation, and effect boundaries;
- separate identity-dependency, occurrence-cause, event-cause, support, and
  correction relations;
- admitted claim, event, and semantic-cut judgments over unchanged subjects;
- exact source and successor frontiers; and
- fresh immutable occurrence identity for each traversal application over the
  then-current subject.

Availability does not adopt the profile. It grants no runtime, operation,
admission, correction, continuation, or closure authority.
