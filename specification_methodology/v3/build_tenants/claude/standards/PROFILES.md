# PROFILES

Tier 3. How domain profiles import the core and what stays out of it. A
profile adds domain obligations, bindings and vocabulary; it imports
[FOUNDATIONS](FOUNDATIONS.md), [ACTIVATION](ACTIVATION.md),
[EVALUATION](EVALUATION.md) and [SPEC_METHOD](SPEC_METHOD.md) by reference
and restates none of them.

## 1. The boundary

The core names no runtime, no carrier and no executor. Terms owned outside
the core, by a profile, a realisation or an rc.2 member, never enter tier
1 or 2 except in a disposition table: graph, edge, vector, ledger, event
log, worksite, HoG, Markov overlay, screen, entity cut, and the design
profile's IACS and Prime. The traversal record and the functor kinds F_D,
F_P and F_H are tier-1 calculus terms owned by AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS §5, and
"module" is a generic term, as the predecessor's generic testing law uses
it; neither is restricted here. A profile may bind a core mechanism to one of these;
the binding is declared in the profile with its direction, preserved
meaning, narrowed scope and invalidation. The declared-binding rule is new
(P0 boundary, P1); its shape follows ODD's specialisations of F_P and F_H,
which
[ODD probabilistic compute](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#probabilistic-compute)
owns in the graph-native context and carries as their provenance.

A worked application, a hello world or a data mapper is an external test
subject; its particulars cannot become generic method functionality by
precedent.

## 2. Graph execution

Imports the core and binds: the step to one vector or edge traversal; the
roles to the graph-native shorthands F_P (lawful probabilistic processing
within one traversal), F_D (deterministic evaluation or proof over declared
properties) and F_H (human adjudication under the traversal's declared
authority); work state to the workspace, governed ledger and event-log
triple; the result refinements to the profile's edge closure decisions with
their declared mapping to the core algebra; refinement to graph functions.
The runtime owns traversal, admission, runtime facts, frames, continuations,
lineage and projection; the product owns domain meaning, the function
catalogue, policy overlays and domain proof interpretation; the framework
never absorbs the worker's internal solution strategy. Conserved by
reference:
[ODD_METHOD](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#probabilistic-compute),
[graph functions](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#graph-functions),
[constructive evaluation and yield loop](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#115d-constructive-evaluation-and-yield-loop),
TRAVERSAL_OCCURRENCE_PROFILE and the baseline's runtime bindings. The loop's
four functions (synthesise model, evaluate gap, evaluate next, evaluate
action) are the profile's realisation of ACTIVATION §4–§5 and keep their
distinct authority.

## 3. Software design

[DESIGN_MODULE](DESIGN_MODULE.md) is the software-design profile. It
imports the core and binds: ontology-first derivation of operation
identities from discovered functionality through logical completeness,
Prime contraction, the irreducible architectural carrier set and its
projections; proportional design sequencing with the decision-complete
design gate; bounded frame conservation; native constructability;
capability and foundation selection; transformation admission
completeness; invariant reconstruction sufficiency; computational
realisation projection; the module taxonomy and realisation rules; and
the design review questions and checklist. Its design-to-module evidence
route, module-derived unit tests and module lifecycle confirmation are
ASSURANCE §14 to §16, and its core rule and non-goals SPEC_METHOD §2
Realisation shape. The per-boundary cost of the derivation chain is
decided there (DESIGN_MODULE §5 Proportional design sequencing, §7 Gate
and retrospective work) (P2, P0).

## 4. UX

Imports the core and binds reader and task obligations, operational
projection and screen-level acceptance. Conserved by reference:
[UX_METHOD](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/UX_METHOD.md).

## 5. World model

Imports the core and binds entity recovery and the entity cut as the
published identity-bearing unit
([entity cut](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#entity-cut-construction-law)),
the attribute ledger
([attribute ledger law](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#attribute-ledger-law)),
composition of local artifacts
([composition law](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#composition-law)),
and the Markov-object construct at candidate status
([epistemic status](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#epistemic-status-of-the-construct)).
All of WORLD_MODEL_METHOD is conserved by reference.
Nothing in the core depends on the construct being established.
