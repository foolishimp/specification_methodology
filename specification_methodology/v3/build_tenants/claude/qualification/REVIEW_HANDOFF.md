## What a reviewer is asked to do

Under the C3 Construction Contract's acceptance criteria, as a reviewer of
another model family:

1. Record expectations from the common sources (the contract, the
   requirements README's two successor requirements, the conformance
   discriminators) before opening the candidate.
2. Sample the conservation claim: pick members and rows yourself, compare
   predecessor sections with the tenant restatement at the recorded locus,
   and return one result per row. The highest-consequence rows are the
   ones the first pass lost: universal intake triage and computed
   classification, test case authority, trace closure, the baseline's
   frame sets and Executive law, the coverage ledger and diagnostic order,
   UP-002 and UP-003, UP-010 and ODD 11.8.
3. Judge the tier-1 corrections made during construction: cumulative
   refinement in ACTIVATION §4 with the statement of reading on AC-016,
   the three-case result boundary in ACTIVATION §3, and the declared
   mappings of profile vocabularies to the core result algebra.
4. Judge the cold-actor evidence: whether the expectations were
   independent of the frames and outputs, and whether the Executive's
   adjudications of indeterminate sub-points (recorded in the ledger) hold.
5. Return findings with severity and the smallest correction; make no
   repair.
