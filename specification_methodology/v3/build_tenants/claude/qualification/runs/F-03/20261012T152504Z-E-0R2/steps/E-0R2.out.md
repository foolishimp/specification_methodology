**2. Composition**

- **Can `route` lawfully obtain `raw_address`?**
  - Not as published. Each function's input is "the output of the previous function", so `route` receives only `normal_address`. `raw_address` is two steps back.
  - Carrying an earlier output to a later step other than the next is allowed only through a declared adapter or specialised contract whose declared domain names it. The ODD contract for this is the cumulative typed environment (§11.8).
  - `parse_address` would have to declare `environment.provides` for `raw_address`. `normalise` would have to declare `environment.carries` for it. `route` would have to declare `environment.requires` for `normal_address` and `raw_address`.
  - With those declarations, `route` may lawfully obtain `raw_address`. Without them it may not.

- **What the method says about the line as published:**
  - Modelling composition as "the last output feeds the next input" is not the ODD law.
  - A product that publishes graph functions without the cumulative environment law has a line that is graph-shaped but structurally wrong.
  - The line is therefore not faithfully refined. It needs the `environment.requires`, `environment.provides` and `environment.carries` declarations on each function.
