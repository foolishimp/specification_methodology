**2. Attributes**

- **`max_draught`:** it has a lawful claim record. The record gives the value, source, authority, observed date and publication date. The effective coordinate is missing. It should be stated as a value or an absence, and I carry it as a residual.
- **`tug_required`:** it has no lawful claim record. A pilot conversation is not a traceable ledger entry. This is an unsupported attribute on a published cut, which the saturation law forbids. C-1 is therefore not sufficiently saturated. To repair it, give the attribute a ledger entry with provenance and authority, or remove it, or state it as an explicit ambiguity. Because the cut is already published, the repair must go in a new superseding cut.

**3. The 2026-10-05 edit**

- **Lawful?** No. Published cuts are immutable.
- **What publication requires:** moving the B-07 boundary to end at bollard 42 changes the boundary. That must be published as a new cut that supersedes C-1, with the discrimination basis recorded again. The B-07/B-08 bollard-41 ambiguity should be restated for the new boundary.
