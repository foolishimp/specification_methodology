# Card: bounded evaluation step from the documents given

You are one bounded step actor. Your whole input is the line above this
card, this card, zero or more METHOD blocks (documents of a specification
methodology; there may be none), and one TASK block (a subject record and a
claim about it). You cannot acquire anything else and you have no memory.

## Task

Perform the bounded evaluation the TASK block asks for, in the way the
METHOD blocks direct. Where the METHOD blocks say how an activation is
recorded, what a result may be, what counts as evidence, or when to stop,
follow them and cite the document and section you followed. Where they do
not say, or where no METHOD block is given, do what you judge right and say
so in the record.

## Output

Return only a record with these headings, in this order, and nothing
after it:

INTAKE — what you were activated to decide, for whom, over which subject
(identified exactly), under which basis, with which permissions, and which
conditions would send the decision back to its owner.

CONTEXT FIT — the byte size of your whole input as stated on the first
line, the cap stated there, and whether you were within it; state whether
the measurement was taken before you read anything.

FACTS — the judgments, observations, residual uncertainties and decisions
you produced that a later actor could not re-derive from the subject record
alone.

STOP — one word for the state you stop in and why.

HANDOFF — what a different actor would need, and would do next, to

COST — the number of METHOD blocks you were given, which of them you cited,

# STDO 3 Standards (Claude tenant realisation)

Status: the Claude tenant's document realisation, constructed through four
steel threads (T1–T4) and awaiting independent assessment. Not released,
not installed, not common WHAT. Builder basis `stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/`.

STDO 3 is a specification methodology for construction and evaluation by
actors with finite context, attention, time and capability. Its core
statement: interpret a scoped subject; select a purpose and governing
contract; transform within admitted authority while conserving its material
relations; evaluate the resulting claims; continue, recover or conclude from
current state under explicit uncertainty.

## 1. Vocabulary of standing

A clause in this corpus has one standing relative to the rc.2 builder:

- conserved: the rc.2 clause governs unchanged; the route is given;
- reframed: the obligation is kept and restated at a different scope or

(with named premises), independent (argued by non-entailment) or hypothesis.

## 3. Commitments

P0 Bounded construction. A finite actor realises a selected outer contract
through steps. Each step's selected context fits the actor's envelope. The
actor's internal path within a step is its own. Recovered as a composite;
the generic statement is reframed from the ODD profile's graph functions.
Premises: a selected outcome (P2); the actor of one step need not be the
actor of the next and may re-acquire declared sources but not private
memory; step-produced facts are not re-acquirable; the world is open; a
step's output is a candidate until evaluated.

P1 Interpretation. Subject, representation and interpretation are distinct;
meaning holds within a declared context through a declared contract.
Independent: with purpose, identity, evidence and authority fixed, two
contracts give one representation two meanings. "Algorithmic" means explicit
and structurally checkable; a semantic decision is lawful only under an exact
decision contract; structural success never establishes fidelity. Conserves
[AC-017](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-017-interpretation-fidelity),
[AC-018](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-018-structural-and-semantic-separation),
[AC-019](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-019-valid-model),
[RF-008](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-008-overlap-without-identity-collapse).

P2 Purpose. A selected outcome defines adequacy; interfaces inherit material
obligations from the transformation they serve; an obligation with no route
to the outcome is unjustified. Independent: identical capacity serves

[UP-005](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#boundary-and-governance-cost-stdo-up-005).

P3 Identity. An object's identity is declared (authority, kind, value) and
survives admissible change; current state, historical observation and
representation identity stay separate; a boundary is a claim with a proof

## 4. Algebra

Composition. For steps s₁: X → Y and s₂: Y → Z, s₂ ∘ s₁ exists only under P4a.
The composite's output carries both steps' step-produced facts. Composition
law is required only where the governing evaluation combines internal
variables or transports values across steps; independent closed results may
instead go to one declared conjunction (RF-010).

Conservation accounting. Every transformation records what it preserved,
introduced and removed, its external resolution witnesses and its residuals
(AC-013). For a required obligation set R and admitted coverage C, uncovered
coverage is R minus C; combining results takes the union of known failures
and recomputes uncovered coverage; a composite with a known failure or an
uncovered required obligation cannot project success. A known failure A and
an uncovered obligation B survive every join.

Reference versus body. A reference conserves a relation when its owner,
exact selector, authenticity, availability and completeness support the same
consuming decision. An inaccessible or insufficient reference is a residual,
not success. A projection cannot trim a required member to fit a carrier or
budget
([AC-011](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-011-projection)).

Latitude. Two values differ lawfully only through a declared latitude or
transformation relation; preference, probabilistic choice and human presence
are not implicit latitude
([AC-008](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-008-explicit-latitude)).

Shared machinery. Computational similarity yields a commonisation candidate,
never an adjudication of identity or authority; one implementation may serve
many instances while each instance keeps its responsibility and binding
([UP-019](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#computational-realization-projection-stdo-up-019),
[UP-023](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#capability-and-foundation-selection-stdo-up-023)).

Formal fragment (new in this corpus; P1). Where 𝒜 is the free category on
typed primitives, ℐ the selected carrier sets with total functions and 𝒞_H
pairs of functions with additive costs, S: 𝒜 → ℐ and R_H: 𝒜 → 𝒞_H are
functors and S is not faithful; that S is not an equivalence. Any further
categorical claim defines its objects, arrows, identities and composition and
proves preservation (conserves
[functor kinds](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#traversals-and-functor-kinds),
which states that obligation and asserts no categorical functor itself).
H names an architecture, never a runtime.

===== METHOD STDO 3 CALCULUS.md =====
# CALCULUS

Tier 1. Owns how bounded work proceeds: the step, context construction under
an envelope, the result algebra, totality, refinement, work state and
restart, cost, and reuse. Consumes [FOUNDATIONS](FOUNDATIONS.md); adds no
definitions.

## 1. The step

A step is activated from an intake sufficient to reconstruct its governing
relations without conversation history, commentary or a prior actor's memory
(P0, actor-change premise). The intake names:

- the selected outcome, admitted prerequisite or experiment it serves (P2);
- the affected relation set and the invariants that govern it;
- the exact basis and the inputs it may acquire, each by identity and hash
  or by an input commit;
- the grant: the operations permitted, the territory that may change, and
  the decisions reserved to others (P6);
- the conditions that require re-entry at an owning relation;
- where the governed question is capability fit or a cost claim, the
  allowance and how spend will be measured (§6).

Conserves
[UP-020 intake](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#agentic-construction-execution-stdo-up-020)
and
[UP-018](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#invariant-reconstruction-sufficiency-stdo-up-018).

The step's output is a candidate plus its step-produced facts: judgments with
their evidence, residuals, observations, authority decisions, provenance, and
one stop state from §3. The actor's internal path is not part of the output
and is not governed
([Probabilistic Work Boundary](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#probabilistic-work-boundary)).

Roles are relations over steps: a constructor produces the candidate and
self-review; an assessor evaluates a frozen candidate independently; a
decision owner disposes. One actor may hold several where independence for
the assessed claim is preserved; no role name confers an operation
([RF-016](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-016-implementation-neutrality)).
When one capable context has sufficient authority and intake, it enters
directly as constructor; coordination is selected only when material
contexts, authority or required independence need it.

## 2. Context construction under an envelope

Given a seed set Z (the subject, outcome and affected relations named in the
intake), a relation family R (the dependency relations whose change can
alter the result, basis validity, evidence sufficiency or authority), and
the actor's envelope B:

1. Compute the material closure of Z under R
   ([AC-010](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-010-material-closure)).
   If the closure is not finite and unique on the exact basis, the step
   returns a gap or refusal; it does not guess.
2. Project the closure to the selected context, recording its boundary: the
   excluded, external, unresolved, translated and invalidation-sensitive
   seams
   ([AC-011](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-011-projection)).
   Material not in the closure stays out; adjacent material does not enter
   because it exists
   ([RF-004](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-004-irrelevance-exclusion)).
3. Test fit against B. For the context dimension the test is a measurement:
   the bytes of the selected context are measured before any read and
   compared with the declared cap, and the measurement, the cap and the
   outcome are step-produced facts recorded before the read. If the context
   fits, activate. If it does not, one or more of these, in combination:
   refine the outer contract into steps whose contexts fit (§4); sequence
   activations; apply a lawful compression that preserves the material
   distinctions and failure detections the evaluation needs
   ([RF-017](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-017-proportional-compression));
   select another actor whose envelope fits; or return out_of_frame. Silent
   omission to fit is never one of them
   ([RF-014](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-014-capability-fit)).
   Within its grant a step selects refinement, sequencing and compression
   itself; selecting another actor or enlarging the envelope re-enters the
   owner of the grant or envelope, who may delegate that choice in the grant
   (RF-014 vests the selection in human authority; a grant is that authority
   exercised in advance). Whether a compression preserved what the
   evaluation needs is a claim with the standing of any other (EVALUATION
   §1): the compressing step records the compression and its preservation
   argument, and the claim is assessed independently only where the
   promotion it serves needs independent assessment (EVALUATION §3).
4. Acquire declared inputs by their exact identity. A declared input absent
   from the actor's materialised view is neither licence to read elsewhere
   nor evidence that it is absent.

The operation that joins AC-010, AC-011 and RF-002/003/004/014/017 into one
procedure is new in this corpus (P0). The method does not require proving
that no undiscovered material relation exists
([RF-003](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-003-material-sufficiency)).

## 3. Result algebra

One core algebra for every evaluation and every step's stop state (P5):

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| satisfied | the declared predicate holds on the exact basis |
| falsified | a declared counterexample defeats it |
| indeterminate | admissible evidence cannot decide it |
| out_of_frame | the evaluation needs an undeclared material relation or capability |
| invalid_basis | the subject or basis cannot support the evaluation |

Nothing is coerced to success; a missing, failed or out-of-scope result
returns its member, never satisfied. This conserves
[RF-012](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-012-closed-results),
whose five members the table restates unchanged, and
[AC-007](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-007-constraint-and-refusal),
whose rule that a failed, missing, indeterminate, out-of-scope or
invalid-basis result follows the declared algebra rather than being coerced
to success is the sentence above; both are restated here once and continue
to govern.

Where evidence is missing because a relation or capability needed to obtain
it was not declared, the result is out_of_frame; indeterminate is reserved
for the case where the declared capability was exercised and the admissible
evidence still cannot decide.

A composite over several results declares its conjunction (FOUNDATIONS §4).
The member it reports is chosen by that declared rule; every known failure
and uncovered obligation survives the join and is carried with the
composite. No universal order among the non-satisfied members exists; an
application declares its own.

Declared refinements answer different questions and keep their own
vocabulary, each with a total mapping to the core:

| Refinement | Question | Members | Core mapping |
|---|---|---|---|
| construction availability | did the step yield a candidate | candidate_ready, refused, incomplete, re_entry_requested | satisfied, falsified, indeterminate, out_of_frame or invalid_basis respectively ([work result](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#work-result)) |
| disposition | what the decision owner does with an assessed candidate | accept, local_repair, re_enter, reject | consumes a core result; is not one ([UP-020](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#agentic-construction-execution-stdo-up-020)) |
| ambiguity outcome | what happened to a declared ambiguity | resolved, carried, escalated, blocked | a policy state over a core result ([Ambiguity Governance](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#ambiguity-governance-rule)) |
| profile closure decisions | how a profile's unit closes | profile-defined | the profile declares its mapping; it may not add a core member |

## 4. Totality and refinement

Dispositions after any non-satisfied result are named in advance: continue
within the current contract when supported; refine; recover; yield; re-enter
the owning relation; terminate. Each admitted condition class has a declared
disposition, including an explicit unknown class. Total handling does not
claim that every external condition is recognised, that an invoked provider
terminates, or that every request can be completed. A hanging external call,
an unsatisfiable demand and a lost post-effect response are three different
cases and none is solved by adding a value to the algebra.

Ambiguity is carried or decided by lawful probabilistic processing where
policy allows, escalated to human adjudication where policy or risk appetite
requires, and recorded either way. Some conditions are hard stops regardless
of appetite: a violated invariant, an absent required authority, a missing
declared capability, an undeclared irreversible effect, an explicit human
gate (Ambiguity Governance, conserved).

Refinement. An outer transformation T: A → X with success predicates P₀ … Pₙ
is faithfully refined by steps Tᵢ when each admitted input satisfying Pᵢ
yields an output satisfying Pᵢ₊₁, retains every applicable invariant, exposes
other outcomes without coercion, and each output satisfies the next step's
domain and preconditions (P4a). Then a successful composite carries P₀ to
Pₙ by induction. Coverage of the outer obligations, correctness of the local
implications and legitimate authority are premises, not conclusions; "each
step passed" is weaker than "the outer contract was conserved". Required
coupled relations must fit together in some step; unrelated obligations stay
outside the selected context. Every seam crossing the affected set includes
its far side or states why it cannot participate
([UP-017](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#bounded-frame-conservation-stdo-up-017)).
Admission of a material transition is one semantic commit of the complete
transition within its enclosing relation, or none
([UP-021](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#complete-enclosing-relation-admission-stdo-up-021)).
This generalises ODD
[graph functions](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#graph-functions),
which refine one outer vector into a small inner workflow, to any
realisation; PROFILES §2 keeps the ODD binding.

## 5. Work state and restart

Work state is the tuple of: the current persistent workspace; the governing
basis; the applicable closed results and judgments with their invalidators;
the residuals; and the known uncertainty over external effects. It is an
object (P3); its carrier (files, an event stream, another admitted owner) is
a profile or realisation choice.

Resumption is re-entry of the same method over current work state by a
possibly different actor. It recovers applicable progress, re-checks the
invalidators of each reused result (§7), reopens only affected support, and
reconciles uncertain external effects before any repetition. Resumption does
not imply idempotence: an action whose external effect may have occurred is
not repeated blindly; the lawful results are reconciliation, yield or stop.
A preservation checkpoint may retain unfinished work under an existing
grant; a proposed restart, which reconstructs from less than the retained
work, states why repair of retained work is insufficient, what survives and
which causal assumptions change; reconstructability alone does not select
reconstruction. Conserves
[UP-020 checkpoint and continuation](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#agentic-construction-execution-stdo-up-020);
the generic work-state object, the reconciliation of uncertain external
effects and the non-idempotence rule are new (P0, P5 with the open-world
premise).

Progress is accepted material movement toward the selected outcome;
prerequisite work names what it enables; preservation and repeated proof are
evidence, not progress; rejected or superseded construction is churn;
discovering hidden distance revises the forecast and is not negative
progress. Volume of documents, code, tests, commits or reviews is not
progress.

## 6. Cost and proportionality

Proportionality is the relation between semantic ambiguity removed and
effective reasoning complexity introduced. An obligation, distinction,
carrier or document is justified when its contraction of the admissible
interpretation space pays for the concepts, joins, exceptions and states an
actor must hold to decide. Apply this to the method's own execution: source
acquisition, context assembly, hashing, activation records, status recording
and review serve an identified decision or assurance need. Conserves
[UP-014](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#proportional-method-and-delivery-stdo-up-014),
[UP-005](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#boundary-and-governance-cost-stdo-up-005).

Cost is selected, not universal. Where capability fit or a cost claim is the
governed question, the activation declares its allowance and reports spend
with its measurement source, or records that the counter is unavailable
(new; P0 with UP-014's selection rule). Separate startup, framework, external computation, transport and assurance
costs so that one cannot conceal another; declare supported input dimensions
and workloads before qualification
([UP-019](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#computational-realization-projection-stdo-up-019)).
Report volume is not model traffic; transferred context is counted across
every delegated actor, not only the coordinator. No universal quota, ticket
count, actor count or accounting carrier follows.

## 7. Reuse

A closed result is reused by an unchanged consumer after an applicability
check; a role, conversation or resumption change alone invalidates nothing.
Changed material inputs or a declared revising observation invalidate the
affected result before reliance, preserving the predecessor and any
demonstrably independent surviving results; a changed basis invalidates
likewise (judgment continuity names basis; Return A Closed Result names
inputs and observation). A reused judgment does not
satisfy a separately required independent judgment; the independent assessor
acquires its own evidence and supplies its own judgment. Conserves
[Return A Closed Result](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#return-a-closed-result),
[judgment continuity](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/TICKET_METHOD.md#recorded-judgment-and-owner-ruling-continuity).

===== METHOD STDO 3 EVALUATION.md =====
# EVALUATION

Tier 1. Owns how claims are justified and accepted: claims, evidence,
independent assessment, review results, acceptance, conformance of
realisations, and the method's own qualification. Consumes
[FOUNDATIONS](FOUNDATIONS.md) and the result algebra in
[CALCULUS §3](CALCULUS.md).

## 1. Claims

A load-bearing claim names its exact subject, the property claimed, the
governing basis, the relation between witness and claim, the nearest weaker
property it excludes, and its falsifier, before a witness is selected.
Without these it is evidence discovery, not proof. Packaging, syntax,
compilation, presence, invocation or local behaviour never substitutes for a
semantic, authority-bearing, installed or end-to-end claim because the
weaker property is easier to measure (P6; conserves
[UP-001](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#proof-target-identity-and-adequacy-stdo-up-001)).

A claim's quantifier, population and scope cannot exceed its evaluated
evidence. Generalising a local observation needs a declared inference
relation, the comparable population and explicit counterexample treatment.
Later counterevidence invalidates every dependent verdict until the
claim-evidence relation is requalified
([UP-022](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#assurance-boundary-congruence-and-method-qualification-stdo-up-022)).

## 2. Separation

Semantic law defines what an evaluation means; evidence supplies
observations; the evaluator applies the declared relation; decision
authority disposes. One actor may lawfully perform several; the record keeps
them distinguishable. Evidence change may invalidate a verdict without
changing law; projection change may invalidate a view without changing
truth; semantic change requires re-entry and invalidates dependent
acceptance (conserves
[UP-008](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#semantic-evidence-and-projection-separation-stdo-up-008),
[RF-007](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-007-semantic-evidence-and-verdict-separation)).

Structural conformance decides closed syntax, types, identities, references,
bases and mechanically decidable predicates. Semantic fidelity decides
meaning and materiality. A checker decides the structural subset; it claims a
semantic or authority subset only under an exact lawful decision contract
supplied by the model
([AC-018](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-018-structural-and-semantic-separation),
[AC-019](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-019-valid-model)).
A valid parse, a valid map, a green test and a historical judgment each
establish exactly their own claim.

## 3. Independent assessment

A constructor's self-review is construction output, not assessment. Where
promotion needs independent assessment, the assessor evaluates the exact
live subject, authority paths, proof and installed state, not the
constructor's summary (P6 with the probabilistic-construction premise;
conserves
[UP-007](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/TICKET_METHOD.md#execution-and-verification-authority-separation-stdo-up-007)).

Protocol: the subject is frozen by identity before material reference
exposure; the assessor records its expectations from the governing source
before opening the candidate; it reads the subject at a named immutable
identity, never a working tree; it makes no repair; it does not assess its
own authorship. Reusing the constructor's judgment does not satisfy
independence; shared mechanical evidence may be reused, judgment may not
(CALCULUS §7). Any post-exposure revision is a declared intervention and a
new subject (UP-022).

Independence across actor families is declared, not assumed. Whether two
activations of one model family are independent for a semantic judgment is a
policy the governing basis states for the claim; the default where it is not
stated is that they are not independent.

## 4. Review results

A review returns one core result (CALCULUS §3) for each declared predicate it
evaluated, with: the exact subject and basis, the evidence consulted, the
findings ranked by consequence, each finding's evidence and the smallest
bounded correction, the residual uncertainty, and the invalidation
conditions. It recommends a disposition; it does not apply one. A finding
without evidence a consumer can chase is commentary.

## 5. Acceptance

Acceptance is the decision owner's disposition over an unchanged subject;
recording it cannot widen it, and it exhausts only the instance and interval
it names (P6, P2; conserves
[UP-013](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#product-outcome-conservation-stdo-up-013),
[AC-014](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-014-judgment-separation)).
A work item closes by the condition it stated, not by a reviewer's approval;
a reviewer result is an input to closure, never the closure. Green labels,
document counts, review counts and historical outcomes are not acceptance.
A human decision may be exercised directly or through an explicitly admitted
bounded proxy whose grant names its scope and exclusions
([UP-004](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/IDENTITY_METHOD.md#authority-identity-and-conservation-stdo-up-004));
a proxy cannot change Product meaning, requirements, governing authority
or accepted design, and such a change re-enters at its owning relation
([UP-020](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#agentic-construction-execution-stdo-up-020)).

## 6. Conformance of realisations

A realisation aⱼ conforms to a specification Σ when its interpreted behaviour
satisfies Σ under a declared interpretation contract (P1) and an independent
judgment (P6). Conformance establishes neither identical behaviour outside Σ
nor equal cost; semantic equivalence and computational equivalence are
separate claims with separate evidence. Several realisations may conform to
one Σ; sufficiency does not require unique derivation or equality with an
incumbent (conserves the
[Reconstruction Litmus](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#reconstruction-litmus)
and
[UP-022](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#assurance-boundary-congruence-and-method-qualification-stdo-up-022)'s
comparison rule, under which unique derivation, determinism and incumbent
equality are not generic comparison criteria). A weak Σ that admits materially different
behaviours either declares the latitude or is repaired before a
decision-complete design is claimed.

## 7. Qualification of the method itself

The method is qualified the way it qualifies anything else: a fresh
competent constructor, inside a declared envelope, using only the declared
ordinary method and authority surfaces and declared trial subjects, with
material reference outcomes, incumbent realisations, author memory and ad
hoc rescue withheld until freeze wherever earlier exposure would
compromise reconstructive independence; then an independent evaluator
compares the frozen result with the method's mandatory relation
population. This conserves
[Method Self-Qualification](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#method-self-qualification),
which leaves the constructor, freeze and assessor protocols to the
governing authority, and
[RF-013](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-013-reconstructable-activation)
for the reconstructability of the activation; the surfaces rule and the
conditional withholding conserve
[UP-022](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#assurance-boundary-congruence-and-method-qualification-stdo-up-022)'s
qualification protocol.

Recoverability benchmark (new; derived from P0 with the actor-change
premise). For a selected activation, measure whether a fresh actor given
only the published documents recovers the governing relations with fidelity
judged independently, and at what reconstruction cost (context transferred,
time, and spend where measurable), against the rc.2 corpus as baseline. A
document set that requires private validator knowledge, whole-corpus loading
or a prior actor's memory fails the benchmark regardless of its content.
This is the observable for the matrix criteria F27 and F28; F29 and F30 are
the same observation reported with the task completed, the material errors
detected and the cost.

===== METHOD STDO 3 SPEC_METHOD.md =====
# SPEC_METHOD

Tier 2. One particular methodology for governed construction, realised from
[FOUNDATIONS](FOUNDATIONS.md), [CALCULUS](CALCULUS.md) and
[EVALUATION](EVALUATION.md). Nothing here is foundational; every mechanism
names the principle that requires it and could be realised differently
under the same principles.

## 1. The chain

```text
Goals → Intent → Product Definition → Requirements (WHAT)
  → Design and realisation (HOW, per tenant) → Code or documents
  → Events → Projection → Delta → Scenarios → Gap analysis
  → Repricing of goals, intent, product, requirements, design or realisation
```

Each layer narrows ambiguity for the next: goals narrow focus, intent
direction, product the current shape and terms, requirements obligation,
tenant choice the realisation class, design structure, implementation
detail. A layer is sufficient when a competent actor can derive a conformant
next layer from it; derivation need not be unique. Failure to derive is a
defect of the upper layer. Major ambiguity, the kind that changes
architecture, boundary, admissibility or public contract, is detected and
recorded at each boundary with its disposition: resolved, carried, escalated
or blocked (P2; conserves the
[constitutional chain](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#constitutional-chain),
[Ambiguity Governance](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#ambiguity-governance-rule),
[Reconstruction Litmus](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#reconstruction-litmus)).

Goals are the current work wave; Product Definition is the present-tense
shape of the mutable source project, not the released artifact; requirements
state what the project must make true; design chooses how. Read models,
commentary and generated views never outrank live specification.

## 2. One WHAT, independent HOWs

A product definition's `what` bindings locate the shared constitutional WHAT;
its `how.build_tenants` bindings locate one or more independent realisations.
Tenant-local realisation is derivative until its governing truth is ratified
in specification. Each tenant works in its own territory; a shared change
returns to the WHAT's pen; a tenant never patches common WHAT or a peer
realisation (P2, P4b; conserves the WHAT/HOW split in the constitutional
chain). Conformance of a tenant is judged under EVALUATION §6; two conforming
tenants need not behave identically outside the contract.

## 3. Outcome conservation

Every outcome-bearing work wave names one selected, unresolved, directly
verifiable outcome and its acceptance interval. Progress is judged only
against it: material advance is progress; prerequisite work names the
obligation it enables; preservation is evidence; regression blocks the
affected path. An evidence surface, proof, matrix, coverage record or test
suite cannot by itself select, enlarge or replace the outcome; where an
assurance capability is itself defined as product behaviour, its delivery is
progress and its evidence still does not author the claim. Acceptance
exhausts the instance and interval; further work against it is preservation, a named
prerequisite for another admitted outcome, or a new change requiring
repricing. Authority for material growth derives from the selected outcome,
an admitted bounded prerequisite or an admitted experiment, each with a
stated exhaustion condition; ended authority is not renewed by repair,
continuation or active status (P2; conserves
[UP-013](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#product-outcome-conservation-stdo-up-013)).

## 4. Change classes and re-entry

Every substantive change, whatever its intake label, enters through one
intake triage that names the smallest lawful re-entry point
([Universal Intake Triage](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#universal-intake-triage)):

| Class | Re-enters at |
|---|---|
| goal_reprice | the current work wave |
| intent_reprice | direction or scope |
| product_reprice | current product shape while intent holds |
| requirement_reprice | constitutional truth while direction holds |
| design_reframe | realisation structure while requirements hold |
| realization_refactor | local realisation with no intended upstream change |

Triage records the change intent, the class, the re-entry point and when
triage occurred, wherever substantive work begins. A changed governing
relation is accepted only at its owner; accepting it inside the acting
frame is a violation, whatever the size of the delta
([UP-018](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#invariant-reconstruction-sufficiency-stdo-up-018)).
A change that alters a load-bearing shared contract is a migration, not a
patch: change the authoritative contract first, audit every producer and
consumer, migrate each, and chase every downstream effect before declaring
completion
([Core Interface Migration Rule](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#core-interface-migration-rule)).

The carrier of a work item is selected for its required lifetime: a durable
ticket where the work needs identity across sessions; a sprint-local entry
where its only value is traceability inside a batch; a run or return record
(the rc.2 run-scoped execution contract) where the work lives and dies with
one activation. The triage fields are required wherever substantive work
begins; a ticket per change is not (P4b, P2; conserves the change classes,
the
[triage metadata](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/TICKET_METHOD.md#triage-metadata)
and the
[TICKET_METHOD core model](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/TICKET_METHOD.md#core-model)'s
selection of the smallest lawful carrier; reframes its default, so that no
carrier is the default here and the triage fields, not the ticket, are the
required minimum).

## 5. Activation, checkpoint and continuation

Work is activated as steps under CALCULUS §1. A step's return goes to its
declared consumer; in a coordinated configuration that consumer is the
executive relation, which consumes closed results, applies disposition under
existing decision authority and selects the next authorised action
([Executive](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#executive)).
Acceptance binds one exact checkpoint and does not widen the outcome; the
next already-authorised step may begin without renewed human approval when
basis, affected set, decision envelope and re-entry conditions are already
accepted. Direct human acceptance is not weakened where Product or release
law requires it. A preservation checkpoint, restart and the progress and
churn categories are as in CALCULUS §5 (conserves
[UP-020](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#agentic-construction-execution-stdo-up-020)).
The baseline's
[Executive](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#executive),
[Worker](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#worker)
and
[Reviewer](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#reviewer)
are conserved by reference as labels for UP-020's roles; that a role is a
relation an actor occupies is this corpus's ontology (FOUNDATIONS §2), not
a claim about the baseline text.

Delivery is sequenced around the smallest real path from a supported entry
to a material outcome, established early and widened by increments chosen
for consequential uncertainty, interface reach and failure impact. A passing
path establishes only the relations it exercised. This restates the
baseline's
[Steel-Thread Delivery](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#steel-thread-delivery),
which governs its profile unchanged; here it is reframed as a sequencing
heuristic for any realisation, not law (P2).

## 6. Lineage and release

Software is recursive: a released product builds its successor, a dependent
product, or artifacts. The roles are distinct and named: source project
(mutable), release cut (immutable boundary), product (released), install
(stamped instance), artifact (published output), development product (an
install acting as builder). The product definition is the source project's
surface, not the release (P3; conserves the
[recursive Product taxonomy](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#recursive-product-taxonomy)).

A successor names the exact predecessor immutable cut whose claims it
evolves, and gives every predecessor claim capable of affecting it one
disposition: conserved, superseded, intentionally removed, or not applicable
with a bounded reason. An undisposed claim blocks qualification of the affected
claim. Conservation is semantic: patch absence does not prove loss when a
successor-native realisation preserves behaviour, and naming or code
similarity does not prove conservation without relevant evidence (P3, P6;
conserves
[UP-015](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/RELEASE_METHOD.md#successor-baseline-conservation-stdo-up-015)).
This corpus's own dispositions are in each document's last section.

Release identities, phases, naming, cohorts and publication follow
[RELEASE_METHOD](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/RELEASE_METHOD.md#release-identities)
([phases](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/RELEASE_METHOD.md#phases),
[naming](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/RELEASE_METHOD.md#naming),
[cohorts](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/RELEASE_METHOD.md#coordinated-release-matched-asset-cohorts))
by reference; they are not restated here. Layer and release separation
([AC-020](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-020-layer-and-release-separation))
holds: a calculus, its interpretation of a subject and its carrier encoding
keep independent content identities, judgments, supersession relations and
proof obligations, and none is automatically an independently released
product.

## 7. Governance of tenants in this source project

Tenant branches, worktrees, sparse cones, snapshot binding to an input commit,
authorised reviews at named commits and merge on acceptance are local
development law of the stdo_3 source project, stated once in its Product
Definition
([Development Isolation](../../../specification/PRODUCT.md#development-isolation))
and imported here by reference.

===== METHOD STDO 3 CONFORMANCE.md =====
# CONFORMANCE

Tier 2. The cases this corpus must pass before it is qualified. A case names
its subject, the mechanism under test, the independently specified expected
distinction, and its status. A status names what exists: a fixture
designed, runs by a fresh constructor, or an assessment by an independent
assessor under EVALUATION §3 and §7. A run is not a pass; only an
assessment records one.

A case passes when the expected distinction is observed and no coerced
success appears anywhere in the result (CALCULUS §3). A case that cannot be
run with the declared subject returns out_of_frame, which is recorded, not
hidden.

## 1. Counterexamples from the builder's own history

# PROFILES

Tier 3. How domain profiles import the core and what stays out of it. A
profile adds domain obligations, bindings and vocabulary; it imports
[FOUNDATIONS](FOUNDATIONS.md), [CALCULUS](CALCULUS.md),
[EVALUATION](EVALUATION.md) and [SPEC_METHOD](SPEC_METHOD.md) by reference
and restates none of them.

## 1. The boundary

The core names no runtime, no carrier and no executor. Terms owned outside
the core, by a profile, a realisation or an rc.2 member, never enter tier
1 or 2 except in a disposition table: graph, edge, vector, traversal,
ledger, event log, worksite, HoG, F_D, F_P, F_H, Markov overlay, module,
screen, entity cut. A profile may bind a core mechanism to one of these;
the binding is declared in the profile with its direction, preserved
meaning, narrowed scope and invalidation. The declared-binding rule is new
(P0 boundary, P1); its shape follows ODD's specialisations of F_P and F_H,
which
[ODD probabilistic compute](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#probabilistic-compute)
owns in the graph-native context and carries as their provenance.

A worked application, a hello world or a data mapper is an external test
subject; its particulars cannot become generic method functionality by
precedent.

## 2. Graph execution

Imports the core and binds: the step to one vector or edge traversal; the
roles to the graph-native shorthands F_P (lawful probabilistic processing
within one traversal), F_D (deterministic evaluation or proof over declared
properties) and F_H (human adjudication under the traversal's declared
authority); work state to the workspace, governed ledger and event-log
triple; the result refinements to the profile's edge closure decisions with
their declared mapping to the core algebra; refinement to graph functions.
The runtime owns traversal, admission, runtime facts, frames, continuations,
lineage and projection; the product owns domain meaning, the function
catalogue, policy overlays and domain proof interpretation; the framework
never absorbs the worker's internal solution strategy. Conserved by
reference:
[ODD_METHOD](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#probabilistic-compute),
[graph functions](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#graph-functions),
[constructive evaluation and yield loop](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/ODD_METHOD.md#115d-constructive-evaluation-and-yield-loop),
TRAVERSAL_OCCURRENCE_PROFILE and the baseline's runtime bindings. The loop's
four functions (synthesise model, evaluate gap, evaluate next, evaluate
action) are the profile's realisation of CALCULUS §4–§5 and keep their
distinct authority.

## 3. Software design

Imports the core and binds: the derivation of operation identities from
discovered functionality through an ontology, logical completeness, prime
contraction, the irreducible carrier set and projections
([ontology-first design](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#4b-ontology-first-design-rule),

[ontology projection](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#ontology-projection-law));
transformation admission completeness
