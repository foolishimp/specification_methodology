# E2E-01: one discrepancy carried end to end through this candidate's law

Purpose: exercise the delivery-and-correction path that the candidate's
own law describes, on a real item of this line, before the remaining I5
items are built on it (PLAN §2 row 2). The path is intake, diagnosis,
correction, reassessment and closure, with an interruption, an uncertain
effect and a valid reuse. Each step names the section of the candidate
that governs it and records what that section required. The record is
written as the steps happen; a step not yet taken is marked so.

Subject: the frames under `standards/frames/` after the I5-01 owner texts
(`e731bd7`, repaired at `9e92e79`). The frames are the candidate's
acquisition surface for criterion 4; each copies owning sections byte for
byte and records their digests.

## 1. Intake (SPEC_METHOD §4 Universal intake triage)

Report, 2026-10-16: after the I5-01 edits, `generate_frames.py --check`
reports drift on all 12 frames and the manifest. At the same time the
ratified common input requires that each frame bind the construction
requirement (REFERENCE_FRAME_BASIS, reconstruction and conservation
paragraphs; requirements/README bounded transitions item 1), which no
frame does.

Triage determines the six things §4 names:

1. Substantive change: yes. A stale frame misroutes a fresh actor; a frame without the required binding fails the ratified frame requirement.
2. Affected boundary and scope: `standards/frames/*.md`, `standards/frames/FRAME_INPUTS.json`, `qualification/instruments/generate_frames.py` and its tests; downstream, the trial's expectation sets that were written from the frames.
3. Lawful change class: two. The drift is a `realization_refactor` (regenerate from unchanged law). The missing binding is a `design_reframe` of the frame contract (a new field of the frame inputs and header), with no change to member text.
4. Re-entry point: `design/` for the frame contract (the generator's documented layout, `design/CROSS_SYNTHESIS.md` part 5 and `qualification/instruments/ROUTE_CARD.md`), then the realisation.
5. Surfaces and evidence to re-derive: the twelve frames and the manifest; the generator tests; the applicability of the eight hashed expectation sets `trial/expectations/TT-01..08.md`, which were written from frame bytes; the frame-size tradeoff TO1 (capacity 150,000 bytes, largest frame 147,702).
6. Release scope: inside the admitted I5 construction (EC-CLAUDE-I5-01, I5-06). No repricing of the release plan.

No intake bypasses this triage to go straight to the generator (§4).

## 2. Diagnosis (ACTIVATION §1 Feedback and diagnosis)

Observation: owner-level drift 12 of 12; section-level drift, recomputed
from the recorded section digests, 6 of 12 frames (`rf-task-a`,
`rf-task-b`, `eb-task-d`, `configuration`, `stale-summary`,
`member-build-assess`) over two changed sections
(`ACTIVATION.md#7-closed-result-and-reuse`,
`FOUNDATIONS.md#foundation-to-law`); the `transitions` section, changed at
`e731bd7` by a misplaced block, is byte-identical again at `9e92e79`.

Comparison with the governing conditions: the frame requirement
(REFERENCE_FRAME §1, ACTIVATION §2) requires each frame to carry the
current owning sections and, since `4c340f7`, to bind the construction
requirement.

The four causes, each judged:

| Cause | Judgment | Ground |
|---|---|---|
| Defective realisation | present | six frames copy sections whose bytes changed; the other six copy unchanged sections but record a stale owner-file digest in their `Source:` lines |
| Inadequate governing requirement or design | present | the frame contract (inputs, header, manifest) has no field for the binding the ratified input requires; this is a design gap, not a defect of any frame |
| Inaccurate predictive model | present, in the check | the `--check` mode predicts invalidity from the owner-file digest; the section digest is the material dependency. The over-approximation is harmless for regeneration and wrong for evidence reuse: it would discard the four unaffected frames' evidence. The reassessment step uses the section dependency, as FOUNDATIONS §4 Change impact and evidence reuse requires (`D*(t)` material to the claim) |
| Insufficient observation | absent | both digests are recorded and reproducible |

Unresolved alternatives: none. Routing: the realisation defect to the
generator run (I5-06, this record); the design gap to the frame contract
(this record, correction step); the model inadequacy to the generator's
`--check`, which gains a section-level report. Severity: a stale frame
blocks criterion-4 evidence for its task; priority: before any trial run
and before the remaining I5 items, which change further sections.
Label: the report entered as a check failure, not as a "bug"; the label
supplied no class (§1).
