## Findings

1. **Medium.** ACTIVATION §7 para 2 and FOUNDATIONS §4 Change impact para 7. Quote: "its three invalidators are instances of that relation's impact cover `I(Δ)`" / "with its three invalidators as instances of `I(Δ)`". Expected: by Change impact para 2, Δ is "a change to material facts or dependencies" and `I(Δ)` is the cover of "the changed facts and their affected dependents"; an invalidator is a kind of Δ, and a result is affected when its `D*(t)` meets `I(Δ)`. The application statement names the wrong formal object. Fix (both loci): "its three invalidators are kinds of change Δ; a closed result is affected when its dependencies `D*(t)` meet the impact cover `I(Δ)`".

2. **Medium.** ACTIVATION §7 para 3 against FOUNDATIONS §4 Change impact para 5. Quote (§7): "An invalidator invalidates the affected result before reliance, even where the subject's own bytes are unchanged" and "a changed basis: a change to any coordinate of the basis the result binds". Quote (FOUNDATIONS, carried from common ASSURANCE): "The original evidence keeps its original subject; a new applicability relation permits its use. A changed hash alone establishes neither invalidity nor equivalence." Expected: one sense of "invalid". §7 uses the predecessor's procedural sense (withdrawn from direct reliance; 4B's "invalidates ... until their impact is re-evaluated"); FOUNDATIONS uses the semantic sense (the claim is not shown false or inapplicable). Read together, a digest-valued basis coordinate changing both "invalidates" the result and "establishes neither invalidity nor equivalence". Fix: one sentence in §7 para 3 after "before reliance": "invalidation withdraws the result from direct reliance; retention of its evidence on the new basis is FOUNDATIONS §4 `retain_applicable`, and the changed coordinate alone establishes neither that the claim fails nor that it holds."

3. **Medium.** ACTIVATION §1 family table, row 11 (Strategy). Quote: "By P0 the resources that planning, checking and construction consume are finite ... so a strategy is selected within the resource envelope". Expected: the Commitments table gives P0 only "A finite actor realises a selected outer contract through steps. Each step's selected context fits the actor's envelope"; the common text places time, spend and other resource limits with "the selected execution binding" (README Bounded Transitions item 3; P5 bounded formulation). The row derives finiteness of all resources from P0, and its instance premises (b, c) do not include a selected resource envelope although the consequence relies on one. Fix: add the selected resource envelope as an instance premise (or cite SPEC_METHOD §11) and restrict P0's contribution to context and attention.
