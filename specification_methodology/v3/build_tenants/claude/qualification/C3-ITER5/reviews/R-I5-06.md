Quote: "the actor's claims keep preservation, progress and outcome satisfaction distinct, returning `unresolved` for what its evidence cannot decide". Expected: the owner FOUNDATIONS l.1259-1264: "`indeterminate` is the core judgment whose admissible evidence cannot decide the predicate (Results). It is distinct from ... work or coverage that remains unresolved, which is a state of the work ... and not a judgment value"; ASSURANCE l.39 says the same. The header claims the item "owns no determination" (l.12-14), but the clause carries a determination, and a wrong one; as a derived view it fails PRODUCT's "derived views meet the constraint-surface fidelity requirement". It is the first thing an actor reads about what to return, and is testable by a `W` item on result vocabulary. Smallest fix: replace `unresolved` with `indeterminate`, or delete the clause after "distinct"; regenerate the twelve frames (owner digests unchanged).

**F6 — Medium.** Locus: §3 l.78-82; GRADER-I5 item 2; WRITER-I5 items 1 and 3. Answer to the brief: the rule needs the writer to name the two returns in the item. Smallest fix: a recorded field in the `A` item naming both returns; grader rule: reads as one of the two → `indeterminate` "does not separate"; silent → "silent"; otherwise → `indeterminate` "outside the described ambiguity" and the item is listed for diagnosis.
