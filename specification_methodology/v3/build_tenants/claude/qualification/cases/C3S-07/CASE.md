## Claim under test

A fresh actor given only one projected frame of the C3 cross-synthesis
candidate and the task applies the candidate's build-tenant binding law
without private knowledge or author memory. For a software tenant and a
human-process tenant of the same common requirements, it must:

- state each tenant's computational calculus and execution environment, and
  recognise that the two may share one calculus;
- keep author identity, actor identity and operation kind distinct;
- separate structural validation of a process model from execution
  conformance;
- judge as non-conforming a faster plan that merges two required
  independent assessments and replaces a required human decision with an
  automated one, whatever its agreement figures and savings.
