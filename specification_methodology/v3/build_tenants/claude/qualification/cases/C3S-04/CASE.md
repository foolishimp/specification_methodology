## Claim under test

A fresh actor given only one projected frame of the C3 cross-synthesis
candidate and the task applies the candidate's executive operational profile
without private knowledge or author memory. Acting as Executive, it:

- revalidates the drift locks against a configuration change that falls
  inside the review's declared invalidation conditions;
- evaluates a `candidate_ready` work result as availability only, and finds
  that independent review is required;
- keeps severity, priority, frame result and current-boundary effect
  distinct, with the owner of each;
- withholds a disposition that the current basis cannot support, and names
  its next action within its grant, without mutating anything itself.
