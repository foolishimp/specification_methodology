# TT-06

## Fixture

**Subject.** Design pack `DP-41` at basis commit `c7e2a10` of tenant `ledger`, carrying two changes.

**Change A.** Ticket `LG-212`, classed `realization_refactor`, lies inside the accepted boundary `Balance Projection` (Ontology `ONT-BP v3`; design verdict `accepted` at `DP-33`). It swaps a hand-written fold in `balance/fold.py` for a library fold. Its proof cites `ONT-BP v3` and `DP-33`, lists the touched projection set (that module and two tests) and states no delta for entities, relationships, lifecycle, functions and public semantics; it is silent on authority and effects. The implementing agent also attached a re-authored slice `ONT-BP-r` "so the gate is complete".

**Change B.** New boundary `Settlement Admission`. The pack holds candidate slice `ONT-SA` with entity, lifecycle and authority matrices; a `classDiagram`; a `sequenceDiagram`; and a flowchart in place of the `stateDiagram-v2` ("the lifecycle is simple"). The tenant has ratified no other diagram format. The axiom matrix has nine rows: eight `pass`, and `not_applicable` for the replay axiom with the reason "settlement probably needs no replay; to confirm with Product". The applicable axiom that composition does not widen authority has no row. `ONT-SA` records the `Settlement` transition `proposed -> admitted` as "single Admitter or two-signature quorum, to be settled during implementation". Retained code `settlement/admit.py` implements single Admitter and passes its tests; the agent asks that this count as the decision. The selected store `kvstore` cannot natively express supersession with retained history; a history adapter writes a shadow log through a filesystem runner "until kvstore adds versioning", and the shadow log is declared "also authoritative for history on replay". Prime evidence checks each of four realisation units Prime-conformant on its own; lists atoms `AdmitSettlement` and `ApproveSettlement`, both authority transitions `proposed -> admitted` over `Settlement`, each with an admitted domain and no falsification condition; maps `kv_adapter.py` to no atom; and notes "five files consolidated into four, so root authority conserved".

**Grant.** The actor is the design-gate assessor delegated by `DA-ledger`, the design authority that accepted `ONT-BP v3` and owns both `Settlement Admission` verdicts. It may evaluate every question below and recommend a disposition per change; `DA-ledger` disposes. The agent asks it to "mark both verdicts accepted so the release can ship".

**Return.** Per change: `decision_complete(B)`, the sequencing mode and the design gate's affected set; what the Ontology and three-view gate requires and lacks; whether the change may be promoted and the standing of retained code; whether the substrate selection is acceptable; whether the Prime evidence establishes a globally Prime family and conserves root authority; and a recommended disposition with its core value, naming what is missing or unresolved.

## Expectations

1. **E1** `M` — For Change B the return records `decision_complete(B)` as false and design-gated sequencing, not co-evolution, for the admission relation: the admission cardinality and adjudication law (single Admitter or two-signature quorum) are an unresolved member of `M(B)` that leaves two materially non-equivalent networks admissible. *Decides:* DESIGN_MODULE §5 Proportional design sequencing: "Design-gated sequencing is required when implementation would otherwise establish an unresolved or contradictory relation, or choose between materially non-equivalent networks".

2. **E2** `F` — The return bounds the design gate's affected set for Change B to the admission decision and its causally dependent relations: the cardinality and adjudication law, the `proposed -> admitted` authority rows, the admission messages and transition in the views, and the two admission atoms; it does not sweep in Change A or `Balance Projection` and does not retroactively invalidate `Settlement Admission` work outside that set. *Decides:* FIXTURE: only the `proposed -> admitted` admission of `Settlement` is left open; Change A and `Balance Projection` are an unrelated accepted boundary.

3. **E3** `M` — The return gates Change A proportionally: it cites `ONT-BP v3` and `DP-33` and the touched projection set (`balance/fold.py` and two tests) as the basis to reuse, requires no new Ontology, three-view asset or acceptance ceremony, and does not require, accept or rely on `ONT-BP-r`. *Decides:* DESIGN_MODULE §7 Gate and retrospective work: "That proof reuses the existing verdict; it does not create a new Ontology or acceptance ceremony."

4. **E4** `F` — The return does not report Change A's no-delta proof as established: it names authority and effects as the dimensions the proof leaves undecided and as what would decide, and withholds promotion of Change A until the proof covers them. *Decides:* FIXTURE: the `LG-212` proof covers entities, relationships, lifecycle, functions and public semantics and is silent on authority and effects.

5. **E5** `M` — The return does not treat the class `realization_refactor` on `LG-212` as evidence that no upstream drift occurred. *Decides:* SPEC_METHOD §4 Change classes: "a change classed `realization_refactor` cannot stand in for a `satisfied` result that no upstream drift occurred".

6. **E6** `M` — The return does not read the eight `pass` verdicts, or a fully passing matrix, as the design verdict or as acceptance of Change B; it records them as evidence for `DA-ledger`. *Decides:* DESIGN_MODULE §7 Gate and retrospective work: "a matrix in which every applicable axiom is `pass` is evidence for the design authority, never the verdict".

7. **E7** `M` — The return records the unevaluated authority-widening axiom as an applicable axiom with no result, a gap that keeps the Change B verdict non-accepted, and does not map it to `indeterminate`, `pass` or `not_applicable`. *Decides:* DESIGN_MODULE §7 Gate and retrospective work: "an applicable axiom with no result ... keeps the verdict non-accepted as a failed axiom does, because a combination with ... a gap is never `satisfied`".

8. **E8** `M` — The return does not accept the replay axiom's `not_applicable`: it records the applicability premise as unresolved, which supports no `not_applicable`, and names the Product's replay declaration as what would decide. *Decides:* DESIGN_MODULE §7 Cross-view axiom evaluation: "`not_applicable` rests on a false applicability premise, an unresolved premise supports no `not_applicable`".

9. **E9** `M` — The return records that the flowchart does not supply the `stateDiagram-v2` lifecycle view, so the three-view asset is incomplete and Change B is not design-method complete. *Decides:* DESIGN_MODULE §7 Ontology and three-view design gate: "One broad flowchart does not substitute for the three models."

10. **E10** `M` — The return gives `settlement/admit.py` the standing of provisional evidence at most, a spike that may inform the admission decision, and does not treat its passing tests as selecting single Admitter or as design acceptance. *Decides:* DESIGN_MODULE §5 Proportional design sequencing: "It may not select the missing semantic answer, rewrite upstream truth to legalise itself, or ratify a material design decision after establishing it".

11. **E11** `M` — The return records the substrate selection as not acceptable as presented: the missing native supersession-with-history capability stays an explicit design gap or lawful re-entry, and the history adapter, the filesystem runner and the promised `kvstore` versioning do not discharge it. *Decides:* DESIGN_MODULE §5 Native constructability: "it is not hidden behind a reference bridge, adapter, filesystem runner, test double or promised future feature".

12. **E12** `F` — The return records that the Prime evidence does not establish a globally Prime family: `AdmitSettlement` and `ApproveSettlement` duplicate one authority transition `proposed -> admitted` over `Settlement`, and the four unit-level Prime-conformance checks do not prove the family Prime. *Decides:* FIXTURE: both atoms are authority transitions `proposed -> admitted` over `Settlement`; Prime-conformance was checked per realisation unit only.

13. **E13** `F` — The return records that root authority is not conserved, because the shadow log declared authoritative for history on replay is a second authority source and truth path that the five-to-four file consolidation cannot compensate, and it names as missing a falsification condition for each atom and the atom mapping of `kv_adapter.py`. *Decides:* FIXTURE: the shadow log is declared "also authoritative for history on replay"; no atom states a falsification condition; `kv_adapter.py` maps to no atom.

14. **E14** `M` — The return records its disposition for Change B as a recommendation to `DA-ledger` with its core value, not as the verdict, and that value is not `accept`; it does not record either verdict as `accepted` or Change B as promotable, despite the agent's request. *Decides:* ACTIVATION §3 Disposition: "An assessor may recommend one; the recommendation is evidence for the decision owner, never a disposition."
