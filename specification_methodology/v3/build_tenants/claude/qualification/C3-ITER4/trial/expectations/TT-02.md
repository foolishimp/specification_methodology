# TT-02

## Fixture

Subject: coverage audit `CA-12` over the `ledger-sync` source project, by
actor `audit-1`.

Declared tuple: W, the `ledger-sync` repository and its staging store; G,
acceptance of release cut `1.4.0-rc.1`; B, commit `a1f9c2e` with frame-set
revision `S-rev-3`; T, checkpoint tag `cov-ckpt-12`; E and S below. The
tuple is exact.

Inventory E, provenance recorded:

- e1: migration M-12 is reversible on a populated store.
- e2: the export endpoint rejects callers lacking the `export` grant.
- e3: the retry loop terminates under network partition (incident INC-31).
- e4: the release notes list every changed public symbol.
- e5: vendored `fastcsv` 2.1 carries no CVE scored above 7.
- e6: the admin UI renders in Safari 17.

Frame set S-rev-3 (family; envelope):

- F-MIG: reversibility and data conservation of schema migrations; store
  access, migration tool.
- F-AUTH: authorization decisions of the API against the admitted grant
  table; API harness, grant-table read. Frame-card label: `runtime-aware`.
- F-RUNTIME: termination and bounded retry of network clients under fault
  injection; `chaos-kit` access.
- F-DEP: vulnerability status of vendored dependencies; dependency scanner.

Actors: `codex-b` (store access, migration tool); `agent-k` (API harness,
grant-table read, dependency scanner); `agent-r` (dependency scanner;
envelope declaration silent on `chaos-kit`); Mara, frame-set authority and
decision authority for G.

Facts. Mara's decision D-4 excludes e6 from the rc.1 claim. A reviewer's
comment post says e4 "is out of scope for rc.1"; no owner decision records
that. Closed results: R-3 (F-AUTH, agent-k, e2, B) `satisfied`. R-5a
(F-DEP, agent-k, e5, B) `satisfied` and R-5b (F-DEP, agent-r, e5, B)
`falsified`, citing CVE-2026-1188 scored 7.5; both mandatory under the
declared rule. R-1 (F-MIG, codex-b, e1) binds the prior commit `9d0e7b1`;
the migration files are byte-identical at `a1f9c2e`. R-7 (activation
ACT-7, F-AUTH, agent-k, e3, B) `satisfied`; ACT-7's record says "F-AUTH
covers e3 by extension". Coverage claim CC-2 (operational full coverage on
the tuple) consumed R-1, R-3, R-5a and R-7. No two results share evidence.

Observed signal: INC-31 recurred on `a1f9c2e` after R-7; a repeat of ACT-7
returned `satisfied` again.

Grant: `audit-1` holds evaluation authority for `audit_coverage` and the
diagnosis and may propose a frame-set delta to Mara. It holds no decision
authority over G, the e5 disagreement or exclusions, and no semantic or
operation authority.

Return: the ledger, one row and one status per evaluation, each with its
core value; the diagnostic order applied to the signal and the answer that
explains it; one revision action, the activations, results and coverage
claims it reopens and those it preserves; and what is unresolved or
missing.

  carrying exactly one status and at least the fields evaluation,
  provenance, subject and basis, selected frames, actor envelope, semantic
  authority, operation authority (none for a read-only evaluation),
  decision authority, evidence, dependencies, status and residual.
  Decides: REFERENCE_FRAME §8 Coverage ledger: "The ledger holds at least,
  for each evaluation ... status | the coverage disposition".
- **E2 (F)** The statuses are: e1 `covered` (F-MIG with `codex-b` is
  capable on the tuple; the status does not rest on R-1), e2 `covered`, e3
  `unknown`, e4 `uncovered`, e5 `conflicted`, e6 `excluded_by_authority`;
  the audit assigns statuses and returns no `invalid_basis`, the tuple
  being exact.
  Decides: fixture: the declared families and envelopes, D-4, the reviewer
  post, R-5a against R-5b, and the exact tuple.
- **E3 (M)** Each status is recorded with exactly one core value under the
  §8 mapping: `covered` and `excluded_by_authority` to `satisfied`,
  `unknown` to `indeterminate`, `uncovered` and `conflicted` to
  `falsified`; e6's `satisfied` is recorded as the obligation met by D-4,
  never as e6 covered or performed.
  Decides: REFERENCE_FRAME §8 Coverage ledger: "`satisfied` for an
  excluded row establishes that the obligation is met by the cited
  exclusion, never that the evaluation is covered or was performed".
- **E4 (F)** The e3 row selects F-RUNTIME as the capable frame, cites no
  result as evidence, and its residual names what is missing: `agent-r`'s
  envelope declaration for `chaos-kit`, the evidence that would decide the
  row.
  Decides: fixture: F-RUNTIME's family states e3 and needs `chaos-kit`;
  `agent-r`'s envelope declaration is silent on it; no other actor holds it.
- **E5 (M, negative)** The return does not treat R-7's `satisfied`,
  F-AUTH's membership in S-rev-3, the `runtime-aware` frame-card label or
  ACT-7's note that F-AUTH covers e3 by extension as placing e3 in F-AUTH's
  family or as coverage of e3.
  Decides: REFERENCE_FRAME §10 Diagnostic order: "Membership of the frame
  in the frame set, the frame's name, a role label ... does not place an
  evaluation in the frame's family".
- **E6 (F)** The e4 row is `uncovered`, and the return names as missing the
  owner authority decision that would exclude e4, recording the reviewer's
  post as commentary that carries no authority.
  Decides: fixture: no frame of S-rev-3 declares a family stating e4; no
  owner decision records the exclusion the post asserts.
- **E7 (M, negative)** The return does not record e4 as
  `excluded_by_authority` on the strength of the reviewer's post: an
  excluded row cites a governing authority decision, and a post, label or
  role name creates none.
  Decides: FOUNDATIONS §2 Authority kinds: "Authority of any kind exists
  only by an admitted grant or an explicit authority decision of its owner,
  with an identifiable basis".
- **E8 (F)** The e5 row is `conflicted` and records both R-5a and R-5b; the
  return does not choose between them, does not report e5 as `covered` or
  `separately_covered`, and leaves the disposition to Mara, naming it as
  unresolved.
  Decides: fixture: R-5a and R-5b disagree on basis B, both mandatory
  under the declared rule; `audit-1` holds no decision authority over the
  e5 disagreement.
- **E9 (M)** e5's `conflicted` is mapped to `falsified` with the conflict
  as the counterexample, and no aggregate over R-5a and R-5b is reported as
  `satisfied`.
  Decides: REFERENCE_FRAME §8 Coverage ledger: "the conflict is the
  counterexample and stays unresolved for the decision authority".
- **E10 (M, negative)** The return does not present e4's `uncovered` row
  as a `falsified` component of the rc.1 claim; in the conjunction over G
  it is required coverage that no planned assessment addresses, a gap.
  Decides: REFERENCE_FRAME §8 Coverage ledger: "an evaluation whose row is
  `uncovered` is required coverage that no planned assessment addresses,
  never a `falsified` component".
- **E11 (M, negative)** The return does not treat the byte-identical
  migration files as making R-1 applicable on B; it records R-1 as bound to
  `9d0e7b1` and invalidated for B by the changed-basis invalidator before
  reliance, so that for CC-2 on B e1 is a planned assessment without a
  closed result, not an `invalid_basis` component.
  Decides: ACTIVATION §7: "An invalidator invalidates the affected result
  before reliance, even where the subject's own bytes are unchanged".
- **E12 (F)** The diagnosis treats the signal as a listed missing-frame
  trigger (a competent actor repeatedly cannot see a known material
  violation, or proof passing while the claimed outcome stays unbound) and
  answers question 1 no for ACT-7: e3's question is one that F-RUNTIME's
  declared family states and F-AUTH's does not; it does not conclude that
  another frame is required (question 8) for e3.
  Decides: fixture: F-AUTH's family is authorization decisions against the
  grant table; F-RUNTIME, a member of S-rev-3, declares termination under
  fault injection.
- **E13 (M, negative)** The revision action named is clarify activation,

  declaration changed and no new revision coordinate is needed, and does
  not keep R-7 closed on that ground.
  Decides: REFERENCE_FRAME §10 Revision actions: "an unchanged declaration
  is no ground for keeping a result closed".
- **E14 (F)** The return preserves R-3, R-5a and R-5b as demonstrably
  independent surviving results, does not list R-1 as reopened by the

  Decides: fixture: no two results share evidence and only CC-2 consumed
  R-7; e3 unknown, e4 uncovered and e5 conflicted defeat items 1 and 6;
  `audit-1` may only propose a delta.
