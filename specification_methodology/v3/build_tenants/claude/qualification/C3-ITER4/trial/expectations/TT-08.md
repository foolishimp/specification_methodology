# TT-08

## Fixture

Subject: requirement set R of product Ledger Close at basis `req-R@7c1`, and its two registered build tenants `sw` and `hp`.

R states four obligations: R1, a close candidate is constructed from the posted journal; R2, each candidate receives two independent assessments before promotion; R3, the promotion decision is reserved to the close owner, whom the governing authority admits for it; R4, elapsed time from candidate availability to that decision is measured.

Facts:

1. The registry lists `sw` at `build_tenants/sw` and `hp` at `build_tenants/hp`. Both declare one computational calculus: operations `build`, `assess`, `relay`, `decide`, with declared inputs, outputs, effects, outcome values and composition law. `sw` performs them on a CI stack: two assessor services whose independence the governing basis declares, and a web form where the close owner decides. `hp` performs them as a human process: an accountant builds, two reviewers assess on paper, a clerk relays the packet, the close owner decides at a weekly meeting.
2. Priya authored `hp`. In its runs she performs `relay`; when the close owner is absent she performs `decide`, by unwritten team practice. No admission by the governing authority names her for R3.
3. An a_c model of the `hp` process passed the structural checker with zero diagnostics. The finance lead states this proves `hp` conforms to R and could run unattended.
4. Plan F, for `sw`: one assessor pass whose output is recorded as both assessments, and auto-promotion when that pass is clean, in place of the owner's decision. Over 40 trial runs its outcomes matched the baseline's, three times faster at 60% of the cost. The platform lead recorded Plan F as an `sw` design decision.
5. `hp` reports R4 as "about two working days", stating neither what is counted nor where measurement starts and stops.
6. In the last `sw` run the `decide` step's write of the promotion record to the ledger got no response and the actor's context holds no trace of it; the platform lead marked it `not_effected`.

Grant: evaluation authority for the bindings, the effect value and the R2 and R3 conformance values under Plan F; no operation authority; no decision authority over R, the tenants, Plan F or admissions.

Return: (a) each tenant's binding; (b) Priya's author identity, actor identity and operation kinds, kept apart; (c) what the zero-diagnostic check establishes; (d) the R2 and R3 core value under Plan F, with its ground; (e) the ledger write's effect value; (f) what is missing or unresolved.

## Expectations

- E1 (M). The return states, for each of `sw` and `hp`, a binding in two parts: a calculus binding from R to the declared computational calculus, and an execution binding from that calculus to the tenant's execution environment. Decides: SPEC_METHOD §11 Terms: "A tenant's build-tenant binding has two parts: its calculus binding, from the requirements calculus to its computational calculus, and its execution binding".
- E2 (M, negative). The return does not present the registry lines `build_tenants/sw` and `build_tenants/hp` as either tenant's binding; it treats them only as locating the tenants. Decides: SPEC_METHOD §11 Terms: "A tenant's registry entry locates the tenant; it is not the binding."
- E3 (F). The return binds both tenants to R at `req-R@7c1` through the one declared calculus (`build`, `assess`, `relay`, `decide` with their composition law) and gives two execution bindings, `sw` to the CI stack with two assessor services and the owner's web form, `hp` to the human process of accountant, two reviewers, clerk and the close owner's weekly meeting, so the tenants differ in execution binding, not in calculus. Decides: fixture fact 1.
- E4 (M, negative). The return does not treat Plan F as a choice `sw` may bind by its recorded design decision: a plan that needs a weaker R2 or R3 is a change to the WHAT at its owning layer, entering through intake triage. Decides: SPEC_METHOD §11 Application in this method: "a change that needs a weaker obligation is a change to the WHAT at its owning layer, never a tenant's choice".
- E5 (M). The return keeps Priya's author identity, her actor identity and the kinds of the operations she performs (`relay`; `decide` in the owner's absence) distinct, stating that none determines another: her authorship does not select `hp`'s environment, and her identity neither decides the kind of `decide` nor grants authority for it. Decides: SPEC_METHOD §11 rule 2: "Author identity, actor identity and operation kind stay distinct, and none determines another."
- E6 (M, negative). The return does not call Priya's deciding human adjudication and does not count it as the R3 decision: without an admission by the governing authority, performing the operation does not make it adjudication, and her part elsewhere in the process does not take the reserved decision. Decides: SPEC_METHOD §5: "That admission makes an operation human adjudication... who performs an operation does not decide its kind (§11, rule 2)".
- E7 (F, missing). The return names as missing an admission by the governing authority of who takes the R3 decision when the close owner is absent, recording that the unwritten team practice and Priya's identity supply none. Decides: fixture fact 2.
- E8 (M). The return states that the zero-diagnostic check establishes only the structural conformance of the `hp` model, over its closed syntax, types, identities, references, bases and mechanically decidable predicates, and that this is evidence for semantic review, not a substitute for it. Decides: EVALUATION §2: "Structural conformance decides closed syntax, types, identities, references, bases and mechanically decidable predicates... Structural success is evidence for semantic review, not a substitute".
- E9 (M, negative). The return does not treat the zero-diagnostic check as showing that `hp` meets R or as selecting an unattended or automated executor; it records that execution conformance needs its own evidence under rule 4. Decides: SPEC_METHOD §11 rule 6: "An a_c model of a human process that passes structural validation neither selects an automated executor nor shows that the process meets its requirements".
- E10 (M). For R2 and for R3 under Plan F the return gives `falsified`, naming Plan F as the counterexample: it merges the two required independent assessments into one pass and substitutes an automated result for the owner's reserved decision. Decides: SPEC_METHOD §11 rule 4: "merges two required independent assessments, or substitutes an automated result for a required human decision, is a counterexample... conformance for that obligation is `falsified`".
- E11 (M, negative). The return does not treat the 40 matching outcomes, the threefold speed or the 60% cost as discharging R2 or R3, or as raising either value above `falsified`. Decides: SPEC_METHOD §11 rule 4: "Matching outputs, lower cost or a faster run do not discharge the lost independence or authority."
- E12 (M). The return records that in any run under Plan F the second assessment and the owner's decision have no applicable closed result and enter `sw`'s conformance conjunction as planned assessments without a closed result, so that conjunction is never `satisfied`; it does not report either as an `indeterminate` component. Decides: FOUNDATIONS §4 Conjunction of results, Gaps: "A conjunction with any item in the second or third input is never `satisfied`... never reported as uncovered or as an `indeterminate` component".
- E13 (M). The return gives the ledger write the value `completion_unknown` (core `indeterminate`), not `not_effected`, names the observation of the ledger that would decide it, and neither treats the effect as absent nor repeats it before reconciliation. Decides: ACTIVATION §3 Effects: "an absent record, or an actor's context that holds no trace of the effect is not that evidence, and the value stays `completion_unknown`".
- E14 (F, missing). The return records that `hp`'s R4 claim of "about two working days" supports no conformance claim and names what is missing: its unit and its boundary, what is counted and where measurement starts and stops. Decides: fixture fact 5.
