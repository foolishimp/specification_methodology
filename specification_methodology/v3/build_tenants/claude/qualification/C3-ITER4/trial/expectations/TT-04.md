# TT-04

## Fixture

Subject: occurrence model `M_7` on profile basis `b`, its frontier `F_7`, and projection `P_7` over `F_7` with precedence law `p`: coordinate admission ordinal, comparison law "greater ordinal selects", basis `b`, admitting authority `A_proj`. Occurrences `o_1`..`o_4` act on ledger `L` under subject binding `b_W`.

Facts:

- `F_7.value.member_event_refs` lists `e1`..`e7` once each. `e4` is `o_2`'s `effect-disposition` event; `e6` is `o_3`'s `occurrence-admission` event; `e5` and `e7` are admitted `subject_scoped` `external-fact-admission` events with ordinals 5 and 7. `M_7` holds `corrects_event(e7, e5)` and `supports_event(e5, e7)` and no `causally_precedes_event` edge between them. `G_id`, `G_occ_cause` and `G_evt_cause` as declared are acyclic. A dashboard's latest-only summary lists `e7` alone, marks `e5` "retired, dropped from `F_7`" and flags "event-causation cycle e5 -> e7 -> e5".
- `c_A` claims `causally_precedes_event(e4, e6)`: qualifiers complete and resolved; claimant `A_cause` holds the owner-issued grant for causal claims over `b_W`; evidence refs resolve `e4` and `e6`'s payload, which names `e4`'s disposition as its input, and the owner's causal-claim law admits this as evidence of material cause; `source_frontier_ref = F_7`.
- `c_B` claims `causally_precedes_event(e5, e7)`: qualifiers complete and resolved; claimant `A_cause`; evidence refs resolve only the two ordinals, their timestamps and the actor `X` who executed both; `source_frontier_ref = F_7`. The claimant calls the cause self-evident from `p`, the correction edge and the shared actor.
- `o_3`: `invokes_effect` `inv_3` under exact grant `g_3`; intended outcome `io_3`: rows `r1`..`r5` of `L` updated within dispatch window `w_3`. The executor's response was lost. Bounded post-observation `q_3'`, taken after `w_3` closed, shows `r1` and `r2` updated and `r3`..`r5` unchanged. Nothing establishes that the executor has stopped acting on `L`. `transition_evaluation` `j_t3` decided `partial`. The executor proposes re-dispatching for `r3`..`r5` now.
- `o_4`: `EffectSubject` `s_4` with pre-observation `q_4` taken before `e7` was admitted; the application's invalidation law invalidates a pre-observation older than an admitted external fact. `effect_readiness` `j_r4` decided `stale`. Nothing was dispatched; no disposition event for `o_4` exists.

Grant: the `claim_admission` judgment over `c_A` and `c_B`, and `model_validation` over `M_7` for the three acyclicity constraints. No event-admission, cut-admission, projection, effect or correction grant; the actor may mint none.

Return: for each claim, the decision, stop or residual, core value and re-entry; the three acyclicity constraint results; what `corrects_event(e7, e5)` and `p` decide and do not decide; for `o_3` and `o_4`, the stop kind, refined by cause where the method refines it, its core value and what survives beside it; and everything unresolved or missing.

- **E1 (F)** The return records `j_c(c_A)` with decision `admitted`, core value satisfied for the claim-admissibility predicate, on source frontier `F_7`. Decides: FIXTURE: `A_cause` holds the owner-issued causal-claim grant; the owner's causal-claim law admits the evidence; qualifiers resolved; `F_7` is current.
- **E2 (M)** The return does not treat `c_A`'s admitted claim judgment as a materialized `causally_precedes_event(e4, e6)` relation, an admitted event or an admitted semantic cut; it records `j_e` and `j_a`, under their own authority, as judgments not yet performed with no closed result. Decides: `TRAVERSAL_OCCURRENCE.md#candidate-judgment-event-and-relation-separation`: "Only `j_c.decision = admitted`, `j_e.decision = admitted` and `j_a.decision = admitted` together admit the complete semantic cut."
- **E3 (M)** The return does not admit `c_B` and does not treat the admission ordinals, the timestamps, the shared actor `X`, `corrects_event(e7, e5)` or `supports_event(e5, e7)` as establishing that `e5` caused `e7`. Decides: `TRAVERSAL_OCCURRENCE.md#causality-and-typed-lineage`: "Timestamp, ordinal, adjacency, containment, a common subject, a shared actor, correlation and arrival order do not create cause."
- **E4 (M)** The return gives `c_B` decision `hold`, not `refused`, with residual `unresolved_cause` and core value indeterminate; it names what is missing, admissible evidence of material cause from `e5` to `e7` under competent authority, and the re-entry as a new claim over the current frontier. Decides: `TRAVERSAL_OCCURRENCE.md#closed-constraint-judgment-residual-and-stop-kinds`: "unresolved cause or stale frontier retains `hold`; re-enter with a new claim over the current frontier ... acquire authority and evidence or retain unresolved".
- **E5 (M)** The return records `identity_dependency_acyclic`, `occurrence_causation_acyclic` and `event_causation_acyclic` as satisfied and does not refuse `M_7` or falsify `event_causation_acyclic` for the `e5 -> e7 -> e5` cycle, which lies in the union of `supports_event` and `corrects_event` and in no declared causal subgraph. - **E6 (M)** The return keeps `e5` and `e7` as two immutable members of `F_7` and records that `corrects_event(e7, e5)` changes later projection only through `P_7`'s explicit selection law; it deletes, rewrites or re-identifies nothing. Decides: `TRAVERSAL_OCCURRENCE.md#candidate-judgment-event-and-relation-separation`, OP-003: "`corrects_event(new, existing)` keeps both immutable events ... it does not delete, rewrite or retrospectively re-identify the corrected fact."
- **E7 (M)** The return does not take the dashboard's latest-only summary ('e5 retired, dropped from F_7') as evidence of `F_7`'s membership or completeness; `F_7`'s members are `e1`..`e7`, each once, as its member set lists them. Decides: `TRAVERSAL_OCCURRENCE.md#event-frontier-and-projection`: "Latest-only summaries do not prove frontier completeness."
- **E8 (F)** The return records that `p` orders `e5` before `e7` (ordinals 5 and 7, greater selects), so `P_7` selects `e7` over `e5` as current truth, and names `p`'s coordinate, comparison law, basis `b` and admitting authority `A_proj` as explicit. Decides: FIXTURE: `p` is declared with coordinate, comparison law, basis and admitting authority; `e5` and `e7` carry ordinals 5 and 7.
- **E9 (M)** The return gives `p` no semantic, admission, causal or decision authority: `p`'s order neither admits `e7`, nor makes `e5` the cause of `e7`, nor decides `c_B`, nor stands in for any judgment. Decides: `TRAVERSAL_OCCURRENCE.md#causality-and-typed-lineage`, OP-004: "The coordinate orders eligible facts; it gains no semantic, admission, causal or decision authority."
- **E10 (F)** The return gives `o_3` the stop kind `partial_effect(not_attained)` with core value falsified, names `r3`..`r5` unreached after `w_3` closed as the counterexample, names `r1` and `r2` as the part that did occur beside the value, and reads `j_t3`'s decision as `partial(not_attained)`, falsified for its own predicate. Decides: FIXTURE: `io_3` bounds the outcome to window `w_3`; `q_3'` after `w_3` closed shows `r1`, `r2` updated and `r3`..`r5` unchanged.
- **E11 (M)** The return does not give `o_3` `partial_effect(undecided)`, `completion_unknown` or indeterminate on account of the lost executor response: `q_3'` is evidence about the subject that establishes non-attainment, and a lost response is no evidence about the subject. Decides: `ACTIVATION.md#effects`: "when an interval that the declared outcome bounds has closed with part not done, the value is `not_effected` while the effect may still be acting".
- **E12 (M)** The return records beside `o_3`'s value, as the `partial_effect` residual, that the end of the effect is not established, naming the observation that would establish it and the re-entry (post-observation and explicit disposition) before any further effect; it does not treat the effect as ended or absent and does not license the proposed re-dispatch. - **E13 (M)** The return gives `o_4` the stop kind `hold` with core value indeterminate, the residual `stale_currentness`, and the re-entry re-observe and re-judge before effect. Decides: `TRAVERSAL_OCCURRENCE.md#closed-constraint-judgment-residual-and-stop-kinds`: "`pre_effect_current` ... `stale` plus `hold` and `stale_currentness`; stop `hold` | indeterminate; `stale_currentness` ... re-observe and re-judge before effect".
