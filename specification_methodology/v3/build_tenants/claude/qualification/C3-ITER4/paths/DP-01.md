## Conventions applied

- A section runs from its heading to the next heading of equal or higher
  level. Hops are counted at the level the route names: an H2 named by a
  route is acquired whole, with its H3 children; an H3 named by a route or
  by its own heading is acquired alone. Bytes are `wc -c` of that span.
- A file-level link lands in the file; the file's own headings are then its
  named sections. The README's Documents row for the file names what it
  owns and selected the heading.
- S0's README names member files in backticks with no link and no section.
  Under the strict reading of "a link, a named section, or a frame's
  `Source:` line" nothing on S0 is reachable by route (see Findings, F1).
  The S0 figures below use the relaxed reading: a named file is a route and
  its headings are named sections. The verdict is the same under both.
- M2 counts a section met on the path that decides a question an M1 locus
  already decides, agreeing or not. M3 counts a pair whose determinations
  differ, or a homonym, that had to be reconciled to decide.
