# Evidence Method

Standing: Codex first-thread implementation under the selected C2 contract.
Installed STDO 2.5.1 RC2 governs construction. This document owns evaluation and
truthful conjunction; it imports [identity and meaning](FOUNDATIONS.md) and
returns action selection to [Work](WORK_METHOD.md#w-05-select-the-next-authorized-action).

## E-01 Bind A Claim And Its Support

A judgment concerns an exact claim on a selected subject, state/basis and
interpretation. Recover the source obligations, predicate, admissible evidence,
evaluator responsibility, scope and applicable independence. Define what would
defeat or leave the claim undecided before using the candidate to answer it.

Keep authored output, observation, assessment, accepted claim and authority
separate. A witness supports only its bound predicate. A valid parser, matching
field shape, historical green test or shorter prompt cannot supply unobserved
semantic fidelity, current practical adequacy or owner acceptance.

Preserve original producer and citations with sufficient authentication under
the selected profile. A declaration of authorship is not automatically an
authenticated judgment. The supported acquisition/admission relation must bind
the actual producer, material and claim. Retain exact sources or sufficient
references under F-04 rather than copying whole bodies into every handoff.

## E-02 One Judgment Family With Preserved Causes

An evaluation returns one declared result over its exact claim:

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| satisfied | Sufficient admissible evidence supports the declared predicate on the exact basis. |
| falsified | An admissible counterexample defeats that predicate. |
| indeterminate | Available admissible evidence cannot decide it. |
| out_of_frame | A material relation or capability required for the evaluation is outside the declared frame. |
| invalid_basis | The subject or basis cannot support that evaluation. |

This recovers RF-012. AC-007's constraint account also preserves failed, missing,
indeterminate, out-of-scope and invalid-basis pressure. A compatible projection
retains original cause and claim; missing evidence cannot become an established
premise. This family is not a proof that every evaluation domain is identical.

Known failure, uncovered required coverage, invalid support and incompatible
basis remain distinguishable facts even when a summary has one verdict. A next
action is not a sixth judgment; it is a separately authorized disposition.

## E-03 Assess The Exact Material

Acquire governing source and independently specified expectations before opening
the frozen candidate. Inspect actual supplied material, relevant state and
producer-to-consumer path at the claim's required altitude. Expose inaccessible
or insufficient material rather than pretending a sufficient assessment occurred.

An author can self-check; applicable required independent judgment remains with
an author-independent assessor. Selected actor/family independence and exposure
limits are declared for the claim. Different labels or multiple activations of
the same authored account do not create additional independence.

Return the exact subject/basis, judgment, source-linked findings, applicable
coverage and evidence limits. A genuine defect-detection claim requires a real
defect against an adequate baseline. Supplied negative judgments can test wiring,
but they cannot demonstrate independent detection of actual material.

A repair creates a successor subject. Preserve the original frozen output and
judgment. Re-assess affected claims against unchanged expectations; a repaired
successor cannot retroactively pass the original attempt or relabel its proof.

## E-04 Conjoin Without Losing Findings

Authenticate every planned assessment slot under its owning contract before
using the set as complete. Missing required slot authentication is a failed
admission relation, distinct from uncovered required Product obligations.

For compatible applicable judgments, retain original producers, failures and
citations. Let R be required obligations and C be applicable supported coverage.
Uncovered coverage is R minus C; known failures are conserved independently.
Shared machinery may implement this relation, but aggregation does not own the
individual assessor's semantics or broaden its judgment.

| Applicable facts | Composite standing |
|---|---|
| An authenticated failure, whether or not required coverage is complete | Non-green; retain the failure, its citations and any uncovered coverage. |
| No known failure, but uncovered required obligations or undecidable support | Incomplete/indeterminate; identify the unresolved obligations. |
| Incompatible subjects/bases or invalid support | Withhold the unsupported conjunction; retain each judgment at its actual historical/current scope. |
| Complete sufficient applicable support and no known failure | Supported only for the declared composite claim and interval. |

In the discriminator A-fails/B-uncovered, every planned judgment is authenticated,
A contains an actual negative assessment and B is an uncovered required
obligation. Both facts survive. Omitting a planned judgment instead exercises
the earlier admission guard and cannot stand in for this discriminator.

Changing the candidate or a material premise can reopen current applicability.
It does not erase the original historical finding, nor make every historical
failure applicable forever. Fresh reads must preserve the subject-specific facts.

## E-05 Evidence Does Not Grant Authority

Assessment constrains the bound claim. Only its owning authority accepts an
outcome or selects additional effects inside its scope. A review GO, actor
capability, sufficient context or mechanically valid map cannot select a new
operation, release, canonical tenant or adoption.

Apply an unchanged owner ruling with its original scope and consequences;
preserve it once rather than re-minting it at every consumer. A material changed
question returns to its actual owner. Completed-instance evidence remains
preservation evidence unless new growth is separately authorized.
