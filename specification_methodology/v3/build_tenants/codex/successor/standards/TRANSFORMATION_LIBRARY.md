# Transformation Library

An instance is bound by purpose, exact subject and state, interpretation,
governing basis, actor capability, inputs, required constraints, permitted
effects, output predicate, failures and consumer. This is a logical contract,
not a mandatory record schema. A sufficient existing carrier can express it.

## Bind

Recover the current purpose, subject identity, governing specification, role,
authority, state and required relations. Conflicting, missing or stale bindings
are explicit results. A name, path, cached result or available tool is no grant.

## Acquire

Resolve enough authentic material for the selected operation or evaluation.
Declare completeness, freshness, coupled invariants and interpretation. Resolve
references when their bodies are needed. Unavailable evidence, insufficient
context or capability returns pressure for refinement, support, re-entry or a
truthful stop. Acquisition cannot silently drop a governing obligation.

## Transform

Construct output or perform effects within the bound contract. Preserve the
original producer, material inputs and observed effects. Return candidate work,
refusal, incomplete work or a re-entry request. Observe uncertain external effects
before repeating them. The worker's internal search is its HOW; it cannot widen
the outer outcome or effect grant.

## Evaluate

Judge a declared predicate on an exact subject using sufficient admissible
evidence. Return satisfied, falsified, indeterminate, out_of_frame or
invalid_basis with original citations and residual coverage. Where required,
the assessor is independent of authorship. A judgment supplies no repair,
acceptance or next-action authority.

## Conjoin

Combine only compatible, authenticated results on a declared common outcome.
Retain each applicable known failure, its producer and citations. Retain required
uncovered coverage separately. Authenticate every planned assessment slot; an
omitted planned judgment is an admission defect, while an authenticated set can
still leave a different required responsibility uncovered. Satisfaction requires
every applicable material predicate and seam to be supported. An incompatible
basis or unknown applicability prevents a wider satisfied claim.

## Dispose

The decision owner consumes exact closed work and evaluation results with the
current mandate. It selects accept, local_repair, re_enter or reject for the
declared cone. It may select a next already-authorized action, yield or terminate
truthfully. It cannot change upstream meaning or take a reserved owner decision.
Accept binds an exact checkpoint and interval; it does not imply release.

## Composition law

For T: X -> Y and S: Y -> Z, S after T is admitted only when carried meaning,
identity, basis, lifecycle, effects and authority match or have a lawful explicit
translation. Composition preserves the outer predicate and material invariant
set. A shared helper may implement several instances; differing semantic owners
remain parameters or distinct contracts. Independent judgment cannot be
compressed into author self-assessment.

An identity transformation preserves the selected subject and interpretation;
associative regrouping is valid only when it preserves observable effects,
failure, material context and decision boundaries. This states a bounded
composition obligation, not a universal category theorem for arbitrary software.

## Recovery law

After interruption, bind the same work identity to its observed current state.
Reassess only materially affected premises and retain applicable work. Reconstruct
needed authority and observations from their declared owners, not private memory.
Uncertainty about effects, freshness or evidence remains explicit. Churn is an
unchanged failed relation attempted again without a supported change in its cause.

## Cost and contraction

Measure costs appropriate to the claim: acquired and transferred bytes, required
attention, I/O, CPU, addressable memory, elapsed critical-path time, aggregate
work and displaced human assurance. A reference reduces storage only when its
resolution preserves adequacy. A smaller document or faster local preparation
does not prove whole-path improvement. Contract duplicated meaning and authority
only while preserving required distinctions and independent evaluations.
