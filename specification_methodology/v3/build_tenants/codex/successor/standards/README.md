# Standards Library

This directory contains the deciding methods, foundational contracts and
non-deciding views of the STDO Specification Methodology. The complete cut also includes its declared
projections, schemas, templates, frames and interaction assets. A selectable
release has one complete immutable constitutional member set with exact cut
identity, manifest and membership. The source-line Product Definition identity
and each immutable Product/release identity retain their distinct source-owned
relations. Subordinate executable and
interaction tooling retain separately declared responsibilities.

## Foundations and shared library

[Foundations](FOUNDATIONS.md) states finite capacity, interpretation, purpose,
identity, typed composition, total handling and separate judgment/authority.
It owns the [single result algebra](FOUNDATIONS.md#result-algebra),
[result conjunction](FOUNDATIONS.md#result-conjunction),
[workspace-derived context](FOUNDATIONS.md#workspace-and-context) and
[configuration contract](FOUNDATIONS.md#configuration).
[Transformation Library](TRANSFORMATION_LIBRARY.md) owns shared
[contract application](TRANSFORMATION_LIBRARY.md#contract-application),
[constraint surfaces](TRANSFORMATION_LIBRARY.md#constraint-surface),
[faithful projection](TRANSFORMATION_LIBRARY.md#faithful-projection) and
[composition](TRANSFORMATION_LIBRARY.md#composition-law). Bind, Acquire,
Transform, Evaluate, Conjoin and Dispose organise these responsibilities. Domain
instances bind their predicates, policies and grants; they do not copy a rival
generic law or acquire a new runtime API.
Responsibility views project selected material clauses:
[work/recovery](frames/WORK_RECOVERY.md),
[evaluation/conjunction](frames/EVALUATION_CONJUNCTION.md),
[constitutional derivation](frames/CONSTITUTIONAL_DERIVATION.md),
[domain binding](frames/DOMAIN_BINDING.md), and
[release/adoption](frames/RELEASE_ADOPTION.md). Each has an explicit scope;
no view is sufficient for every task.

For intake triage, lawful re-entry, work-carrier selection or execution-contract
admission, acquire the [triage/carrier frame](frames/TRIAGE_CARRIER.md).
For typed construction handoffs and transformation admission, acquire the
[constitutional-derivation frame](frames/CONSTITUTIONAL_DERIVATION.md), which
projects the library specialization, its Design Method consumer and the
SPEC_METHOD enclosing-admission owner. These selections follow the task's
responsibility. Each view names its owner sections and exact member digests. Material
coupled responsibilities outside the selection require source re-entry under
the existing sufficiency law; a prepared frame grants no work or admission.

Each member's declared applicability and scope remain at that owner; this table
does not reclassify a constitutional method as a guide or profile. The calculus
and optional Traversal profile retain their own signatures; the Baseline
retains its adoption boundary. Reference Frame Method supplies generic frame
law; particular actor configurations remain scoped profiles. The Glossary
owns resolution of its locators, while each located method owns its meaning.
Foundations and the library
declare the premise layer and shared conditional contracts under the Methodology
Product authority. Particular methods apply those shared decisions and retain their additional
policies and grants.

A derived profile retains its owning determinations under the
[Baseline authority boundary](STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#status-and-authority-boundary). The calculus is distinct from
interpreted a_c subject models and separately owned mechanical encodings.
Traversal adoption is optional and binds an exact signature and basis.

## Distribution and entry

[Compressions](authority_compressions/README.md) are digest-bound, non-deciding
read models. Each raw member retains its source-owned semantic standing; the detailed
deciding owners remain authoritative. A sufficient
current projection conveys their duties without acquiring authority, and stale
or insufficient views require acquisition of their exact owners. [Templates](templates/README.md)
provide starter realizations: a single per-Product Definition, project specification
templates, a project frame basis and agent bootstrap templates. Normative
interoperability schemas under `schemas/` define the layout-neutral Product
Definition Overlay and deterministic installed-release manifest. Plugin interaction assets route work;
they mint no authority or workflow engine.

The toolchain installs a complete immutable cut in a shared versioned store and
resolves logical release URIs without machine-local paths in Product Definitions.
A local copy is operative only when explicitly bound and verified; it is not
the default. An exact cut, Product Definition basis URI, manifest digest
and member inventory establish consumer basis. A mutable source head or moving
selector cannot replace it. Distribution artifact presence conveys no release
or operation authority.

Under [Frame Method sufficiency](REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-003-material-sufficiency)
and [irrelevance exclusion](REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-004-irrelevance-exclusion),
acquire the sufficient responsibility frame, exact subject and material
dependencies for one action. The entire corpus is acquired only when material
to the selected claim. The applicable owner determines how the frame's result,
required independent assessment and existing disposition/control contract are
consumed. In ODD, the next work decision remains the exact evaluate_next path;
this entry creates no alternative continuation owner. The projected frames are
declared members of the complete
constitutional member set. Their source owners, exact bytes and fidelity remain
required; a frame is not an independently selectable method version.

The [method interpretation](../interpretation/README.md) supplies the encoded
determinations, source routes and unresolved conditions for symbolic acquisition.
Its document-source inventory is distinct from the complete package inventory.
Use its declared projection and re-entry contract when an interpretation is
material to the task. The underlying owners decide meaning, while the encoded
relation and its checker supply only their declared evidence. Reading this
source package for assessment neither changes an operative Product basis nor
establishes an installed release.
