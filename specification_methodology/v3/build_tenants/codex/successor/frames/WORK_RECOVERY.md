# Bounded Work and Recovery Frame

Subject: one named current document construction task. Builder law during this
construction is installed STDO 2.5.1 RC2; this is candidate v3 method content.
The activation consumes this frame, its exact task/subject and sufficient supplied
material. Full-corpus acquisition is not required by this bounded claim.

1. Bind the selected outcome, current subject, governing requirements and accepted
   design, role, exact write territory and reserved decisions. If a material
   binding is absent or conflicted, return the gap before effects.
2. Observe current workspace progress and the premises relevant to it. Reuse
   applicable work; a changed premise reopens affected claims. Evidence of a
   previous result is not proof that its subject is still current.
3. Check sufficient context and capability. Refine or acquire authorized support
   when needed, conserving the outer contract. Otherwise return refused,
   incomplete or re_entry_requested with the unresolved obligation.
4. Construct only the granted bounded output. Record actual effects and original
   producer coordinates. For an uncertain external effect, observe/reconcile it
   or stop unresolved before a potentially duplicating retry.
5. Return candidate_ready, refused, incomplete or re_entry_requested. Include
   the exact subject, changed paths, focused checks and retained/open seams.
   Self-review does not satisfy required independent assessment.
6. An author-independent Reviewer assesses the frozen subject and sufficient
   source. Preserve known failures and their citations alongside uncovered
   requirements. Authenticate planned judgments; do not invent a missing one.
7. The Executive consumes those closed results and the current mandate. It may
   select accept/local_repair/re_enter/reject and a next already-authorized action
   within its grant. It performs no file or Git mutation under that role. A
   separately declared Writer performs exact granted effects and records the
   decision. Product, common checkpoint, merge and release decisions remain with
   their named owner.

Recovery is a new activation over observed current state. Preserve valid
authored progress; disclose affected evidence and unknown effects. No historical
green result broadens a grant. Unknown causes remain unknown. Before retrying a
failed relation, triangulate the requirement, design, producer/consumer seam,
identity/basis, effects/authority and proof; repair the actual owner or request
its re-entry. A repeated unchanged attempt is not causal repair.

Closure requires the selected outcome, applicable evidence and the named owner's
decision over an exact checkpoint. Useful incomplete progress may be retained
without declaring closure.
