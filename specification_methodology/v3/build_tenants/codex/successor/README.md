# STDO Specification Methodology v3.0.0 candidate

This is the Codex tenant's standalone successor realization. It is a candidate
for review, not an installed release. STDO 2.5.1 RC2 remains the construction
builder until an owner selects a different exact released basis.

Start at [the standards entry](standards/README.md). The method is organised by
semantic owner, not by a mandatory sequence of files or actors. Foundations and
a shared transformation library explain the principles; Spec Method applies
them to constitutional construction; optional profiles refine particular domains.

An actor receives a sufficient projected frame and exact subject. It does not
load the complete corpus merely because the corpus is the source authority.
[The work/recovery frame](standards/frames/WORK_RECOVERY.md) is the first bounded example.
Its exact-source pins and the limits of historical use evidence are recorded
in the tenant's C3 qualification.

[Design](DESIGN.md) explains ownership and contraction. [The conservation map](CONSERVATION.json)
records every predecessor claim and exact successor locus. A claimed disposition
and a resolving link are author traceability; independent semantic comparison
and applicable use evidence remain separate acceptance conditions.

[The candidate inventory](CANDIDATE.json) pins Product members separately from
review carriers. [The construction return](../qualification/C3/RETURN.md)
records exact review subjects, practical exercises, measurements and residuals.

The old tenant standards and proof receipts retain their historical subjects.
They are not retroactively evidence for this whole successor. The common Product,
checkpoint decisions and release cohort remain with their owners.
