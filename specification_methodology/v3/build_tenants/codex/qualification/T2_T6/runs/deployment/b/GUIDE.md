# Reusable guide for bounded documentary work

Bind the selected purpose, exact subject and basis, required output, responsibility, permitted effects, sufficient sources and completion or return condition before acting. An owner may select a comparison, explanation, translation or another documentary outcome. The selected subject determines the domain and units; no cloud service, rate in cents, node design or vendor is a universal premise.

For this guide's actual use, the selected job is to produce a fictional first-month comparison and useful reusable guidance. The parent grants the constructor three documentary files in its own B directory. The example remains an external business subject. It does not redefine STDO 3's general method, select a deployment or supply owner acceptance.

## Acquire what the responsibility needs

Begin at the selected method entry and recover its applicable owners and deciding sources. Keep exact source identity, revision, interpretation and permitted effects visible. A route, digest, summary or familiar field name helps locate a source but cannot supply missing meaning. If an input is unavailable, materially inconsistent or insufficient for the next action, identify the missing relation and return or acquire it within the grant.

One capable context may do the work where allowed. Decomposition, file layout, helpers and calculation strategy are choices inside the selected contract; they do not create generic stage counts, actor counts or resource defaults.

## Compose meaningfully typed transformations

Before a producer's output enters a consumer, check the material subject, basis, interpretation, input domain and preconditions together. Matching numbers or field shapes do not establish that relation.

A unit-normalization helper can be reused when its contract actually fits. For this example its contract is:

```text
Input:
  actual quote; source revision and offering; numeric value;
  AUD-cent currency unit and denominator (node-hour or GB)
Precondition:
  one AUD dollar = 100 AUD cents; same quote identity and denominator
Output:
  numeric value/100; AUD-dollar unit and unchanged denominator;
  retained source/producer coordinates and recorded unit translation
Effects:
  documentary calculation only
Unready input:
  missing/mismatched revision, offering, unit or denominator ->
  withhold dependent total; recover the deciding fact or return the limit
```

The helper may serve a cost-construction instance and an independent cost-assessment instance. Each binds its own input subject, responsibility, evidence and effects. The helper's deterministic arithmetic supplies local evidence; sharing it cannot provide author-independent assessment or prove that the total has complete required inputs.

For a different documentary task, derive the helper and its units from that task's sources. Reuse requires compatibility of the relevant contract, effect and authority relations, rather than similar code or labels.

## Preserve the useful business example

The source-v1 example fixes a 30-day interval, two continuously running nodes, 40 GB outbound traffic, interval backup quotes and a separate AUD 160 ceiling for each offering. Those particulars govern this estimate.

One lawful realization first converts the actual cent rates to typed AUD dollar rates, then calculates whole-offering totals, then formats presentation. Harbor's 6 AUD cents/node-hour becomes 0.06 AUD dollars/node-hour. For 1,440 node-hours its compute charge is AUD 86.40; adding AUD 18 backup and AUD 1.60 outbound gives AUD 106.00. Cove totals AUD 148.00. Each meets this estimate's separate ceiling. Tax, discounts, currency exchange and other charges are excluded by the fixture.

The accompanying [comparison brief](BRIEF.md) preserves these calculations, source coordinates and assumptions for the owner. A line-item realization or another supported HOW may satisfy the same business WHAT. A worker does not select Harbor as a precondition for a method outcome; the example compares both offerings and keeps the owner's choice reserved.

## Keep observation, judgment and action distinct

The constructor may observe its output and check its arithmetic, coverage and effects. Required independent assessment belongs to an author-independent assessor inspecting the actual frozen subject against the relevant sources and separately acquired expectations.

Carry a review judgment forward only with its original producer, exact predicate, frozen subject, basis, scope and limitations. A changed subject or material premise requires affected applicability to be resolved; a historical judgment does not automatically apply to the successor. An unchanged applicable owner ruling is retained at its original scope, rather than recreated as a new ruling.

A judgment answers its declared claim. It does not select additional effects. Even a correct cost judgment leaves deployment, purchase, network work, common-source changes, acceptance, release and adoption with their respective owners and separate grants. The permitted continuation here is to return the authored documentary material to the parent for freezing and independent assessment.

## Return useful progress and truthful stops

Return the useful output, exact sources and method routes, actual observations, retained unresolved obligations, authority boundary and next permitted action. Report measured acquisition and elapsed time with their scope; identify unavailable counters rather than inventing them.

Stop dependent work when a deciding source or typed join is unresolved, the context cannot adequately support the coupled obligation, the basis is incongruent, or the proposed action exceeds or exhausts the grant. Preserve the reason and resumption condition. A new revision or granted effect needs its own current binding. A passing review alone cannot supply it.
