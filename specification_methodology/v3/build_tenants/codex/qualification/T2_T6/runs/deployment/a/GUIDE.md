# A Reusable Guide For Bounded Documentary Work

Produce the document the owner requested, using deciding sources and the
permitted effects of the current work grant. Make its useful outcome, material
interpretation, evidence and remaining decisions recoverable to another reader.
A document can support an owner's choice without making that choice.

## Start With The Actual Document Job

Before an effect, recover the intended audience and useful result; the exact
subject and source revision; the owner of each material term and decision; the
current output preimage; the permitted output paths; and the completion and stop
conditions. Use the existing adequate work carrier. These facts need no fixed
file layout or additional registry merely because a new document is requested.

For example, an estimate, comparison, policy explanation or assessment summary
has its own purpose and source interpretation. It need not concern a cloud
service or contain any rate. If the grant requests a comparison, produce the
comparison while keeping an ungranted choice reserved to its owner. If a needed
source identity or effect grant is ambiguous, withhold the dependent action and
return the missing relation; do useful unrelated authorized work where possible.

Use the candidate entry's W-01 to bind the work and W-02 to recover the material
owners. An index is a route to deciding source, not replacement authority. This
particular comparison run acquired all 12 published core/profile documents to
observe a full-path configuration. That choice does not require every future
documentary worker to load the entire corpus or select every optional domain.

## Make Interpretation Visible Before Composition

For each consequential supplied fact, preserve its producer or source route,
scoped identity, revision/basis and interpretation. Rates need a currency,
unit, denominator and application interval; other facts need their own material
types and meanings. Equal labels or numeric field shapes establish only shape.

Before feeding a producer output to a consumer, compare their actual input and
output meanings, subject/basis and preconditions under F-03 and S-04. If a
supported translation exists, state what it preserves and what it changes. If
the interpretation remains unresolved, retain that uncertainty and stop the
dependent calculation or conclusion. Do not fill the gap with an implicit
default.

In the external example below, the actual Harbor/source-v1 producer supplies
`rate = 6` in AUD cents per node-hour. A consumer expecting AUD per node-hour
cannot consume that unqualified 6. Explicit translation yields 0.06 AUD per
node-hour while retaining Harbor/source-v1 identity. The brief instead uses a
typed cents consumer and applies the actual producer value 6 directly.

## Choose And Execute A Lawful Realization

Choose a calculation strategy, decomposition and presentation suited to the
document's outcome and available capability. The source obligations constrain
that choice; an incumbent algorithm or example does not make it unique. Keep
coupled material facts together sufficiently to resolve their join. A smaller
view is useful only if it preserves the obligations and sufficient source access.

For quantitative documents, show the quantities, unit cancellation, included
and excluded charges or terms, intermediate relations and final comparison basis.
Use exact arithmetic where practical and declare any rounding. For other
documents, expose the corresponding reasons and evidence so the reader can
recover how the conclusion follows. Perform only the document effects the grant
permits. Record actual writes and preserve uncertainty about any unobserved effect.

The example's author chose to retain cents through the complete per-offering
sums, then present AUD. Another brief may use an explicit normalization step and
a different internal design while satisfying the same source contract. A
two-node Harbor implementation is not a prerequisite for documentary work.

## Check, Carry Evidence, And Preserve Decisions

An author check can recompute amounts, reconcile assumptions, inspect joins and
confirm the actual files written. State what those observations establish and
what they leave open. When an independent assessment is required, an
author-independent assessor acquires governing source and its independent
expectations before assessing the frozen output. A renamed author check does
not supply that independence.

A reusable conversion or acquisition helper may serve both construction and
assessment. Bind each instance's subject, responsibility, evidence and effects
separately. Sharing the helper supplies neither an independent judgment nor
authority to perform another instance's operation. A review result travels with
its exact predicate, subject/basis, producer and validity conditions.

An owner may then use applicable evidence to make a decision within that owner's
authority. Passing cost review supports its declared cost claim; it authorizes
neither deployment nor modification of common method source. A request to
perform either effect needs its own current grant. Preserve unfinished review,
uncertainty and reserved owner decisions in the return. Re-entry refreshes
affected premises while retaining unchanged useful work and its original standing.

## Retained External Example: A Fictional First-Month Comparison

The source identity is `cost-brief/source-v1`, currency AUD, interval 30 days
at 24 hours/day, workload two continuously running nodes and 40 GB outbound.
Backup quotes apply once to the complete interval. Only compute, backup and
outbound are included; tax, discounts, currency conversion and all other charges
are excluded by the fixture. The ceiling is AUD 160 per complete offering.

| Offering and source-v1 quotes | Complete calculation in AUD cents | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Harbor: 6 cents/node-hour, AUD 18 backup/interval, 4 cents/GB | (2 × 30 × 24 × 6) + (18 × 100) + (40 × 4) = 10,600 | AUD 106.00; below ceiling by AUD 54.00 |
| Cove: 10 cents/node-hour, AUD 3 backup/interval, 2.5 cents/GB | (2 × 30 × 24 × 10) + (3 × 100) + (40 × 2.5) = 14,800 | AUD 148.00; below ceiling by AUD 12.00 |

The independently usable [brief](BRIEF.md) gives source coordinates, each
dimensional line item, the explicit unit boundary and the comparison. Both
offerings fit the fixture's ceiling; Harbor's quoted total is AUD 42.00 lower.
Neither offering has been selected. The owner retains the deployment choice.
These business results are the example's outcome, not generic method obligations.
