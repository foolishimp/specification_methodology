## Premises Before Derivations

Let κ denote the interpretation context: the selected subject, material definitions, scope, basis and admissible observations. Let U denote the purpose and owned adequacy condition. O denotes a scoped object with identity, current state and declared material relations. Σ denotes the common WHAT for a particular construction; it is distinct from an a_c signature Σ_ac.

The remaining premises are permitted effects/decisions, the admissible claim-evidence relation, and an actor's finite capability envelope B. These are inputs to a governed model. Context does not grant permission, and capacity does not select an outcome.

| Conclusion | Premises actually needed |
|---|---|
| Materiality of a relation | κ, U/Σ and the selected claim: changing the relation can change adequacy, evidence or authority. |
| Sufficient bounded work | Materiality, B, available source/access and a supported construction/evaluation relation. |
| Faithful decomposition | A sufficient outer Σ, composable local contracts, preserved invariants, coverage of material seams and admitted authority. B motivates decomposition when required; it does not prove that a feasible decomposition exists. |
| Truthful closed handling | Represented outcome classes, a reliable enough observation/admission boundary, admitted dispositions and preservation of uncertainty. B explains pressure to need it. |
| Reuse and recovery | O's identity/current state, claim/evidence validity dependencies, observability and permitted next actions. |
| Mechanism proportionality | U/Σ, material rival interpretations, B and the actual reasoning/operational cost of resolving them. |

This is a dependency graph, not a traversal order. Purpose, identity, evidence and authority can constrain the same transformation simultaneously. The algorithmic, informational and computational accounts are also simultaneous views, not lifecycle stages.

### What Finiteness Can And Cannot Establish In This Account

The qualified proposition is: **given a purpose-bound outer contract and a commitment to adequate construction by a finite actor, work whose sufficient joint context does not fit needs a supported refinement, another capability or a truthful non-completion disposition.** The conclusion follows because silent omission defeats sufficiency and the original actor cannot hold the stipulated material relation together. It does not guarantee that refinement exists.

The historical Claude proposal's phrase “finite capacity under a governing outer contract” already includes purpose, meaning and governing obligations. Conservation can be derived conditionally from faithful refinement of that contract. It would be circular to then credit finiteness alone with having created the same obligations.

These finite countermodels test particular implications:

| Claimed implication | Countermodel and conclusion |
|---|---|
| Finite capacity selects purpose | One two-state machine can serve an owner who wants its bit set and another who wants it cleared. Its capacity is identical; adequacy is opposite. Purpose needs selection/interpretation beyond B. |
| Finite capacity supplies identity | Two finite stores can key people by persistent identity or key transient observations by byte digest. Both fit; neither capacity bound decides which “same object” relation the contract needs. |
| Finite capacity entails lawful conservation | A bounded aggregator can discard a known failure while retaining a short green summary. Its output fits B. Faithfulness to Σ, not fit, rejects it. |
| Evidence implies authority | The same correct assessment is available to an authorised approver and an unauthorised reader. Observations agree; their permitted effects differ. Permission is a separate normative relation. |
| A declared purpose entails justified claims | A finite constructor can want a correct result while using an unsound oracle. Having U does not establish the witness-claim relation or independent judgment. |
| Adequate local shape entails semantic sufficiency | Two identical numeric representations can mean dollars and cents. A shape-compatible join changes the result by a factor of 100 unless κ supplies the translation. |

These arguments establish non-entailment from the named weak premises. They do not prove mutual independence of all six headings. P4 and P5 each combine descriptive/formal and normative elements; P6 combines justification with authority. A smaller basis might expose those constituents differently. C1 should accept supported arguments, rather than selecting an axiom count for aesthetic reasons.

## Conditional Derivations And Their Failure Cases

### D1 Interpretation Gives Operational Meaning Within A Declared Scope

For a representation r and context κ, let Interpret_κ(r) be its proposed meaning. An algorithmic description can specify the operations by which a consumer distinguishes admissible inputs and consequences. If an evaluation relies on a distinction but neither κ nor an admitted interpretation provides it, the evaluation cannot claim a reconstructable result.

This recovers P1 for governed work. “All information becomes meaningful only through an algorithm” has at least three readings: interpretation involves some process; every material interpretation can be made operationally explicit; all possible meaning is captured by a selected formal algorithmic language. The first is weak, the second is a useful methodological ambition, and the third is a universal representability claim. They should not share one unqualified axiom.

The two numerically identical dollar/cent values are a concrete discriminator. A parser can accept both. Their meaning requires a unit interpretation and conversion law. An operational representation that omits the unit has not reduced the required interpretation; it has hidden it.

An interpretation contract may leave declared ambiguity or unavailable meaning as a residual. It must not claim that an unmodelled meaning is nonexistent. This permits finite explicit models without making completeness of the world a prerequisite.

### D2 Interface Obligations Follow The Transformation They Serve

Take a governed transformation T:X→Y, a selected U/Σ, and a boundary through which some material input, output or effect must pass. If altering that boundary can alter T's adequacy or permitted effects, its corresponding distinctions are material. Its contract must expose enough of them for its consumer to act lawfully. Removing an irrelevant distinction does not weaken that argument.

For example, an assessment boundary may need candidate identity, required coverage, applicable judgments and source citations. A mandatory second receipt with the same facts does not follow unless it establishes another material relation. Conversely, removing a producer identity because the output “looks the same” breaks a required relation even if it shortens the interface.

This endorses interface teleology as a design derivation, subject to the declared outer purpose and constraints. It does not assert that the producer's algorithm alone determines every interface obligation. Other governing outcomes, access authority and consumer needs can be material at the same seam.

The interface's own purpose is derivative where it is purely subordinate. A reusable library contract can serve multiple purposes without inheriting ownership of all their Product decisions.

### D3 Faithful Decomposition Conserves The Outer Contract

Let the material successful-state predicates be P_0,…,P_n. For each relation T_i:X_i→X_(i+1), require that an admitted x satisfying P_i produces a successful y satisfying P_(i+1), retains each applicable invariant, and exposes other outcomes without coercing them to success. Each actual result must satisfy the next relation's domain and preconditions.

Then a successful composite carries P_0 to P_n: applying the first local implication supplies the next premise, and induction supplies the outer postcondition. Conserved invariants survive by the same argument. This is a conditional composition proof; coverage of the outer obligations, correctness of local implications and legitimate authority are premises.

If local contracts omit a coupled invariant, induction proves only the weaker chain. If both workers separately pass while their joins use inconsistent units or bases, there is no supported composition to which the argument applies. “Each step passed” is therefore weaker than “the outer contract was conserved.”

The proof establishes neither a fixed partition of all obligations at every step nor a requirement to copy the full governing body to every consumer. Required coupled relations must fit together somewhere sufficiently; unrelated obligations can stay outside the selected frame.

### D4 Conservation Includes Known Failure And Uncovered Coverage

Use two result coordinates: authenticated known failures with citations, and uncovered required obligations. A global status is a projection over both, not their replacement.

For a finite required set R and admitted applicable coverage C, uncovered coverage is R minus C. Under the fixed aggregation contract, combining authenticated results takes the union of the known failures and recomputes required uncovered coverage. A composite with either a known failure or uncovered required coverage cannot project complete success.

The discriminator is a real authenticated negative judgment for A and uncovered required B. Every planned assessment slot still has its required authenticated judgment. B is not a silently omitted planned slot; that would fail an earlier admission contract and test a different property.

Adding B's uncertainty does not invalidate A's applicable failure or its citations. A later changed candidate or basis can invalidate A's current applicability; then retain the historical judgment as historical and obtain the required new support. “Failure survives” never means “every historical failure remains a current verdict forever.”

This proposed finite aggregation law supplies a precise conformance expectation. It does not claim that an ABG path has been executed or repaired in this attempt.

### D5 Finite Representations Admit Graceful Handling Without Modelling Every World State

Separate the epistemic result from the next permitted action. An evaluator may return satisfied, falsified, indeterminate, out_of_frame or invalid_basis. A disposition may continue, refine, recover, yield, re-enter or terminate according to the selected contract and authority. A negative judgment does not itself grant repair effects.

A finite control account can map represented observation classes to defined dispositions, including an explicit unknown class. Total handling means that each admitted class has a declared outcome. It is not a guarantee that every external condition is correctly recognised, that an invoked provider terminates, or that every requested product can be completed.

A hanging external call tests supervision and observation assumptions. An unsatisfiable demand tests the non-completion disposition. A lost post-effect response tests whether an effect may have occurred and needs reconciliation. None is solved merely by adding an enum value.

The strongest generic commitment is to preserve unknowns and return a lawful disposition at the supported boundary. Specific timeouts, retries, actor counts or watchdog implementations belong to the selected realisation. The finite classifier's adequacy still requires an honest operating envelope and unknown-pressure treatment.

### D6 Restart Is Re-entry Of The Same Method Over Current State

Let W be the current persistent workspace, with its applicable governing basis and observable external dependencies. A restarted actor can apply the same observation, assessment and action-selection relation to W. An interruption changes the actor occurrence; it does not require erasing authored work or redefining the Product.

For this to be lawful, the next decision must have sufficient current state, applicable supporting claims and a permitted recovery action. Unchanged valid support can be reused. Changed dependencies reopen affected support. Evidence of an authored artifact does not prove that an independent assessment occurred.

Restart-as-iteration does not imply universal idempotency. For idempotence, a transformation T would need T(T(W))=T(W) under its declared effects. A worker that charges an external account and loses the response can observe the same local workspace after success or before success. Blindly repeating the action can double-charge. Without enough external observation, the lawful result is unresolved effect state, reconciliation, yield or stop.

Logs can supply audit, provenance and recovery evidence. They are not required to be copied into every actor's working context. Nor can current local bytes alone guarantee recovery of all material external effects. The generic principle is current-state sufficiency and truthful reconciliation; a runtime may implement the necessary persistence through files, events or another admitted owner.

### D7 Justification And Acceptance Preserve Separate Questions

A claim is evaluated against its exact subject, basis, property and evidence relation. An operation additionally needs permission. Acceptance additionally needs its declared owner's disposition. The same actor can occupy several roles when the applicable independence relation permits it; that does not merge the roles.

A valid parser can establish shape. A deterministic checker can establish a selected predicate. A source-first assessor can judge fidelity. An owner can accept a governed candidate. Each supports its stated claim. Moving an artifact between carriers or attaching a “green” label cannot enlarge those claims.

A justification relation is itself challengeable. The method's self-application is useful evidence about its own usability, not proof that all its premises are true. Required independent review still supplies its own judgment; reusing valid mechanical facts does not replace that judgment.

### D8 Shared Libraries Follow From Equal Machinery Under Preserved Semantics

Project a material transformation into data structures, algorithms, effects and semantic/authority roles. If different occurrences differ only in admitted parameters or closed variants, a shared definition can provide the mechanism while each occurrence retains its own responsibility and binding.

This is a conditional commonisation argument, not a consequence of similar names or code alone. Different freshness, identity, access, retention or failure laws may require different contracts. They may still share subordinate pure machinery. Conversely, artificial peer functions that own no irreducible distinction add reconciliation without resolving a material alternative.

Conservation concerns a recoverable material relation, not a duplicated body. A reference can replace a body when its owner, exact selector, authenticity, availability and needed completeness support the same consuming decision. A digest alone does not establish those relations. An inaccessible or insufficient reference preserves a residual instead of earning “reference-only” success.

This is the foundation for a library-oriented realisation. It does not mandate a content-addressed runtime, cache design or universal event payload limit.

## The Categorical Proposal: A Defined Fragment And Open Extensions

The proposal retains 𝒜, ℐ and 𝒞_H with S:𝒜→ℐ and R_H:𝒜→𝒞_H. A collection of algorithms is not yet a category. Objects, arrows and their equality must be defined.

For the mathematical vocabulary: categories have typed arrows, identities and associative composition; functors preserve identities and composition; categorical equivalence uses functors in both directions with natural isomorphisms to the identities. These are substantive laws, not agreement of outputs or existence of an interpretation. [Fong and Spivak, version 1803.05316v3, Definition 3.6, Definition 3.35 and Remark 3.59](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1803.05316v3#page=93).

### A Small Witness For The Span

The following is this author's proposed construction, restricted to finite deterministic sequential work.

1. **𝒜.** Objects are selected finite carrier types. Primitive arrows are declared typed algorithmic instructions with total interpretations on those carriers. Arrows are finite composable paths of primitives; the identity is the empty path and composition concatenates paths. Associativity and identity follow from concatenation. Equality here is path equality, retaining alternative algorithms.
2. **ℐ.** Objects are the selected carrier sets. Arrows are total functions between them, with ordinary function identity/composition. S interprets each primitive and each path in the fixed κ. Empty paths interpret as identities and concatenation as function composition, so S preserves those laws.
3. **𝒞_H.** For an explicitly chosen abstract machine H, fix one cost monoid M=(ℝ_≥0^k,+,0), with a fixed finite k and declared component units. Objects are the same carriers. Arrows are pairs (f,c), where f is a total function and c maps each input to M. Define identity (id,0) and the composition below. R_H assigns a primitive its interpreted function and declared cost, then extends through paths.

~~~text
(g,d) ∘ (f,c) = (g ∘ f, x ↦ c(x) + d(f(x)))

Triple composition has cost
c(x) + d(f(x)) + e(g(f(x)))
under either parenthesisation.
~~~

The cost rule is associative because function composition and vector addition are associative; the zero vector supplies both identities. S and R_H therefore have explicit laws on this fragment, by construction. No real machine measurements or general compiler-correctness claim are supplied.

Consider two primitives on integers modulo four: increment and add_two. The path increment;increment and the primitive add_two can have the same interpreted function but machine-operation costs two and one. S loses their algorithmic distinction while R_H retains a selected computational distinction. Thus this particular S is not faithful and cannot be the asserted equivalence.

The last implication has a short proof. If S had a quasi-inverse G and a natural isomorphism η:id_𝒜⇒G∘S, then S(f)=S(g) for parallel arrows would imply G(S(f))=G(S(g)). Naturality gives η_Y∘f=G(S(f))∘η_X=G(S(g))∘η_X=η_Y∘g. Cancelling the invertible η_Y gives f=g. Our two distinct paths violate that necessary faithfulness condition. This excludes this S as an equivalence; it does not exclude every possible categorical reformulation.

Quotienting algorithms by interpreted behaviour can remove that distinction and improve a semantic comparison. It simultaneously makes the proposed cost map ill-defined if equivalent representatives have different costs. This is a useful reason to keep informational and computational concerns orthogonal rather than forcing an equivalence.

### What This Fragment Does Not Prove

Real computation includes I/O, effects, communication, retained memory, failures, concurrency and scheduling. H must include the state and observations needed to interpret those operations. A scalar additive time or byte count does not model peak RSS, parallel elapsed time or arbitrary contention. Alternative cost-composition laws need their own definitions and checks.

R_H as an actual compilation/execution mapping remains proposed. Optimisation, runtime startup and architecture-dependent scheduling can defeat a naive strict cost-preservation claim. Define the selected observable behaviour and cost relation before calling the mapping a functor. The abstract witness is an available model, not a chosen runtime.

A tenant realisation a_j conforms when its interpreted behaviour satisfies the selected Σ; S(a_j)⊨Σ is shorthand for that bound claim. It does not establish categorical equivalence of 𝒜 and ℐ, identical behaviour outside Σ, or equal hardware cost. If a weak Σ permits materially different behaviours, either declare the latitude or resolve that insufficiency before claiming a decision-complete design.

Purpose U supplies the adequacy relation; O supplies the relevant subject/state boundary. Neither is automatically an additional workflow stage or a “superior” category. The proposed maps establish relationships, not a ranking of algorithmic meaning over informational meaning.

The universal algorithmic-meaning claim would require a defined domain of meanings, an interpretation language and a fidelity criterion, then a representation argument over that domain. It is not established by the existence of the small witness. HoG's proposed monadic binding likewise needs a specified categorical construction and demonstrated laws. No HoG subject is selected here, so its monad claim remains open without affecting the utility of the generic transformation account.

## World Model, State Sufficiency And Markov Claims

P3 can use a bounded identity-bearing O without committing to a universal statistical boundary. The RC2 World Model profile already preserves this distinction: an entity cut can be lawful while a Markov overlay remains candidate-class. It reports failed promotion predicates for the stronger construct; this author has not reacquired those experimental records.

Four claims need separate treatment:

| Claim | Required account |
|---|---|
| Semantic objecthood | Scoped identity, declared material relations and admissible changes/treatments. |
| State sufficient for a selected decision | For the stated decision family, materially different relevant worlds are distinguishable through the admitted state/observations, or remain explicit uncertainty. |
| Statistical boundary / temporal Markov property | Selected random variables, distribution or process, conditioning state, treatments and the particular independence predicate. |
| Markov-category structure | A specified symmetric monoidal category with the requisite copy/discard structure and compatibility laws. |

Fritz's Markov category is a formal categorical structure. Its conditional independence and statistical sufficiency are specified relations, not consequences of an object's name or an API boundary. Statistical sufficiency is relative to a statistical model. [Fritz, version 1908.07021v8, Definition 2.1, Definition 12.1 and Definition 14.3](https://arxiv.org/html/1908.07021v8).

For a proposed method state q(W), a useful decision-relative test is: if q(W_1)=q(W_2), must the selected justified decision be the same, or does the model expose uncertainty? A lossy summary that omits an applicable failed assessment provides a counterexample. Adding the missing distinction may repair this decision model without establishing any statistical Markov theorem.
