Source scope and interpretation limits
# Selected textual interpretation and finite test scope

The source is the frozen Codex successor at the commit and member digests in
SOURCE_INVENTORY.json. The program contains selected textual interpretations
of core rules and material-family consumption relationships. This is not a
complete formalization, complete core implementation or whole-corpus fidelity
claim. The finite executable covers only the domains listed below.

The preceding interpretation and its findings remain at `../../synthesis/a_c/` with their original subjects. They supply no current acceptance.

## Results

The source owners are Foundations, Result algebra and Result conjunction.
The textual clauses carry selected result, refinement, conjunction, operator
and effect obligations. The finite code tests a small AND decision projection,
evidenced undertaking/decision compatibility, selected reason-sensitive
refinement cases, disjoint effect joins, stipulated temporal normalization and
Boolean operator validity. It is not a full core-result carrier and does not
integrate conjunction truth with material effect dependencies.

## Recovery

The textual owner is Foundations, Workspace and context. Selected clauses
state acquisition, freshness, conditional reuse and uncertain-effect handling.
The finite recovery table checks five stipulated rows, assuming already acquired
current authority, fresh observations and established applicable idempotence.
No coverage is claimed for its other conservative fallback inputs. It does not prove reacquisition,
claim invalidation, authority renewal, independent-work retention, concurrency
or recovery from context loss. The former dictionary-copy trace is withdrawn.

## Construction

Selected textual clauses state outer preservation, total control responses and
progress/stopping requirements under Foundations, Bounded construction. There
is no finite executable construction interpreter or progress proof here.

## Configuration

Selected textual clauses state allocation constraints under Foundations,
Configuration. The optional Executive procedure is additional policy owned by
STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE. There is no executable allocation,
reconfiguration, optimality or independent-review model here.

## Material families

The six library responsibilities and twelve source families consume shared
contracts. Consumption is a separate vocabulary relation from reason-sensitive
outcome refinement. The material-family index selects all eighteen consumption
clauses. Other indexes select named roots and the fixed tool obtains their
transitive premise closure; direct indexing of every clause is not asserted as
a tool requirement. Validation reports the actual closure.

Each family retains its source ownership, adoption conditions, grants and finer
policy. No family relationship supplies a semantic fidelity judgment merely by
resolving. Detailed family sources are not all reproduced in the review input;
their independent fidelity remains unresolved. Traversal's j_a output relation
is a textual claim only, not an implemented admission signature or graph proof.

## Finite oracle

The AND projection has exactly two required predicate slots A and B, one
material join, established or unknown coverage, and an interpretable or invalid
outer admission basis. S/F/U/O/I denote authenticated satisfied, falsified,
indeterminate, out-of-frame and invalid-basis judgments. M denotes a missing
judgment with evidenced nonundertaking; H a missing judgment after evidenced
partial undertaking; N an absent return with undertaking unconfirmed; X an
unauthenticated claim. N remains an absent slot. Required joins range only over
S/F/U/O/I. The projection is not the full result/effect composition rule.

The evidence grid ranges undertaking over true, false and unknown, claimed
support over no claim and all five verdicts, and independence/completion facts
over booleans. Claimed support is retained separately from admissible deciding
support. Every evidence state must have exactly one guarded result, and every
evaluation result must pass the same guard. Evidence authentication itself is
stipulated, not performed by the fixture. Performance reports what the evidence
establishes, not an omniscient fact about a run whose record is missing.

The refinement domain contains five named outcomes, their declared predicate
and reason cases, plus unsupported predicate pairs and an explicit missing
reason. Unmapped or unsupported cases return an undecided claim while retaining
raw outcome, reason, performance and effects. A separately supplied negative or
diagnostic claim needs its own same-predicate provenance, stipulated admissibility
and independence even when the original evaluation was performed. The mapped
result remains explicit; optional claims cannot erase its supported falsification.
Positive separate-evaluation combination is outside this finite model; the
original missing-reason or unconfirmed record is never promoted. Subject, basis
and external authentication are fixed fixture premises, not verified here. This bounded total map is not an
implementation of every domain family's outcome signature or reason policy.

The fixture's precedence for conflicting single-predicate evidence is an
explicit finite-model policy: any mapped or admitted falsification wins; otherwise
an admitted diagnosis replaces the mapped verdict; without either, the mapped
verdict remains. The source's
conjunction precedence does not derive this policy. Both records and a conflict
flag remain; this model establishes no authority to resolve such real conflicts.
The support status distinguishes an inadmissible claim from positive or undecided
combination outside the model. Predicate-name equality and nonblank provenance
tokens do not authenticate the subject, basis, independence or producer.

Boolean operators declare truth/falsity over two leaves and one required join.
Negation constructs a new bound predicate and checked table under the same
required join, retaining the original result and leaves. It never flips a bare
verdict. Unknown well-formedness gives neither truth nor falsity. Implication
is a mathematical counterexample to arbitrary associativity, not a mutant.
The 125-case AND associativity check covers verdict precedence only; it supplies
no evidence for union of retained component results, citations or coverage.

Temporal normalization consumes ordered fixture observations and separately
stipulated identity, supersession and reversal support. List order is the
fixture's declared order; this model does not authenticate or infer that order.
The effect join covers disjoint normalized parts, not raw temporal stages.
Material possible unintended effects stay in the boundary. Unknown observation
tokens cannot establish nonstart. A verified unrequested nonstart retains its
observation and contributes not_requested; requested nonstart is not_started.

ORACLE.json contains fixed expectations; finite_check.py also states a separate
exhaustive decision table. The same Writer authored these and model.py. Their
agreement is consistency evidence, not independent source-fidelity acceptance.
Nine counted faulty functions execute against same-typed expectations from the
oracle or explicit literals. The suite is not rerun against mutated modules.
Literal workspace-reset values, timestamp selections and the implication
counterexample receive no mutant credit.

## Residual scope

Unimplemented finite core scope includes applicability/exclusion judgments;
nonperformed or unconfirmed conjunction slots carrying independent negative
support; missing or unauthenticated joins; conjunction/effect dependency
integration and parts owned by absent slots; full result-carrier coordinates;
retained-component union; non-Boolean or undecided operator leaves, undecided
operator joins and material-dependency ordering;
workspace reacquisition, freshness, changed-support invalidation and reuse;
bounded construction and configuration. The textual program is a selected
interpretation, not a claim to encode every source clause or domain policy.
Exact subject and basis, external authentication and independence are stipulated
fixture premises. Conflict precedence is a finite-model choice; positive separate
evaluation combination remains unimplemented.

Material-family source fidelity, their fine policies, traversal native
admission, predecessor dispositions, ordinary entry paths, native transport,
installation acceptance, general temporal supersession, categorical construction
or equivalence, practical superiority and release remain unresolved here.

The encoding/validation basis is the pinned RC2 Axiom Indexer and its RC2
calculus. The successor AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS is a distinct interpreted source
family. This carrier does not assert their equivalence or claim the historical
checker validates the successor's full semantic signature. Structural validation,
projection and exact joining do not execute or prove these predicates.

The retained finite model combines only the
declared negative and diagnostic support; no general evidence-merging engine
is claimed. The two 17,640-case grids separately check untrusted original claims
and separately supplied support; 540 binding refusals, 30 completion cases, 60 support-status cases and four fixed
reviewer counterexamples supplement them. These remain same-Writer consistency checks.

## Constraint application

The library's Contract application, Constraint surface, Faithful projection and
Composition law supply the common deciding relation. Domain-consumer clauses
reference that relation while retaining their source-owned predicates, policies
and exceptions. The program expresses its dependency graph and explicit
residuals. A projected closure supplies selected constraints, not a runtime grant
or proof that supplied facts are authenticated.

The new clauses require textual source-fidelity and source-use challenge cases.
They are not an implemented general admission engine. The retained finite
executable still covers only the domains stated above; it does not execute these
new application clauses. Whole-method coverage, permission inference from
unknowns, general Markov properties and categorical equivalence are not claimed.

The common-contract index selects the shared relation and its transitive premises.
It does not select a domain consumer or its fine policy. Its residual preserves
that boundary explicitly; an actual domain task must acquire the applicable
consumer and exceptions before deciding. The material-family index remains
available for the distinct task of inspecting all consumer relationships.
