# Selected textual interpretation and finite test scope

The source is the frozen Codex successor at the commit and member digests in
SOURCE_INVENTORY.json. The program contains selected textual interpretations
of core rules and material-family consumption relationships. This is not a
complete formalization, complete core implementation or whole-corpus fidelity
claim. The finite executable covers only the domains listed below.

The preceding interpretation and its findings remain at `../../synthesis/a_c/` with their original subjects. They supply no current acceptance.

## Finite oracle

The AND projection has exactly two required predicate slots A and B, one
material join, established or unknown coverage, and an interpretable or invalid
outer admission basis. S/F/U/O/I denote authenticated satisfied, falsified,
indeterminate, out-of-frame and invalid-basis judgments. M denotes a missing
judgment with evidenced nonundertaking; H a missing judgment after evidenced
partial undertaking; N an absent return with undertaking unconfirmed; X an
unauthenticated claim. N remains an absent slot. Required joins range only over
S/F/U/O/I. The projection is not the full result/effect composition rule.

The evidence grid ranges undertaking over true, false and unknown, claimed
support over no claim and all five verdicts, and independence/completion facts
over booleans. Claimed support is retained separately from admissible deciding
support. Every evidence state must have exactly one guarded result, and every
evaluation result must pass the same guard. Evidence authentication itself is
stipulated, not performed by the fixture. Performance reports what the evidence
establishes, not an omniscient fact about a run whose record is missing.

The refinement domain contains five named outcomes, their declared predicate
and reason cases, plus unsupported predicate pairs and an explicit missing
reason. Unmapped or unsupported cases return an undecided claim while retaining
raw outcome, reason, performance and effects. A separately supplied negative or
diagnostic claim needs its own same-predicate provenance, stipulated admissibility
and independence even when the original evaluation was performed. The mapped
result remains explicit; optional claims cannot erase its supported falsification.
Positive separate-evaluation combination is outside this finite model; the
original missing-reason or unconfirmed record is never promoted. Subject, basis
and external authentication are fixed fixture premises, not verified here. This bounded total map is not an
implementation of every domain family's outcome signature or reason policy.

The fixture's precedence for conflicting single-predicate evidence is an
explicit finite-model policy: any mapped or admitted falsification wins; otherwise
an admitted diagnosis replaces the mapped verdict; without either, the mapped
verdict remains. The source's
conjunction precedence does not derive this policy. Both records and a conflict
flag remain; this model establishes no authority to resolve such real conflicts.
The support status distinguishes an inadmissible claim from positive or undecided
combination outside the model. Predicate-name equality and nonblank provenance
tokens do not authenticate the subject, basis, independence or producer.

Boolean operators declare truth/falsity over two leaves and one required join.
Negation constructs a new bound predicate and checked table under the same
required join, retaining the original result and leaves. It never flips a bare
verdict. Unknown well-formedness gives neither truth nor falsity. Implication
is a mathematical counterexample to arbitrary associativity, not a mutant.
The 125-case AND associativity check covers verdict precedence only; it supplies
no evidence for union of retained component results, citations or coverage.

Temporal normalization consumes ordered fixture observations and separately
stipulated identity, supersession and reversal support. List order is the
fixture's declared order; this model does not authenticate or infer that order.
The effect join covers disjoint normalized parts, not raw temporal stages.
Material possible unintended effects stay in the boundary. Unknown observation
tokens cannot establish nonstart. A verified unrequested nonstart retains its
observation and contributes not_requested; requested nonstart is not_started.

ORACLE.json contains fixed expectations; finite_check.py also states a separate
exhaustive decision table. The same Writer authored these and model.py. Their
agreement is consistency evidence, not independent source-fidelity acceptance.
Nine counted faulty functions execute against same-typed expectations from the
oracle or explicit literals. The suite is not rerun against mutated modules.
Literal workspace-reset values, timestamp selections and the implication
counterexample receive no mutant credit.
