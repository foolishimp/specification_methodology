## UP-007: conserved nonauthorship

The predecessor's exact requirement is that the required independent reviewer
is not the author of the reviewed subject. The successor applies Foundations'
Independent assessment owner to that same exact subject. Its first paragraph
now states that the assessor is not an author of the reviewed subject or
repair; the material-contribution examples do not narrow that exclusion.

| Material case | Predecessor determination | Current successor determination |
|---|---|---|
| The implementer reviews the implementation at a required independent-review boundary. | This is useful self-review, not independent evidence. Human acceptance does not change the authorship relationship. | The shared owner makes the same determination; UP-007 selects the same boundaries and exact subject. |
| A reviewer supplies wording, policy or design adopted into the reviewed repair. | The reviewer is an author of the reviewed subject and cannot supply its required independent review. | Material adopted contributions are explicitly included in authorship; their contributor cannot independently assess that claim. |
| A reviewer reports a defect, supplies a counterexample or identifies behavior already required by the owner, without constructing the repair. | The reporting act alone is not authorship of the repair. The required nonauthor condition still applies to the actual reviewed subject. | The same act alone does not author the repair; the assessor may recheck it while all applicable independence conditions remain in force. |
| A contributor calls its authorship minor or incidental. | Calling it minor does not make the author a nonauthor. | The unqualified nonauthor condition applies. The illustrative material-contribution sentence supplies no exception for an actual author. If the relationship cannot be established, the required independent claim is unsupported. |
| An assessor authored one claim but assesses a separate unaffected claim. | The exact reviewed subject controls; a review cannot claim independence for the authored portion. | The same exact-claim condition holds. Unaffected judgments retain only their supported scope; they do not accept the authored claim or the whole containing member. |
| No governing contract imposes a contributor-specific disclosure act. | UP-007 requires a traceable actual verdict and subject; it does not assign a new general disclosure act to every contributor. | The generic added duty has been removed. An additional contributor-disclosure duty comes from the applicable governing contract. This does not waive evidence needed to establish actual authorship. |
| The governing construction contract does impose disclosure. | The selected applicable contract binds the activity. | That contract still binds it. C3's disclosure rule is retained for this construction without becoming an extra universal method duty. |

The comparison also covers the remainder of the UP-007 section. Required
Product, qualification, release and explicitly risk-bearing review boundaries
remain; not every local step needs independent review. Exact subject, basis,
scope, exclusions, evidence, verdict and unresolved findings remain bound to
a durable existing carrier. Promotion-blocking conditions, outside-boundary
repricing, human-ruling continuity and the UP-020 execution binding remain.
The separate core-result account clarifies that promotion relevance is not a
verdict. The existing shared replacement dependencies remain pending; this
comparison supplies no new owner acceptance of them.

The generic contribution-disclosure obligation removed by this repair was a
candidate addition. It was not predecessor law. Removing that unsupported
addition restores the retained requirement rather than proposing weaker
nonauthorship. New explicit examples explain the unchanged author boundary;
they cannot establish authorship, innocence or evidential independence merely
by an actor's chosen label.

## Reuse and operational authority

The Frame Method worked binding is explicitly non-normative. Its subject,
exact coordinates, evidence and grants remain. The current stop example
makes three conditions explicit: an uninterpretable governing basis yields
`invalid_basis`; insufficient supporting evidence under an interpretable basis
leaves the predicate `indeterminate` unless other admissible evidence decides
it; a historical result remains bound to its original subject, and reuse needs
established applicability. This is conservation of the illustrative binding,
with its required RF-005 carrying relation stated. The normative RF-005
replacement `RC1-CONSUMER-R002` remains an unresolved shared dependency.

Baseline's short prohibition on editing or directing repair now distinguishes
authoring the assessed contribution from exercising repair decision authority.
Reporting findings and rechecking remain evaluation, subject to the shared
independence owner. A Reviewer still lacks authority to choose or issue the
repair. Existing Derived Reviewer, Actor Binding, Failure Classification and
Anti-Patterns item 31 supply those boundaries; the clarification creates no
additional reviewer count or review stage.

Baseline Anti-Patterns item 11 literally prohibited reuse after any candidate
or basis change. The successor permits supported applicability while retaining
the historical subject. This changes the decision for an unaffected, explicitly
applicable prior result and is recorded as proposed supersession
`RC4-ASSESSMENT-R002`, not merely conservation. Its carrying owner is Change
impact and evidence retention. The other numbered prohibitions remain.
