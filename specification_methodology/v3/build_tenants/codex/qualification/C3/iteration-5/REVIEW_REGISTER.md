- an authored source/discriminator route, which carries no passing judgment;
- a nonauthor source judgment on an exact obligation and material case;
- an author or independently reproduced mechanical result, with its finite scope;
- an observed execution or actual-use result, on its exact supplied inputs;
- assessment against independently fixed expectations, with observation and
  assertion fidelity supported in the relevant scope;
- an owner decision, which grants only the acceptance or authority it records.

material validity dependencies and unchanged or preserved affected relations on

impact widens reassessment or leaves dependent assurance unresolved. New
obligations and mandatory gates remain covered. Known failures and their
citations persist until supported repair, reassessment or authorized obligation
change disposes them. Repeated evidence and different actors do not establish
