## Agentic Construction Execution (`STDO-UP-020`)

When construction is delegated to a probabilistic or agentic worker, the
method governs the execution relation without prescribing the worker's model,
prompt, tools, internal search, process topology, repository layout, or
orchestration runtime.

The normative relation is:

```text
accepted governing basis
  -> bounded affected relation set
  -> selected computational relations
  -> delegated execution and decision authority
  -> construction and self-review
  -> independent live-surface assessment where required
  -> accept | local repair | re-enter | reject
  -> exact accepted checkpoint
  -> next already-authorized bounded action
```

### Sufficient Execution Intake

A bounded action begins from an authority basis sufficient to reconstruct its
governing relations without conversation history, commentary, prior-worker
memory, or implementation folklore. The intake identifies:

- the selected Product outcome, prerequisite, or admitted experiment;
- the bounded affected relation set and applicable governing invariants;
- accepted transformation-admission relations;
- selected computational or common-library dispositions;
- construction and assessment authority; and
- the conditions that require upstream re-entry.

Where `DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md` applies, `STDO-UP-017` bounded-frame
conservation, `STDO-UP-016` transformation-admission completeness,
`STDO-UP-018` invariant-reconstruction sufficiency, and `STDO-UP-019`
computational-realization projection supply these design inputs. This section
consumes those laws; it does not create a second design package.

### Bounded Causal Construction

The execution unit is the smallest coherent causal dependency cone capable of
delivering working behavior while preserving every affected governing
relation. It need not coincide with one function, file, module, ticket field,
or design document.

A seam whose far side can causally affect, or be affected by, the action is
inside the cone unless the basis establishes that it cannot participate.
Unrelated incompleteness outside the cone is recorded as repricing input and
does not block the bounded claim.

### Construction, Assessment, And Delegation

The constructor owns bounded implementation and self-review. Where independent
assessment supports promotion, the assessor evaluates the exact live code,
authority paths, proof, and installed subject rather than treating the
constructor's summary as sufficient evidence. This preserves the independent-
review relation; it does not prescribe an actor count. One actor may occupy
different roles across different claims when independence for the assessed
claim is preserved.

The governing authority relation declares whether a bounded proxy may accept
and advance routine actions. A proxy may accept preservation, require local
repair, reject a violating candidate, and advance to the next already-
authorized action. It cannot change Product meaning, requirements, governing
authority, or accepted design. Such a change re-enters at its owning relation.

Human ceremony is not required at every transition when the governing basis,
affected cone, decision envelope, and re-entry conditions are already
accepted. This delegation does not weaken direct-human acceptance where
Product or release law requires it.

When one capable context has sufficient existing work authority, exact intake
and operation grant, it may enter directly as constructor or Writer. No
Executive activation is required solely to appoint that Writer. The applicable
frame configuration selects coordination when material contexts, authority,
capability or required independence need it; a role or frame name alone does
not create that need. Direct entry preserves execution-contract admission,
the smallest lawful carrier, required assessment and reserved decisions. It
permits bounded work and condition-based result recording, not self-acceptance
at a boundary owned elsewhere.

One bounded operation grant may cover its declared construction, status and
checkpoint sequence. Record actual role transitions in the existing work
carrier; a status change alone requires neither a new grant nor rehashing an
unchanged subject. A material change to the operation, subject, territory,
authority or validity conditions still requires the affected binding to be
resolved before effect. Executive mutation restrictions remain applicable.

### Proportional Disposition

Assessment returns one of these semantic dispositions:

| Disposition | Condition |
|---|---|
| `accept` | the candidate closes its declared cone and preserves every affected governing relation |
| `local_repair` | implementation or proof is locally defective while the governing relation remains coherent |
| `re_enter` | Product, requirement, identity, lifecycle, authority, public contract, or accepted design must change |
| `reject` | the candidate violates a governing relation or leaves an undisposed competing truth path |

Global correctness means conserving every global relation affected by the
bounded action. It does not require proving the whole Product inside every
local action.

Repeated rejection against one unchanged boundary requires reassessment of the
frame altitude, causal cone, common components, design-versus-code placement,
and authority sufficiency. The method sets no rejection-count threshold,
mandatory review-round count, or review state machine; it prohibits repeating
an unchanged failed construction relation as if iteration alone could close it.

### Transition Evidence And Proof Cadence

A reviewable transition identifies the exact candidate, affected relations,
changed realization paths, authority paths added or removed, retained and open
seams, focused proof, any required integration proof, explicit non-changes, and
the assessor disposition. Consumers choose the representation. Transition
evidence is a read model and does not become specification, design, or
admission authority.

Construction uses the cheapest focused proof capable of falsifying the active
relation. Expensive whole-candidate qualification occurs at a declared
candidate boundary rather than after every local edit, unless the affected
risk makes whole-candidate proof necessary.

A durable or reconstructable authority claim is not established solely by a
same-process proof when loss of incidental process state is a material
counterexample. Proof must destroy or exclude the incidental authority and
compare reconstructed semantic outcomes.

### Checkpoint, Continuation, Progress, And Churn

Acceptance binds one exact checkpoint. The next already-authorized bounded
action may begin without renewed human approval; acceptance does not widen the
Product outcome or affected relation set.

A preservation checkpoint may retain unfinished or unaccepted work under an
existing exact operation grant. Identify its parent or predecessor, included
territory, concurrent-writer disposition and durable evidence location. It is
neither acceptance, promotion nor publication. A proposed restart identifies
why repair of retained work is insufficient, which obligations and evidence
survive, and which causal assumptions change; reconstructability alone does
not select reconstruction.

Accepted material Product movement is progress. Prerequisite work names the
Product obligation it enables. Preservation and repeated proof are evidence,
not new progress. Rejected or superseded construction is churn. Discovery of
previously hidden distance revises the forecast but is not itself negative
implementation progress. Paperwork, code volume, test count, commit count, and
review count are not Product progress by themselves.

These are semantic categories, not a numeric progress algorithm. Consumers may
derive their own progress and churn projections from them.

This protocol is false if execution proceeds from unreconstructable context,
if a constructor's report substitutes for required independent live evidence,
if a bounded proxy changes a governing relation outside its delegation, if
unrelated incompleteness blocks a closed causal cone, if a same-process test is
used to prove materially cross-process authority, or if rejected activity is
reported as accepted Product progress.

# STDO Foundations

STDO specifies purposeful construction by actors with finite resources. A method
claim is scoped to a purpose, interpreted subject, governing basis, actor and
evidence. These foundations organise the method; they do not make draft v3 text
the builder of its own construction.

## Premises and standing

An actor has finite attention, context, time, storage and capability. This is a
resource premise. It does not choose an outcome, confer authority, identify an
object or prove compatible composition. Those commitments require their own
premises.

A selected purpose U supplies an adequacy predicate and reserved value choices.
An object O supplies a scoped identity, boundary, current state and material
relations. An interpretation context kappa determines the meaning available to
a consumer. A governing specification Sigma declares obligations and admissible
variation. A grant bounds who may act and decide. Observations and arguments
support claims only on their declared subject and basis.

The method's obligations follow from these premises together: preserve the
selected outcome and constraints; supply sufficient context for each material
relation; conserve identity and authority through composition; and report an
honest supported result when construction or evaluation cannot complete.
Finite capacity alone does not prove all those obligations.

## Interpretation and representation

A representation is an encoding. An interpretation relates that encoding to
meaning under a declared context. Equal encodings can have different meanings;
different encodings can represent the same scoped meaning. A parser, structural
validator or successful join does not establish interpretation fidelity.

An operational algorithm can make an interpretation testable. Universal claims
that every meaning has an algorithmic encoding require a defined domain,
adequacy relation and argument; they remain hypotheses outside that domain.
Loss or abstraction is admissible only when the governing contract permits it
and the required consumer distinctions remain recoverable.

## Purpose and interfaces

The purpose owner fixes the observable adequate outcome. An interface exists
because a transformation needs to acquire, convey, judge or effect a material
relation. Its obligations derive from that relation and the outer contract.
A convenient signature, existing function or local test cannot invent Product
meaning. An obligation with no requiring purpose or invariant must be justified
or removed by its owner.

## Identity, state and evidence

Identity distinguishes an object within a declared scope. Identity may survive
a rename, relocation, representation change or allowed state transition.
Content equality does not establish identity; unchanged identity does not
establish current content. A digest binds bytes, not all semantic meaning or
permission.

Work state is the current subject under construction. An event, assessment or
receipt is evidence of a scoped observation or judgment. Acceptance is an owner
decision. None of these substitutes for another. Historical evidence remains
historical when current premises change. Reuse requires supported applicability,
including identity, basis, freshness and the predicate actually assessed.

## Typed composition and conservation

Relations compose only when their source and destination types, interpretation,
identity, lifecycle, preconditions, authority and effects are compatible. A
handoff conserves the material outer contract and original producer coordinates.
It retains known failures and their citations, required uncovered coverage and
uncertainty. Local success cannot erase a failed or missing material relation.

Conservation concerns recoverable meaning, not copying bodies. A reference is
sufficient when its authentic owner, exact selector, availability and consumer
resolution preserve the required relation. A digest of unavailable material
does not supply context. Shared machinery may serve several instances while
their semantic owners, responsibility and independence remain distinct.

## Finite capacity and total handling

Each activation declares the actor and resource envelope needed by its claim.
Before acting, establish sufficient material context and capability. If the
coupled relation does not fit, refine it without losing the outer contract,
acquire support, reconfigure within authority, request owning-layer re-entry,
yield or stop with an explicit unresolved result. Never replace a difficult
required relation with an easier claim merely to report success.

A total control function has a defined response for every input in its declared
control domain, including unknown external conditions. It need not solve every
world state or guarantee termination of every problem. Deterministic functions
can have a small closed state machine; probabilistic workers can explore more
outcomes while an enclosing finite control algebra handles failure, incomplete
work, refusal, recovery and exhausted authority truthfully.

Restart is another iteration over the current work state. Observe the workspace
and affected dependencies, retain applicable progress, then choose a permitted
action. This does not imply every side effect is idempotent. An uncertain effect
must be observed, reconciled or returned unresolved before any repetition that
could duplicate it. Logs supply observability and audit, and may support
reconstruction; requiring an actor to reload all history is not a generic law.

## Judgment and authority

Definitions, assumptions, observations, candidate judgments and accepted claims
have distinct standing. A judgment binds a predicate, exact subject, basis,
evidence, assessor and any required independence. An authority grant permits
specified operations or decisions. Neither evidence shape nor actor capability
mints a grant. A Reviewer judges; a decision owner accepts or selects the next
action within existing authority.

Unknown applicability, missing context, incompatible basis and failed predicates
remain observable. Severity describes evidenced harm; priority selects work;
promotion effect follows the applicable mandate. Those are distinct from the
semantic result and from the decision to repair, re-enter, reject or accept.

## Orthogonal descriptions

Keep three accounts distinct. An algorithmic account specifies the selected
transformation. An informational account explains interpreted distinctions and
their preservation. A computational account realizes the operation over a
hardware and I/O architecture, with material resource costs. They are
simultaneous descriptions, not successive SDLC stages.

To assert a category, specify objects, typed arrows, identities and associative
composition. To assert a functor, show identity and composition preservation.
For a proposed span A -> I and A -> C_H, conformance to Sigma, semantic
equivalence and computational equivalence remain different claims. Categorical
equivalence additionally requires the appropriate quasi-inverse and natural
isomorphisms on a stated domain; an analogy or matching result is insufficient.

State sufficiency for a selected decision, statistical Markov properties,
conditional independence, a mathematical Markov category and a runtime monad
are separate claims. The method neither asserts nor rejects them universally.
Their domains, laws and falsifiers must be supplied before using them as proof.

## Canonical transformations

The [transformation library](TRANSFORMATION_LIBRARY.md) expresses Bind, Acquire,
Transform, Evaluate, Conjoin and Dispose. Instances bind contracts and owners;
they are not six compulsory stages or six runtime APIs. The detailed method
members refine the relations for their domains without acquiring authority from
the library. A new primitive needs a material distinction the existing algebra
cannot preserve.

## Construction and use

The corpus may exceed one actor's capacity. An activation consumes a sufficient
projected reference frame and exact subject, with acquisition paths for required
material. The projection has declared fidelity and scope; it is not a rival
source of authority. Independent evaluation may require overlapping context at
a different capability or evidence altitude.

These foundations preserve the accepted Codex C1 argument and the common
principle questions. The successor member map records their relationship to
the predecessor. Product acceptance, universal mathematical truth, operational
efficiency and release each need their own evidence and authority.

# Transformation Library

An instance is bound by purpose, exact subject and state, interpretation,
governing basis, actor capability, inputs, required constraints, permitted
effects, output predicate, failures and consumer. This is a logical contract,
not a mandatory record schema. A sufficient existing carrier can express it.

## Bind

Recover the current purpose, subject identity, governing specification, role,
authority, state and required relations. Conflicting, missing or stale bindings
are explicit results. A name, path, cached result or available tool is no grant.

## Acquire

Resolve enough authentic material for the selected operation or evaluation.
Declare completeness, freshness, coupled invariants and interpretation. Resolve
references when their bodies are needed. Unavailable evidence, insufficient
context or capability returns pressure for refinement, support, re-entry or a
truthful stop. Acquisition cannot silently drop a governing obligation.

## Transform

Construct output or perform effects within the bound contract. Preserve the
original producer, material inputs and observed effects. Return candidate work,
refusal, incomplete work or a re-entry request. Observe uncertain external effects
before repeating them. The worker's internal search is its HOW; it cannot widen
the outer outcome or effect grant.

## Evaluate

Judge a declared predicate on an exact subject using sufficient admissible
evidence. Return satisfied, falsified, indeterminate, out_of_frame or
invalid_basis with original citations and residual coverage. Where required,
the assessor is independent of authorship. A judgment supplies no repair,
acceptance or next-action authority.

## Conjoin

Combine only compatible, authenticated results on a declared common outcome.
Retain each applicable known failure, its producer and citations. Retain required
uncovered coverage separately. Authenticate every planned assessment slot; an
omitted planned judgment is an admission defect, while an authenticated set can
still leave a different required responsibility uncovered. Satisfaction requires
every applicable material predicate and seam to be supported. An incompatible
basis or unknown applicability prevents a wider satisfied claim.

## Dispose

The decision owner consumes exact closed work and evaluation results with the
current mandate. It selects accept, local_repair, re_enter or reject for the
declared cone. It may select a next already-authorized action, yield or terminate
truthfully. It cannot change upstream meaning or take a reserved owner decision.
Accept binds an exact checkpoint and interval; it does not imply release.

## Composition law

For T: X -> Y and S: Y -> Z, S after T is admitted only when carried meaning,
identity, basis, lifecycle, effects and authority match or have a lawful explicit
translation. Composition preserves the outer predicate and material invariant
set. A shared helper may implement several instances; differing semantic owners
remain parameters or distinct contracts. Independent judgment cannot be
compressed into author self-assessment.

An identity transformation preserves the selected subject and interpretation;
associative regrouping is valid only when it preserves observable effects,
failure, material context and decision boundaries. This states a bounded
composition obligation, not a universal category theorem for arbitrary software.

## Recovery law

After interruption, bind the same work identity to its observed current state.
Reassess only materially affected premises and retain applicable work. Reconstruct
needed authority and observations from their declared owners, not private memory.
Uncertainty about effects, freshness or evidence remains explicit. Churn is an
unchanged failed relation attempted again without a supported change in its cause.

## Cost and contraction

Measure costs appropriate to the claim: acquired and transferred bytes, required
attention, I/O, CPU, addressable memory, elapsed critical-path time, aggregate
work and displaced human assurance. A reference reduces storage only when its
resolution preserves adequacy. A smaller document or faster local preparation
does not prove whole-path improvement. Contract duplicated meaning and authority
only while preserving required distinctions and independent evaluations.
