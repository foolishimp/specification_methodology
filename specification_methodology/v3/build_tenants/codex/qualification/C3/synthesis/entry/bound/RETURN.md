The candidate actor explicitly declined candidate `TRIAGE_CARRIER` as an
unverified fallback and acquired installed rc.2 intake clauses. The continuation
likewise acquired rc.2's continuation owner. This follows the actual selected
basis, rather than showing failure to discover the candidate files.

- `plugins/spec/skills/stdo-work/SKILL.md:20–37`: select the Product basis's
  standards index and views; only available material from the verified basis
  is eligible, and mutable candidate material is no fallback. Line 56 selects
  that basis's Spec Method.
- `plugins/spec/references/PRODUCT_BASIS.md:24–36`: verify the selected
  definition's exact installed basis and return that selection.
- `standards/SPEC_METHOD.md:506–511`: the definition's basis URI and manifest
  are its sole authored STDO selection.
- `standards/SPEC_METHOD.md:2874–2892`: candidate source does not become operative
  consumer law before release and explicit selection; a local exact copy must
  be explicitly bound and verified.

The fixture truthfully selects rc.2. Its experimental source grant does not
change that authored selection. No package defect requiring removal of the
no-fallback guard is established. The smallest consistent action is to preserve
these claims at their actual scope. Explicitly supplied frozen-frame trials
can assess candidate behavior as experimental subject material. A genuine
unchanged ordinary-v3 entry proof needs a real immutable candidate installation
and fixture basis selection through the release/qualification owner. Relabeling
the rc.2 receipt or silently treating candidate source as installed law would
invalidate the experiment.

Consequently the lower rc.2 cost in this one pair is an observation, not a
general performance conclusion. The candidate calls used a different entry
and additional candidate discovery while still relying on rc.2 deciding
clauses. They do not demonstrate the effectiveness of candidate frames, its
core result algebra in ordinary use, or whole-method superiority. The actors
also disclosed incomplete acquisition of baseline evaluation owners; the host
file oracle and behavior tests do not close those broader method obligations.
