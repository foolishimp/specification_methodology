## Join corrections

The profile/core join exposed a real distinction: a rejected invocation may
prove that the selected effect did not begin even if transport dispatch occurred.
The core Writer accepted the correction that `not_started` needs evidence of no
selected effect, not necessarily no dispatch. Traversal retains the authenticated
`refused_effect` case. A lost response remains unknown.
Its refusal clause now requires evidence that no mutation began; absence of
authorisation cannot conceal an observed or uncertain unauthorised mutation.

The subsequent shared repair distinguishes known non-performance from
unconfirmed performance, and known partial execution from an uncertain remainder.
ODD retains `unconfirmed` where performance is not established. Traversal maps
`partial` only when both a completed part and an unexecuted remainder are known;
an uncertain remainder remains `unknown`.

The `out_of_scope` Traversal decision requires particular care: in the closed
constraint table it follows a false `event_scope_compatible` predicate and thus
retains core `falsified`. It is not mechanically renamed `out_of_frame` by its
spelling. A request outside the admitted evaluation domain uses `out_of_frame`
at its invocation boundary. Assessment judgments remain distinct from a separate
lawful owner-disposition result.
