# Rebuilt core interpretation scope

The selected source is the Codex successor at the file digests in
SOURCE_INVENTORY.json. This interpretation covers the shared result,
workspace/context, bounded construction and configuration laws and the imports
of material method families. It is not a complete interpretation of all domain
policy, all predecessor claims, or every released member.

## Results

Source owners: Foundations, Result algebra and Result conjunction. Evaluate
qualified predicate results, authentic conjunction, non-performance, uncertain
effects, candidate availability, and domain refinements. The result record is a
semantic contract; no added production serialization is required.

## Recovery

Source owner: Foundations, Workspace and context. Reacquire current state and
authority, invalidate affected claims, preserve supported independent work and
observe unknown effects before dependent repetition. Context loss is not a
workspace reset and creates no authority or idempotence guarantee.

## Construction

Source owner: Foundations, Bounded construction. Preserve the selected
destination through bounded transitions with explicit joins and total control
responses. Local success, activity counts and a changed destination do not
establish the original outer outcome.

## Configuration

Source owner: Foundations, Configuration. Allocation depends on tasks,
capabilities, information, authority and assurance. Fixed roles or hierarchy are
not derived from finite capacity. Independent review remains claim-relative.

## Material families

The six library responsibilities and the reference-frame, engagement, Spec, Design,
Ticket, Release, a_c, traversal, ODD, UX, world-model and identity families
consume the shared contracts. Their source-owned grants and fine policies
remain distinct. A family import does not establish full interpretation of
that family, source fidelity, or conformance of its implementation.

## Finite oracle

The selected finite test boundary is an interpretable conjunction with two
required predicates A and B, one explicit material-join condition, known or
unknown required coverage, and a valid or invalid composite basis. A and B may
be authentically satisfied, falsified, indeterminate, out of frame, invalid,
known never started, known aborted, conclusion unconfirmed, or unauthenticated. Missing results
stay coverage slots rather than synthetic child judgments. Only authentic
evidence enters the truth decision. Performance pairs and effect-state joins
have their own finite domains, retaining component scope. Known rollback is a
reverted effect within its proved boundary, not evidence the effect never began.

The independent expectation specification is ORACLE.json, authored from the
source contract before the test implementation. It contains fixed expected
outcomes and retained evidence for discriminating cases. The exhaustive oracle
in finite_check.py is a separately stated set-based decision table; the tested
implementation is model.py. The same Writer authored these artifacts, so
algorithmic separation is not independent-assessor validation of fidelity.

Additional fixed cases distinguish unavailable assessment from undecidability,
candidate availability from fitness, unknown completion from no dispatch,
negative scope compatibility from an out-of-frame evaluation, and a reset of
private context from a reset of material work. Mutants deliberately erase these
distinctions and must be detected. They are qualification subjects outside the
method's product members.

## Residual scope

Detailed domain policy, all clause-level predecessor dispositions, installation
acceptance, external transport behavior, distributed exactly-once delivery,
general categorical equivalence and practical superiority are unproved here.
The fixed Axiom Indexer checks structure, sources, projection and exact joining;
it does not execute these semantic predicates. Finite checking qualifies only
the explicitly enumerated interpretation. Independent source-first review,
actual use and the selected whole-candidate acceptance remain separate.
