# Rebuilt core interpretation scope

The selected source is the Codex successor at the file digests and frozen input
commit in SOURCE_INVENTORY.json. This interpretation covers the shared result,
workspace/context, bounded construction and configuration laws and the imports
of material method families. It is not a complete interpretation of all domain
policy, all predecessor claims, or every released member. The archived
pre-9982 directory contains superseded a746 checks and supplies no current
qualification credit. It is excluded from current program inputs, frame
selection and mechanical artifact pins.

Source owners: Foundations, Result algebra and Result conjunction. Evaluate
qualified predicate results, authentic conjunction, actual evaluation
undertaking independently of extent and conclusion, unknown effects, supported
temporal reconciliation, candidate availability, and domain refinements. The
result record is a semantic contract; no added production serialization is
required. A possible verdict/performance pair still needs its evidence guard.

## Finite oracle

The selected conjunction domain has two required predicates A and B, one
explicit material join, known or unknown required coverage, and a valid or
invalid composite admission basis. Required slots may be satisfied, falsified,
indeterminate, out of frame, invalid, known never undertaken, known partly
undertaken and aborted without a return, undertaking unconfirmed, or accompanied
by an unauthenticated claim. Missing results remain coverage slots rather than
synthetic child judgments. Only authentic evidence enters the truth decision.
An actual incomplete or inadequate run is performed; this differs from whether
it concluded and whether it can decide its predicate.

The coordinate examples separate undertaking evidence from admissible deciding
support. Nonperformance and unconfirmed undertaking can retain independently
established counterevidence or a scope/basis diagnosis; neither supplies
satisfaction. Thirteen pairs are potentially legal, subject to these guards.
The examples retain observed extent, conclusion and effects.

Effect joining applies only to disjoint normalized current parts. Temporal
examples consume stipulated same-part supersession evidence, identity, ordering
and reversal support; they do not prove an arbitrary supersession algorithm.
Deployment followed by rollback and reapplication is reconciled at the current
boundary. Missing requested or material possible unintended parts remain unknown. A later timestamp alone does
not resolve conflict; an irreversible external message survives local rollback.
An established effect-free scope and a nominally read-only operation with a possible unintended effect are different. No request alone is insufficient.

The finite operator domain is two Boolean leaves and a required join. Its truth
and falsity conditions must be disjoint and exhaustive; a required false join
is included. Unestablished well-formedness supplies neither truth nor falsity; negation cannot promote it. Conjunction precedence and disjoint normalized effects have bounded
associativity checks. Implication supplies a counterexample to extending that
claim to arbitrary operators. No arbitrary effect reordering is tested or
licensed.

ORACLE.json fixes expected outcomes separately from the executable model.
The exhaustive oracle in finite_check.py is a separately stated set-based
decision table; the implementation is model.py. The same Writer authored these
artifacts. Algorithmic separation is not independent-assessor source-fidelity
acceptance. The modeled evidence tokens stand for the declared fixture facts;
they do not authenticate external evidence. The functions are bounded
interpretations, not a complete implementation of the method's result carrier.

Actual faulty-function counterexamples cover erased failures, invented
performance, inadequate work called unperformed, candidate availability promoted
to fitness, unknown effect promoted to nonstart, negative scope compatibility
mislabelled out of frame, unknown remainder called partial, raw temporal labels
joined, timestamp-only supersession, arbitrary operator associativity, blind
retry and workspace erasure. These fixtures are outside product members.
