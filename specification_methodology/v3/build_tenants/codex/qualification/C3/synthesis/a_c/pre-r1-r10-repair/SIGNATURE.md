# Synthesis interpretation signature

This is the author-owned vocabulary of a bounded a_c.text interpretation, not
another method constitution or a semantic engine. The exact owning source
sections accompany every clause. Validation checks their resolution and
authored relation graph. An independent assessor must judge source fidelity.

## Concept

A named semantic subject used by the selected method clauses.

## Requires

The declared guard and premises entail the named obligation within the source
contract. An absent premise does not establish its conclusion.

## Preserves

The named transformation retains the specified identity, meaning, evidence or
authority relation under its declared conditions.

## Refines

A source-owned domain supplies a reason-sensitive refinement of the common
contract, preserving its own predicate and qualified outcome. This does not
declare the full domain's policy equivalent to the common contract.

## Consumes

A responsibility or source family uses the named shared constraints while
retaining its own semantic owner and adoption conditions. Consumption alone
is not a reason-sensitive domain-outcome refinement or a conformance proof.

## Assumes

A foundational premise or separately chosen policy enters the interpretation.
It is not claimed as a consequence of resource finitude alone.

## Input

The named subject, value or source-owned domain being constrained.

## Premise

A supporting clause whose satisfaction is required for the argument.

## Guard

The explicit condition selecting the obligation.

## Consequence

The required relation after all material premises hold.

## Qualification

A restriction, exception or boundary that travels with the clause.

## Standing

The clause is a premise, a source-owned method law, or an additional domain
policy. Those standings do not imply adoption or independent acceptance.
