# Method Structure

## Binding and outcome

The common requirements determine the method's meaning and permitted outcomes.
This tenant binds them to document construction, source comparison, frame
projection, predicate evaluation and authorised disposition. Sequential
composition preserves the declared subject and obligations; independent tasks
may run concurrently only within disjoint write territories and compatible
inputs. Effects are document edits, commits, local checker execution and
explicitly granted decisions. The execution environment is a mixed process:
Codex construction, separate review activations, Git worktrees, Python checkers,
the pinned Axiom Indexer and the product owner's decisions. Byte counts measure
selected inputs; wall time and CLI spend measure the declared process boundary.
Unmeasured parent-session overhead remains unknown.

The workspace is the continuing truth surface. Actor context is a bounded
projection acquired from its current state and authoritative external inputs.
The method must still work after that private projection is discarded. A stale
summary cannot override current observation. External effects need their actual
owning observation; a local file is no proof that a remote effect did not occur.

## Ownership and consumers

The method has three kinds of material: shared deciding contracts, domain
bindings with additional policies, and source-bound views for actual use.
These are ownership relations, not three compulsory stages or a file-count
constraint. A domain application uses the shared contract directly and supplies
its own predicate, evidence rule, grants and exceptions. No additional
admission record or generic validation pass is introduced.

## Operations and execution binding

The generic construction path uses the owners below without adopting a typed
graph, runtime ledger or Executive profile. These rows describe the operations
in this tenant's realization, not a compulsory sequence, API or actor count.
One granted operation can discharge several rows while preserving their distinct
predicates, effect boundaries and authority.

| Operation and actual consumer | Governing contract | Concrete tenant binding and observation |
|---|---|---|
| Form a candidate account of the destination or realization | Spec constitutional chain and WHAT/HOW binding; Library Transform and Faithful projection | A Writer proposes document/design bytes against the selected common requirements. Candidate availability does not establish their meaning, fitness or acceptance. Changing the destination needs its owner's decision. |
| Observe the current subject and characterize a gap | Foundations Workspace and context; Spec Universal Intake Triage and Computed Classification And Treatment | Source reads, exact Git coordinates and external observations support a classification. A missing acquired file is not proof of absence; a stored conclusion does not override its current support. |
| Select the next permitted bounded action | Foundations Bounded construction; Spec UP-013 and UP-020 disposition; Library Dispose | The actor carrying the existing mandate selects work within the outcome and grant, or returns unresolved re-entry. A gap, priority or proposed remedy cannot create authority. |
| Construct the selected candidate or effect | Library Contract application and Transform; the selected Spec/Ticket execution contract | The named Writer edits only its granted territory. Git or external effects need their own applicable grant. Record the actual subject, affected relations and observed effect boundary. |
| Assess the action's product claim | Foundations Result algebra and Result conjunction; Spec UP-020; domain owner mappings | Deterministic checks establish their declared predicates; source judgment addresses meaning and applicable coverage. Required nonauthor assessment remains separate from the constructor's self-check. |
| Decide acceptance or another disposition | Foundations Judgment and authority; the exact Spec, Ticket or Release decision contract | The competent decision owner records the exact subject and mandate. A lawful rejection can satisfy the decision predicate while candidate fitness remains failed or unresolved. |
| Continue, recover, yield or terminate | Foundations Workspace and context and Bounded construction; Spec UP-013/UP-020; Ticket Work-Carrier-To-Execution Rule | A later activation reacquires current authoritative state, reuses applicable work and reconciles uncertain effects before dependent repetition. Lawful stopping retains the destination result and outstanding obligations. |

ODD's `synthesize_model`, `eval_gap`, `evaluate_next` and `evaluate_action`
specialize modelling, gap observation, next-action selection and action
assessment for an adopted graph-native Product. Their published actions,
particular authority split, admitted history and replay duties remain at ODD.
The generic path above acquires those duties only through the applicable
adoption relation; it does not copy them into common construction.

Operation kind, actor and executor remain distinct. The calculus-owned
`F_D`, `F_P` and `F_H` classify an exact traversal under
AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS; the graph-native shorthand has its own ODD context. A
person executing a deterministic check is not thereby exercising human
adjudication, and a model producing a candidate has not proved its fitness.
This design introduces no `F_C` kind or fixed sequence of kinds.

The concrete binding measures selected and acquired input bytes, repeated
acquisition, elapsed time, spend and other resources only at the boundaries
needed by the claim. Supplied context, cumulative acquisition and actively
retained context are separate quantities; unknown host or reasoning portions
remain unknown. Sequential preservation does not establish reordered or
parallel-effect equivalence. Source observations and independent assessment,
not the tool or actor's name, supply the claimed conformance evidence.

|---|---|---|

| Source-to-view fidelity | Design projections, ODD publication, frame views, World Model projections and a_c interpretation use Faithful projection | Authoritative source and publication selectors, domain completeness, approximation policy, invalidators |
| Currentness and re-entry | Product triage, carrier reuse, design reconstruction and profile continuation apply Foundations workspace/context | Frozen-subject selection, causal-event requirements, release immutability, actual concurrency protocols |
| Evaluation and disposition | Use one result record and exact predicate; remove repeated generic tests from consumer checklists | Original reason-sensitive domain outcomes, declared coverage, independent assessment, reserved authority |

The reconstruction removes a duplicate deciding body only after every unique
condition has a successor owner and the consuming operation binds it. Keep
useful source anchors as domain entry points where they still carry a complete
binding; a bare route or new introductory table does not conserve a clause.
Application checklists select owners instead of maintaining copies of their
rules. A unique policy may remain unchanged when no shared contract expresses
its distinction. The full predecessor map retains all obligations and authorised
dispositions; no obligation is removed to achieve a text-size target.

## Qualification design

The iteration 3 evidence boundary is `qualification/C3/iteration-3/`.
Historical C3 and synthesis artifacts retain their original subjects. Current
entry points select the current inventory and interpretation explicitly.

[The determination account](../qualification/C3/iteration-3/RULE_ACCOUNT.md)
and its [machine-readable inventory](../qualification/C3/iteration-3/DETERMINATIONS.json)
bind the current source owners, arguments, consumers and unrepresented clauses.
They are construction evidence, not additional law or acceptance. The account
distinguishes predicates, definitions or domain data, consumer bindings and
non-deciding locators. Available source whose conditions have not yet been
decomposed remains unfinished interpretation work. It is neither an unknown
source nor a new assessment predicate, and supplies no permission.

Qualification combines source-first challenge cases with mechanical
reproduction. Source cases include legal distinct implementations, omitted
conditions, unsupported source projections, valid failures beside uncovered
coverage, stale basis, uncertain effects, bounded Markov claims and domain
exceptions. Fixed oracles are specified before model execution. A finite model
can check only its declared interpretation; it cannot establish source
completeness or authenticate its own premises. Model/source disagreements are
retained and repaired at their owning layer.

Compare deciding operations with iteration 2 and the applicable rc.2 source:
which independently interpreted rules remain, which have become one owner,
which domain choices survive, and what a cold actor actually acquires. Report
raw bytes, measured execution and comparison limits separately. Neither a
smaller file nor checker success proves whole-method improvement.

Ordinary entry must locate sufficient current law and material inputs through
its actual supported basis. Source-only trials do not claim an installed v3
release. A final independent source review covers the changed owners and
bindings; per-member acceptance and the unselected categorical work item remain
separate until their conditions are met.

## Dependency and proof order

1. Shared state/context, result and conditional construction contracts.
2. Core deciding families and profiles using those owners.
3. Source-bound ordinary entry frames, formal interpretation and distribution.
4. Frozen logical reviews and fresh actual-use trials with independent oracles.
5. Repairs over affected dependency closure, then a frozen review return.

A core-owner change affects every consumer even if its bytes do not change.
Historical judgments are not promoted to the new cut. The predecessor frontier
is checked row by row for coverage and meaning; automated route checks establish
coordinates only. Categorical hypotheses remain hypotheses unless their objects,
arrows, equality, identity, composition and preservation are demonstrated.
