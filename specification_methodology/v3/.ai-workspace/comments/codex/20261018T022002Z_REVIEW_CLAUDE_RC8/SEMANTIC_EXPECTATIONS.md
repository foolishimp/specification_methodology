## Predictions to challenge

1. Repeated implementation errors under a sufficient, unambiguous design
   remain repairable as implementation errors. Repetition alone proves no
   defect in Product meaning, specification, or design.
2. A demonstrated missing design consequence stops downstream construction
   at the relevant design gate. A demonstrated meaning conflict re-enters
   the authority owning that meaning. A cause that remains unknown cannot
   silently acquire either determination.
3. An observed difference may come from implementation, design, Product
   meaning, the test oracle, inadequate observations, or several of these.
   Each operative route requires enough evidence to distinguish the relevant
   cause and preserves unresolved dependencies.
4. Difficulty writing an acceptance scenario is evidence needing diagnosis.
   Missing specification, incomplete environment knowledge, a faulty test
   harness, and an unsupported interface are materially different cases.
   Failure of an executed scenario does not itself select upstream repair.
5. A wrong-Product escalation requires an attributable mismatch with the
   accepted Product or Intent. A valid Product with a faulty realization
   cannot be redefined to excuse its failed implementation or test oracle.
6. Local consumers, governing diagnosis and result rules, and changed
   foundation-law or argument rows carry compatible obligations. A consumer
   cannot discharge a law by naming it while retaining a contradictory
   unconditional route in its surrounding text.
7. Causal diagnosis grants no effect, acceptance, or release authority.
   A qualified finding remains distinct from an accepted repair, and
   independent assessment remains required wherever the owning law requires
   it. The review can establish only bounded source agreement or a concrete
   source-backed counterexample; it cannot establish C3 acceptance.
