**Counterexample.** The requirement and justified expected answer are 10;
the implementation actually returns 10; a faulty decoder records 1; the
scenario reports FAIL. Actual behavior and expected outcome agree. The
reported failure establishes an observational discrepancy until the
instrument is checked, not the actual disagreement asserted at :62-64.
If the decoder has not yet been checked, actual disagreement remains
unknown instead of established.

This is distinct from a wrong oracle: if actual output is 10 and the
expected answer is wrongly 11, their disagreement is real even though it
does not establish Product nonconformance. RC8 explicitly preserves the
wrong-oracle alternative and handles that case correctly.
