## Required Distinctions

1. **Observation sufficiency is predicate-relative.** Common
   `requirements/ASSURANCE.md:39-56` requires the truth of the selected predicate
   to be constant within each observation class. It does not require the
   observation to identify every subject. For domain `{1,2,3,4}`, predicate
   `even(n)` and supported observation `n mod 2`, the observation suffices even
   though 1/3 and 2/4 remain observationally identical. A constant observation
   concealing even/odd cases cannot support a sound complete decision; it needs
   further evidence, justified narrower scope or an indeterminate judgment.
   Unknown instrument fidelity does not establish satisfaction merely because
   the idealized observation map would suffice. Universal soundness covers all
   admitted conclusions; a sound checker may sometimes return indeterminate.
2. **One core carries material decisions.** Common
   `requirements/README.md:155-176` requires each evaluation or decision outcome
   to return through the core, with its predicate, domain outcome and conditions
   that select exactly one core value. Action data may remain ordinary data.
   Calling an output data or a state does not exempt a material refusal,
   admission, authority, closure or disposition determination. A later
   evaluation does not retroactively supply a missing result for an initial
   decision, and candidate availability does not establish its fitness.
3. **Initial no-action cases remain distinguishable.** Start with no preceding
   action and no closure to reuse. Examine (a) a known unsupported desired
   capability under a known catalogue, (b) unknown catalogue/applicability facts,
   (c) a task outside the declared scope, (d) a known absence of grant, (e) unknown
   grant conditions, (f) established completion and (g) unestablished completion.
   Each material determination must supply its declared predicate and appropriate
   core result; missing information must not be converted to absence, permission
   or completion. Acquiring more evidence or requesting re-entry may be lawful
   selected actions without making the desired substantive action admissible.
   The review will assess the candidate's chosen predicates and mapping rather
   than impose an unowned output label.
4. **Consumers preserve owners.** Common `requirements/README.md:133-151` and
   `75-87` require direct consumers, function tables, frames and formal/derived
   views to preserve the repaired permissions, conditions, exceptions and
   unknowns. Relevant acquisition routes must supply the missing facts; source
   hashes and structural checks alone do not prove semantic fidelity. A declared
   historical or deferred fragment retains its limited scope.
5. **Evidence retains its supported scope.** Common
   `requirements/ASSURANCE.md:58-70,97-129` requires assertion provenance,
   execution provenance and change-dependent applicability. Source-first case
   judgments precede reading author outcomes. Author tests or favorable reports
   can establish their execution/record facts but do not decide semantic
   correctness. This review's conclusion is bounded by the material paths
   actually acquired.
