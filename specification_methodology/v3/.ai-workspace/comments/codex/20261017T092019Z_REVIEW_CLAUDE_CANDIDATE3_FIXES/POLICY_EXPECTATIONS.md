## Cases fixed before candidate inspection

1. **Necessary mutable carrier.** The host materially requires mutation, the
   necessity is justified, the design confines it to explicit transitions and
   declares the effect/authority boundaries. The functional preference must
   leave this owning exception usable. A summary or procedure cannot prohibit
   this permitted case.
2. **Unjustified mutation.** Mere convenience, an unspecified mutable design,
   or mutation with hidden transitions/effects does not acquire the exception.
   Repairing an absolute summary cannot erase the actual preference or the
   explicit-boundary obligations. Unproved material necessity remains unknown
   rather than automatically accepted.
3. **No necessity.** A supported ordinary functional design remains governed
   by the predecessor's actual modal force. A preference cannot silently become
   an absolute ban, or disappear into unqualified latitude.
4. **Third local rebuild without selected Design recurrence family.** Inspect
   the predecessor ticket/design owners before classifying this case. If the
   predecessor imposes a generic recurrence policy, the successor must make
   that same decision available at a generic owner, with its actual trigger,
   counted condition, exceptions and modal force. Optional-profile nonadoption
   cannot erase a generic obligation. If it is an optional profile addition,
   nonadoption cannot silently activate it.
5. **Adopted Design recurrence family.** Any genuinely additional policy applies
   only on its established adoption condition. Its consumer names its owner
   and supplies necessary domain bindings; a profile cannot be its own generic
   fallback or send the determination back to its referring consumer.
6. **Recurrence negatives and boundaries.** Compare an isolated failure, a
   repeat caused by missing local technique, a repeat exposing missing method
   authority, repeated boundary repairs, an unsupported cause, and any stated
   first/second/third-rebuild threshold. Preserve discretionary versus required
   actions, the material distinction between causal recurrence and mere count,
   and every conditional stop or escalation.
7. **Affected consumers and evidence.** Trace TICKETS, DESIGN_MODULE, PROFILES,
   the family argument and operational procedure consumers. The policy's
   owner/consumer mapping must agree with the source. Once source conclusions
   are fixed, inspect changed accounts/proposal standing if material. A changed
   interpretation does not inherit an incompatible earlier accepted judgment.
