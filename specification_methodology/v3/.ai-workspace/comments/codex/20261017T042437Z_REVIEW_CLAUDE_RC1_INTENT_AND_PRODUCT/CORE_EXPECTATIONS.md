## Intended observable decisions

1. **Construction must connect action to the selected destination.** A is the bound current state, with material unknowns; Z is a predicate over acceptable outcomes. The method must distinguish an action's lawfulness, invariant preservation, useful progress, completion and acceptance, while allowing multiple adequate realised states and strategies. Source: `INTENT.md:5-13,21-23`; `PRODUCT.md:66-82`; `requirements/README.md:103-118`.
2. **Foundations must govern actual consumers.** The ten pillar contributions supply shared deciding contracts, with their premises, derived consequences and additional policy distinguished. Shared content needs one owning locus, and ordinary construction/evaluation consumers must use it. Generic decisions cannot require an optional profile. A conceptual correspondence or a preamble pointing at foundations does not meet this relationship. Source: `PRODUCT.md:35-52,84-105`; `requirements/README.md:38-73,133-153`; `PRINCIPLES.md:7-104`.
3. **Results must preserve what was evaluated.** There is one core result algebra; every material domain decision binds its predicate, original domain result, conditions and exactly one core meaning. Known failure, missing coverage, undecidable evidence, nonperformance and unknown effects must remain distinct through joins. Availability does not establish fitness; assessment does not grant acceptance. Source: `PRODUCT.md:127-138`; `requirements/README.md:155-176`; `PRINCIPLES.md:43-77`.
4. **The action loop must diagnose the cause before choosing its owner.** A bug and a feature enter the same delivery process. A discrepancy can arise from implementation, governing requirement/design, prediction or observation. Diagnosis may remain unresolved; a prediction error alone does not falsify the required outcome, and changing Z cannot establish the old Z was achieved. Severity and priority have different meanings. Source: `PRODUCT.md:72-80`; `requirements/README.md:107-116`.
5. **Recovery must use current reality without erasing valid progress.** An actor can discard and rebuild context from the persistent workspace and authoritative external observations. Missing acquired material is not absent material. Reuse binds material dependency validity; unchanged artifact bytes alone do not establish unchanged applicability. Unknown effects are resolved enough before dependent action. No universal reset, full-history replay, hierarchy or role sequence follows from finite context. Source: `INTENT.md:47-53`; `PRODUCT.md:54-64`; `requirements/README.md:89-101,111-114`; `PRINCIPLES.md:55-65`.
6. **Preservation requires the whole relation and composition boundary.** A claim about successful steps alone says nothing about refusal/interruption or uncertain effects. Postconditions must satisfy next preconditions or require acquisition of the gap. Concurrency and alternatives need their own compatibility; local approximation tolerances need an end-to-end bound. Source: `requirements/ASSURANCE.md:7-23`; `requirements/README.md:109,178-195`.
7. **A check must justify the source predicate and its observations.** A necessary condition is not a sufficient test of the obligation. Each assertion has source/interpretation provenance; each execution has subject/checker/input/observation provenance. Equal observations with opposite obligation truth defeat a sound complete decision. A favourable test run or source digest alone cannot prove oracle fidelity or instrument adequacy. Source: `requirements/ASSURANCE.md:25-70`.
8. **Assurance must retain shared error and change impact.** Two implementations or checks can agree because they share an incorrect assumption. Independence cannot be inferred from model/team diversity. Numeric assurance needs supported probabilities; qualitative assurance remains available. Targeted reassessment considers transitive material validity dependencies, new obligations and mandatory gates; absent dependency edges establish nothing. Valid old evidence can remain applicable on a new subject through an explicit preservation relation. Source: `requirements/ASSURANCE.md:58-121`.
9. **Formal and empirical claims must retain their limits.** A formula is not its discharge, structural validation is not semantic fidelity, and a bounded use case is not universal proof. Context/attention fit, ordinary usability, actual reduction and quantitative quality claims need appropriate evidence. Deferred complete a_c coverage, remaining actual-use sets and compressions are reported as selected scope limits, not fresh baseline defects. Source: `PRODUCT.md:313-321,414-427`; `requirements/README.md:68-87`; `requirements/ASSURANCE.md:123-131`; activation's owner-scope record.
