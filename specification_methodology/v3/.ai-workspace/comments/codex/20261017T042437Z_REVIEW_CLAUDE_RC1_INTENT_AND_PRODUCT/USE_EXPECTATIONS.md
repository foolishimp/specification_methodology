## Decisions ordinary consumers must be able to make

1. **Bind the problem and its authority.** Entry resolves the selected Product, current A, required Z, applicable basis, subject and work authority. A requirement states what must hold; observation states what does hold; prediction proposes what an intervention may cause. A candidate version label supplies neither acceptance nor publication. Source: INTENT 5–17, 47–53; PRODUCT 66–82; requirements/README 38–56, 103–118.
2. **Choose a lawful sufficient frame and configuration.** A directly authorised bounded task may use one capable actor; peer or other arrangements may also be adequate. A frame supplies necessary dependencies, effects, authority, evidence and stop conditions without requiring a universal Executive/Worker/Reviewer sequence. Extra required independent assessment remains required. Capacity pressure cannot erase a material obligation. Source: PRODUCT 54–64, 197–207; requirements/README 89–101; conformance 39–53.
3. **Preserve human and mixed execution choices.** An adequate process using people, current records and direct observation is not rejected for lacking application code, event logs or complete history replay. A computational calculus and execution binding retain their actual operations, laws, effects and resource assumptions. An optional graph, world-model or operational profile cannot own an indispensable generic rule. Source: PRODUCT 84–113; requirements/README 120–151; conformance 20, 24, 45–59.
4. **Construct toward the selected outcome.** Decomposition, delegation and successive steps preserve outer obligations and compatible joins. A candidate produced, a property established, work completed and delivery accepted are separate determinations. The strategy can change within authority and budget without pretending that lawful activity, a local pass or budget exhaustion proves Z. Source: PRODUCT 66–82, 127–138; requirements/README 103–118, 155–176.
5. **Handle bugs through the same delivery loop.** Intake does not infer the repair layer from the word bug. Diagnosis distinguishes realisation defect, inadequate requirement/design, prediction error and inadequate observation. Severity describes consequence; priority is a resource-and-authority decision. A requirement correction preserves the old counterexample and follows the actual owner's re-entry rule. Source: INTENT 7–11; PRODUCT 72–80; requirements/README 105–116; conformance 74–82.
6. **Recover from reality.** On resumption, current bound workspace observations override stale summaries; a different observed state does not silently change the selected immutable basis. Partial valid work survives. Unknown external effects remain unknown until sufficiently resolved for the next dependent action. Evidence is reused only through supported applicability and validity conditions, not because an identifier or file bytes stayed equal. Source: INTENT 47–53; requirements/README 97–101, 111–118; ASSURANCE 97–121.
7. **Keep results and authority separate.** All material assessments and disposition determinations preserve their predicate and domain outcome through one core result system. Known failure survives missing coverage; nonperformance differs from undecidable evidence; uncertain effect differs from absent/completed effect. Recorded state alone neither evaluates compliance nor grants acceptance. A material assessment authenticates producer, subject, basis and evidence. Source: PRODUCT 127–138; requirements/README 101, 155–176; conformance 37–38, 60–64.
8. **Use sufficient assurance at acceptance and release.** An assertion's source derivation and observation sufficiency are distinct from executed-check provenance. A test pass does not establish its parent obligation if the asserted predicate is only necessary. Agreement does not remove shared error. Changed material dependencies select reassessment while preserving valid evidence and mandatory gates. Release and adoption require their own authority and exact subject, beyond writer completion or reviewer agreement. Source: INTENT 11, 53; PRODUCT 181–185, 213–228; ASSURANCE 25–121.
9. **Supply operative standalone laws at the actual consumers.** Ordinary README/plugin entry, work templates, ticket lifecycle, review handoff and release procedure must use their shared owners without adding rival decisions, removing exceptions or sending essential meaning to an unavailable predecessor. Glossary, guides and formats may supply scoped conventions, not extra method permissions. Source: PRODUCT 84–105, 358–380; requirements/README 38–87, 133–153.

## Independent source-derived cases

These cases precede candidate acquisition. They are desk traces, not actor trials.

- A human team changes a paper-based appointment procedure under one directly granted bounded task. It has current records and observations but no code or event stream. Determine definition, frame, effect grant, required assessment and acceptance without adopting a software profile.
- A peer team divides two independent document changes, while a third join must preserve a shared external obligation. Determine lawful configuration, authentic closed results and the remaining join without inventing a central Executive requirement.
- A task produces a candidate and passes its local tests, but the selected end-to-end feature fails. Preserve the outer failure and decide diagnosis/re-entry before completion or release.
- A bug report says a queue is too slow. The permitted latency requirement, an inaccurate queue model and a missing observation permit different diagnoses. The label alone chooses neither repair nor priority.
- A new actor resumes a partly written artifact. Its handover says nothing was sent; current external evidence shows the send might have occurred. Keep valid edits, resolve the effect before retry, and reopen the stale claim without resetting all history.
- An unchanged artifact's environment invalidates its prior evidence. Retain evidence for demonstrably independent obligations; reopen the affected result and preserve the counterexample.
- Assessment A is an authenticated failure and required B was never performed. The work record must retain failure A and its citations while showing missing B. A lifecycle label must not imply fitness.
- A release packet has correct version/hash fields and successful mechanical checks, but no justified assertion or release authority. Distinguish structural validity, conformance, acceptance, publication and adoption.
