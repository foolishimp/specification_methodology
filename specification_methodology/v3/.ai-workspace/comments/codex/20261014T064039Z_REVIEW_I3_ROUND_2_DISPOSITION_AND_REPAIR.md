## Concrete counterexamples for the successor review

1. A tenant writes “accepted” into its frontier without a ticket decision:
   no acceptance exists.
2. A member's local evidence is complete but the integrated review has not run:
   member scope can be evaluated; C3 closure remains open.
3. A 30 KB frame retains another 200 KB under a 40 KB context limit:
   it does not fit. Sequential acquisition needs a supported retained-context
   bound and preserves cumulative acquisition cost.
4. A member has no superseded rows: check that actual cone and record the false
   applicability of that conditional obligation; do not omit criterion 3.
5. A required criterion is falsified and another lacks evidence: preserve the
   known failure and missing coverage; no favourable summary supplies acceptance.
6. A common rule removes a repeated determination but creates unexplained
   acquisition growth against one comparator: retain the narrow elimination
   witness and withhold the broader improvement conclusion.
7. A reviewer reports a counterexample and later rechecks it: permitted. The same
   reviewer authors an adopted material policy: its judgment of that contribution
   cannot supply independent acceptance.
8. A new routing contract is drafted: the old admitted contract's bytes and digest
   remain exact; only a separately recorded successor admission starts new work.
