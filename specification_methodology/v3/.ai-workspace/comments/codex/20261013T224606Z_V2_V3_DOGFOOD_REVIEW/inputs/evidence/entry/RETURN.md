## Failures and causal limits

The candidate proposed compatibility fallbacks for unobserved external
deployments. This left old-name references and added a future removal condition
that the frozen oracle rejects. The continuation preserved that added policy
and repaired a discrepancy inside it. Both returns withheld closure and named
unresolved frame/admission obligations. These are observed candidate failures
against the oracle, not accepted completion. The phrase “anything that depends
on that name must keep working” also led the rc.2 actor to identify compatibility
as an unresolved owner choice. The boundary between task ambiguity and a method
defect requires adjudication; one stochastic observation does not resolve it.

The rc.2 actor exposed a definite harness binding defect: the supplied authentic
receipt verified the STDO construction definition, while that definition's
declared WHAT/HOW did not cover the disposable `workspace/`. It stopped at
PRODUCT_BASIS. An authentic installed builder receipt alone does not establish
subject applicability. The candidate actors also recorded this unresolved
binding, though they returned unapplied proposals. Consequently this trial does
not provide a matched construction comparison or admitted ordinary-entry pass.

The Executive assigned a separate repair: create a schema-valid disposable
fixture definition that binds the unchanged task and workspace, verify it with
the real installed tool, and rerun both methods on that basis. The successor
also receives a newly frozen candidate cut; changes between the two candidate
trials therefore cannot be attributed solely to the harness repair.
