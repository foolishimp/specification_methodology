## Source-first expectations

These expectations were fixed and returned to the Executive before candidate acquisition:

1. A distinct Product boundary carries a distinct WHAT and owner. Tenants, actors, folders and work subdivisions do not become Products through naming. A leaf is relative to a selected decomposition and may retain declared dependencies.
2. Standalone claims identify the local construction/evaluation/use outcome and dependency closure. They do not promise dependency-free operation or independent deployment. Recursive and shared constituents preserve identity, separate use coordinates and the outer outcome's proof obligations.
3. The existing edge carrier binds useful capability contracts, exact or explicitly moving bases, authority, effects, refusals, evidence and invalidation. The relation locates admitted authority; it creates none.
4. Durable consumption, authorized causal-cone inspection and implementation reachability are different relations. Grants do not propagate transitively or merge at a shared leaf. Adequate encapsulation need not be flattened into direct dependencies on every internal implementation.
5. Permission and context adequacy are separate predicates. Clean-room exclusions apply to derived and transitive information, prior exposure and actual acquisition channels. Owner-approved export also requires receiving-policy eligibility and the actor's actual grant; revocation cannot erase exposure.
6. The Skills witness must distinguish STDO's constitutional role, Skills' two tenants and a_c's provider role; preserve its supported native fragment, actual operator path and unsupported operations; and avoid creating grants or qualification from a declaration.
7. Replacement/refinement preserves material consumer obligations or enters an authorized successor relation. Change handling preserves exact historical evidence and supported reuse, while unknown impact remains unresolved. A cycle needs executable prerequisites rather than a named leader.
8. Future tests distinguish mechanical validity, semantic conformance and actual-use evidence. The tooling plan builds on the current supported contracts without pretending that arbitrary source prose is already admitted machine-readable authority.
