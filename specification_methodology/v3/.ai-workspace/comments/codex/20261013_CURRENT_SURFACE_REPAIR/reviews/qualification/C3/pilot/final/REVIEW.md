## Result: **PRESERVED.** The revised Recovery law carries the source churn relation exactly, with no gaps or extra scope.

**Evidence**

1. **Same wording.** RC2 (*Checkpoint, Continuation, Progress, And Churn*, paragraph 3) says: "Rejected or superseded construction is churn." The candidate (*Recovery law*, paragraph 1) has the same sentence, word for word. The subject is the same ("rejected or superseded construction"), and so are the predicate ("is churn") and the strength (an unconditional "is", not a "may" or a list of examples).

2. **The retry clause doesn't narrow or widen it.** "Repeating an unchanged failed relation without a supported causal change is a prohibited retry practice; it is not the definition of all churn." This prohibits a practice. It doesn't redefine churn, and it says outright that it is not the definition of churn. That stops a reader from treating "churn" as meaning only "retry", which was the likely cause of the earlier indeterminate finding. RC2 has no retry rule, so the prohibition is new material. But it sits next to the churn relation without changing what that relation covers.

3. **The neighbouring categories still separate it from progress.** In the candidate, progress is "accepted material movement". Preservation and repeated proof are "evidence, not new Product progress". Discovered distance "is not negative progress". So rejected or superseded construction can't be counted as progress. That matches both RC2's split between progress and churn and its falsifier: "rejected activity is reported as accepted Product progress".

**Scope:** I only checked this one relation. I'm not judging the other changes in the candidate. For example, "Discovery of previously hidden distance" became "Discovered distance", and RC2's "semantic categories, not a numeric progress algorithm" sentence was dropped. I also haven't judged acceptance or the corpus as a whole.
