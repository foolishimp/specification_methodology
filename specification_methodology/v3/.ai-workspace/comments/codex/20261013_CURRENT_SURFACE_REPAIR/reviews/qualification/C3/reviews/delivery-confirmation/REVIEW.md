## New findings

**N1. LIBRARY: composition-admission ownership attribution conflicts with README.**
- *Severity:* low-to-medium (discovery and attribution).
- *Location:* LIBRARY intro says "Frame Method owns evaluation and composition". Its § Composition law then states an admission rule: "S after T is admitted only when …".
- *Conflict:* the README Method-owners table assigns "transformation admission" to Design Module Method and only "frame composition" to the Reference Frame Method. FOUNDATIONS § Typed composition likewise routes the design obligation to `DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD#bounded-frame-conservation`.
- *Effect:* two realizing texts name different owners for the same determination. No duty is overridden, because member law governs and the library disclaims superseding. But a reader routed by the library may consult the wrong owner.
- *What it needs:* RFM and DMM, which were not supplied, to decide which attribution is correct.
- *Repair:* attribute the composition law to its actual owning clause, or narrow "composition" to "frame composition".

**N2. FOUNDATIONS: possible unconditional generic declaration duty.**
- *Severity:* low.
- *Location:* § Finite capacity: "Each activation declares the actor and resource envelope needed by its claim."
- *Problem:* it is unconditional and adds a per-activation declaration. Ticket's execution-contract minimum fields do not include it, and Ticket elsewhere disclaims extra records and ceremonies. It does not contradict Ticket, because Ticket's fields are an "at least" list. But if RFM frame laws do not already carry this duty, it is a new generic quota. LIBRARY has an equivalent guard ("logical contract, not a mandatory record schema"); FOUNDATIONS does not.
- *Repair:* add that guard, or cite the exact clause that owns the duty.

**N3. README: two operating sentences without an owner.**
- *Severity:* low.
- *Sentences:*
  - § Method owners: "A profile adds only its material domain relations. It cannot redefine generic meaning, authority or another Product's runtime." This generalizes the predecessor's Traversal-specific "without changing the calculus or selecting a consumer" to every profile.
  - § Distribution and entry: "acquire the sufficient responsibility frame … rather than the entire corpus".
- *Assessment:* both are plausibly restatements of the Baseline, RFM or SPEC_METHOD, but neither cites an owner.
- *Side issue:* POSTING now calls itself a "commentary profile" and carries STDO-UP-012, which is method-wide propagation law. UP-012 states rather than redefines authority, so there is no contradiction, but the README sentence makes the scope of "profile" ambiguous.
