## 5. Observations (no defects; severity is informational)
1. **Two result vocabularies.** The preamble ties §5E's cross-view evaluation to Library Evaluate. Library Evaluate returns satisfied / falsified / indeterminate / out_of_frame / invalid_basis, while §5E requires `pass` / `fail` / reasoned `not_applicable`. The Library resolves this itself: "Domain members determine their admitted outcome and disposition sets… does not supersede those meanings". The preamble's owning-clause sentence also covers it. There is no contradiction, but a reader has to rely on those precedence statements.
2. **Two notions of "total".** Foundations defines a "total control function" and names §8 as the owner of "closed design handling". §8's obligation is weaker and differently scoped: semantic functions "should be total or as close to total as… allow". The Foundations sentence is a definition, not a duty on semantic functions, so §8 governs.
3. **"Actual design grant" as a premise.** This could be misread as narrowing the method's scope, for example to exclude §5E retrospective review. It is a premise in an explanatory paragraph, and the owning clauses are unchanged, so they govern.
