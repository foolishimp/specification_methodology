## Orthogonal descriptions

Keep three accounts distinct. An algorithmic account specifies the selected
transformation. An informational account explains interpreted distinctions and
their preservation. A computational account realizes the operation over a
hardware and I/O architecture, with material resource costs. They are
simultaneous descriptions, not successive SDLC stages.

To assert a category, specify objects, typed arrows, identities and associative
composition. To assert a functor, show identity and composition preservation.
For a proposed categorical model, A names the algorithmic account, I the
interpreted informational account, and C_H the computational realization on a
declared architecture H. They name categories only after the required objects,
arrows and laws have been supplied. For a proposed span A -> I and A -> C_H,
conformance to Sigma_contract, semantic
equivalence and computational equivalence remain different claims. Categorical
equivalence additionally requires the appropriate quasi-inverse and natural
isomorphisms on a stated domain; an analogy or matching result is insufficient.

State sufficiency for a selected decision, statistical Markov properties,
conditional independence, a mathematical Markov category and a runtime monad
are separate claims. The method neither asserts nor rejects them universally.
Their domains, laws and falsifiers must be supplied before using them as proof.

## Agentic Construction Execution (`STDO-UP-020`)

When construction is delegated to a probabilistic or agentic worker, the
method governs the execution relation without prescribing the worker's model,
prompt, tools, internal search, process topology, repository layout, or
orchestration runtime.

The normative relation is:

```text
accepted governing basis
  -> bounded affected relation set
  -> selected computational relations
  -> delegated execution and decision authority
  -> construction and self-review
  -> independent live-surface assessment where required
  -> accept | local repair | re-enter | reject
  -> exact accepted checkpoint
  -> next already-authorized bounded action
```

### Sufficient Execution Intake

A bounded action begins from an authority basis sufficient to reconstruct its
governing relations without conversation history, commentary, prior-worker
memory, or implementation folklore. The intake identifies:

- the selected Product outcome, prerequisite, or admitted experiment;
- the bounded affected relation set and applicable governing invariants;
- accepted transformation-admission relations;
- selected computational or common-library dispositions;
- construction and assessment authority; and
- the conditions that require upstream re-entry.

Where `DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md` applies, `STDO-UP-017` bounded-frame
conservation, `STDO-UP-016` transformation-admission completeness,
`STDO-UP-018` invariant-reconstruction sufficiency, and `STDO-UP-019`
computational-realization projection supply these design inputs. This section
consumes those laws; it does not create a second design package.

### Bounded Causal Construction

The execution unit is the smallest coherent causal dependency cone capable of
delivering working behavior while preserving every affected governing
relation. It need not coincide with one function, file, module, ticket field,
or design document.

A seam whose far side can causally affect, or be affected by, the action is
inside the cone unless the basis establishes that it cannot participate.
Unrelated incompleteness outside the cone is recorded as repricing input and
does not block the bounded claim.

### Construction, Assessment, And Delegation

The constructor owns bounded implementation and self-review. Where independent
assessment supports promotion, the assessor evaluates the exact live code,
authority paths, proof, and installed subject rather than treating the
constructor's summary as sufficient evidence. This preserves the independent-
review relation; it does not prescribe an actor count. One actor may occupy
different roles across different claims when independence for the assessed
claim is preserved.

The governing authority relation declares whether a bounded proxy may accept
and advance routine actions. A proxy may accept preservation, require local
repair, reject a violating candidate, and advance to the next already-
authorized action. It cannot change Product meaning, requirements, governing
authority, or accepted design. Such a change re-enters at its owning relation.

Human ceremony is not required at every transition when the governing basis,
affected cone, decision envelope, and re-entry conditions are already
accepted. This delegation does not weaken direct-human acceptance where
Product or release law requires it.

When one capable context has sufficient existing work authority, exact intake
and operation grant, it may enter directly as constructor or Writer. No
Executive activation is required solely to appoint that Writer. The applicable
frame configuration selects coordination when material contexts, authority,
capability or required independence need it; a role or frame name alone does
not create that need. Direct entry preserves execution-contract admission,
the smallest lawful carrier, required assessment and reserved decisions. It
permits bounded work and condition-based result recording, not self-acceptance
at a boundary owned elsewhere.

One bounded operation grant may cover its declared construction, status and
checkpoint sequence. Record actual role transitions in the existing work
carrier; a status change alone requires neither a new grant nor rehashing an
unchanged subject. A material change to the operation, subject, territory,
authority or validity conditions still requires the affected binding to be
resolved before effect. Executive mutation restrictions remain applicable.

### Proportional Disposition

Assessment returns one of these semantic dispositions:

| Disposition | Condition |
|---|---|
| `accept` | the candidate closes its declared cone and preserves every affected governing relation |
| `local_repair` | implementation or proof is locally defective while the governing relation remains coherent |
| `re_enter` | Product, requirement, identity, lifecycle, authority, public contract, or accepted design must change |
| `reject` | the candidate violates a governing relation or leaves an undisposed competing truth path |

Global correctness means conserving every global relation affected by the
bounded action. It does not require proving the whole Product inside every
local action.

Repeated rejection against one unchanged boundary requires reassessment of the
frame altitude, causal cone, common components, design-versus-code placement,
and authority sufficiency. The method sets no rejection-count threshold,
mandatory review-round count, or review state machine; it prohibits repeating
an unchanged failed construction relation as if iteration alone could close it.

### Transition Evidence And Proof Cadence

A reviewable transition identifies the exact candidate, affected relations,
changed realization paths, authority paths added or removed, retained and open
seams, focused proof, any required integration proof, explicit non-changes, and
the assessor disposition. Consumers choose the representation. Transition
evidence is a read model and does not become specification, design, or
admission authority.

Construction uses the cheapest focused proof capable of falsifying the active
relation. Expensive whole-candidate qualification occurs at a declared
candidate boundary rather than after every local edit, unless the affected
risk makes whole-candidate proof necessary.

A durable or reconstructable authority claim is not established solely by a
same-process proof when loss of incidental process state is a material
counterexample. Proof must destroy or exclude the incidental authority and
compare reconstructed semantic outcomes.

### Checkpoint, Continuation, Progress, And Churn

Acceptance binds one exact checkpoint. The next already-authorized bounded
action may begin without renewed human approval; acceptance does not widen the
Product outcome or affected relation set.

A preservation checkpoint may retain unfinished or unaccepted work under an
existing exact operation grant. Identify its parent or predecessor, included
territory, concurrent-writer disposition and durable evidence location. It is
neither acceptance, promotion nor publication. A proposed restart identifies
why repair of retained work is insufficient, which obligations and evidence
survive, and which causal assumptions change; reconstructability alone does
not select reconstruction.

Accepted material Product movement is progress. Prerequisite work names the
Product obligation it enables. Preservation and repeated proof are evidence,
not new progress. Rejected or superseded construction is churn. Discovery of
previously hidden distance revises the forecast but is not itself negative
implementation progress. Paperwork, code volume, test count, commit count, and
review count are not Product progress by themselves.

These are semantic categories, not a numeric progress algorithm. Consumers may
derive their own progress and churn projections from them.

This protocol is false if execution proceeds from unreconstructable context,
if a constructor's report substitutes for required independent live evidence,
if a bounded proxy changes a governing relation outside its delegation, if
unrelated incompleteness blocks a closed causal cone, if a same-process test is
used to prove materially cross-process authority, or if rejected activity is
reported as accepted Product progress.

The following steps describe the coordinated branch selected under Engagement
Applicability. Direct Worker engagement preserves the same sufficient intake,
work-result algebra, closure conditions and owner boundaries without inserting
Executive appointment or a review that no applicable condition requires.

### 1. Existing Authority Selects The Basis

Goals, Product, requirements, accepted design, ticket, release, or an admitted

owner. The Product-owned Project Reference-Frame Basis projects that decision.

### 2. Executive Activates Worker

In the coordinated branch, Executive applies `REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md`
activation to the `STDO-UP-020` sufficient execution intake. The activation identifies:

- selected complete STDO and project authority;
- exact governed outcome and checkpoint;
- affected causal relation set and governing invariants;
- selected computational relations and specialist frames;
- testing claims, exact subjects, frame topology, material combination


The Worker does not send a candidate directly into an implicitly active
Reviewer. It stops after returning the result.

For `refused`, `incomplete`, or `re_entry_requested`, the Executive verifies
the exact basis and applies the existing authority relation. It records the
applicable `STDO-UP-020` disposition or reactivates work only when the current

accepted progress merely because work occurred.

missing, bound to another subject, or contradicted by a material frame result.

consumes the exact candidate, Worker self-review, transition evidence, and any mandatory
specialist-frame results. It applies `STDO-UP-020` disposition within its
decision envelope.

Self-review remains Worker evidence. The disposition does not relabel it as
independent.

When independent review is required, the Executive activates the Reviewer with:

- exact candidate and governing basis;
- exact claim, composition boundary, population, scope, and exclusions;
- applicable STDO and project authority;
- required evidence and ordinary assurance path;
- required user-acceptance, end-to-end, integration, unit, combinatorial, and

- the Product-selected severity scale and exact affected Product claims;
- Reviewer capability and independence envelope; and
- invalidation and stop conditions.

The candidate is frozen before material reference exposure where

required by `STDO-UP-007`, `STDO-UP-020`, and the claim.

# STDO 3 Intent

## Purpose

Construct a coherent specification methodology that lets finite actors do useful work against explicit purpose, meaning, constraints and evidence.

Recover existing STDO principles and ABG lessons, then distinguish general foundations, the particular Spec Method, and domain or operational realisations. Reduce repeated interpretation and competing semantic ownership while retaining material distinctions.

This scope constructs v3.0.0 of the STDO Specification Methodology, continuing the existing product line; `specification_methodology/v3/` is its construction directory, beside the 2.* source project at `specification_methodology/v2/`. The predecessor is the installed v2.5.1-rc.2 cut. Under that cut's successor law (STDO-UP-015) every predecessor claim receives one disposition: conserved, superseded by a named relation, intentionally removed by Product authority, or not applicable with a reason. This Product further requires that the successor stand alone: every conserved claim is restated in the successor's own text, so the successor is complete without the predecessor remaining operative (the standalone successor requirement in [requirements/README.md](requirements/README.md#standalone-successor-requirement)). The construction order is: establish the foundational axioms first, then restate the full predecessor corpus on them. The corpus may be as large as the law requires. A finite actor is never activated on the corpus; it is activated on a projected reference frame, which is the unit that fits one bounded activation. A route to the predecessor is not restatement.

## Direction

The methodology is implemented as documents. A common Intent, Product and specification contract supports independent tenant designs, prose organisations and representations.

Intent → Product → Specification → Realisation recurs where a material constituent needs its own bounded account. Orthogonal algorithmic, informational and computational interpretations do not become consecutive workflow stages.

Deep review of principles precedes capability selection. Observations from later actual use determine lawful repair and re-entry. Incomplete knowledge, failure and interruption must have truthful dispositions within selected authority.

## Standing

Jim owns Product direction and common acceptance. This is a working source-project definition. Installed STDO 2.5.1 RC2 governs its construction; no STDO 3 release, consumer adoption or universal mathematical claim is established.

# Working Common Principles

Status: common WHAT for the principles layer. Each tenant's C1 realisation of these questions is reviewed and accepted separately (see C1 Disposition); this file is not replaced by a merged tenant basis and is not STDO 3 law.

These six commitments organise the independent deep attempts. Existing STDO source provides recovery inputs; a related clause does not prove that a proposed generalisation is equivalent or universally true. Tenants may challenge the formulation, dependency graph, independence or completeness.

## P1 Contextual Interpretation

**Bounded formulation.** Distinguish a subject, its representation and an interpretation under a declared context. An interpretation must explain what distinctions its consumer can rely on. An algorithmic description can make such interpretation operationally explicit.

**Standing.** Interpretation/representation separation is recovered; the general algorithmic account is proposed. The claim that all meaning must be algorithmically representable remains an unresolved hypothesis.

**Premises/dependencies.** A selected subject, context, interpretation contract and adequacy relation. Any proposed derivation from finite capacity must expose how these are supplied and which premises it uses.

**Recovery.** [Interpretation and models](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#interpretation-and-models), [AC-017 fidelity](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-017-interpretation-fidelity) and [AC-018 separation](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-018-structural-and-semantic-separation).

**Review question/falsifier.** Can structurally identical representations have materially different interpretations? A proposed universal algorithmic claim fails or narrows when its domain or fidelity criterion cannot be defined or an admissible meaning is unrepresented.

## P2 Purpose And Adequacy

**Bounded formulation.** A selected outcome U defines adequacy for a designed transformation. Its interfaces inherit material obligations from the transformation they serve; the outcome's owner fixes reserved value choices.

**Standing.** Purpose and outcome conservation are recovered methodological commitments. The proposed derivation or independence of purpose relative to finite capacity remains a C1 question. Deriving every interface obligation from an explicit outer contract is a proposed formulation to test.

**Premises/dependencies.** A selected purpose, observable completion condition and governing constraints; interpretation explains what an observation means.

**Recovery.** [Product outcome conservation](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#product-outcome-conservation-stdo-up-013) and [reconstruction sufficiency](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#reconstruction-litmus).

**Review question/falsifier.** Does a proposed interface add an obligation without a material outcome or authority relation? Can local green checks substitute for the selected outcome?

## P3 Identity, State And Representation

**Bounded formulation.** An object O has a declared identity, boundary and material relations. Its identity can survive admissible state or encoding changes. Current state, historical observations and representation identity remain separate.

**Standing.** Scoped identity and recursive Product distinctions are recovered. The general object formulation is proposed; stronger Markov-boundary and state-sufficiency claims remain hypotheses.

**Premises/dependencies.** Declared type/context, scope, owner, basis and admissible change relation. A proposed derivation of identity from attention limits must expose its premises; any independence claim must be argued.

**Recovery.** [AC-002 scoped identity](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-002-scoped-identity), [recursive Product taxonomy](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#recursive-product-taxonomy) and [Markov object](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md#markov-object).

**Review question/falsifier.** Does equal content establish identity, or does an unchanged identity falsely establish current content? State sufficiency, conditional independence, temporal Markov properties and mathematical Markov-category structure are separate claims with separate assumptions.

## P4 Typed Composition And Conservation

**Bounded formulation.** Two transformations compose when their typed contracts, standing and preconditions are compatible. Composition preserves the outer contract's material obligations, original identity, known failures, uncovered requirements, uncertainty and authority. Shared machinery supplies instances without merging responsibilities.

**Standing.** Typed composition and bounded conservation are recovered. Their general information-transformation formulation is proposed; any claimed broader derivation must expose its premises and domain.

**Premises/dependencies.** A sufficient outer contract, selected source/destination types and explicit permitted loss or variation. Compatibility and materiality are assumptions requiring support.

**Recovery.** [AC-016 typed composition](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-016-typed-composition) and [bounded frame conservation](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#bounded-frame-conservation-stdo-up-017).

**Review question/falsifier.** Can a locally valid join erase failure A or uncovered obligation B? Can a reference become inaccessible? Conserving the required relation does not require copying immutable bodies or rebuilding history at each handoff.

## P5 Finite Capacity And Graceful Total Handling

**Bounded formulation.** Actors have finite context, attention, time and capability. A selected control model has explicit dispositions for unknown or inadequate conditions: refine, recover, yield, re-enter or terminate. It can continue within the current contract when supported.

**Standing.** Finite attention, material sufficiency, closed results and practical totality are recovered. The unified graceful-recovery account is a proposed generalisation. C1 assesses whether proposed derivations of purpose, identity, composition or decision authority follow, require additional premises or leave independent commitments. This setup selects no outcome.

**Premises/dependencies.** A selected actor/capability envelope, sufficient material relations, permitted actions and observable outcome distinctions. Complete world knowledge and guaranteed termination of every problem are not assumptions.

**Recovery.** [RF-002 finite attention](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-002-finite-attention), [RF-003 material sufficiency](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-003-material-sufficiency), [RF-012 closed results](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-012-closed-results), [RF-014 capability fit](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-014-capability-fit) and [Totality Rule](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md#8-totality-rule).

**Review question/falsifier.** Can insufficient context cause silent omission or an unhandled state? Restart is another iteration over current persistent work state: recover applicable progress and observe changed dependencies. Logs supply audit/observability and may support recovery evidence; no universal obligation requires every history body at every step.

## P6 Justified Claims And Separate Authority

**Bounded formulation.** Definitions, assumptions, observations, candidate judgments and accepted claims have distinct standing. Evidence supports a selected claim on a basis; authority permits operations or disposes acceptance within declared scope.

**Standing.** Claim/evidence/projection and judgment/authority separation are recovered commitments. Their foundational derivation or claimed independence remains to be argued. A general epistemic account must preserve those distinctions.

**Premises/dependencies.** Exact subject, claim, evidence, evaluator, applicable independence and actual owner authority. Capability, evidence shape or a reviewer result cannot mint an operation or acceptance grant.

**Recovery.** [Proof target identity and adequacy](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#proof-target-identity-and-adequacy-stdo-up-001), [semantic/evidence/projection separation](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#semantic-evidence-and-projection-separation-stdo-up-008), [RF-006 authority](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-006-authority-conservation), [RF-007 separation](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-007-semantic-evidence-and-verdict-separation) and [AC-014 judgment](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-014-judgment-separation).

**Review question/falsifier.** Does a successful parse, valid map or historical judgment acquire unsupported semantic acceptance or authority? Can an unchanged judgment rename its subject?

### Authority

The method distinguishes:

- **frame-set authority**: the human authority that declares or accepts the
  project frame set;
- **semantic authority**: the owner of meaning for a material relation;
- **evaluation authority**: the role permitted to perform an evaluation;
- **operation authority**: the owner-issued grant to construct, transform,
  admit, publish, or mutate a material subject where an evaluation performs
  such an operation;
- **decision authority**: the role permitted to accept, reject, or otherwise
  dispose its result; and
- **actor identity**: the human, agent, tool, or process performing work.

These relations may coincide in a lawful case. They never collapse by default.
Declaring, selecting, activating, or executing a frame does not grant semantic,
operation, or decision authority. A read-only evaluation declares no operation
authority. An effectful evaluation inherits the exact operation grant from its
existing owner; the frame does not create that grant.

Human authority may act directly or through an explicitly admitted bounded
proxy where the governing identity and authority law permits it. Proxy identity
and human-authority identity remain distinct.

### Computed Classification And Treatment

Intake supplies one reusable classification result rather than requiring each
role to reconstruct the same facts. `C` denotes reproducible facts and declared
rule application; `J` denotes residual semantic or materiality judgment; `O`
denotes a ruling reserved to its actual owner. These annotations grant no
authority and introduce no calculus kind, runtime, classifier interface, or
mandatory document.

The explicitly selected source, worksite, or qualification capability produces
C over the exact request and desired outcome, known targets and effects,
operation grant and work lifetime, Product Definition and governing basis,
baseline and available candidate, declared relations, accepted design and
trace bindings, evidence, and applicable rules. Its output identifies the
producer and procedure revision, inputs, result, scope, source routes,
reproduction relation, unknowns and invalidators. Missing candidate or evidence
is an input state, not a claim that nothing changed.

The computed result must expose:

1. **Authority traversal.** Follow explicit source, ownership, dependency,
   composition and projection declarations from the affected targets and
   effects. Identify each reached layer and owner, its exact basis and
   changed/unchanged/unknown relation, the traversed edges, and the unresolved
   frontier. Include root, authority and grant changes even when Public bytes
   are equal. An absent edge does not prove irrelevance; undeclared or
   semantically uncertain materiality remains J.
2. **Public delta.** Enumerate the complete relevant serialized member universe
   under its owning declaration and applicable projection, including helper and
   generated dependencies. Report added and removed identities and every
   retained member with changed typed value or bytes, with exact before/after
   values. A rename is removal plus addition unless its owner supplies an
   identity-preserving relation. Missing candidate, incomplete enumeration,
   ambiguous identity or unavailable projection leaves the affected delta
   unknown; membership equality alone proves no value equality.
3. **Accepted-design coverage.** Join changed bytes and relations to explicit
   accepted scope, traces and acceptance evidence. Distinguish covered,
   missing, stale, ambiguous and outside-scope evidence. A filename, listed
   artifact or digest alone establishes no accepted coverage. Current trace
   coverage is a computed fact; residual semantic sufficiency remains J.
4. **Rule-determined treatment.** Apply only declared rules with sufficient
   input facts, recording their owners, matched predicates and consequences.
   Expose the smallest lawful re-entry, next action, applicable evidence or
   assessment, reserved ruling and carrier lifetime, with any conflict or
   missing premise still unresolved. A label or filename cannot select the
   route. When rules settle the question, invent no residual judgment.

A Public delta or other nonconformance does not itself require changing the
governing contract. If that contract remains sufficient for the authorized
outcome, reject or locally repair the candidate within its grant. Re-enter
the actual governing owner when the explicitly selected desired outcome
requires changed meaning or an applicable assessment establishes governing
insufficiency. C determines that consequence only from sufficient declared
premises; unresolved intended meaning or necessity is J and any reserved
decision remains O. Candidate behavior cannot author its own target law.

Unknown treatment withholds dependent reliance or promotion, not unrelated
authorized work. An existing bounded construction or evidence grant may permit
the cheapest useful discriminator, including obtaining the first candidate,
without asserting that the candidate is conformant. It carries its own exact subject,
territory, operations and stop conditions. Update the affected C result before
dependent effects or promotion rely on newly available evidence. Correct a
mistaken route at its smallest affected owner; preserve the original cause,
attempted subject, observed effect state and valid progress. Retry requires
the applicable outcome, authority and safe-recovery conditions; an unknown
effect state cannot be silently treated as no effect.

A new role, conversation or resumption reuses valid C after checking exact
applicability. Changed request, candidate, basis, owner/root relation,
projection, design trace/acceptance, rule, configuration or material evidence
invalidates its affected claims before reliance. Preserve predecessor and
successor identities, changed inputs, surviving facts and affected J
dependencies; recompute the wider frontier where independence is unproved.
`TICKET_METHOD.md` owns the existing carrier and recorded-J/O continuity rules.
