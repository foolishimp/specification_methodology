# Codex C2 Capability Proposal

Status: tenant proposal for Jim's C2 selection. C1 is accepted for this tenant;
the common capability and conformance contract is not yet accepted. Construction
uses installed STDO 2.5.1 RC2, not these proposed method documents.

Read the [accepted C1 realization](PRINCIPLES.md), [conformance cases](../qualification/C2_CASES.md)
and [build plan](BUILD_PLAN.md) together. The exact inputs and output pins belong
in the [return](../qualification/C2_RETURN.md). These eight capabilities are
observable abilities, not eight stages, APIs, actors or mandatory document types.

## Proposed Audience And Outcome

The initial audience proposed here is a human Product owner directing capable AI
builders and reviewers on software and method-document work. Actors can read
source, maintain a persistent workspace and use ordinary development tools;
their context, access, independence and operational capabilities are declared
for each selected task. No knowledge of an unpublished validator or the author's
private reasoning is assumed. Broader audiences require their own adequacy cases.

The proposed outcome is that a fresh capable actor can recover a bounded work
contract from published method documents, construct useful output, obtain the
applicable assessment, and continue, recover or conclude truthfully. Distinct
tenant HOWs may satisfy that outcome. An owner still selects value choices,
capabilities and acceptance intervals; the method does not choose them from
resource capacity or evidence alone.

Audience and criteria remain proposals pending Jim's selection under
[Product Checkpoints](../../../specification/PRODUCT.md#checkpoints).

## Capability Contracts

### CAP-01 Interpret A Governed Subject

Requiring commitments: P1, P3 and P6; C1 D1 and D7. Recover the selected subject,
identity, current state, representation, interpretation context and governing
basis. Distinguish definitions, assumptions, observations, derived conclusions
and hypotheses. When a material interpretation or source is unresolved, expose
that uncertainty rather than supplying a guessed premise.

Adequate outcome: another actor can identify what the representation means and
which claim it supports. Equal shape, content or a successful parse cannot
establish equal meaning, current identity or authority. Q01, Q02 and Q20 test
these distinctions. Categorical equivalence, universal algorithmic capture,
statistical Markov closure and HoG monad laws are not required premises.

### CAP-02 Specify And Compose Distinct Realizations

Requiring commitments: P1, P2, P3 and P4; C1 D2, D3 and D8. Derive interfaces and
implementation obligations from a selected outer outcome. Keep Intent, Product,
common WHAT, tenant HOW and instance binding distinct. Allow different designs
when they satisfy the same material obligations. Derive architectural identities
through the applicable design model rather than turning each observed verb into
another public operation.

Adequate outcome: two different HOWs can be judged against the same common
expectations, and an incompatible typed join is detected even if both sides
pass local checks. Shared machinery preserves responsibility, basis, freshness
and authority. Optional software, graph, world-model and work-record profiles
add declared domain meaning; example-specific content stays in the example.
Q03, Q04, Q09 and Q26 cover this capability. A graph engine is one realization.

### CAP-03 Obtain Sufficient Bounded Context

Requiring commitments: P1, P4 and P5; C1 D3 and D5. Select the material relation
set needed for the current decision. Recover sufficient source or a supported
projection within the selected actor's capability envelope. If it does not fit,
use a supported refinement or capability; if none is supported, return a truthful
limit. Coupled invariants must be resolved sufficiently together somewhere.

Adequate outcome: obligations and material seams survive a successful refinement;
an infeasible refinement cannot obtain success by omission. Immutable references
can replace bodies when identity, authenticity, availability and needed
completeness remain sufficient. A digest alone is insufficient. Q05-Q08 cover
fit, refinement, refusal and inaccessible evidence. No inventory cross product,
whole-corpus load, particular retrieval system or universal numeric cap follows.

### CAP-04 Construct, Observe And Evaluate Truthfully

Requiring commitments: P2, P3, P4 and P6; C1 D4, D5 and D7. Perform admitted work,
observe its actual effects and evaluate the exact resulting claim. Keep authored
output, observed evidence, assessment, aggregation and operation authority
distinguishable. Authenticate applicable assessments before combining them.

Adequate outcome: actual defects produce source-linked non-green results;
uncertainty stays explicit; known failures and uncovered required coverage
survive conjunction. Every planned assessment slot is authenticated. Q10-Q13
and Q21 distinguish a failed assessment plus uncovered coverage from a missing
planned judgment, and both from structural green. Applicable independence is
preserved; a reviewer result grants neither repair effects nor owner acceptance.

### CAP-05 Continue, Recover And Conclude From Current State

Requiring commitments: P2, P3, P5 and P6; C1 D5 and D6. Evaluate the current
persistent work state, applicable support and material external effects, then
choose the next already-authorized action. Distinguish material advance,
prerequisite readiness, preservation, regression and churn. Continue while
adequate progress is possible; recover or yield according to the diagnosed
cause. Suspend, withdraw and complete have different consequences.

Adequate outcome: interruption changes the actor occurrence without discarding
valid authored progress. Changed dependencies reopen affected support; independent
valid work remains usable. An uncertain external effect cannot be treated as
absent or blindly repeated. Completion closes only the selected outcome instance
and interval; exhausted authority cannot be renewed by a run or ticket label.
Q14-Q18 cover these obligations. Restart is iteration over current state, not
a universal idempotence or guaranteed-termination theorem. Logs can support
observability and recovery without becoming every actor's working memory.

### CAP-06 Publish A Method Fresh Actors Can Use

Requiring commitments: P1, P2, P4 and P5; C1 D8 and the selected audience/task
contract. Publish stable source routes, usable entry points and responsibility
bound views. One canonical owner supplies each meaning; views import that meaning
instead of creating competing obligations. Preserve useful independent overlap
when it supplies a different evaluation, evidence altitude or capability fit.

Adequate outcome: fresh actors recover and use the selected work contract without
hidden author knowledge. Targeted retrieval, onboarding, effectiveness and
resource improvement receive separate evidence. Q19, Q20 and Q22 cover these
claims. A compact index alone does not demonstrate successful use or effectiveness.
No new receipt hierarchy, actor count or universal workflow template is required.

### CAP-07 Diagnose And Repair At The Responsible Owner

Requiring commitments: P2, P4 and P6; C1 D3 and D7. Observe a reproducible shortfall,
triangulate its material cause and identify the owner of the required change.
Distinguish an implementation defect, insufficient design, source interpretation,
fixture/projection fault, effect uncertainty and governing-contract change.
Repair only inside admitted authority; re-enter the owning governing relation
when its meaning must change. Re-evaluate affected paths before claiming recovery.

Adequate outcome: a failed integration does not cause speculative generic law or
application strategy to enter the framework. An unresolved or plural cause remains
explicit. A changed governing relation produces a named successor with applicable
work retained and affected claims reopened. Q23 and Q25 cover diagnosis and
successor disposition. Framework execution may observe a domain worker without
absorbing that worker's internal solution strategy.

### CAP-08 Qualify Claims And Representations Separately

Requiring commitments: P1, P3, P4 and P6; C1 D7. Bind qualification to the exact
candidate, intended use, evidence and dependency contract. Distinguish mechanical
structure, semantic fidelity, formal derivation, practical adequacy, acceptance,
publication and adoption. Preserve assurance provenance; an author repair cannot
retroactively pass the original frozen attempt.

Adequate outcome: actual-use evidence supports only its selected claims. An a_c
program is checked against a verified selected mechanical dependency; independent
source-first fidelity and practical trials remain separate. A neutral finite
subject can test one calculus fragment without proving full STDO or full-calculus
validity. Q21, Q24 and Q25 cover qualification boundaries. Neither this proposal
nor a tool's success selects a release lineage, dependency Install or publication.

## Existing Law And Realization Work

Context sufficiency, typed composition, continuation, closed results, authority
separation and proportionality already have owners in RC2. The realization work
is a usable publication, sufficient bounded context, clear current-state handling
and demonstrated adequacy for a selected audience and workload. The accepted
[C1 source recovery](PRINCIPLES.md#exact-source-recovery) supplies the deciding
clauses; new documentary arrangement does not make these new axioms.

The [6 October coverage matrix](../../../../specification_methodology/.ai-workspace/comments/codex/20261006T015451Z_MATRIX_AI_DEVELOPMENT_METHODS_STDO_251_BASELINE.md)
distinguishes F27 structured source recovery, F28 onboarding, F29 effectiveness
and F30 resource measurement. Its 4/12 predecessor score is dated documented
coverage, not current effectiveness or a STDO 3 score. All four need distinct
claim-appropriate evidence before improvement is asserted.

The eight capabilities cover this proposed scope. They are not a clause-complete
successor claim. [UP-015](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/RELEASE_METHOD.md#successor-baseline-conservation-stdo-up-015)
needs the owner-selected predecessor, lineage and claim dispositions before
complete successor qualification. RC2 as builder does not itself select that
migration relation.
