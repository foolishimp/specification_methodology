# Foundations Of Governed Transformation

Standing: first documentary implementation slice of the Codex tenant. The
selected [capabilities](../design/C2_CAPABILITIES.md) are realized here; installed
STDO 2.5.1 RC2 governs construction. This document is not a released builder law.

## F-01 Purpose, Interpretation And Object

U is an owner-selected purpose and adequacy condition. O is an object with a
scoped identity, boundary, current state and material relations. κ is the
interpretation context: selected definitions, basis and admissible observations.
Σ is the common WHAT that a realization must satisfy. It is distinct from an
a_c signature and from the worker's chosen internal HOW.

Information is a representation interpreted under κ. Equal representations can
have different meanings; an unchanged object identity can have changed state.
An operational algorithm can specify an interpretation within its domain.
Universal algorithmic capture of all meaning remains a separate hypothesis.

An observation is adequate relative to a selected claim and U/Σ. Evidence and
capacity do not select purpose or grant decision authority. A decision owner
resolves reserved value choices; a worker does not infer them from local success.

## F-02 WHAT And Its Realizations

Intent expresses direction; Product owns outcomes and boundaries; specification
states the common obligations; a realization supplies a conforming HOW. A
material constituent can require its own bounded account of those relations.
Their physical documents and worker decomposition need not be identical.

A sufficient WHAT admits different conforming HOWs. Reconstruction means the
consumer can recover the material contract without hidden major decisions. It
does not require a unique algorithm or equality to the incumbent implementation.
Interfaces derive their material obligations from the transformation and outcome
they serve. A use-case verb alone does not establish an architectural identity.

An example, test fixture or domain solution remains in its declared scope.
Shared mechanism does not promote its particulars into generic method behavior.

## F-03 Typed Composition

Let T_i relate admitted input X_i to output X_(i+1), with a selected contract,
permitted effects and explicit non-success outcomes. T_(i+1) can consume a
successful result only when its input domain, interpretation, subject/basis and
preconditions are compatible. Equal field shapes do not establish that join.

If each local implication preserves the applicable invariants, all material
seams are covered and the next relation's premises hold, induction establishes
the outer successful-state predicate. These are premises of the composition
claim. Finite capacity can motivate refinement; it does not prove that a
faithful feasible refinement exists.

Materiality is claim-relative: a relation is material when changing it can alter
adequacy, evidence, permissible effects or acceptance. Required coupled
relations must be resolved sufficiently together somewhere. Obligations outside
the current responsibility need not be copied into every local input.

## F-04 Conservation Through Change

A governed transformation preserves the selected outer contract's material
identity, meaning, applicable support, known failures, uncovered obligations,
uncertainty and authority. A change in those governing relations requires
re-entry at their owner before dependent promotion. Current applicability and
historical truth are separate: an old failure remains historical when a changed
candidate requires new assessment.

Conservation concerns a recoverable relation. A reference can replace a body
when its owner, exact selector, authenticity, availability and required
completeness are sufficient for the consumer. A digest alone supplies none of
the missing availability or semantic sufficiency. Copying the entire history
does not establish conservation either.

## F-05 Bounded Actors And Open Conditions

Actors have finite context, attention, time, access and capability. Obtain the
material context needed for the selected responsibility. If it cannot fit, use
a supported refinement, another supported capability or a truthful non-completion
disposition. Silent omission weakens the outer contract.

A finite control account handles its represented classes and an explicit
unknown-pressure class. Graceful total handling preserves unresolved conditions
and provides a lawful recovery, yield, re-entry or termination route. It is not
a theorem of complete world knowledge, universally correct observation or
termination of every external call. Supervision and effect observability have
their own operational assumptions.

## F-06 Work, Judgment And Authority

Construction changes a candidate or work state. Observation supplies evidence.
Evaluation judges an exact claim. Conjunction combines compatible applicable
judgments. Disposition chooses a permitted next action. These responsibilities
may share machinery while retaining their semantic owners and independence.

The [Work Method](WORK_METHOD.md) owns binding, acquisition, execution and next
action. The [Evidence Method](EVIDENCE_METHOD.md) owns evaluation and conjunction.
The definitions are reused by instances; a differently named instance is not
automatically a new primitive, authority or mandatory workflow stage.

## F-07 Orthogonal Accounts And Proof Limits

Algorithmic description, informational interpretation and computational
realization over architecture H are simultaneous views. A task's computational
account may include I/O, CPU, addressable memory and time when relevant to its
claim. Metrics need a domain, workload, state and architecture; shorter
representation does not itself prove lower total work or equivalent behavior.

The [accepted C1 account](../design/PRINCIPLES.md#the-categorical-proposal-a-defined-fragment-and-open-extensions)
defines a deterministic path/function/costed-function fragment with preserved
typed composition. Distinct algorithmic paths can have the same informational
function and different costs; that chosen interpretation is not faithful and
does not establish equivalence. This implementation imports that supported
argument rather than claiming categories from names alone.

Statistical Markov sufficiency, mathematical Markov-category structure,
universal algorithmic meaning, real hardware-cost functors and HoG monadic
binding remain separately scoped research claims. Operational state sufficiency
for a selected decision is a bounded claim, not any of those stronger theorems.

## Recovery Owners

F-01/F-02 recover contextual identity, interpretation and the constitutional
chain; F-03/F-04 recover typed composition and bounded-frame conservation;
F-05/F-06 recover finite attention, closed results and judgment/authority
separation. Exact deciding source routes and the conditional derivations are in
[C1 source recovery](../design/PRINCIPLES.md#exact-source-recovery). These duties
are reorganized implementation, not additional axioms derived from finiteness.
