## Why the green checks did not close these defects

The common failure pattern is that owners state the relevant qualifications,
but downstream consumers fail to preserve them in decisions or acquisition.
F1 loses a profile condition. F2 retains inherited instructions after the owner
was broadened. F3 retains relation labels and conditions as data while its
acquisition behavior treats their references alike.

Digests, reproducible generation and structural validation cannot decide those
fidelity questions. The review supports the recorded 30/30 assertions per arm;
those results remain evidence for their selected tasks. Its trace audit reports
that none of the matched actors acquired the interpretation. They cannot prove
native-index usefulness. The prior construction return already left broad
reduction and practical compression open; this review now supplies concrete
repair targets inside those open obligations.

The scope also matters in the favourable direction: this feedback supplies no
new confirmed contradiction in the examined result/effect and recovery core.
Preserve that work and its applicable evidence. The unchanged tests do not need
a blanket rerun; changed dependencies require their own applicability decision.
