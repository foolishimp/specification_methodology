## 1. Current reality, retained knowledge and bounded state

The common text preserves three different coordinates: the material subject, justified observations or knowledge of it, and the actor's acquired representation. Product's description of A includes both material state and justified knowledge (`PRODUCT.md:68-72`), while its earlier context-management consumer distinguishes workspace state from each actor's account and observes external state through its owning interfaces (`PRODUCT.md:56-64`). The normative frame requirement binds exact snapshots and external systems, says absence from the view is not absence from the subject, and forbids silently rebinding the governing basis when contrary evidence appears (`requirements/README.md:93-101`). Intent's resumption account consumes the same rule (`INTENT.md:47-53`).

The new observation-sufficiency rule reinforces this relation. If two admissible cases have identical acquired observations but different truth values for the predicate, those observations cannot establish a sound complete decision (`requirements/ASSURANCE.md:39-56`). This does not assert a temporal Markov law. P3 separates state sufficiency, conditional independence, temporal Markov properties and Markov-category structure (`requirements/PRINCIPLES.md:33-41`); Product's pillar and conformance case preserve that limit (`PRODUCT.md:42`; `conformance/README.md:26`).

Discriminator: a cached summary says a grant is valid, while the bound current system has revoked it; alternatively, two externally different states expose the same local observation. A view-driven process would act on the cached success or declare the unseen change absent. This source requires reacquisition or an unresolved conclusion, preserves the exact authority boundary, and keeps the stronger state-sufficiency claim unproved. Its existing consumers test that distinction in conformance `README.md:35,50-53,82,92` and CAPABILITIES `10,15-19`.

## 5. Bugs, causal diagnosis and lawful repair

The construction model gives bugs and new work one intake/delivery mechanism while separating the possible faulty relation: realisation, requirement/design, predictive model or available observation (`PRODUCT.md:72-80`; `requirements/README.md:110-111`). The model does not select a repair from the word “bug,” and severity remains consequence rather than priority or disposition. The older Product refinement chain sends work to the earliest affected description (`PRODUCT.md:94-101`); C3 reserves changed predecessor meaning and common changes to their owner and separates Reviewer severity from Executive decisions (`224-226`). Cross-synthesis likewise classifies a divergence before changing the common requirement (`303`).

ASSURANCE now constrains those existing consumers: expected answers have assertion provenance; agreement through a common wrong model cannot validate that model; a changed requirement or refinement reopens its affected expectations and evidence rather than rewriting the historical observation (`requirements/ASSURANCE.md:62-70,99-121`). Product explicitly applies those owners to testing, triage, recovery and release (`PRODUCT.md:80`).

Discriminator: a feature meets its normative requirement but misses a model's performance prediction; conversely, a model predicts an observed outcome accurately while a required feature is absent. The shared process cannot infer compliance from prediction accuracy or infer a defective realisation from prediction error alone. A third case weakens Z until the existing output passes: authority and re-entry are still required and the old failure remains. Conformance tests all three distinctions (`conformance/README.md:76-78,98-99`), and the integrated path must carry one feature through observation, diagnosis and lawful continuation or closure (`115-117`).
