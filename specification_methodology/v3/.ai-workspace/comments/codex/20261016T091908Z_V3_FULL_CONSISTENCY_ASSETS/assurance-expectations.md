## Expected consistency

1. The universal A-to-Z construction account must state enough structure to distinguish a lawful transformation from a merely asserted association. Algebraic, informational, and computational descriptions should name compatible subjects, domains, composition and identity laws, and observable consequences without claiming a theorem from notation alone.
2. Domain bindings must provide concrete interpretation and deciding constraints. A universal abstraction must not silently inherit software-specific predicates or permit a domain to erase a common obligation.
3. Preservation must identify the preserved proposition or structure, the transformation and assumptions, and the deciding observer or proof. Exact equality, approximation and refinement must not be interchanged. Approximation needs a metric/order or otherwise decidable relation, tolerance, domain and composition rule; excluded detail must not later support a stronger claim.
4. Assurance must participate in construction decisions. For each new requirement, there should be a named producer, bound evidence, a consuming decision and a fail-closed consequence. A declaration, graph path, test count or mutually agreeing pair is insufficient by itself.
5. Assertion provenance must distinguish source authority, derived assertion, evidence, review and acceptance. Circular support, self-attestation and stale evidence must remain visible and unable to manufacture closure.
6. Parallel and mutual assurance must preserve subject identity, separate independent judgments from coordination, account for shared failure modes and reconcile conflicting results without averaging away a falsifier.
7. Risk and incremental testing must select the smallest sufficient reassessment while preserving changed-dependency invalidation. Material unknown risk, missing oracle, untested new paths and unsupported evidence reuse must not become implicit success.
8. Acceptance and C3 requirements must consume the above obligations at the appropriate boundary. Unbuilt implementation, unavailable qualification evidence and source inconsistency are different findings; the review must not demand implementation proof merely to accept an adequate source requirement.
