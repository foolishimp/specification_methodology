# Evaluation of feedback on the Codex v3 candidate

Standing: author response to the supplied external review, not an author-independent assessment of my own realization. Review subject: Codex commit `b4310c0c05664a8c1a546859b0571b5bafa37de9`. Builder/comparison basis: installed STDO v2.5.1-rc.2, manifest SHA256 `3d860ff4c1746f06ac25295a9e205cffb8e7725869615ac77cf2304b70ff2782`. All 71 recorded installed member hashes were checked in this evaluation and match. No peer realization was inspected.

## Verdict

Accept the three material findings. The candidate improves explanation and provides bounded reading views. It has not established the foundation-based reconstruction required by the common outcome or comparative practical superiority. The first finding is a delivery gap; the others concern ordinary use and the evidence for improvement.

My previous “ready for review” means frozen and inspectable. It does not mean the C3 outcome is satisfied. Closing the last authority and recovery findings does not close these broader relations.

## Finding 1: foundation-based reconstruction is incomplete

The common [Intent](../../../specification/INTENT.md#purpose), at line 9 of the subject commit, requires establishing foundational axioms first and restating the full predecessor corpus on them. [Product](../../../specification/PRODUCT.md#c3-construction-contract), lines 120–130, also describes foundations, a particular Spec Method and importing profiles.

The implemented [Foundations](../../../build_tenants/codex/successor/standards/FOUNDATIONS.md), lines 7–14, explicitly abstracts recurring relations from detailed owners and creates no derivation provenance or constitutional specialization relationship. [Transformation Library](../../../build_tenants/codex/successor/standards/TRANSFORMATION_LIBRARY.md), lines 3–15, likewise supplies explanatory families without a specialization relationship. The [Design ownership table](../../../build_tenants/codex/design/DESIGN.md#ownership) calls these governing distinctions and formal refinements, but the implemented declarations do not establish that relationship. Correspondence labels are insufficient evidence of it.

This supports the review's high conceptual shortfall. Literal conservation keeps existing determinations available and is useful migration evidence; retaining every nonblank line of fifteen original bodies does not itself reorganize generic meaning or show why particular rules follow from foundations. The stronger outcome remains open.

One qualification matters: foundational reconstruction need not deduce every rule from finite capacity alone, change every permissible outcome or centralize every authority. Purpose, normative choices, domain assumptions and grants require explicit premises. A rule can be a justified specialization or a declared additional commitment. A documented conditional relationship is the missing result; a universal theorem or an invented mandatory six-stage process would overshoot it.

## Finding 2: ordinary acquisition is weakly integrated

The five views are discoverable from the new [standards index](../../../build_tenants/codex/successor/standards/README.md#foundations-and-shared-library). They are genuine additions, not absent routes. However, [stdo-work](../../../build_tenants/codex/successor/plugins/spec/skills/stdo-work/SKILL.md), including its ordinary intake and construction paths, is byte-identical to the installed predecessor: SHA256 `39d9f88af4b574c4e30f45adc3b19ed324c25ae453d7359112af71aa9d4d4d8f`. It does not explicitly route its relevant responsibilities to those views.

The review's moderate usability finding is therefore supported. Provision of a view and successful use after a Host selects it do not establish reliable ordinary discovery. Repair should let the selected work and applicable frame basis locate sufficient material through the usual entry, retaining exact source acquisition for coupled obligations. It should not hard-code the same view as sufficient for every task or add another mandatory workflow.

## Finding 3: comparative effectiveness is unproved

The [return's bounded-use section](../../../build_tenants/codex/qualification/C3/RETURN.md#bounded-actual-use) accurately distinguishes actual document continuation and supplied-material exercises from native execution. Its adequate fixture baselines are not runs under the predecessor method. The chosen views and substantial task expectations were supplied by the Host.

These exercises support selected comprehension and defect detection. They do not compare ordinary discovery, construction quality, acquired context, elapsed time or rework between versions. The finite a_c interpretation, manifest checks, exact pins and literal excerpt checks establish their declared predicates; they cannot substitute for this comparison. Recorded construction churn is evidence of a problem in this construction, not a controlled estimate of v3 versus v2.5.1 performance.

Comparative superiority is therefore indeterminate. It is not disproved. Unchanged deciding behavior can coexist with a better architecture or acquisition path, but those improvements must be shown on their own claims.

## Root cause in this realization

The construction emphasized source conservation and local summary fidelity. As source-owner counterexamples arrived, repairs constrained the introduced layer to an explanatory overlay. That protected existing distinctions but left the predecessor architecture as the source of deciding meaning. The [execution record](../../../build_tenants/codex/qualification/C3/EXECUTION.md#execution-record) records this movement explicitly, including the selection of non-deciding standing and the acknowledged convergence failure.

The missing evaluation was whether the resulting foundation/library relationship still met the common outcome. Narrow repaired claims and structural completeness became the focus while that architectural relation stayed unresolved. My realization fell short of the requested foundation-based reconstruction; more hashes, review counts or preserved text cannot repair that failure.

## Bounded repair direction

Keep the conserved corpus, exact owner distinctions, explanatory vocabulary and useful views. Change the own-tenant HOW before seeking broader acceptance:

1. For one work/recovery chain, state the premises and their standing, the general transformation contract, and the Spec Method/profile specialization. Identify which obligations follow conditionally and which need independent purpose, domain or authority premises. Use the existing design and method text rather than creating a new compulsory ledger.
2. Give shared generic meaning one clear owning definition and make specializations import it without merging distinct responsibilities, authority or result algebras. Equivalent deciding behavior is compatible with this repair.
3. Connect the ordinary work entry to adequate material acquisition for that chain, including resolution of coupled obligations outside the selected view.
4. Run matched predecessor and successor tasks through their ordinary entries with equivalent subjects, available evidence, actor capability and grants. Give independent assessors the same outcome criteria. Measure only costs material to the claim and retain uncertainty; one matched thread supplies bounded evidence, not universal superiority.

A suitable first discriminator is resuming interrupted work where one external effect is uncertain. It exercises current-state observation, source acquisition, retained applicable work, authority, truthful unknown handling and lawful continuation in one chain. The comparison should require neither private author memory nor a Host-selected view that bypasses the entry behavior being tested.

This evaluation selects no implementation effect, common change, merge, acceptance or release. These are repair directions within the existing target, not new common requirements. The final independent authority/recovery confirmation remains applicable at its frozen subject; this feedback supplies no new counterexample reopening it.
