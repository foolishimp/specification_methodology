## Consolidated conclusion

The three Codex findings remain actionable. Claude adds concrete predicate,
argument, coverage, consumer and record defects. Its totals of unargued units,
hollow arguments and unmapped sets are not additional repair counts. The work
is organized by the source-owned relation that fails, with affected consumers,
interpretations and evidence repaired together.

The strongest common cause is a consumer losing a condition or distinction
owned upstream: optional applicability becomes mandatory, an admission
predicate becomes an outcome predicate, source arguments become generic account
text, or a provenance route becomes mandatory acquisition. The repair preserves
one owner and its conditions through the consuming paths. It adds neither a
second result system nor a mandatory record for each heading or helper.

## Repair packages

### RP1: applicability and generic method consumers

**Confirmed findings:** Codex F1 and F2; Claude CB1-10; relevant consumer and
packaging parts of CB4-F2 and CB4-F4.

Codex F1 is a concrete fidelity failure. The construction paragraph in
`S/authority_compressions/spec_method.compressed.md:357` requires DMM inputs,
while its owner at `S/SPEC_METHOD.md:1807` requires them only where DMM applies.
A lawful non-DMM agentic construction receives a prerequisite that the raw
owner does not impose. Preserve the applicability condition and generic intake.

Codex F2 is the same defect class in procedures. The feature-change procedure
at `S/SPEC_METHOD.md:3272` requires Code, Events, Projection and Delta despite
the generic owner permitting human and mixed realization and direct
observation. Bootstrap at `:3284` requires `requirements/` despite the
Product-bound, layout-neutral authority contract. Restate those consumers using
the existing generic owners; retain software and directory choices only in
their declared specialization.

The Design opening labels the whole member an adopted profile at
`S/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md:5`, while Spec imports generic operational and
unit-proof law at `S/SPEC_METHOD.md:1746` and `:2546`. Declare which responsibilities
are generic method law and which apply through profile adoption. Do not make
all Design implementation policy mandatory or remove required generic law.

The bounded Executive owner also has stale consumers:
`S/STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md:1764` and
`S/templates/PROJECT_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASIS_TEMPLATE.md:95` still say
"complete Product view". Bind those consumers to the sufficient bounded view.

**Exit evidence:** source-first positive, prohibited and unresolved cases for
non-DMM construction, human/mixed realization, alternate requirement locations,
generic Design law versus profile adoption, and bounded Executive acquisition.
Rebuild affected summaries and exact views; pins alone are not fidelity proof.
