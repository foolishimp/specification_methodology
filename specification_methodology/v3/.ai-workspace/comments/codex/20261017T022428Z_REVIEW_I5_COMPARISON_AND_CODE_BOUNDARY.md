## What code is being built

The [Product boundary](../../../specification/PRODUCT.md#product-boundary) defines STDO v3 as method documents and their a_c interpretation. It expressly excludes application runtimes such as ABG and HoG. The agent or person applies the method; software automates particular assembly, acquisition and checking operations.

It does not assess whether the method or a construction outcome is correct. |
| Qualification instruments | Account/package checks, adverse controls, measurement scripts, trial helpers and executable finite assurance witnesses exist. | They test their stated mechanical or finite-model claims. They are not a general A-to-Z execution engine. |

For Codex, the frozen `successor/` package contains one Python file, `interpretation/bindings.py`; most executable support lives under qualification. Historical scripts and fixtures also remain in the tenant, so a whole-tenant code-file count would exaggerate the size of a new framework. Claude similarly has assembly, frame-generation and qualification code.

Thus there is real executable tooling. The main deliverable remains a methodology and its formal interpretation. Calling the latter a “whole-corpus interpretation” must not imply that a new autonomous Executive/Worker/Reviewer runtime has been implemented or that the full corpus has been semantically proved.

## Corrections to the comparison

1. **“No nonauthor conservation reading” is too absolute.** The quoted review itself records 13 amendment readings and seven comparisons on Codex, while the later reciprocal review adds a bounded matched-claim inspection. Full required conservation remains open; zero reading is not the correct description.
2. **Claude's claimed fidelity lead is scope-dependent.** Its 318 consumer rows are Writer traces, with 46 sampled by a nonauthor according to its own comparison. Its 140 historical conservation readings covered owning text and left consumers unread. The reciprocal review found 37 stale locus occurrences across 32 current index rows, including nine with historical conserved judgments. Those facts do not erase valid historical evidence, but they require current applicability before claiming a current fidelity advantage.
3. **The 389 count is real, but heterogeneous.** The readback found 198 constraint rows, 147 definition/data rows, 32 consumer-binding rows and 12 locators without a named consumer. These are accounting units, not 389 independently proved defective laws. Actual rule consumers or a supported scope of absence still need assessment.
4. **Reduction and version standing are separate issues.** Codex has positive sampled governing joins and measured serialization reduction; complete task-level reduction and practical benefit remain unproved. The unreleased `3.0.0` label merits clearer package-entry wording. It is not evidence that the methodology is semantically inferior or that a release occurred.
5. **The actual-use trial is necessary for its criterion, not sufficient for qualification.** It cannot supply missing predecessor-conservation judgments, replacement decisions, complete interpretation fidelity or integrated coverage. Conversely, those open acceptance obligations do not prevent a clearly scoped recommendation about which current construction basis to retain.

Continuing both lines to fixed comparison subjects is reasonable if a matched experiment remains the owner's objective. Open-ended further borrowing is not itself qualification progress: every change creates a new subject and may require evidence to be checked again. The bounded next step is to close selected material defects, freeze the subjects, execute the shared tasks and complete the other owed assessments and decisions.
