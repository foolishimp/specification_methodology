### 5. Material corrections and honest limits

**F1 — Claude: the non-faithfulness assertion lacks a necessary premise.** FOUNDATIONS lines 187–195 asserts that its interpretation S is not faithful merely from a free category on typed primitives, selected carrier sets/total functions and weighted computational functions. Those ingredients alone do not imply non-faithfulness.

Counterexample: take two objects X and Y with only one primitive p: X → Y, no cycles, and the induced identities. Interpret X as {0}, Y as {1}, p as the unique function 0 ↦ 1 and each identity as its identity function. Every hom-set has at most one arrow, so S is injective on every hom-set: S is faithful. Nothing in the selected Claude premise excludes this case. This counterexample concerns the asserted implication; it does not prove equivalence of the intended categories.

Smallest correction: supply the intended primitives/interpretation and a pair of distinct parallel paths with equal interpretations, or make non-faithfulness conditional on such a witness. Codex supplies that kind of witness for its particular construction. Owners: Claude FOUNDATIONS, with affected a_c claim/fidelity re-evaluation. No broader algebra redesign follows from this finding. Witnesses: Claude [FOUNDATIONS](../../../build_tenants/claude/standards/FOUNDATIONS.md), lines 181–195; Codex [constructed example](20261013_CURRENT_SURFACE_REPAIR/historical-drafts/design/PRINCIPLES.md), lines 204–236; common PRINCIPLES lines 85–87.
