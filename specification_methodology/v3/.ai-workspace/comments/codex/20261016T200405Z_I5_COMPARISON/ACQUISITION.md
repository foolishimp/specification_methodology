## Ordinary acquisition versus curated frames

Both candidates distinguish governing owner text, optional derived aids, profile choices and provenance. C's standards README and work skill require direct owner fallback when a view is insufficient. L ACTIVATION `787–825` permits a current sufficient derived surface, otherwise requiring exact owner sections, actual subject and coupled obligations; `845–855` explicitly requires every material owner for a cross-owner question. L `878–895` neither demands proof that no undiscovered relation exists nor permits an automatic corpus dump. These are useful acquisition rules, but their effectiveness still needs task use.

The required material is the selected deciding law plus the actual subject, constraints, grants and material dependencies. Conditional material becomes required when its applicability predicate holds: for example, change-impact/evidence reuse on resumption; a selected profile; or a domain-specific design obligation. Provenance, historical disposition tables and explanatory examples do not become governing prerequisites solely because they are linked. An unrelated domain profile may be irrelevant to the five tasks, while a supposedly peripheral owner becomes relevant if it decides a coupled obligation. Neither a short packet nor an index label decides that question by itself.

## Recommendation and conditions that change it

Continue from **Codex as the better currently usable construction basis**, subject to the other review cones and a subsequent integrated decision. The reasons within this cone are the reusable governing application contract, generic realization vocabulary, successful current interpretation acquisition and source-pinned conditional routes. Its less explicit task discovery and the conditional follow-up burden remain real limitations.

Preserve Claude's task-to-owner index and named owner separation as substantive design evidence. Claude could become the stronger basis if its current interpretation is completed and source-faithful, and matched ordinary task use shows its clearer routes reduce wrong decisions, reconstruction or irrelevant acquisition without losing obligations. Completion alone would remove a maturity disadvantage; it would not by itself settle comparative usefulness. Conversely, a material Codex source/interpretation counterexample, or matched use showing consumers systematically miss applicable conditional routes, would weaken or reverse the present recommendation. Demonstrated simpler governing ownership with the same semantics would matter more than a smaller artifact.

The next deciding evidence is matched ordinary task use at declared host/context boundaries and the remaining conservation/replacement/claim-accounting work. No expansion to all native selectors was needed to close this bounded review.
