# Independent comparison expectations

These expectations derive from ratified common C3-I5-06, the owner's construction principles and the installed builder; they precede the fresh reviewers' candidate acquisition. They are questions to falsify, not expected winning text. The coordinating Codex context authored one candidate and has read both entry/status records; it does not supply independent acceptance evidence. Fresh reviewers disclose any source contribution and distinguish source judgment, mechanical observation, actual-use evidence and owner decision.

## Shared material cases

1. Current reality and restart. A worker's belief or old result conflicts with the persisted workspace. Observe the current subject; preserve still-valid work, distinguish uncertain external effects, and recover without automatically replaying them. The workspace itself need not be a complete observation of external reality.
2. Bug/change feedback. A reported failure can arise from realization, requirement/design, prediction or observation. Diagnosis selects the responsible owner and re-entry; severity, priority and repair authority remain different. New features and bugs enter the existing work chain; a bug label neither grants a spec change nor invents another carrier.
3. Results and composition. An authenticated failure for A coexists with missing assessment for B. Failure and citations survive; not-performed differs from an undecidable performed assessment. Domain values/refinements and effect uncertainty preserve their deciding meaning in the one result system.
4. Assertion soundness and observation. A necessary property can pass while the intended obligation fails. Observationally indistinguishable states cannot support a claimed distinction. Assertions derive from source obligations independently of the implementation path to the degree claimed; execution provenance is separate.
5. Mutual assurance. Correlated checks agreeing with a wrong shared model cannot validate that model. Quantified assurance states probability/conditioning/dependence support. Multiple actors, paths or matching answers alone do not manufacture independence.
6. Change and targeted reassessment. A changed upstream obligation affects its dependent realizations, assertions and observations. Determine a supported impact cover; retain justified unaffected evidence on its original subject; preserve known failures, unknown influence, new obligations and mandatory gates. Avoid unconditional whole-suite repetition and unsupported skipping alike.
7. Human/mixed realization. Generic procedures permit a human process and direct observation; Code, Events, filesystem shape and DMM are required only by applicable profiles. A build tenant supplies the concrete computational binding without redefining the common WHAT.
8. Foundational reduction. At deciding consumers, shared laws actually govern; arguments distinguish consequence from extra policy. Counting headings, copying prose, adding owner links or shrinking bytes alone establishes no reduction. Algorithmic, information and computational accounts, F_D/F_P/F_H, category claims and world-model sufficiency retain their scopes.
9. Bounded ordinary work. Follow each candidate's ordinary entry for triage, bounded construction and recovery. Trace the selected source/constraint routes; count relevant supplied/acquired artifacts, fidelity and omitted dependencies. Desk tracing is not a fresh-actor or context-fit result. Keep curated and ordinary entry separate.
10. Conservation and authority. Per-claim dispositions retain trigger, scope, obligations, permissions, exceptions and unresolved premises. Proposed replacements need owner decisions; author frontier and reused review cannot manufacture acceptance. Package identity, source and authored/projection accounts agree on exact subjects.
11. Honest evidence. Adverse checks test the advertised property; historical runs, task revisions and corrected grading remain on their subjects. Complete generation, mapped cases, use evidence and acceptance remain distinct. Report unavailable evidence as unassessed, not a fabricated text failure.
12. Progress and completion. Lawful construction, preserved obligations, useful progress and completing Z are distinct. A bounded step has failure, unknown, stop and recovery behavior. Strategy trades resources and feedback under authority; no calculus proves an unconstrained desired outcome.

## Decision and reporting

For each cone, assess the same cases and material dependencies on both candidates. Give exact commit/path/heading or line evidence, the violated common property, a concrete consequence/counterexample, and any workaround or uncertainty. Assess all six member criteria with honest scope: claim accounting; semantic conservation; accepted replacements; actual task use/context fit; discrimination and interpretation fidelity; governing reduction. Unassessed criteria remain unresolved.

Compare conceptual fit, useful ownership/reduction, ordinary readability/acquisition, demonstrated correctness and qualification readiness separately. Label unfinished publication differently from a defective completed package. Recommend the better current basis with reasons and identify changes that would reverse that recommendation. A recommendation is not qualified selection: the common comparison law requires all six criteria and integrated closure; if neither qualifies, record no qualified choice. No candidate repair, merge, retirement, adoption or release follows.
