## Analysis

### The substantive correction is sound

Lines 46–66 give the right comparison basis. Unchanged text can conserve a capability and implement a valid specialization. Changed text can improve an explanation or introduce a regression. Neither sentence-retention percentage establishes whether foundational commitments govern the resulting method.

The useful question is whether the deciding contracts have explicit premises, additional choices, a semantic owner and actual consumers. Inspecting those relationships avoids rewarding either prose novelty or the mere addition of foundational cross-references. Coverage remains distinct from conservation.

The shared rc.2 baseline and dependency-aware delta review are proportionate. Reuse applies to unchanged meaning and dependencies, not just unchanged characters. Sampling can support this comparative recommendation without being represented as closure of every C3 conservation obligation.

The predecessor-link test at line 56 is also sound: availability after predecessor access is removed matters more than the URI count. Include schemas, bootstrap and frame acquisition in that test, as the revision proposes.

C17 is useful and consistent with the installed calculus. F_D is scoped deterministic evaluation or proof; F_P includes bounded interpretation, construction and proposal; F_H is explicitly granted human adjudication. These classifications do not confer authority, collapse traversal identities or establish category-theoretic functor laws.

The proposed categorical capability is assessed prospectively, with current-contract fit reported separately. A single execution of the selected steel thread can establish that instance; it cannot establish comparative performance across all three methods. Lines 90 and 112 preserve these distinctions.

### 1. Make the acquisition condition explicit for each measured probe

C15 asks whether a fresh user finds and applies the method. Lines 114 and 120–122 permit a supplied frame or routed material and require measuring the ordinary path. The activation should make those alternatives explicit:

- Prepared-frame application measures whether the supplied frame is sufficient for the task.
- Ordinary acquisition measures how the actor obtains the applicable material from the method's normal entry route.

Supplying one candidate's frame while requiring another actor to discover its frame would confound the comparison. Supplying all frames supports application evidence but does not establish improved discovery. Record selection and projection costs in the ordinary acquisition account, and use rc.2's actual compressed route with necessary source re-entry.

These can be scoped observations within the existing probes; they need not become additional mandatory trials.

### 2. Keep the fresh probe actor separate from the calibrated assessor context

The two-family arrangement is useful: each assessor covers both candidates, while same-family results remain disclosed and actual authors cannot provide independent acceptance of their own work.

The activation must distinguish an assessor who has read the baseline and both candidates from a fresh task actor. Reusing that assessor context for a cold-use probe would give the actor knowledge that the measured ordinary route did not supply. Fresh probe contexts should receive only their declared starting material and subject.

Counterbalance the candidate reading order between the two assessors, and freeze each assessor's returns before conjunction. Do not expose one assessor's unclosed ratings to the other. Baseline calibration can establish the rubric while leaving subsequent judgments independent.

This concerns the arrangement of the already proposed actors and observations; it does not require another permanent reviewer role.

### 3. Treat the proposed budget as a cap, not a completion estimate

The 90-minute-per-assessor and USD 10 total proposal is explicit and appropriately pending. Its adequacy for complete coverage cannot be established without the selected models, billing basis, probe scope and actual acquisition costs.

The activation needs an allocation within the shared monetary cap, an account of which calibration and probe calls it includes, and a truthful stop result. When a cap is reached, return completed evidence with unassessed rows and outstanding coverage. Do not shrink scope silently or turn missing observations into an Absent rating.

If both assessors have equal scope, a USD 5 maximum allocation each is a simple starting configuration. If baseline preparation or shared probes consume paid calls, reserve those costs first. Report unavailable monetary counters and any estimation basis; elapsed time or token counts alone do not verify a dollar cap.

The reported rc.2 scratch count remains an acquisition lead. The revision correctly withholds it as independent performance evidence.
