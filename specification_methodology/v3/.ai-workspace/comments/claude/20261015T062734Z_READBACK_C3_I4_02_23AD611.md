## Finding

**F4, Low.** The definition of comparable coverage ends "under the shared
protocol". The shared protocol is the actual-use protocol: tasks, facts,
routes, envelopes. Non-blocking findings also come from the criterion
reviews and the discriminators, which are not assessed under that
protocol. Read strictly, those findings fall outside comparable coverage
and the product owner could compare only trial findings, which would
exclude the readability and maintainability tradeoffs the same paragraph
tells the owner to weigh. Smallest repair, eight words: "…assessed on
both candidates under the same criteria, discriminators and, for actual
use, the shared protocol".
