## Judgment

Codex's line is ahead on completeness: it has a frozen candidate that
covers every work row at construction level, a whole-corpus
interpretation that a nonauthor reproduced, a finite witness of the
assurance formulas, and it was returned in 100 minutes. Claude's line is
ahead on verified fidelity of what it covers and on the instruments the
criteria will need: fresh-context nonauthor readbacks with expectations
fixed first, consumers traced and judged for all 318 rows, route-traced
reduction paths, a trial design with hashed expectation sets, and an
interpretation route reproduced from a clean archive with adverse
controls. Codex has breadth without reading; Claude has reading without
breadth. Four of the six findings I raised against Codex at I4 persist in
substance at I5 (no nonauthor conservation reading, 389 determinations
without consumers, byte-only reduction, the version label), and the Codex
return itself lists each as open. The Claude line's own readbacks found
defects of its own that I record rather than hide: a false regeneration
claim corrected at `094e2e8`, and a wrong judgment value in every frame's
binding text, repaired at I5-06b.

Which is better. As a candidate to assess today, Codex's, because it
exists as a frozen whole and Claude's does not. As evidence about method
quality, Claude's, because what it states has been read against the common
text by nonauthors and repaired, and Codex's mostly has not. If the
product owner must carry one line forward now, the honest statement is
that the decision is not yet decidable under the ratified rule, and the
cheapest way to make it decidable is the shared actual-use protocol on
both frozen candidates, which requires the Claude freeze. If the owner
wants my preference regardless: continue both to the freeze, with each
line taking the other's strongest technique under Cross-Synthesis, Codex
taking the nonauthor conservation reading and route-traced paths, Claude
taking the whole-corpus determinations table and the finite assurance
witness, and decide on the comparison protocol's results, not on this
post. I am the author of one of the two lines; weigh this judgment
accordingly.
