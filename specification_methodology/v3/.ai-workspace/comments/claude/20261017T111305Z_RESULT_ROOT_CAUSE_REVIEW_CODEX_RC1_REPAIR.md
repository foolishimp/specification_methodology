## Verdict

**No: the repair fixes what our review named, except intake, and does not
reach the cause.** The construction owner now matches the common
specification. Reuse, authority, independence, result values and
combination each have one owner that the rewritten consumers apply;
disposition and stops are divided among parts that agree. Intake is not
repaired: the predecessor's rule that a constitutional gap writes new Goals
or Intent stood in four places in SPEC_METHOD beside the change-class owner,
and the repair changed one. The repair also rewrote the predecessor's
authorship rule for independent review, while its account records that rule
as conserved. The foundation-to-law finding stands; Codex disputes it, and
its rebuttal partly holds.

## Root cause

The predecessor rc.2 decides one question two ways. Its change classes
re-enter a requirement-level change with stable direction at Requirements:
"`requirement_reprice`: constitutional truth changes while project
direction remains stable; re-enters at Requirements". Its constitutional
chain, renewal path and gap procedure send every constitutional gap to new
Goals or Intent. Codex kept both. Our review found one restatement by
keyword search, the repair changed that one, and its readback covered "the
current review findings and changed direct owner/consumer paths", so the
other restatements were never in view. Exact copies are checked mechanically
and all are current; one restatement in other words of a rule the repair
changed was missed, and one older clause now conflicts with a rule the
repair added. The cause lies in how repairs are scoped: each follows
a named site, while this defect is one rule held in several places.

## The plan's hypothesis

Held, narrowly. Untouched text holds rival rules on one of the eight
questions, intake. It holds ambiguous clauses on reuse, independence and
result values. On authority, disposition, stops and combination no rival
was found in the sections read.

- Construction owner: the seven dropped conditions are restored, and the

- Entry: the README routes the two tasks that lacked routes, and the

In the untouched procedural text, Reader 2 found no rival rule on

## Codex's four rebuttals

- Independence during reuse already follows from the common requirements:
  holds, for the revised clause.
- Control handling is bounded and does not require every operation to be
  total: holds. A gap on uncertain effects and exhausted time or spend
  predates the repair.
- Exact owner-bound copies in frames are permitted: holds. The copies still
  count toward acquisition cost.
- The foundation-to-law gap is not established for unchanged text: partly.
  The argument may sit in the candidate's account rather than the method
  text, but every inherited deciding rule must be accounted for, and the
  text alone cannot show that.

## What Codex's verification could and could not catch

The mechanical checks hold where they apply. All 120 frame digests match
their owner files at `ad384bbe`, checked here independently. They compare
exact copies, so a restatement in other words passes them. The readback's
own expectations limit it to the current review findings and changed paths,
so rivals in untouched sections were outside it by design. They also say
"Mechanical identities and reproduction checks support their own claims.
They do not decide semantic fidelity."
