# Expected distinctions (open only after recording your traces of all ten scenarios on both candidates)

Each is quoted verbatim from a source neither candidate's author wrote: the common conformance tables
(`conformance/README.md`), the common requirements (`requirements/README.md`, `requirements/ASSURANCE.md`)
of the governing specification, or the predecessor release the candidates succeed. Where a line begins
"Test manager's reading", it is the test manager's application of the quoted sources to the scenario; grade
against the quoted sources, and say so if that reading misstates them. Grade the candidate's decision
against the distinction, not against its wording.

S1. Conformance, universal construction: "Apply one intake and causal diagnosis. Correct the supported
affected relation, or acquire evidence and retain unresolved alternatives. The label supplies no repair
class or permission."

S2. Conformance, universal construction: "Reacquire the bound current subject, reopen affected claims and
preserve independently applicable work. Resolve uncertain effects sufficiently before a dependent action."

S3. Conformance, universal construction: "Preserve the distinction between admissible construction and
delivered outcome. The specification supplies Z; a calculus or lawful execution supplies no automatic
satisfaction or acceptance."

S4. Conformance, universal construction: "A change to Z needs its owning decision and downstream re-entry.
It neither proves the former Z nor erases the original counterexample."

S5. Conformance, assurance: "Affected assertions and applicability are reassessed through the owning
change authority. The original evidence and counterexample retain their subjects."

S6. Common requirement (Universal Construction, item 4): "Diagnosis distinguishes a defective realisation,
an inadequate governing requirement or design, an inaccurate predictive model, and insufficient
observation. ... Diagnosis preserves unresolved alternatives and routes each supported repair to its
affected owner rather than inferring a repair from the intake label." Predecessor change class (rc.2
SPEC_METHOD): "`requirement_reprice`: constitutional truth changes while project direction
remains stable; re-enters at Requirements and flows through Design, Code, and Evidence". Test manager's
reading: with direction and scope stable and the Product commitment absent from the requirements, the
supported repair re-enters at Requirements and flows forward; a code patch alone leaves the requirements
gap open; no new Goals or Intent is required by the gap alone.

S7. Conformance, member: "Each tenant supplies its computational calculus, concrete execution binding and
conformance evidence. Common requirements decide fitness; the kind of executor alone neither rejects nor
accepts the realisation. Generic procedures preserve those permitted choices, including an adequate human
process using current records and direct observation without code or complete event-history replay."

S8. Common requirement (Assurance, observation sufficiency): "A checker that always decides `q_t`
correctly using only π requires: For all s, s' in Ω: π(s) = π(s') implies q_t(s) = q_t(s'). ... If two
admissible cases have the same observation but opposite truth values, that observation cannot support a
sound complete decision between them. Further evidence, a justified narrower scope or an indeterminate
judgment is required." Test manager's reading: the parity bit is sufficient for "even" (equal observations
never carry opposite truth values), so a sound decision is available, given a supported observation
binding; the constant observation is insufficient and requires further evidence, a narrower scope or an
indeterminate judgment.

S9. Common requirement (Assurance, assertions and observation): "An observed test pass remains an
observation when the oracle's fidelity is unestablished; it does not become established satisfaction of
the source obligation. The same distinction applies to a reported failure. The observation binding itself
needs support: an idealised π does not establish that the instrument, actor or execution acquired the
required facts correctly." Test manager's reading: the reported failure is an observation; it does not
establish that the actual behaviour disagrees with the requirement while the decoder is unchecked;
diagnosis locates the cause among realisation, expected outcome and observation.

S10. Conformance, construction and integrated closure: "Review may support the proposal; dependent member
acceptance waits for the exact replacement decision recorded in the ticket. A construction grant does not
supply that acceptance." Grade the general distinction: a reviewer's support does not accept a changed
meaning; acceptance that depends on it waits for its owner's decision.
