Judgment. Candidate A's owner text is the more faithful carrier of the specification's obligations, by a clear margin: 64 of 65 expectations carried with the same meaning and force (59 in the owner text, 5 elsewhere in its files) against B's 49 (42 plus 7). The 16 B "changed" items split into two kinds. Twelve drop a determination that no grep found anywhere in B's packet file: the build-tenant binding and conditional-proof premises (CR-3), the concurrency permission (CR-8), the categorical-universality disclaimer (CR-27), unresolved-is-no-judgment-value (AR-8), the prescribed-provenance-format non-requirement (AR-17), the external-predicate and truth-label rule and no-tool selection (AR-28), the conformance demonstration cases (AR-29), criteria-assess-owners and the separate-evidence rule (AR-30), the corrected-run rule (AR-31), the relocation clause (FB-2, also missing in A), the hazard rationale (CR-23), and task sufficiency (FB-3). Four are rewordings that alter a condition or distinction: outcome evidence replaced by an argument (CR-9), authority dropped from the composition-compatibility list (CR-7), the next-step-availability distinction dropped (AR-3), scope dropped from the interpretation retention list (AR-6). The assurance group shows the largest gap: A carries all six relations including formal representation and demonstration; B carries five subrelations and lacks the sixth in its owner text. Neither candidate contradicts the specification. Both carry the frame-basis items mostly outside their named owner sections, which is expected since those items come from the frame basis rather than the construction or assurance requirement.

What the tallies cannot show. They count expectations, not weight: a dropped non-requirement (AR-17's provenance format) counts the same as a dropped lawfulness condition (CR-3). They cannot show whether B carries the missing items in members outside the packet (B links to SPEC_METHOD, TRANSFORMATION_LIBRARY, AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS and the Baseline, none supplied), or whether A's linked EVALUATION, RELEASE, CONFORMANCE and SPEC_METHOD state what A's owner text promises of them. They do not measure the two candidates' additions, which are of comparable number and all consistent with the specification. They say nothing about reduction, acquisition cost, consumer binding at entry points, or the a_c interpretation, which are outside the owner-text fidelity question. Judgments of "same meaning and force" on reworded clauses are interpretive; I have marked the minor cases as such so they can be discounted.
