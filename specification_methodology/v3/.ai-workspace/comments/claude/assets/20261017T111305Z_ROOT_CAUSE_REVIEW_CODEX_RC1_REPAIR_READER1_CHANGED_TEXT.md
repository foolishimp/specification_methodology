## 3. New defects

1. **UP-007's authorship rule changed at its new owner. Class (d), with (b). Moderate.**
   - Predecessor (installed rc.2 TICKET_METHOD, UP-007): "Where independent review is required, the reviewer is not the author of the reviewed subject and identifies the exact subject, basis, scope, exclusions, evidence, verdict, and unresolved findings."
   - Subject, FOUNDATIONS Independent assessment: "its assessor has not materially authored the subject or the repair being assessed", "Material wording, policy or design adopted in the subject is authorship. Its contributor discloses the contribution and cannot supply the required independent assessment of that claim".
   - Subject, Ticket UP-007: "The review carrier identifies … with the assessor's authorship and evidence conditions bound."
   - **Partial contributions.** For a reviewer whose suggested wording was adopted, or who contributed something immaterial, the predecessor's "the author" does not decide either way. The successor decides both. Under the disposition requirement, conservation is therefore unresolved: "if the comparison cannot decide, conservation remains unresolved". I did not read the disposition records, so I cannot tell whether a replacement relation is recorded.
   - **Disclosure duty.** The common text states one only in PRODUCT's C3 Independence paragraph, which configures this release's construction ("Its author discloses that contribution…"). The generic requirements state only "Porting retains authorship" (ASSURANCE) and "Ported contributions retain their authorship" (conformance). The disclosure sentence is not itself conditioned on independent assessment being required.
   - The derivation row names a purpose for the authorship rule but none for disclosure. C3 criterion 6 requires one: "adds no new obligation without naming its purpose and material relation".

2. **Generic multi-role permission dropped. Class (a). Low.**
   - Spec Construction, Assessment, And Delegation, before: "One actor may occupy different roles across different claims when independence for the assessed claim is preserved."
   - After: "The selected configuration binds actors to that claim under the shared independence conditions; this construction binding prescribes no actor count."
   - The explicit statement now survives only in the optional Baseline: "One actor may occupy several frames across different activations…".
   - In generic text it can only be inferred, from Configuration ("An adequate configuration can use one actor…", "even where one actor is permitted to perform several") and from the Independent assessment section's per-claim conditions.
   - Measured against single-ownership item 8: "A required generic determination is available without adopting an optional profile."

3. **AC-005 non-transfer made implicit. Class (c). Low.**
   - Before: "Copying, interpreting, encoding, evaluating, or transporting a record does not transfer authority."
   - After: "Bind each copied, interpreted, encoded, evaluated or transported record to those existing grants; the calculus representation is their carrier under that law."
   - "Bind … to those existing grants" can be read as letting the copy carry the grant. The non-transfer now rests on the owner's "a document's presence … cannot … transfer" and "A grant is carried only within its declared scope".
   - Measured against constraint-surface item 5: authority distinctions "are not silently weakened".

4. **Frame-scoped vocabulary under a shared owner. Class (c). Low.**
   - Judgment and authority now claims shared ownership, yet its vocabulary "keeps its source meanings". The imported operation authority is "the owner-issued grant … where an evaluation performs such an operation"; decision authority is "the role permitted to … dispose its result".
   - Identity, Design and Ticket now apply the owner outside frame evaluation.
   - Only "Meaning, evaluation, operation and decision retain their distinct authority relations" depends on those frame-scoped meanings. I found no decision that changes.

5. **Design Totality Rule lags the revised owner. Class (d). Low. Predates the repair, now stale.**

## 4. The four claims

- **C1: holds, for the revised clause itself.**
  - The frame requirement says "The permitted allocation and reconfiguration preserve … admitted authority and required independence". It also says reuse of closed results checks "actor-condition and dependency changes … unchanged artifact bytes alone do not establish applicability or independence".
  - REFERENCE_FRAME_BASIS: "A judgement that must be independent stays independent."
  - ASSURANCE lists retention conditions without independence: "the claim and scope still apply, its assertion and observation basis remain justified, and affected dependencies are unchanged…". Its "the claim … still appl[ies]" entails only a claim's own independence condition.
  - So "any independence required for that claim still holds" adds no force. The earlier "required independence holds" read more broadly. Two caveats: assurance supports the clause only by entailment, and the clause imports whatever Independent assessment requires (new defect 1).

- **C2: holds.**
  - Source: "The selected control model handles its admitted control states … this does not require every operation to be total." This is consistent with requirement item 6 (binding) and P5 (supporting only, since PRINCIPLES is under evaluation): "A selected control model has explicit dispositions…".
  - Limit: item 6 requires handling of "uncertain effects and exhausted resources". The owner's list names "partial effects" and "exhausted capability". Foundations keeps `partial` distinct from `unknown`, and RF's capability envelope omits time and spend.
  - Unknown effects are reached only through the cited Workspace and context. Exhausted time and spend appear only as a claim limit ("Exhausting a limit proves neither completion nor impossibility"), not as admitted states. With the domain now "selected", that gap matters; it predates the repair.

- **C3: holds.**
  - Single-ownership item 4: "A reference frame's exact copy of a clause names and matches its owning locus … Neither is a second owner." The activation unit says "Exact copies match their owners."
  - Limits: a copy that no longer matches its owner, such as an old RF-005 copy, is a fidelity defect; I could not check the frames. Repetition still counts toward acquisition under reduction item 5, so the claim does not answer the earlier cost finding.

- **C4: partly.**
  - The requirement places the argument with "The candidate", not the method text: "The candidate states its grouping and binds the covered rules … to each argument", and "Numbering and file boundaries do not prescribe the grouping". So inherited sections need not each carry an in-text argument.
  - But the "existing design and proof surfaces" wording comes from reduction item 3, about justifying retained primitives, not about foundation to law.
  - The obligation remains: "The whole set of material deciding rules is accounted for, including inherited rules"; "Copied text does not establish the foundational relationship just by being present"; "a document preamble or coarser grouping cannot hide an unsupported rule". Whether the candidate's records meet it cannot be told from what I was allowed to read.
