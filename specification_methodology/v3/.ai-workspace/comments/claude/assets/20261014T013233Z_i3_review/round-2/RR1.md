## The M1 decline to confirm the pillar list verbatim

- **Stated reason.** DISP:36 says: "Existing owner statements supply this direction; no verbatim reconfirmation is required." T-001:25 says: "The owner's existing instructions name the algebraic and computational calculi separately … no new confirmation of those pillars is required."
- **What the record holds.** Only the instruction sentence is the owner's own words: T-001:45, "update v3/specification given the current pillars, and then do v3 iteration 3". The pillar list itself is reported speech: "He explains that the algebraic and computational calculi are two of the essential reductions, alongside …". GOALS:40 paraphrases that paraphrase. The specification records no owner wording for the list.
- **A second rendering.** The frozen subject holds another pen rendering of the pillar set, at I2POST:23. T-001:47 links that post as the "review applicability record". Its list is: "Spec Method and project structure, world models and Markov-object claims, functional design, ODD transformations and control, information semantics, reference frames, computational realisation, evidence and authority". It has no algebraic calculus, and it names "computational realisation", which is the round 1 defect. Whether the two renderings describe the same conversation cannot be told from the record. They do show that the pen's accounts of the list vary.
- **Judgment.** For the specific round 1 defect, the reason holds: the table now matches T-001:45 on the two calculi. As a general claim it overstates the record. "Existing owner statements" are the pen's paraphrases, and the pen also wrote the table checked against them, so the check is circular. The owner's own words are not recorded.
- **Status.** Asking the owner remains his decision. This review reports the decline as **not closed**. The cheapest remedy is for the owner's I3 sign-off (GOALS:18) to confirm PRODUCT:39–50 explicitly.

## What holds

- B1, M3, M5 and M10 meet their round 2 expectations in the specification. M3 holds across the Outcome, criterion and Closure, and partial coverage is explicitly barred.
- The repairs keep one owner for each rule. Replacement acceptance is owned at PRODUCT:202 and referred to from REQ:29, criterion 3 and CONF:59. The conjunction is owned at PRODUCT:200 and referred to from RFB:28 and CONF:60.
- The core reduction rule can now fail a candidate. The positive witness is at REQ:61, the blockers at REQ:64, and the integrated check at PRODUCT:403 and CONF:12. Removing "where available" closes the escape route.
- The declines judged here are reasonable, except the M1 confirmation: SR4 T-4, the numeric reduction threshold, the paragraph label, the narrowing of SR1-04, and the member-to-member map.
- No regression to round 1 results 5, 10 or 12 was found in these areas. The theory, interpretation, carrier and checker keep separate identities (ACD:5–10, :43), and checker selection stays separate (ACD:20–26, REQ:75).
