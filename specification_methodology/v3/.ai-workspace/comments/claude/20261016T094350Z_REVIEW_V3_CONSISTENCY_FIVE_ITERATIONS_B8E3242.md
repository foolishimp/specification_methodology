# Consistency review of the v3 common specification across its five iterations, at b8e3242

**Reviewer**: Claude, cross-family, nonauthor of the common source.
**Subject**: the ten prose members of `specification/` at `b8e3242` (C3-I5-04): INTENT, PRODUCT, GOALS, REFERENCE_FRAME_BASIS, requirements/README, PRINCIPLES, CAPABILITIES, ASSURANCE, A_C_DEPENDENCY, conformance/README. PREDECESSOR_CLAIMS.md, the claim inventory, was consulted only for references.
**Question from the product owner**: the specification grew over five iterations, each adding requirements to the transformation process (the construction from a current state A to an acceptable outcome Z). Are the additions consistent within the processes they govern?
**Standing**: commentary. It claims no ratification, admission or acceptance. The product owner is the human who ratifies an exact commit. "The pen" is Codex, holder of the common Writer grant. "Owner" below means an owning locus, one section of one member, as the single ownership requirement defines it. Line numbers refer to the files at `b8e3242`.

**Result: the five layers agree in meaning. Neither reading found a clause that requires, permits or prohibits what another clause contradicts. The inconsistencies are of ownership and vocabulary. Iteration 5 added two new owners, the construction model and the assurance requirement, without rewiring the earlier layers' statements of the same determinations as references to them. Several words that decide outcomes carry two or more meanings (A, join, specification, interpretation, independence, unresolved, control), and several deciding terms are undefined (the calculus that decides lawfulness, work authority, mandatory gate, ordinary entry, axioms). Thirteen Medium findings, nineteen Low. One successor commit by the pen can fix them; two of the Medium findings need the pen to decide a meaning rather than rewire a reference (M2, M9). Ratify that commit exactly.**

## Method

Two readings. This reviewer read every member in full once, built the layer map below from the GOALS revision record and the iteration 5 records in T-001, and checked each process of the transformation for seven defect kinds:

- (a) one determination (a rule, definition or allowed-value list) stated in two or more places with no owner-and-refinement relation, or with different scope, triggers, exceptions or modal force;
- (b) a contradiction in required, permitted or prohibited work;
- (c) one symbol or term with two meanings, or two names for one meaning;
- (d) a deciding term used without definition;
- (e) text from an earlier layer that a later layer superseded without updating it;
- (f) link text naming a rule the target does not define under that name;
- (g) a rule imposed on the candidates that the specification's own text does not meet.

A second, independent reading was made by one fresh-context activation (Claude Fable 5.1, maximum effort) given the same ten files and the same seven kinds, with this reviewer's findings withheld. Its return is attached verbatim as `assets/20261016T094350Z_INDEPENDENT_READING_V3_CONSISTENCY_B8E3242.md`. Every row of it used below was checked against the source text by this reviewer; rows the reviewer judged not to be defects were left in the asset and not carried here. The "found by" column records B (both readings, independently), C (this reviewer only) or R (the independent reading only, verified here).

Mechanical checks: all 403 relative links in the pinned members resolve; no member uses "should", and "shall" appears once as a quoted word, so modal force is carried by "must", "may", "never" and "cannot" throughout; term and result-name counts; link text compared with target headings.
