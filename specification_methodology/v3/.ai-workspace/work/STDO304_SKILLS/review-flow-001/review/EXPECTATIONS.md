## Predetermined whole-path cases

1. A small admitted direct correction with sufficient reproduction/suite evidence, no independent boundary and no finding-carrier obligation closes without manufactured orchestration. Contrast a similar fix at a required review boundary: landing may be lawful, but dependent closure remains held.
2. A landed unit enters QA while the next authorized step uses its exact provisional input. An in-scope high-priority finding and a lower-priority residual arrive. Follow both into their distinct fix/closure/release decisions, retaining an unrelated downstream result and invalidating one materially dependent result.
3. Many findings include duplicates and uncertain causes. Follow Reviewer report → bounded triage Worker → Executive priority/owner/disposition → tracked items. A Worker grouping must not rewrite severity; an Executive must not acquire withheld evidence simply because it appears in a summary.
4. A capture-derived finding is written to its exact restricted report, appears through the permitted session/ledger projection, and remains unavailable to excluded actors. Missing write authority or a leaking prescribed command returns a refusal/re-entry rather than widening the grant.
5. A named A→B gate permits a bounded experiment on A's landed bytes but withholds B's admitted-input acceptance/dependent closure. Test pending review, complete adverse review, satisfied applicable review, stale/new evidence and no declared gate. Gate satisfaction does not supply B's missing acceptance authority.
6. A fix pass has one no-reproducer repair or uncovered prior-review slice: the applicable independent recheck runs once for the bounded pass. A different pass meets no trigger and uses valid tests. Neither branch hides unperformed criteria or adds a universal review ratio.
7. A parent ticket closes with release-only residuals. Follow each residual to its authorized continuing carrier and eventual verification/go-live decision; test accidental disappearance, duplicated state ownership, and no-findings/no-ticket.
