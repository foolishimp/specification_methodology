## Worked boundary

Product A's admitted step produces candidate C. The Writer's grant permits
landing C at a checkpoint; selected QA has not yet reported. Product B has an
explicit gate requiring that QA and a satisfied data-contract claim before it
may accept C as an input. B may perform authorized provisional adapter work
against C, carrying that dependency, but cannot close its dependent claim.

QA reports one non-hard-stop field-format defect F in its exact granted report
R, linked to the issuing work item. The report preserves the Reviewer's severity
and restricted evidence. The Executive groups F directly (no triage helper is
needed), assigns its Product-selected priority with a reason, and records item
I on the reviewed work item's ledger. That priority does not make the false
field-format claim true. If the local step's closure needs that claim, even a
release-later priority cannot close it; unrelated completed work may remain
closed with its retained obligation.

A repair Writer returns C2 with a failing-before/passing-after reproduction and
the declared full suite passing. I is fixed, naming C2. For an ordinary local
repair with no recheck trigger, those tests may establish its repair predicate,
and I can be recorded test-verified without pretending a Reviewer ran. Here B's
gate requires independent assessment of the repaired field-format claim, so
the Executive selects one bounded independent recheck; I and B's dependent
acceptance stay unresolved until the required report supports them. B refreshes
the adapter results affected by F/C2, retaining unrelated valid work. The
decision owner can then accept the exact gated input and close only satisfied
claims. Release still consumes its separate obligations and operation grant.

No ratio is inferred from the number of fixes. If only a sample was assessed,
the report names that population and leaves the remaining required coverage
open; a review budget cannot convert it to complete coverage.

Under the later selected severity convention, a severity-3 defect's material
effect on Product B normally leads the Executive to select repair. A contained
local severity-3 defect may instead be deferred with its owner and supported
reason. This is not a conversion to priority P3 and cannot make an unmet
mandatory claim satisfied. Missing dependency evidence keeps the impact
uncertain rather than establishing that the defect is local.

## Projection and qualification

The shared skill reference presents one deciding route and compact example;
entrypoints supply their role-specific actions. `stdo-review` protects the
subject and may record only an exactly granted report. `stdo-ticket` records
the report/ledger relations, and status/help preserve undecided policy choices.
Old installed bases keep their old semantics until explicitly migrated.

Mechanical checks cover generated source identity, package links and skill
shape. Independent source-first cases assess the decisions. Any native scenario
must bind the exact unreleased candidate as its declared evaluation premise,
separate from the actual installed builder; it cannot claim a published 3.0.4
or silently substitute candidate owners into an older installed basis.

The first source-use campaign remains bound to its preserved first candidate.
Its Executive proposed an already-authorized combined review with unsettled
pass grouping and presumed a repair Writer grant absent from the scenario.
No dispatch occurred. The shared return path now briefly distinguishes a
proposed action from an executable activation by requiring those existing
bindings; this clarifies existing law and creates no gate or new actor. The
later severity convention and reminder receive bounded source/mechanical
assessment, with no new native-use claim.
