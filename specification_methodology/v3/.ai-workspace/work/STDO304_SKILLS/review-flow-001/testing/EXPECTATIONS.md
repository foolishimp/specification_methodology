## Minimal forward-test proposal

Two native Claude sessions are proposed, not yet authorised to launch. Both use the current configured model, fresh unresumed processes, the unchanged shipped per-call gate, explicit permitted source/fixture paths, and read-only tools. The admitted relay persists only a completed successful final return to its named result carrier. An incomplete or timed-out actor is preserved as incomplete, not relayed as a closed result.

1. **Reviewer on raw work.** A tiny landed invoice/export artifact and its requirements contain independent arithmetic and date-format errors, plus correct control rows. The raw task requests review of that exact subject under the prospective candidate profile and names a sole findings carrier. The actor receives neither expected findings nor an author's summary. Assess actual source acquisition, exact findings, evidence/severity, role limits, whole declared coverage and carrier use. The correct control is assessed as part of the declared population, not as a separate artificial no-finding review. This does not by itself qualify the no-finding-carrier branch.
2. **Executive consumes the real return.** A fresh actor receives the actual closed Reviewer result, exact candidate/mandate metadata, a small existing fix frontier and a dependency table. Five concise repair records independently exercise the ordinary-test-complete control and four targeted-recheck triggers. The Product profile explicitly declares one gated handoff and separately admits provisional work. The raw task requests the next authorised decisions and result-state projection. Assess severity conservation, priority ownership, finding/bug-item identity, targeted selection, landed/accepted separation, affected-only invalidation and exact acceptance boundary. No reviewer rationale or oracle is forwarded. If case 1 has no closed usable return, the dependent case is withheld and reported unperformed.

The frontier records are raw synthetic historical fixture evidence, not claims of native Worker executions. Any illustrative cutoff or gate is explicitly fixture-owned. No expectation requires the actor to reproduce particular prose or headings. Grading traces each substantive decision to the raw facts and applicable governing clause; unsupported, missing and unperformed decisions stay separate from errors.

Suggested limits: Reviewer 180 seconds / USD 4, Executive 240 seconds / USD 5; at most two actual sessions, total wall cap 420 seconds and API budget cap USD 9, with no automatic retry. Native CLI supports per-session `--max-budget-usd`; actual usage/cost is recorded separately. A time cap is enforced on the process group with descendant cleanup. Managed/system ambient context remains unmeasured. This plan intentionally does not requalify every skill mode, demonstrate live Git landing, or execute the future repair/recheck activations selected by Executive.

Before launch the Executive selects the prospective source/profile binding and the exact raw fixtures; the tester pins those, the candidate, policies/settings, host/interpreter/gate and prompt bytes. Candidate skills may be loaded through the session plugin route or, if denied, by the exact declared skill-file path with that limitation recorded. No fake installed successor or test-only replacement of the reader/gate is permitted.

After source freeze: validate the exact package and affected frame generation against their owners, and run the five skill structure checks. Reuse unchanged reader/gate mechanical evidence with explicit hash/dependency checks; rerun only if the candidate materially changes those dependencies. Prior seals remain untouched.
