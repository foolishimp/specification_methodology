## Present evidence and the smallest next implementation

The prior [tool probe](retained-tool-probe/results.json) observed C01–C05 on the
installed 0.1.5 CLI. The four broken-edge passes are evidence of the defect, not
passing evidence for the required behavior. Its original fixtures/outputs are
archived to avoid introducing discoverable synthetic Products into this workspace.
The [retained evidence note](retained-tool-probe/README.md) preserves provenance
and explains how original absolute paths limit replay as copied.

[VALIDATION.json](VALIDATION.json) records package-only checks: source pins,
new-document links/anchors and in-memory insertion of the proposed edge into the
Skills definition against its selected schema. These establish neither adoption,
authority congruence nor actual-use qualification. No a_c actor execution, host
confinement trial, build, release or efficiency comparison ran for this package.

Implement C01–C07 first in the manager's owning scope. Reuse supported resolver
semantics, bound traversal and preserve per-edge diagnostics; don't convert an
unsupported resource into invented resolution success. Then design the a_c
binding needed by C13–C23 around its existing source/context/preflight operations.
Define the machine-checked fragment and F_P judgments before choosing any new
carrier. The actual producer → acquisition → assembled context → actor → return
consumer path, including late reads and tool output, is the evidence subject.

Independent review should assess semantics and material coverage, not count rows.
Release qualification selects the cases required by the eventual accepted scope,
retains valid prior evidence and adds only affected execution. A new mandatory
gate is an explicit owner decision, not an automatic full-suite rerun.
