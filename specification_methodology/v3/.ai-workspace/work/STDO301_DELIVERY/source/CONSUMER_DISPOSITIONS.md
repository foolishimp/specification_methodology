## Migration standing

The complete finite examples have declared finite domains, pure material
dependencies, bounded operation arguments and exhaustive results; that is
bounded author evidence for their eligibility only. Generic carrier admission,
ODD admission/closure, runtime defaults and external application operations
are templates or consumer obligations, not implemented subjects proved total
by this source amendment. Their actual owners select RELEASE's established,
pending-evidence/bounded-successor, or unresolved-legacy row. No existing
external consumer is silently migrated or reclassified.

The proposed supersessions above remain meaning-acceptance obligations until
their exact covering review and owner decision. Other predecessor claims
retain their original subjects and inherited standing; byte identity supports
the bounded unchanged-source claim, not new whole-method qualification.
