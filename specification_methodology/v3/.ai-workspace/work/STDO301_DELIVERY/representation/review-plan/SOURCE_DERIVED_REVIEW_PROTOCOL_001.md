- Preserve eligibility for deterministic construction under an exact traversal: closed declared domain/basis/material state and dependencies, one declared outcome per input and a termination argument. Infinite domains are allowed with support. Reject the obsolete evaluation-only definition, a finite-domain-only restriction, hidden material dependencies, or a claim that total handling alone proves totality. Returning the first inversion for `[2,1]` differs from sorting it and reporting the successor's property as the original's. Witness persistence does not authorize domain mutation. ODD retains its named Product admission, ABG evidence admission and closure-fold owners; merely naming an evaluator, deterministic kind or useful F_P output supplies none of those consequences.
