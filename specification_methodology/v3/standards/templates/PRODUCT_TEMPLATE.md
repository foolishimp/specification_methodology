# Project Product

Define the intended end state of the product under the currently adopted
Product definition. State that destination in present-tense terms, including
capabilities still to be built.

Resolve and verify the exact installed STDO basis declared by the applicable
Product Definition while you fill this document out.

Link to the governing Intent for purpose, direction and constraints. Keep the
current epics and work-wave priorities on the Goals surface; reprice this
definition when the intended Product's meaning or boundary changes. Reference
imported method concepts through their declared owner and selected basis.

## Product Position

State what kind of product this is, the domain it provides, and its owned scope
and boundaries under the governing Intent.

## Product Terms

Declare each bounded context, its stable identity, owning authority, applicable
basis, and governed scope. Define the key Product concepts that downstream
requirements and design will use.

Treat term spelling as a label rather than a context-free concept identity.
Where another context uses the same term, either keep the meanings isolated or
declare the exact authorized import, translation, equivalence, or
disambiguation relation. Define Product meanings such as user, tenant, account,
actor, owner, frame, and product explicitly when they are material; do not rely
on a shared glossary default.

## Product End State

Describe the intended complete Product: its capabilities, guarantees and
ownership boundaries, in terms that requirements can decompose and make true.
The definition is the destination in force for the source project. Current
implementation status and delivery claims use their own progress and evidence
records.
