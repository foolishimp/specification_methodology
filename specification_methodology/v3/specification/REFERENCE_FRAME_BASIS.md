## Activations And Owners

The existing common-pen and separate Executive/Writer/Reviewer responsibilities
continue under [Construction Authority](PRODUCT.md#construction-authority).
Each new task binds its exact subject, current grant, admitted carrier,
permitted reads/effects, actor capabilities and limits, applicable evaluations,
result consumer and durable evidence surface. A role grants no operation.

| Activation | Current responsibility and boundary |
|---|---|
| Executive | Coordinate within the owner grant, consume bounded closed results and choose an already-authorized next action. No file or Git mutation. |
| Writer | The separately activated common pen or granted tenant actor performs bounded effects in its exact territory, checks them, records evidence and returns the exact subject. |
| Reviewer | Independently assess the frozen subject and applicable obligations; no repair and no review of material authorship as independent evidence. Exact independence conditions and limits belong to that assessment grant. |

Common meaning and acceptance remain with the Product owner. An existing exact
owner instruction can admit its bounded implementation without another approval
round. Work-carrier selection follows installed
[TICKETS §5](stdo://releases/v3.0.4/standards/TICKETS.md#5-from-carrier-to-execution).
Applicable obligations and closed results use the selected release's law;
missing authority, uncertain applicability, subject drift and unavailable
required capabilities withhold the dependent action or claim. The current
host supplies ordinary tools and instruction-following, not a proven exact-file
acquisition gate or clean-room boundary.

This adoption carries forward these generic responsibilities only. Original
C3 concrete operations, six-criterion acceptance, trials and evidence keep
their historical basis below. Their reuse under 3.0.4 needs task-specific
applicability and any required deterministic-operation classification; no
blanket compatibility, renewed qualification or new method acceptance follows.

Common preparation is assigned to Codex; the exact review activation and the product owner's ratification are recorded in T-001. Tenant activations bind their own ratified input and admission. The common [comparison contract](PRODUCT.md#candidate-comparison) governs the subsequent shared tasks; an actor's model family, material authorship and task-context freshness are separate facts. A fresh task actor supplies actual-use evidence; a nonauthor assessor judges it against independently fixed expectations. A donor's participation in the competing line is disclosed even where it did not author the receiving part.

Each activation binds the following:

- its purpose;
- its exact subject and the inputs it came from;
- the governing basis and source routes, which are links to the exact source clauses;
- capability and access;
- permitted effects;
- the relations it must preserve;
- evidence;
- stop conditions;
- what it returns.

These apply within the scope actually selected. A role or status label alone confers no authority.

For reconstruction work, the frame includes the applicable [pillar contributions](PRODUCT.md#foundational-reduction), affected shared owners and consumer decisions. A construction-model task binds the [construction requirement](requirements/README.md#universal-construction-requirement), including the distinction among preservation, progress and outcome satisfaction. Its return distinguishes semantic conservation, demonstrated reduction and mere relocation. An a_c or acquisition frame additionally binds the [constraint-surface requirement](requirements/README.md#constraint-surfaces-and-logic-compression), so a structural check cannot stand in for source fidelity or task sufficiency.

## Conservation And Stops

An assurance or reassessment frame binds the applicable [assurance contract](requirements/ASSURANCE.md), source obligation, claimed predicate and scope, observation basis, subject and material dependencies. A reuse decision acquires the change and evidence-validity conditions needed to support applicability. A comparison acquires shared failure causes and the limits of any quantified assurance. These are task-specific bindings of the existing frames; no new actor role or separate carrier follows.

The following are kept distinct:

- the common specification (WHAT);
- each tenant's realisation (HOW);
- work state;
- observations;
- candidate output;
- acceptance judgement.

Unknown premises and unknown applicability stay explicit. Source text recovered unchanged from the previous release, and valid judgements, may be reused while their validity conditions hold. A judgement that must be independent stays independent.

An authority relation that matters and is missing, stale, contradictory or outside the actor's territory stops the action that depends on it. The case is returned to the relation's owner. Tenant territory and stops are bound by the [tenant entry contract](../build_tenants/README.md).

Verifying the bootstrap shows only that the installed release is present and that the Product Definition points agents to it correctly. This file adds no proposed axiom to the installed release's law.
