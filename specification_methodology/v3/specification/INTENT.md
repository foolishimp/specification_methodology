# Intent

## Mission

To define a precise method for constructing software and processes, with a categorical direction and an axiomatic representation of its laws, so that the method can be mechanically checked and applied to itself.

People and language models apply the method with limited context, so each task receives only the part of the method it needs.

## What The Product Is

- Foundations: identity, justification, authority, results and bounded work, common to any method.
- Spec Method: intent, product, specification and realisation, applied recursively, with change re-entry, assurance and releases.
- Profiles: bindings to particular kinds of construction, such as outcome-driven development, user experience and world models.
- a_c: the axiomatic calculus in which the laws are written and checked.
- Reference frames: the part of the method one task needs.

Every rule has one owner. A lower tier refines a rule and never states a second one ([single ownership](requirements/README.md#single-ownership-requirement)). Every outcome is stated in one result algebra.

The specification is the program: declared laws are the building blocks, refinement layers stack them, and construction is constraint-based and requirements-based. Categories are joined, such as algorithms with computation.

## What It Achieves

- A task completes, or stops with a stated reason, from its reference frame alone.
- Every result records its basis and evidence. A change re-opens only the results that depend on it.
- Producing, checking and accepting a candidate are separate results.
- Every existing rule is kept and restated on the axioms, replaced, removed, or shown not to apply. None is lost silently.
- A categorical claim is a hypothesis until it meets the [categorical demonstration standard](requirements/README.md#categorical-demonstration-standard).

## Direction

The method is built with its installed earlier release, as a compiler builds its next version. A steel thread joining an algorithm category with a computation category comes first, and the method is amended from what it shows before the categorical product is generalised.
