# Common Conformance Questions

A discriminator is a case that separates a correct candidate from a defective one. The member-conformance table supplies acceptance criterion 5 of the [C3 Construction Contract](../PRODUCT.md#c3-construction-contract). Construction and integrated-closure checks have their own section and are evaluated at those decisions.

Both tenants are evaluated against the same independently specified expectations. The reviewer takes in these expectations and the actual source before inspecting the candidate, states applicable obligations and reports uncovered scope. No passing result is recorded here. Evidence binding follows the [frame requirement](../requirements/README.md#bounded-transitions-and-reference-frames); reduction and interpretation coverage follow their common requirement owners.

## Member Conformance

Apply each case to the member's owned determinations and affected consumer bindings. Record applicability under [Construction Authority](../PRODUCT.md#construction-authority). A local review accounts for its scope; integrated assessment covers cross-member coverage and comparisons. An absent integrated assessment does not by itself fail a member's local criterion 5.

| Discriminator | Expected distinction |
|---|---|
| A derivation or independence argument is claimed | Each claimed derivation shows its premises. Claimed independence is argued, not assumed. |
| A family argument accounts for edited rules but omits inherited deciding rules | Coverage includes the material rules in the declared family. Coarser grouping or a whole-member preamble cannot supply a missing premise, consequence or owned policy choice. |
| A new foundations introduction accompanies unchanged rival rules in the consumers | Correspondence alone does not establish reduction. The deciding consumers must use one shared determination or a justified refinement. |
| A candidate is shorter but ordinary entry still requires the same independent reconciliations | Compare the actual deciding paths and acquisition against the exact predecessor and preceding tenant candidate. A smaller body alone is not a positive reduction witness. |
| A candidate unifies a recurring rule and retains a necessary domain exception | Establish actual shared-law use, preserved outcomes and the exception's owner. Reduction can hold without a universal percentage or word-count decrease. |
| Outcome-driven development is presented only as a vocabulary for operations | Show how generic outcome-driven transition contracts govern construction, evaluation, continuation, recovery and termination, with independently specified cases and justified additional policies. These generic obligations do not require adoption of the optional typed-graph profile. |
| Several predecessor clauses are implemented by one shared successor law | Evaluate each material obligation and its consuming bindings. Shared ownership can conserve several claims without retaining their former document partitions; a bare link cannot supply omitted meaning. |
| A candidate deletes an exception to make the core smaller | Size reduction cannot erase a material obligation. Give it a valid disposition and preserve its required behavior, or obtain the owning removal decision. |
| A domain rule is replaced by a shared-law reference | Preserve its material trigger, modal force, adoption conditions and exceptions. The shared owner and domain binding must supply the determination without circular referral. |
| A required generic rule is owned only by an optional profile | Generic use can obtain that determination without adopting the profile; profile-specific additions retain their selection boundary. |
| A functional helper is promoted into a new public mechanism | Require the material distinction or independent boundary that needs it. Shared machinery and subordinate helpers do not create new semantic or authority owners by naming alone. |
| A bounded object is declared a Markov object because its interface is small | An ordinary useful boundary supplies no proof of the stronger sufficiency or independence claim. State its assumptions and evidence; an unsupported claim stays unresolved. |
| An a_c model omits a source condition but passes its schema check | The model fails fidelity for a decision affected by that condition. Mechanical validity cannot authorise the work that the owning source prohibits. |
| A faithful a_c fragment is offered as the whole method's interpretation | Account for every material owned determination and required dependency. The fragment establishes only its covered scope; missing material coverage prevents C3 closure for that tenant. |
| A frame uses an a_c projection rather than copying a clause verbatim | Assess fidelity and task sufficiency against the owning source. Different encoding alone is no defect; changed obligations or permissions are. |
| A compressed constraint surface treats an unresolved premise as unrestricted freedom | Preserve the unresolved dependency and apply the relevant stop, acquisition or refinement rule. Unknown permission is not a grant. |
| A task has several solutions inside the same constraint surface | Permit the alternatives that satisfy its contract. The method constrains admissibility without absorbing the worker's internal solution strategy. |
| A projection changes “not required” into “prohibited”, or preference into obligation | The changed permitted work is a fidelity defect unless an authorised successor relation establishes the change. Matching digests or valid schema do not repair it. |
| Ordinary entry points to an earlier formal interpretation marked current | Bind the current source, signature, program, maps and evidence boundary. Earlier models retain historical standing and cannot qualify the successor by selection error. |
| Information and its representation differ | Agreement in structure cannot establish identical meaning or semantic fidelity. |
| Identity is unchanged while content changes | The current state, and whether an earlier observation still applies, are considered separately. |
| Typed producers and consumers disagree | The incompatible domain or precondition is made explicit. Fields of the same shape do not make a valid join. |
| Assessment A failed and required coverage B is uncovered | A's authenticated failure and its citations survive, and B stays unresolved. Every planned assessment's returned judgement is authenticated against its producer, subject, basis and supporting evidence. B is required coverage outside those assessments, not an omitted planned judgement. |
| A genuinely planned assessment carries a judgement from a different subject | Plan membership does not authenticate the returned judgement. The misbound result cannot satisfy the assessment obligation. |
| The context a task needs is interdependent and does not fit one actor | Narrowing the task lawfully, handing it to another capable actor, or a truthful stop keeps the material obligations. |
| A frame fits its byte limit but irrelevant material obscures a required dependency | Size alone does not establish adequacy. Keep the material dependency, exclude irrelevant material and evaluate actual use under the [frame requirement](../requirements/README.md#bounded-transitions-and-reference-frames). |
| A frame or task changes after its trial, or actor instructions supply the expected answer | Bind the actual tested packet and independently specified expectations. Report the narrower evidence; a rewritten trial or matching source pin does not establish current task sufficiency. |
| An author opens a fresh context to review its own candidate | A new context does not establish independent authorship or supply missing review authority. Ported contributions retain their authorship; the contributor's review cannot supply independent acceptance of them. Relevant common sources may still be shared by an independent reviewer. |
| A coordination frame omits frontier items from its current attention | Excluded work remains open; a bounded view cannot silently dispose it. The next decision retains its material dependencies. |
| A new observation exposes more obligations, or a destination is revised to make the current candidate pass | Discovery may increase the frontier while improving knowledge. Counts alone do not measure progress. A revised destination requires its owning decision and cannot establish that the original destination was reached. |
| One directly authorised task fits a sufficient frame | The generic method permits the direct activation, subject to any separately required assessment. It imposes no fixed role inventory or actor sequence. |
| A peer configuration has no central Executive, or a task uses responsibilities outside the familiar three roles | Evaluate the configuration against the task, dependencies, actor limits, authority and required assurance. Different labels or topology neither reject nor accept it. The installed configuration selected to build this release retains its own scope. |
| Actors redistribute work during execution, including work with effects of uncertain completion | The permitted reconfiguration retains outstanding obligations and result dependencies, resolves material effect uncertainty before dependent action, and preserves authority and required independence. Self-organisation supplies no exemption from these conditions. |
| An arrangement is called optimal because it is familiar, persistent or self-organised | Adequacy and optimality remain separate claims. An optimality claim needs stated objectives, constraints and comparison scope with supporting evidence. Different models or objectives can favour different configurations. |
| A coordinator receives a compact task result that omits a blocker material to delivery | Small size is insufficient. The coordinator retains the longer-term outcome and acquires the information needed for the next decision through its permitted frame inputs. Missing information cannot be assumed favourable. |
| A material dependency changes or a new observation invalidates an earlier claim | Affected applicability and validity are checked before reuse, including when the candidate's bytes are unchanged. Demonstrably independent supported work is kept. |
| A task packet contains relevant rules but lacks material context or authority | A rule packet alone is insufficient. The activated frame binds the contract, subject, evidence, capability and permitted effects required for the task. |
| A fresh actor has no private history, and a retained summary disagrees with the current workspace | Acquire the current subject and its authoritative inputs, correct the stale account, retain applicable work and continue within the grant. Discarding context does not erase the workspace or prove an uncertain external effect absent. |
| An interruption leaves work partly written | Recovery uses the current workspace and the applicable work state. It does not erase progress or invent a completed assessment. |
| A parse or map succeeds | The claim stays mechanical until independently sufficient semantic and practical evidence exists. |
| A change to work carriers or process is proposed as new law | The claimed obligation is justified by its purpose and material relation, or identified as unsupported overhead. |
| The same requirements are realised by software, a human team or a mixed process | Each tenant supplies its computational calculus, concrete execution binding and conformance evidence. Common requirements decide fitness; the kind of executor alone neither rejects nor accepts the realisation ([binding requirement](../requirements/README.md#build-tenant-binding-requirement)). |
| A faster execution plan merges two required independent assessments or substitutes an automated result for a required human decision | Matching outputs or lower cost cannot discharge the lost independence or authority obligation. The binding fails conformance. |
| Two environments use one computational calculus, or one actor performs different kinds of operation | Each binding must satisfy the calculus's laws. Different stacks do not force duplicate calculi; actor identity does not determine operation kind or grant authority. |
| An a_c model of a human process passes structural validation | The representation neither selects an automated executor nor establishes that the human process meets its requirements. Execution conformance needs its own applicable evidence. |
| Two loci in one candidate decide the same question | One is the [owning locus](../requirements/README.md#single-ownership-requirement). The other refers to it, or refines it with a mapping back to it. A frame's copy names its owner. No rule of precedence between loci substitutes for an owner. |
| A member or profile defines its own result values | Its owner maps each value, with its declared predicate and meaning-determining conditions, to exactly one core result. Failure, undecidability, non-performance and unknown effect completion never map to a higher standing. The predicate and domain outcome travel with the core value ([requirement](../requirements/README.md#single-result-algebra-requirement)). |
| A lifecycle label or classification is used to decide admission, compliance or closure | Recorded data and the evaluation or authorised decision remain distinct. The actual determination uses the core result contract; calling it data cannot exempt it, and a core referral cannot replace a missing domain map. |
| A conserved heading now carries a different obligation, or a superseded row names only the old section | Compare the required and permitted decisions on the same material case, including modal force, triggers, scope and exceptions, under the [disposition requirement](../requirements/README.md#successor-disposition-requirement). An indicative rewrite is judged by its meaning, not its grammatical form. Each superseded claim identifies its replacement relation and owning loci; a proposed relation remains pending until accepted. |
| A frame's task returns a result | The frame carries the core result algebra or its profile's mapping into it. |
| A functor or categorical join is claimed | Before the claim, objects, arrows, arrow equality, identity and composition are given and the functor laws are shown. Otherwise the claim is a hypothesis ([standard](../requirements/README.md#categorical-demonstration-standard)). |
| A rewrite or refinement step is applied | The law that permits it is declared for the subject. An exact step preserves meaning. An approximate step states its adequacy relation and an end-to-end bound. |

## Construction And Integrated Closure

These checks evaluate the recorded construction decisions and integrated candidate under the installed builder. They do not add candidate-text predicates to member criterion 5. The Executive applies the acceptance checks; the integrated Reviewer evaluates the whole-candidate obligations before C3 closure. Head-to-head evaluation follows [Candidate Comparison](../PRODUCT.md#candidate-comparison), with the same tasks, independently specified expectations and acquisition conditions. An unassessed candidate is not evidence that its peer qualifies.

| Discriminator | Expected distinction |
|---|---|
| Each member passes its local checks while two members still decide the same shared question differently | C3 closure requires the integrated reduction assessment. Separate satisfied member results cannot erase the cross-member contradiction. |
| A member reviewer agrees that a proposed replacement is clear, but no owner decision accepts its changed meaning | Review may support the proposal; dependent member acceptance waits for the exact replacement decision recorded in the ticket. A construction grant does not supply that acceptance. |
| Five member criteria are satisfied and the sixth is missing, stale or falsified | The installed conjunction withholds member acceptance and preserves all component results. A general favourable verdict cannot replace missing or adverse evidence. Supported inapplicability of a conditional obligation is recorded separately; it does not waive any of the six criteria. |

Logical review is a reviewer's reasoned reading. It finds counterexamples and contradictions without running anything. Actual-use cases do real construction, observe genuine defects, keep the uncertainty of effects, and re-enter the method at the right stage.

The token-transfer test in [A_C_DEPENDENCY](../requirements/A_C_DEPENDENCY.md#proof-claims) tests a_c separately from the method's interpretation fidelity. Source comparison independent of the author, mechanical checks, formal derivations and practical adequacy remain different kinds of evidence.
