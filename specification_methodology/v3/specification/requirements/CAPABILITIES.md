# Capabilities And Evidence

The current reconstruction must provide the capabilities required by the [pillars](../PRODUCT.md#foundational-reduction), [standalone successor](README.md#standalone-successor-requirement), [reduction](README.md#foundational-reduction-requirement) and [constraint-surface](README.md#constraint-surfaces-and-logic-compression) requirements. The questions below are review prompts for those obligations, not separately optional capability contracts. A trial binds explicit expectations, applicability and coverage in its work contract; its selection does not make an existing obligation optional. The categorical steel thread retains its separate selection.

Which capabilities follow from the accepted principles, and with which assumptions?

- Foundational derivation: can a reader tell which commitments are independent, which are derived from others, which generalise others, and which are unresolved?
- Foundational reduction: do shared laws replace repeated determinations at actual consumers, with the remaining domain distinctions justified and the predecessor obligations accounted for?
- Constraint surfaces: can an actor recover what is required, permitted, prohibited and unresolved from the current prose and its a_c interpretation, then work inside that boundary without a prescribed internal solution strategy?
- Objects and transformations: do identity, state, object boundaries, functional composition and ODD traversal retain their meanings across the algorithmic, informational and computational accounts? Are stronger Markov or categorical claims supported only within their demonstrated scope?
- Purpose and specification: can one common specification (WHAT) admit different conforming realisations (HOWs)? It must do so without confusing two things that mean the same with two things that compute the same.
- Universal construction: does the [construction relation](README.md#universal-construction-requirement) connect observed A, required Z, bounded transformations, strategy, assurance and feedback at the decisions that deliver an end-to-end feature?
- Strategy and resources: can work select and revise a strategy under stated resource, risk and feedback tradeoffs, including the costs of planning and checking, without assuming a universally best strategy or one scalar measure?
- Correction: do bugs and new features use the same work mechanism while diagnosis distinguishes inadequate realisation, governing model and observations, then carries the affected obligations through repair and renewed evaluation?
- Bounded work: can an actor get enough context, refine its work, combine results, and stop truthfully with an unknown when it must?
- Evidence and authority: can real claims be evaluated, material failures kept on record, and owner decisions reused without widening their scope?
- Progress and recovery: can interruption, changed dependencies, persistent work state and effects of unknown completion be handled without discarding valid progress? Does recursive work distinguish preservation, useful discovery and eventual completion, with explicit unresolved stops?
- Method usability: can a fresh actor apply the method given only one reference frame and a subject? It must do so without private knowledge of the validator or the author's memory.
- Optional profiles: can a domain add the meaning it needs while generic rules and test subjects stay separate?
- Self-improvement: can observations identify the right owner of a repair, then support a successor release that is evaluated independently?

The previous release's [thirteen logical capabilities](../../../v2/specification/SCENARIOS.md#logical-functions), the logical functions its scenarios define, are inputs when deciding which capabilities the new release keeps. They are read under that release's [rule for agent-performed construction](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#agentic-construction-execution-stdo-up-020), STDO-UP-020. They are not thirteen mandatory stages, and they are not automatically accepted features.

Every selected capability identifies:

- the principles that require it;
- the current obligations it preserves;
- its applicable scope;
- its observable adequate outcome;
- its conformance expectations, specified independently of the author.

Cost measures are selected where a claim needs them. Selecting a capability introduces no universal budget, no requirement that all work in the method starts from a ticket, and no fixed number of actors. The construction of this release does run from tickets, under [Development Isolation](../PRODUCT.md#development-isolation).

The steel thread is the one named in [Categorical Construction](../PRODUCT.md#categorical-construction). Its selection is an open owner decision.
