# Tentative Capability Questions

Status: C2 questions, OPEN behind C1; not accepted STDO 3 closure obligations.

Which capabilities follow from the accepted principles, and with which assumptions?

- Foundational derivation: can a reader recover which commitments are independent, derived, generalised or unresolved?
- Purpose/specification: can one common WHAT admit distinct conforming HOWs without conflating semantic and computational equivalence?
- Bounded work: can an actor obtain sufficient context, refine work, compose results and select a truthful unknown/stop?
- Evidence/authority: can actual claims be evaluated, material failures conserved and owner decisions reused without widening scope?
- Progress/recovery: can interruption, changed dependencies, persistent work state and unknown effects be handled without discarding valid progress?
- Method usability: can a fresh actor, given one projected reference frame and a subject, apply the method without private validator knowledge or author memory? The frame is the activation unit; the corpus is as large as the law requires and is never the thing loaded.
- Optional profiles: can domains add necessary meaning while keeping generic semantics and test subjects separate?
- Self-improvement: can observations identify the correct repair owner, then support an independently evaluated successor?

The predecessor's [thirteen logical capabilities](../../../v2/specification/SCENARIOS.md#logical-functions) inform recovery under its [agentic construction law](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md#agentic-construction-execution-stdo-up-020). They are inputs, not thirteen mandatory stages or automatically accepted 3.0 features.

At C2, every selected capability identifies its requiring principles, preserved current obligations, applicable scope, observable adequate outcome and independently specified conformance expectations. Cost measures are selected where a claim needs them; no universal budget, ticket-first workflow or actor count is introduced.

Finite-context/graceful-totality is a candidate first steel-thread direction only. No thread or runnable trial is selected before C2.
