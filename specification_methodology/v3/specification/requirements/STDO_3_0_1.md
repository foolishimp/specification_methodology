## Deterministic processing and operation authority

P301-01 is a substantive semantic amendment. One shared F_D kind classifies
total deterministic processing under an exact traversal: closed logical
domain, basis, material computational state and dependencies, outcomes and
termination evidence. Every domain input terminates with one declared
outcome; infinite domains are allowed where the argument establishes this.
Undeclared material dependencies and open-world semantic choices do not
qualify. The kind can classify construction; it grants no operation,
assessment, effect, admission or closure authority.

Existing unchanged-subject evaluation/proof permissions and guarantees
survive as explicit operation profiles: subject, reads, witness/log outputs,
permitted writes, results/stops, authority and invalidation. Authorized
witness persistence is distinct from constructing the judged domain subject.
Construction feeding assessment keeps the exact traversal identities,
intermediate results, evidence and stops. F_P, F_H and the five core values
are unchanged. Missing totality proof never automatically selects F_P.

ODD imports the common F_D kind by exact edition while retaining distinct
operation grants, evidence and closure contracts. Its former kind-level
authority wording is superseded by explicit authority owners; evaluation
profiles remain enforceable. Each existing use receives one evidence-sensitive
migration disposition: established common-kind eligibility with preserved
profile; pending additional evidence or a separately identified bounded
implementation; or retained exact legacy meaning with an open successor
classification. Stable URN spelling cannot migrate immutable history.

Refusal and exhaustion retain their cause and evaluated predicate. Missing
candidate output, an available indeterminate assessment report, and an
assessment not performed remain distinct. Each declared traversal stop maps
once to the five-value core and never to satisfied. Availability satisfaction
does not become satisfaction of the assessed property or release acceptance.

## Clarification and evidence scope

P301-02–05 clarify existing preservation, observation, context and evidence
obligations. They add no mandatory annotation, carrier, universal functional
model, experiment or review cycle. A representation's sufficiency is relative
to fixed consumer observations, policies, inputs and relevant horizon. It
retains every required standing, obligation, validity, causal and authority
distinction. Equal representations requiring incompatible decisions falsify
that claim. Functional claims demonstrate their declared factorization and,
where claimed, transition/readout preservation; relational frames retain
their own contracts. A four-state witness that aggregates no states proves
neither compression nor cheapest sufficient context.

Qualified resolvable references may preserve the required record. A typed
annotation or candidate digest alone supplies no sufficiency proof. Category
and functor laws establish their structural claim, while consumer adequacy
requires its own preservation evidence; H1 remains unresolved. Optional
representation experiments compare substantive information and access,
counterexample retention and validity, the existing contract and the simplest
adequate plain-text repair, with declared oracles and total relevant costs.

P301-06 carries self-contained positive and negative cases into native
conformance, including human, mixed and code bindings, authority and the
three exhaustion predicates. Every F_D consumer and material nonliteral
consumer is reconciled against the shared owner; generated frames and
metadata are source coherent. The representation must be renewed from the
exact frozen source; historical representations remain historical.

## Compatibility and inherited obligations

The version label promises no automatic patch compatibility: this amendment
broadens purposes and strengthens qualification. Old consumers keep their
exact selected meaning. Downstream adoption, ABI/GTL runtime implementation,
the v2 cohort and sibling Product versions are outside this amendment.

Existing standalone-conservation, reduction, interpretation/fidelity,
independence, actual-use and acceptance obligations remain applicable under
their owning conditions. Finite examples and structural checks are bounded
evidence, not whole-method semantic acceptance. Source dispositions identify
affected predecessor claims and their replacement loci; proposed
supersessions remain pending until their owner accepts them.
