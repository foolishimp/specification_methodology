# Common Requirements

The common requirements bind both build tenants' candidates. This file holds the requirements that come from this Product. The other files hold the rest:

- [PRINCIPLES](PRINCIPLES.md): the working foundations, the candidate first principles both tenants built from at C1, and their review questions.
- [CAPABILITIES](CAPABILITIES.md): the capabilities required by the reconstruction and the questions that test their adequacy. Each trial binds its own subject and acceptance scope.
- [ASSURANCE](ASSURANCE.md): preservation, assertion and observation obligations, mutual assurance, provenance, risk and targeted reassessment through construction and change.
- [A_C_DEPENDENCY](A_C_DEPENDENCY.md): the exact a_c theory and checker used for construction and checking, and the limits of what the checker proves. The released encoding basis follows its separate owner decision.
- [PREDECESSOR_CLAIMS](PREDECESSOR_CLAIMS.md): the common claim inventory under STDO-UP-015. Each tenant's frontier names its proposed dispositions and required carrying loci; its ticket supplies acceptance standing.
- [STDO_3_0_1](STDO_3_0_1.md): the selected bounded amendment for the common successor, its exact native predecessor, operation-profile migration and preserved acceptance obligations.

The working foundation statements in PRINCIPLES are content under evaluation, not installed law. The installed 2.5.1-rc.2 release is the law the builders work under, and it is the predecessor the new release evolves from.

The Product Definition (`stdo_3.json`) adds two local axioms: the Product's [identity](../PRODUCT.md#product-identity-and-lineage) and [development isolation](../PRODUCT.md#development-isolation) sections. Its override and disambiguation lists are empty, so it overrides no installed rule and disambiguates no installed term.

A determination is a rule, definition, list of allowed values or procedure step that an actor must apply.

A consumer is a rule or task entry that uses another rule's determination. Contracting repeated determinations means replacing their independent versions with use of one shared owner, while keeping necessary domain bindings.

A material determination can affect the required meaning, permitted work or acceptance of the successor. The candidate accounts for that scope; the independent Reviewer assesses its coverage and the reasons given for exclusions. Sampling supports only its stated scope and cannot make missing coverage immaterial.

## Successor Disposition Requirement

Source: [STDO-UP-015](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/RELEASE_METHOD.md#successor-baseline-conservation-stdo-up-015), a rule of the installed release. Every predecessor claim that could affect the successor receives exactly one disposition before the successor is accepted:

- `conserved`: the successor keeps the claim;
- `superseded`: an accepted successor relation replaces the claim, and is named;
- `intentionally removed`: the product owner, as Product authority, removes the claim and gives the reason;
- `not applicable`: the claim has no effect on the successor, with a bounded reason.

Conservation is about meaning. Similar naming does not prove it, and the absence of an edit carrying a claim does not prove the claim was lost. UP-015 does not by itself require a kept claim to be rewritten in the successor's wording. `reframed` is a note on how a conserved claim was carried, not a disposition.

A conservation judgment compares the decisions permitted and required on the same material case, scope and authority: what must happen, what may happen, what is prohibited, and what follows when a trigger, exception or required premise is absent. The reviewer examines the owning text and necessary consumer bindings, including counterexamples that would expose a changed decision. If the successor changes one of those determinations, the change needs a replacement relation; if the comparison cannot decide, conservation remains unresolved. Shared arguments and cases may cover equivalent claims with their coverage stated; the test does not require a new artifact or execution for every row.

Modal wording is judged by that meaning. Present-tense indicative wording can state a binding law; replacing “must” or “shall” is neither automatic conservation nor automatic supersession. An obligation must remain obligatory, a preference must remain a preference, and every permitted exception and scope condition must survive. A package-wide assertion that its wording is equivalent does not establish those relationships at its deciding consumers.

Each supersession names the affected claim, the replacement determination and its owning successor locus or loci. A shared relation may replace several claims, but each row identifies how its claim is replaced and cites the product owner's recorded acceptance under [Construction Authority](../PRODUCT.md#construction-authority). A changed obligation is not conserved merely because its heading survives. During construction a proposed disposition and replacement remain distinguishable from an accepted one; a refreshed hash supplies neither semantic comparison nor acceptance.

## Standalone Successor Requirement

Source: this Product. The successor stands alone. Every `conserved` claim is stated in the successor's own text, so that the successor is complete and usable without the predecessor in force. A link to the predecessor shows where a claim came from, not that it is conserved. Under this requirement a link alone satisfies no disposition. Construction establishes the axioms, meaning the candidate's declared foundational premises, then restates the predecessor corpus on them under the relationships below.

Restated on the axioms. The successor is on its axioms when four relationships hold:

1. Foundation to law. A family of deciding contracts is a group of related rules that decide outcomes; a material family is one whose outcomes matter to the product. Each material family identifies three things:
   - the foundational premises it rests on;
   - the consequences that follow from them;
   - any additional domain or policy choices, with their owners.

   The whole set of material deciding rules is accounted for, including inherited rules. The candidate states its grouping and binds the covered rules, applicable pillar contributions and actual consumers to each argument. A rule with no separate consumer states the supported scope of that absence; a blank field or generic referral does not establish either coverage or non-applicability. Independent review assesses that coverage; a document preamble or coarser grouping cannot hide an unsupported rule. Numbering and file boundaries do not prescribe the grouping. A demonstration over a few members is not presented as a derivation of every member.
2. Ownership and use. The Spec Method and the profiles use the shared contracts at the points where an actor actually enters and applies them, not only by cross-reference, without conflicting definitions or competing owners. An explanatory cross-reference alone does not establish this relationship.
3. Standalone conservation. Text the successor owns supplies every conserved determination needed for use.
4. Reduction. The pillars determine the shared laws and their specialisations under the [reduction requirement](#foundational-reduction-requirement). Repeated determinations are contracted into their shared owners, and material independent mechanisms that remain have a stated need.

Unchanged predecessor text satisfies these where it carries the relationships. Copied text does not establish the foundational relationship just by being present, and the share of text rewritten is not evidence that a relationship holds.

Ordinary entry identifies the selected candidate and governing input. Its method, interpretation and qualification routes agree on their respective subjects and standing; historical artifacts remain bound to theirs. A target version in a manifest is distinguished from acceptance or publication at the package entry. A version label alone establishes no released status.

## Foundational Reduction Requirement

Source: this Product's [Foundational Reduction](../PRODUCT.md#foundational-reduction). The reconstruction must make the method simpler to determine and apply while preserving its necessary distinctions.

1. Account for each pillar's material contribution. Separate foundational premises, derived consequences, domain policies and implementation choices. A named calculus or a conceptual correspondence is insufficient without the deciding rules and consumers it governs.
2. Identify recurring determinations across the predecessor and the candidate. Establish one shared owner and replace their independent determination at each affected consumer with that law or an explicit refinement. Adding a common introduction while the consumers retain rival rules does not satisfy reduction.
3. Preserve required distinctions while consolidating machinery. Different domains may use the same transformation without acquiring equal identity, meaning, authority or evidence. A retained material primitive or independent policy identifies the distinction it supplies and why the shared contracts do not already decide it. This is stated in existing design and proof surfaces, not a new record for every helper.

   Conservation includes a rule's scope, modal force, triggers and exceptions. Moving a conditional or preferred policy into shared machinery cannot silently make it unconditional; replacing a domain rule with a reference cannot lose a required trigger or send responsibility back to the referring consumer. The shared contract supplies its determination, and the domain owner supplies the additional binding or policy it needs.
4. Reconstruct member boundaries from those responsibilities. Predecessor files, headings, procedural stages and actor arrangements impose no one-to-one successor partition. A conserved determination may move to a shared owner. Its consumers carry their necessary bindings; they do not reproduce the common law as a second authority. Intentionally removing a material obligation still requires its owning decision under the disposition requirement.
5. Demonstrate reduction on actual deciding paths. A deciding path is the rules, bindings and acquired information used from a declared entry to determine a permitted result or action for a task. Bind the exact predecessor and the preceding candidate named in that tenant's admitted execution contract before the reconstruction being assessed, with materially comparable tasks, inputs, authority and acquisition conditions. If no preceding candidate exists, record that fact; it does not waive the predecessor comparison. Cover each claimed recurring determination and its materially affected entry and consumer paths. Justified equivalent cases may share a comparison; the independent Reviewer checks the grouping and uncovered material paths. Acquisition includes the material actually obtained through the entry and its dependencies, with supplied context, repeated reads and measurement boundaries identified. Comparing file counts or loading a whole corpus does not establish ordinary acquisition cost.

   Reduction is established where a material recurring determination is supplied by one shared law used by its affected consumers, removing a previously independent determination, mechanism or necessary reconciliation. Identify the removed work and justify the material distinctions that remain. Preserve the required permissions, obligations, failures and uncertainty; changed meaning needs its accepted replacement relation. Declare the measured dimensions and any proposed tradeoff before the comparison, against the selected task and outcome, in the existing activation or qualification record. Disclose adverse changes against both comparison subjects. An unexplained increase in acquisition or necessary reconciliation prevents a satisfied conclusion that the affected path is simpler to determine and apply. A justified increase preserves a necessary distinction or binding, or follows an explicitly declared tradeoff whose adequacy the independent Reviewer assesses; a favourable change in another measure does not silently excuse it. A supported elimination witness remains supporting evidence when the required improvement is not established; it cannot make criterion 6 `satisfied` for that path. The judgment is `indeterminate` when evidence cannot decide the required improvement and `falsified` when a counterexample defeats it. Word count, a diagram, rewritten prose or a passing structural checker alone supplies no reduction witness, and no universal numerical reduction threshold is imposed.
6. Challenge the reconstruction with independently specified successful, refused and unresolved cases where applicable, including cross-domain composition, failure preservation and recovery. Disclose compared subjects, tasks, acquisition conditions, results and evidence limits. The expectations are fixed independently of the author before the trial. The Reviewer checks applicability and uncovered obligations rather than inferring complete coverage from selected examples.

The integrated candidate assessment covers its material shared owners and consumers across member boundaries. The construction supplies complete accounting; independent review evaluates its adequacy and the positive reduction evidence. Member assessment supplies the applicable arguments, local path comparisons and counterexamples for its cone; integrated assessment checks their composition, material coverage and overall conclusion. Unaccounted material rules, unjustified rival determinations, lost distinctions, unexplained adverse changes or missing required comparison evidence prevent a satisfied reduction conclusion. A smaller corpus is not required where retaining necessary meaning costs more; that cost remains visible in the comparison.

## Constraint Surfaces And Logic Compression

Source: this Product's [Foundational Reduction](../PRODUCT.md#foundational-reduction) and [Method Structure](../PRODUCT.md#method-structure).

Purpose and material relation: actors need to recover the constraints governing a task within finite context and attention. Logic compression reduces acquisition and interpretation work while preserving the obligations, permitted choices and unresolved dependencies that determine lawful work.

1. A constraint surface states the obligations and permitted freedom for a declared subject and work boundary: its objects and meanings, relevant predicates and dependencies, admissible operations, required results, authority, evidence and explicit unresolved conditions. It constrains work without prescribing the worker's internal solution strategy beyond those obligations.
2. The method's a_c interpretation represents this logic from its owning prose. It binds both the exact method source it represents and the calculus basis and signature used to represent it. Its objects, relations and constraints distinguish a premise, a derived consequence, a policy choice, an observation and an unresolved claim. The dependency structure may share premises and contain revisable relationships; no tree-only encoding or execution order is imposed.
3. Coverage accounts for every material owned determination and its required dependencies, conditions, exceptions, permitted choices and unresolved matters. Each resolves to its current owning source and basis. Grouping and encoding remain the tenant's choices. An explicit residual or external predicate retains its source and required acquisition; it cannot authorise dependent work before its required premise is established. Missing or unrecoverable material obligations prevent C3 closure for that tenant. A selected projection or mechanically checked fragment supports only its declared scope. A missing premise or omitted obligation cannot become permission or a satisfied result.
4. A task projection carries sufficient relevant constraints and the required material inputs or acquisition routes under the frame requirement. Abstract rules alone do not supply observations, a valid effect grant or task context. Actor context remains disposable; the workspace and selected authoritative inputs remain the basis of reacquisition.
5. Prose, formal interpretation and acquired views express the same owned determination for their declared use. A representation that changes the permitted or required work is a fidelity defect unless the change is an explicitly authorised successor. An approximate representation declares its adequacy relation and end-to-end bound; exact authority and permission distinctions are not silently weakened by approximation.
6. Ordinary entry selects the current exact interpretation and its limits. The offered source, signature, program, maps, bindings and acquisition instructions form a reproducible publication on the named subject. Evidence follows that entry through its declared dependencies; a successful internal generation check does not qualify an untested or stale published route. Historical models are identified as historical. Required fidelity evidence compares source and representation on independently specified permissible, prohibited and unresolved cases; a source-first reviewer checks the relationship, coverage and actual task use. Member assessment covers the affected interpretation and views; C3 closure for that tenant requires complete required coverage and independently assessed fidelity. Separately selecting a mechanical check does not make semantic fidelity optional.
7. Mechanical validation, mathematical argument, semantic judgment and actual-use evidence establish their respective claims. Logic compression does not make every predicate decidable or confer operation or acceptance authority. The [a_c dependency contract](A_C_DEPENDENCY.md) retains its separate theory, interpretation, carrier and checker boundaries.

## Bounded Transitions And Reference Frames

Source: [Construction And Context Management](../PRODUCT.md#construction-and-context-management), [P4–P6](PRINCIPLES.md#p4-typed-composition-and-conservation), and the installed [Reference Frame laws](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#reference-frame-laws), including [RF-009 No Assumed Hierarchy](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#rf-009-no-assumed-hierarchy).

1. Each frame binds the construction problem and the applicable transition obligations under the [universal construction requirement](#universal-construction-requirement). Its subject, current state, destination constraints and permitted operation remain sufficient to decide its bounded task.
2. Context management selects a frame that fits the actor's declared capability and contains the contract and material sufficient for its task. Known material relations appear in the frame or enter through declared authoritative inputs. Irrelevant material stays outside active attention unless needed to establish irrelevance. Capacity pressure never licenses omission of a material obligation; refine the task, select a capable actor or return the applicable stop.
3. Context fit and adequate attention are evaluated separately. Bytes or tokens measure the selected representation's size. Actual-use evidence evaluates whether the actor applies the frame adequately to its declared task and conditions. It establishes no universal attention score. Time, spend and other resource limits remain those of the selected execution binding.
4. A configuration assigns responsibilities and information exchanges to frames from the task's operations, dependencies, actor capabilities and assurance requirements. Its design explains the allocation, including material resource limits and assumptions about failure. It may use one frame or several, fixed or adaptive allocation, with no prescribed role inventory or hierarchy. The permitted allocation and reconfiguration preserve material dependencies, outstanding obligations, effects, admitted authority and required independence. Functional decomposition, communication, authority and evaluation dependency remain distinct. Selection or naming of a frame grants no authority. Each activation still binds its subject, result, capability and admitted authority. Required reviewer independence retains its authorship and evidence conditions; common sources and relevant evidence may be shared, while a fresh context alone does not satisfy independence.
5. The workspace is the persistent material state on which construction operates, including work in progress and its declared authoritative external inputs. An actor's context is a disposable projection, not a second truth surface. An activation observes the subject and basis its contract binds, including selected snapshots and external systems; material absent from its acquired view is not thereby absent from the bound subject. Admissible contrary evidence reopens the affected claim under its drift and re-entry rules. It does not silently rebind the basis, change a governing requirement or confer authority. Acquisition reconciles material disagreement with cached beliefs or summaries. Discarding context does not reset the workspace. Resumption starts from current state and retains applicable valid work. New observations may invalidate affected claims, and unknown effects are resolved sufficiently for the next dependent action. The applicable recovery contract determines necessary observations and effects; no universal reset or complete-history replay is imposed. Closed results support bounded handoffs without private working context. Their reuse follows [change impact and targeted reassessment](ASSURANCE.md#change-impact-and-targeted-reassessment), checking material source, input, task, actor-condition and dependency changes for these handoffs; unchanged artifact bytes alone do not establish applicability or independence.

Purpose and material relation: finite context and attention require bounded, sufficient work and assessment. Task structure and assurance requirements constrain how that work is organised. These obligations preserve the selected destination through decomposition, coordination and recovery without deriving purpose or authority from resource limits. The configuration is stated in existing design, frame and work records; no additional carrier is required.

An assessment result is bound to its actual producer, subject, governing basis and supporting evidence before it satisfies a required assessment. Planned membership alone does not authenticate a returned judgement. A failure and its citations remain visible when separate required coverage is missing. This authentication of a returned assessment remains distinct from the [assertion and execution provenance](ASSURANCE.md#parallel-derivation-and-mutual-assurance) supporting its claims. No cryptographic or transport protocol is prescribed.

## Universal Construction Requirement

Source: this Product's [Universal Construction Model](../PRODUCT.md#universal-construction-model), connecting [P1–P6](PRINCIPLES.md#universal-construction-synthesis). The model governs actual work selection, construction, evaluation, correction and closure.

1. Bind A and Z under the [universal construction model](../PRODUCT.md#universal-construction-model), including the described current state's material unknowns and the required outcome and acceptance conditions. Several realised states may satisfy Z. Lawful transformations follow the applicable declared calculi under the [build-tenant binding](#build-tenant-binding-requirement); the governing specification supplies Z. Lawfulness alone establishes neither delivery nor optimality. Distinguish required conditions, observations and any causal or predictive model used to select an intervention. The applicability and adequacy of those models are separate claims. The workspace and authoritative external observations supply current evidence under the [frame requirement](#bounded-transitions-and-reference-frames).
2. A strategy arranges admissible construction and assurance to serve Z within the selected resource envelope. Its material choices account for context, attention, time, effort, computation, communication and other relevant resources, together with feedback delay, uncertainty, integration exposure and rework. Planning, checking and coordination consume resources too. Measures used to justify the choice state their units, boundaries and composition rules. Estimates, assumptions and observations remain distinguishable. Multiple objectives can leave alternatives incomparable; the owning criteria decide their tradeoff. A strategy states its material rationale in existing design or work records. No exhaustive optimisation, universal numerical budget, scalar cost measure or preferred strategy is imposed.
3. Selected steps compose through compatible preconditions, effects, results and authority, preserving the applicable outer obligations at their composition boundaries. Refinement can add detail and construction can change state within the permitted variation. Decomposition, delegation, concurrent work and recursive application retain the affected obligations. Local success supplies end-to-end success only when the required coverage, composition compatibility and outcome evidence hold. Any categorical claim meets the [demonstration standard](#categorical-demonstration-standard).
4. Feedback observes the current subject, compares it with the governing conditions, diagnoses a material discrepancy, selects an authorised response, acts and observes the result. For work management, bugs are a category of feature/change work within this process. Diagnosis distinguishes a defective realisation, an inadequate governing requirement or design, an inaccurate predictive model, and insufficient observation. A prediction error alone does not decide whether the required outcome holds. Diagnosis preserves unresolved alternatives and routes each supported repair to its affected owner rather than inferring a repair from the intake label. Severity describes consequence; priority orders response under the selected outcome, dependencies, resources and authority. A bug list projects existing work state; its label creates no separate delivery or authority mechanism.
5. A change to Z follows its owning authority and re-entry rules. It does not establish that the prior Z was reached. New observations can reopen affected claims while independently applicable work remains usable. Resumption reacquires current reality and retains valid progress; unknown effects are resolved sufficiently before the next dependent action under the frame and recovery contracts. A revised model does not erase the counterexample or automatically repair the realisation.
6. Recursive and repeated work distinguish preservation, progress, termination and outcome satisfaction. A formal induction or termination claim supplies its premises and argument; no universal termination proof is required before bounded work can begin. Where such a proof is absent, the claim stays within the available evidence. Continuing work has a supported purpose under the selected strategy, which can include reducing uncertainty or testing a bounded hypothesis. Discovery may enlarge the known work, and repeated activity alone establishes no progress. Control handles failure, unavailable information, uncertain effects and exhausted resources through the applicable continuation, repair, recovery, re-entry, yield or stop. Ongoing maintenance can continue under its mandate; a delivery claim needs its completion condition.
7. Evaluation uses the [single result algebra](#single-result-algebra-requirement), preserving failure, missing coverage and uncertainty. Formal proof, mechanical checking, testing and observed use establish their respective claims on their stated subjects, assumptions and boundaries. Producing a candidate, proving a property and accepting delivery remain distinct. Completion requires sufficient evidence against Z, including its acceptance conditions; exhaustion or an unresolved attempt proves neither completion nor impossibility.
8. Each candidate states this relation at its shared owner and makes its material consumers apply it under the [single ownership](#single-ownership-requirement) and [reduction](#foundational-reduction-requirement) requirements. Domain strategy, actor configuration, execution mechanisms and additional assurance policies retain their owners. Existing work and evidence carriers hold the necessary state; the model adds no independent ledger, required role sequence or orchestration runtime.

Purpose and material relation: bounded activity can consume resources without delivering the intended outcome, and isolated checks can conceal a failed composition. The construction model connects purpose, action, observation and correction so that both features and defects remain governed by their end-to-end obligations.

[Assurance of Construction](ASSURANCE.md) owns the formal obligations and evidence relationships used by this construction requirement. Its preservation account applies at composition; its assertion and observation contracts apply at evaluation; its dependency and applicability conditions apply at change, recovery and closure. The required a_c interpretation and acquired views preserve these relationships under the constraint-surface requirement. Domain consumers bind their tests and other assessments to those owners rather than introducing separate meanings for assurance or reuse.

## Build-Tenant Binding Requirement

Source: this Product's [Build-Tenant Binding](../PRODUCT.md#build-tenant-binding). Each candidate's Spec Method states and applies this relation between a product's common WHAT and its tenant realisations.

1. A build tenant binds the common requirements calculus to a declared computational calculus and binds that calculus to a concrete execution environment. These bindings are part of its existing design and realisation surfaces.
2. The execution environment may be a technology stack, a human process or a combination. Its selection remains within the common requirements, including any required human decision. Generic procedures are method procedures whose scope is independent of the selected execution environment. They and their projections preserve those permitted bindings. Code, an event stream or reconstruction from complete history is required only when the governing obligations or selected execution binding require it. Author identity, actor identity and operation kind remain distinct; a person performing an operation does not by itself make that operation human adjudication.
3. The binding identifies the operations, composition laws, effects, resource measures and operational assumptions material to conformance. Human effort, elapsed time, communication and machine resources are measured where the selected obligations need them, with their units and boundaries stated.
4. Conformance evidence establishes how the bound realisation satisfies the required meaning and constraints, including applicable authority, independence, failure and recovery conditions. A convenient stack cannot weaken an obligation. A categorical preservation claim also meets the [demonstration standard](#categorical-demonstration-standard).
5. A computational calculus may be reused across tenants and execution environments where the bindings satisfy its laws. A different language, tool or team does not by itself require a different calculus.
6. Representation of the requirements and bindings in a_c does not prescribe their executor or establish execution conformance through structural validation alone.

Purpose and material relation: the same common obligations can be realised through different means. The tenant owns those choices and their evidence, while the common specification retains its meaning. This keeps a particular stack from becoming universal method machinery and permits human and mixed processes without a parallel methodology.

## Single Ownership Requirement

Source: this Product's [Intent](../INTENT.md#what-the-product-is).

A determination's owner is an owning locus: one section of one member. Owner here names a place in the text, not a person with decision authority.

1. Within one candidate, every determination has exactly one owning locus. Each tenant candidate is its own scope, so the same determination appearing in both candidates is not a second owner.
2. The owning responsibility follows the [C3 responsibility table](../PRODUCT.md#c3-construction-contract):
   - the Foundations own what holds for any method;
   - the Spec Method owns what holds for this method;
   - a domain profile, such as world modelling, or an operational profile, such as the frame catalogues and Executive procedure, owns only its own bindings: the terms and choices it adds for its domain or operation;
   - the guides own their posting, writing and glossary conventions;
   - packaging owns representation formats and distribution mechanics, deriving method determinations from their method-law owners.
3. Every other locus refers to the owner or refines it. A refinement names its owner and maps each of its values or cases back to the owner's. It never contradicts or redefines the owner's determination.
4. A reference frame's exact copy of a clause names and matches its owning locus. A derived view, including an a_c projection, may express it differently while meeting the [constraint-surface fidelity requirement](#constraint-surfaces-and-logic-compression). Neither is a second owner. A changed determination is a fidelity defect, repaired at the source or derivation that caused it.
5. Conserved text restated under the [standalone successor requirement](#standalone-successor-requirement) is the successor's own owning locus for that determination, not a duplicate of the predecessor.
6. A part ported under [Cross-Synthesis](../PRODUCT.md#cross-synthesis) takes one owning locus in the receiving candidate.
7. Where two loci decide the same question, one is made the owner and the other refers to it or refines it. No rule of precedence between loci substitutes for that.
8. A required generic determination is available without adopting an optional profile. The generic owner supplies that obligation; a profile owns the additional policy that applies when it is selected.

Purpose and material relation, meaning why the requirement exists and what it protects: a question decided in two places is interpreted twice and can be answered two ways. One owning locus gives one answer per question within a candidate. The [single result algebra requirement](#single-result-algebra-requirement) is this requirement applied to results.

## Single Result Algebra Requirement

Source: this Product ([Result Algebra](../PRODUCT.md#result-algebra)). It unifies the previous release's two evaluation algebras:

- RF-012, the closed results of reference-frame evaluations;
- AC-007, the constraint and refusal outcomes of a_c.

1. The Foundations own exactly one core result algebra. Results of constraints, judgements, frames, stops, work and effects are stated in it or as refinements of it.
2. A member or profile that needs finer outcomes defines a refinement at its owning locus. Every refined value, on its declared predicate and with the conditions needed to determine its meaning, maps to exactly one core value. Missing required conditions remain explicit; the consumer does not guess from a label. A referral to the core is insufficient when the core requires the domain to supply that mapping. No evaluation or decision outcome exists outside the core.
3. A refinement never raises a result's standing:
   - a falsified or failed result never maps to a satisfied or successful one;
   - a result that admissible evidence cannot decide never maps to satisfied;
   - an evaluation that was not performed stays distinct from one whose evidence cannot decide;
   - an effect whose completion is unknown never maps to no effect or to completed.
4. The core value travels with the evaluated predicate and the original domain outcome. A mapping never owns domain meaning; the domain owner keeps it.
5. A step having produced a candidate never maps to the candidate's fitness or acceptance. Availability, assessment and acceptance remain separate results.
6. Conjunction and composition of results are defined once, over the core.
7. A reference frame whose task returns a result carries the core algebra or its profile's mapping into it.

A recorded lifecycle state, classification or selected action is not automatically an evaluation result. Where it contributes to a material evaluation or authorised decision, its owner states that purpose and how the determination returns through the core. Calling an output a state does not exempt a determination that decides compliance, admission, closure or disposition. Assessing a condition and exercising authority to act on that assessment remain distinct, even when they use the same domain word.

Purpose and material relation: this is the [single ownership requirement](#single-ownership-requirement) applied to results, as the [Intent](../INTENT.md#what-the-product-is) directs. One set of outcome values across the tiers removes competing ownership of results. Items 3 to 5 keep, when results are combined, the distinctions between failure, undecidability, non-performance and uncertain effects. Those distinctions are required by [P5 and P6](PRINCIPLES.md): finite capacity with graceful handling, and justified claims with separate authority.

## Categorical Demonstration Standard

Source: this Product ([Categorical Construction](../PRODUCT.md#categorical-construction)).

A categorical claim about the method, or a construction made with it, is established only when the applicable obligations in items 1 to 5 are met. Its author states the claim and needed premises; the Reviewer checks that applicability. The defining laws of a claimed category or functor cannot be excluded by declaring them inapplicable. Acceptance of the categorical capability, and any claim asserting the worked join itself, also require item 6:

1. Objects and arrows. For each category claimed: its objects, its arrows, when two arrows are equal, its identities, and associative composition.
2. Preservation per layer. Each refinement layer states what it preserves of the layer above and discharges that obligation. An exact step preserves meaning. An approximate step states its adequacy relation (how close is close enough) and an end-to-end bound. A fixed tolerance at each step does not compose into an end-to-end bound.
3. Licensing laws. A construction step is admissible under the laws declared for its subject. With no law declared, only universally valid steps, which hold whatever laws the subject has, are admissible.
4. Categorical joins. A map between categories is defined on objects and arrows. Its functor laws, that it preserves identities and composition, are shown before it is claimed to be a functor. Conformance, equivalence and functorial preservation are distinct claims.
5. Representation and evidence.
   - Laws, records and maps are represented in a_c, retaining which statements are premises, derived consequences or declared choices.
   - the a_c theory, its interpretations, their encoded carriers and the checkers that evaluate them keep separate identities and proof boundaries.
   - Structural validation checks structure only.
   - Each claim names its supporting evidence and scope, distinguishing mechanical checks, mathematical arguments and tests against independently specified oracles. What that evidence leaves unresolved is recorded separately.
6. Worked join. One algorithm category joined with one computation category is the acceptance instance for the categorical capability.

A categorical statement without the demonstration it requires is a hypothesis. Purpose and material relation: the categorical direction ([Intent](../INTENT.md#mission)) is useful only where its constructions preserve meaning, and these items are what preservation must show.
