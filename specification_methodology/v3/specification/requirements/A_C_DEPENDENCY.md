# a_c Dependency And Evaluation Boundary

## Standing

Three things are kept distinct:

- the a_c theory, which is the calculus itself;
- a subject's interpretation in a_c: a model whose symbols and constraints are bound to that subject's meaning;
- the carriers and tools that hold and check it.

This product uses the separately owned Axiom Indexer, a program that checks a_c, for later evaluation. Each candidate owns its required method interpretation. Its documents, interpretation and encoded carrier have distinct content identities and proof boundaries; that separation does not exclude the interpretation from the candidate.

The Product Definition's composition field names `../../axiom_indexer/stdo_default.json`, resolved relative to the v3 source root containing `stdo_3.json`. It locates the neighbouring Axiom Indexer Product Definition. Its source path can change, and the definition found there does not replace the fixed dependency pin below. No installed copy of the Axiom Indexer 2.5.1-rc.2 release is selected or verified for this source project. A fixed, tagged version is available. Nothing is acquired or run until an evaluation is separately selected.

## Directed Mechanical Dependency Relation

Relation identity: `urn:stdo-3:relation:a-c-mechanical-evaluation-dependency`. It runs from this product (`urn:stdo:product-definition:stdo-3`) to the separately owned Axiom Indexer (`urn:stdo:product-definition:axiom-indexer`). Its purpose is mechanical checking of an explicitly selected, frozen part of this product under this contract. The neighbouring definition says where the Axiom Indexer is; the fixed pins below identify the exact Axiom Indexer release used, the dependency's mechanical subject.

The product owner selects and accepts this dependency's use within this product. The Axiom Indexer keeps its own Product meaning and its own repair and release authority. Before any mechanical use, an activation requires:

- a separately selected evaluation;
- its exact subject and contract in this product;
- verification, through the selected installed copy, of the required Axiom Indexer release, its member list and their bytes.

A tag being available does not make it installed. No installed copy or evaluation is selected here.

The following invalidate the affected use or reuse:

- a change to the Axiom Indexer's Product Definition or identity;
- drift in its release or members;
- a change in the contract's meaning;
- results bound to a different subject or basis.

The dependent claim is then withheld and returned to its actual owner for verification and explicit rebinding, which records the new exact version the claim depends on. Results that rely only on unchanged, still-supported relations need not be discarded. This section defines this one dependency and introduces no general installation or runtime protocol.

## Proof Claims

The interpretation serves the [constraint-surface and logic-compression requirement](README.md#constraint-surfaces-and-logic-compression). It makes the source's governing dependencies and remaining freedom recoverable for work. This relation is evaluated separately from checker validity: a well-formed model that omits a material condition can admit work the source prohibits. Required semantic coverage follows the method's owned determinations, not the number of clauses the checker happens to accept.

For each selected, frozen tenant output, keep:

- the source-to-model dispositions, which record how each source clause is represented in the a_c model;
- the signature, which is the declared vocabulary;
- the exact program, maps and bindings;
- reproducible mechanical results;
- a fidelity judgement made independently of the author, reading the source first;
- evidence from actual use.

The installed a_c rules [AC-017 and AC-018](stdo://releases/v2.5.1-rc.2/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md#ac-017-interpretation-fidelity) separate two properties:

- fidelity: whether the model preserves the source's meaning, including its premises, consequences and declared choices;
- structure: whether the model is well-formed under the selected checking contract.

[Axiom validation](../../../../axiom_indexer/specification/requirements/REQ-P-VALIDATION.md) checks structure. Unless a separately selected contract requires it, it does not interpret every logical, operator, arity or type rule. It never proves truth, satisfiability or acceptance, that a full model satisfies the axioms, or that a theorem holds.

a_c is derived from the method's own principles. Running independent procedures over it does not give it an independent axiomatic origin.

A later test may use a neutral, finite token-transfer example, in which tokens move between holders and which belongs to no project. It would test a selected part of the calculus against independently specified expectations about ownership, conservation and permission, including successful, refused and uncertain cases. It would not establish the whole calculus or qualify this product.

When results disagree, the cause is located by testing four candidates:

- the method's meaning;
- the interpretation and signature;
- the carrier and tooling;
- the calculus.

The cause may be uncertain or plural. A tool's inability to handle something does not by itself prove a defect in the calculus. Any repair goes to the actual owner through an explicit successor, and the checker used for comparison stays fixed.

Tenant a_c outputs do not become the released STDO Representation, the separate product that publishes the method's canonical a_c map. [The existing Representation](../../../../stdo_representation/specification/PRODUCT.md) stays bound to its exact 2.5.1-rc.2 subject, with its own acceptance and release conditions.
