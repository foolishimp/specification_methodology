## Shared Coordinates, Evidence, And Results

Semantic coordinates compare by URI, owner, scope, and exact content identity.
Candidate coordinates compare by complete inventory digest, Product member set,
claim bytes, dependency identities, and qualification basis. Exact-cut
coordinates add the annotated tag object, peeled commit, tree, and remote refs.
Same names, paths, counts, or prose do not establish equality.

Admissible evidence is exact source or immutable reacquisition evidence,
URI-addressed claims and counterexamples, deterministic diagnostics, observed
tool outputs, retained dogfood results, Git object identities, and independent
judgments where required. Schema, hash, parse, or test success proves only its
declared mechanical property.

Each evaluation returns exactly one of:

- `satisfied`;
- `falsified` with a URI-addressed counterexample;
- `indeterminate` with missing evidence;
- `out_of_frame` with the required additional frame; or
- `invalid_basis` with the unresolved subject, source, identity, or authority.

Every result names the frame, exact activation basis, evaluator, evidence,
verdict, residuals, and invalidation conditions.

## Evaluation Inventory And Selected Frames

### F-AUTHORING-ESSENCE

- Evaluation: does the exact authored program capture operative constraints
  rather than merely restate prose, preserving source-owned symbols and material
  uncertainty, including explicit frame membership and
  role-labelled supporting premises, conditions, exceptions and qualifications?
- Evidence: exact program, selected calculus/vocabulary/frame and source URIs,
  authored dependency chains, residuals and independent source comparison where
  required by the supported claim.
- Invariants: every authored item and index remains source-grounded; a selected
  conclusion retains its declared support and uncertainty; a rule reference is
  not evidence that its premise holds. Owners retain semantic interpretation.
- Actor envelope: `K-AUTHORING`.
- Exclusions: code does not infer missing implications, conditions or frame
  applicability. Size, structural validity and apparent completeness do not
  prove useful compression or source fidelity.

### F-SYMBOLIC-INTEGRITY

- Evaluation: do exact program, index and projection identities preserve the
  declared frame/scope and finite supporting closure through reproducible URI
  bindings, including materially overlapping selections?
- Evidence: exact program, Binding Set, current source observations, map,
  selected indexes, both projection views, and missing-reference, stale-source,
  mismatched-map, alias and confinement falsifiers.
- Invariants: unique absolute identities, binding-root confinement, complete
  declared reference closure,
  exact program/map/source binding and deterministic output; shared items retain
  one identity. Both views retain the same closure, ordered relationships,
  literals, source routes and affected residuals. Cycles terminate without
  establishing semantic soundness.
- Actor envelope: `K-SYMBOLIC`.
- Exclusions: physical paths, matching names or successful resolution do not
  establish semantic identity, applicability, truth or task disposition.

### F-VALIDATION-BOUNDARY

- Evaluation: do validation, projection and joining remain inside their exact
  declared mechanical contracts and preserve all input/source bytes?
- Evidence: frozen implementation, Product/requirements, normal and optimized
  positive/refusal cases, input/source preimages and exact output comparisons.
- Invariants: code neither authors nor repairs meaning; projection requires
  explicit selection and current whole-map evidence, withholds invalid views
  and protects inputs and attempted/resolved sources, including output aliases.
  Materialization copies unchanged authored rows. The existing pure joiner
  preserves caller labels, text and order, and existing programs without indexes
  retain their applicable mechanical behavior.
- Actor envelope: `K-VALIDATION`.
- Exclusions: green tests, safe refusal or a valid projection do not establish
  source fidelity, premise satisfaction, semantic acceptance or usefulness.

### F-DOGFOOD-USEFULNESS

- Evaluation: can a fresh LLM use the exact map, explicit frame selection and
  either projection view on real bounded work, obtaining the warranted result
  compared with the same source and task evidence?
- Evidence: frozen native skill, program/map/projection identities, comparable
  source and view tasks, fresh-agent results, visible frame/scope choices,
  source re-entry and retained improvements or regressions.
- Invariants: initial context omits the complete corpus and expected answers;
  overlapping frames preserve their distinct evaluation contracts. A missing
  premise or applicable exception changes the supported disposition; unknown
  evidence remains unknown. Mechanical success and semantic-use assessment
  remain distinct. STDO-specific authored meaning stays with Representation.
- Actor envelope: `K-DOGFOOD`.
- Exclusions: fixture success, artifact presence, one native target or token
  reduction alone does not establish broader usefulness or semantic truth.

### F-CANDIDATE-READINESS

- Evaluation: is one frozen candidate ready for immutable RC publication under
  the declared Product member, claim, dependency, exclusion, qualification,
  and successor relations?
- Evidence: `releases/v2.5.1.md`, complete deterministic candidate inventory,
  member and claim bytes, dependency verification, pre-RC results, publication
  plan, and remote namespace observations.
- Invariants: candidate readiness requires no published tag; excluded carrier
  bytes gain no Product status; a satisfied result grants no publication
  operation authority.
- Actor envelope: `K-CANDIDATE`.
- Exclusions: a branch, path list, local test total, or intended tag name does
  not establish candidate readiness.

### F-EXACT-CUT

- Evaluation: does the published immutable RC exactly carry the ready candidate
  and satisfy exact-cut qualification and acceptance prerequisites?
- Evidence: annotated RC and selector tag objects, peeled commit and tree,
  remote refs, reacquired Product members and claim bytes, closed candidate
  results, and independent exact-cut review.
- Invariants: immutable RC tags never move; `axiom_indexer/v2.5.1` is only the
  mutable highest-published selector; candidate and cut bytes must agree;
  qualifying-byte repair produces a coordinated successor cut.
- Actor envelope: `K-EXACT-CUT`.
- Exclusions: this frame is not activated before publication. A lightweight
  tag, local-only ref, source-only test, or approval lacking exact-cut identity
  cannot establish exact-cut qualification.

## Current applicability, coverage and conjunction

Each activation selects the actual owning question, exact subject, actor,
evidence, source/role basis and return consumer. Available frame names activate
nothing by themselves. All applicable mandatory constraints retain valid
support; absent, stale, wrong-subject, conflicted, incapable or unknown support
withholds only its dependent conclusion. Unknown is neither false nor satisfied.

The current producer/consumer relations are:

- Exact Axiom member conservation and applicable mechanical checks produce
  structural facts. Reproduced RC1 map/report, both projection modes and actual
  stale-source refusal qualify the changed input relation. Reuse unchanged
  mechanical evidence within its exact original scope; no duplicate runtime
  matrix follows solely from a matched version change.
- Independently assessed source/program/index fidelity consumes exact source
  and current mechanical facts. It preserves all applicable permissions,
  exclusions, invalidators, premise/exception links and residuals. Structural
  success does not supply that judgment.
- Fresh native source/map contexts consume frozen subjects, the same ordinary
  task and evidence, and permitted source routes. Material executable fixture
  observations qualify their own finite properties. The separately activated
  comparison assessor acquires both finished conditions and source independently
  under the unchanged independence envelope; author agreement, private oracles
  and green deterministic checks cannot substitute for observed ordinary use.
- Current Product/source/mechanical/native results and exact dependency/member
  closure feed complete local candidate readiness. Local commit/ref construction
  follows its separate grant. No published child ref, remote publication result,
  external adoption or ABI ticket closure is a prerequisite to preparation.
- Publication, published cohort integrity and `F-EXACT-CUT` remain unselected.
  A later authorized publication requires its then-current full ref graph and
  remote expectations plus applicable release checks. Published-cut assessment
  produces its own verdict after actual publication; neither a local tag nor
  this proposal establishes it.

At proposal handoff, construction/mechanical and source-assessment records retain
their exact reported states. Native comparison, complete candidate disposition

records supply their own claim, subject, time, actor and evidence.

Only closed applicable results cross frame boundaries. A changed source,
program/map, skill, operation, grant, frame, actor capability, evidence population,
workspace observation or dependency invalidates affected conclusions. Valid
independent survivors remain usable; role or status change alone requires no
repeat derivation. A declaration change is a new assessment subject.
