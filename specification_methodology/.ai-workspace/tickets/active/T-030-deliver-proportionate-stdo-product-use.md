**M01-C1 — A computed classification walk with explicit residual judgment.**
The model requires one source-bound C output over the selected request, known
targets/effects, governing basis, and available candidate evidence. It exposes:

- affected authority layers and owners reached through explicit declarations,
  source routes, and dependency relations;
- the exact Public serialized set, including membership and value changes,
  when a candidate or applicable projection is available;
- accepted-design coverage of the proposed/actual changed bytes and relations,
  checked against explicit accepted scope and trace bindings;
- treatment determined by already applicable declared rules, alongside
  unresolved applicability, materiality, or semantic consequences; and
- exact input/result identities, producer owner, validity conditions, and
  observations that invalidate the result.

Classification is not wholly J. A declared rule over sufficient C facts can
settle treatment directly. J addresses the residual and is recorded under
M01-C2. Initially unavailable candidate bytes, missing or ambiguous mappings,
incomplete Public enumeration, and uncertain design coverage produce an explicit
unknown. A filename, matching digest, or listed design document cannot prove
semantic coverage. New candidate/effect evidence updates the affected C inputs
before dependent effects or promotion rely on the classification.

A Public delta may establish candidate nonconformance without establishing a
need to change the governing contract. Preserve and locally repair/reject under
the current contract where it satisfies the authorized desired outcome; re-enter
its actual owner where that selected outcome requires changed meaning or an
applicable assessment establishes governing insufficiency. Unknown desired
meaning remains residual J/O as applicable. A separately sufficient source
construction grant can authorize obtaining the missing candidate evidence,
without admitting its unknown behavior or widening the grant.

The model cases must distinguish an unchanged repeat consumer, a helper change
that alters Public serialization, and missing/invalidated design coverage. Roles
consume the same valid C result; a role change alone does not rerun the walk.
