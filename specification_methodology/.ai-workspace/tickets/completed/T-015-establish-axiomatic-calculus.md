## Governed Outcome

1. `AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md` owns the domain-specific, carrier-neutral `a_c`
   constitutional calculus in its own bounded context.
2. The theory defines closed signatures, typed models, relations, constraints,
   latitude, residuals, transformations, judgments, closure, projection,
   interpretation, and validity.
3. `F_D`, `F_P`, and `F_H` are exact `a_c` functor-kind identities applied only
   through `F_K[v]`; actors and domain operations are not aliases.
4. `a_c`, every `a_c.X`, and every `a_c.X.C` remain distinct governed layers
   with compositional content identities and proof boundaries; none becomes an
   independently released Product without separate authority, and this ticket
   constructs no interpretation or encoding.
5. Every fundamental record directly carries the context, owner, scope, and
   basis coordinates required by its laws, and each constraint names its
   judgment kind.
6. Exact calculus identity keeps predecessor derivation authority distinct
   from the successor publication carrier.
7. Neither the calculus nor its qualification tests define a selected subject
   interpretation, carrier profile, runtime, executable calculus, or
   conformance implementation.
8. The glossary remains a locator index and the aggregate/source-specific
   compressions bind exact deciding-source digests.

## Non-Closure Conditions

- `a_c` and a concrete interpreted subject remain collapsed into one identity;
- a carrier or downstream model defines common calculus law;
- the pure calculus defines, imports, or owns a downstream method, profile,
  Product, runtime, application binding, or specialization;
- a test-local interpreter or model becomes a rival source of calculus
  semantics or conformance authority;
- deterministic evaluation is written as carrier or subject transformation;
- structural conformance is claimed as semantic fidelity or human acceptance;
- exact calculus identity cannot bind its principle provenance and published
  bytes;
- compression digests or glossary owner locators are stale; or
- a release or downstream adoption is inferred from source authoring.
