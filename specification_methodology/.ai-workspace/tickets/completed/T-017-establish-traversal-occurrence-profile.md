   lineage, externally mutable subject bindings, observations, effect
   invocations, and projection frontiers remain distinct.

   lineage is not falsely collapsed into one acyclic graph.

   the then-current subject. Evidence, snapshots, results, and projections do
   not replace the mutable subject.
