## Product Boundary And One Owning Model

STDO retains one normative standards library and bounded subordinate manager and
plugin. It owns governing meaning, authority, applicability, evidence, and
completion conditions. Representation owns its compression, navigation, and
native frame-use capabilities; its proposed executable profile remains a later
Product decision. Axiom Indexer owns its exact mechanical dependency. ABIogenesis
owns generic execution and runtime truth. This work does not introduce an STDO
workflow engine, a second executor, or consumer-local updater law.

The proposed detailed model carrier is `specification/SCENARIOS.md`, selected and
accepted during M01. `PRODUCT.md` owns the functional capability boundary and
references that model. Generic method rules remain in their existing owning
standards. The model's distribution/qualification role must be explicit; source
Product acceptance and released standards membership remain distinct. The
proposal's thirteen identities become references to the accepted model, with no
second maintained accepted copy in this ticket or the commentary.

The model covers both the relationship with STDO and individual work. The former
includes understanding applicability, establishing context, use, evolution, and
suspension/conclusion. The latter includes outcome, proportionate treatment,
authorized action, condition evaluation, and conclusion/continuation. Owner
decisions, observation, interruption, and recovery apply wherever material.
These are logical functions, not prescribed screens, schemas, or agent stages.

### M01 Acceptance Conditions

These conditions close the three gaps identified in the follow-up review. M01
defines and evaluates the logical contracts and their case oracles; the owning
implementation follows later admission. Naming a future tool without defining
its output, scope, reuse, and falsifiers does not satisfy the model condition.

**M01-C1 — A computed classification walk with explicit residual judgment.**
The model requires one source-bound C output over the selected request, known
targets/effects, governing basis, and available candidate evidence. It exposes:

- affected authority layers and owners reached through explicit declarations,
  source routes, and dependency relations;
- the exact Public serialized set, including membership and value changes,
  when a candidate or applicable projection is available;
- accepted-design coverage of the proposed/actual changed bytes and relations,
  checked against explicit accepted scope and trace bindings;
- treatment determined by already applicable declared rules, alongside
  unresolved applicability, materiality, or semantic consequences; and
- exact input/result identities, producer owner, validity conditions, and
  observations that invalidate the result.

Classification is not wholly J. A declared rule over sufficient C facts can
settle treatment directly. J addresses the residual and is recorded under
M01-C2. Initially unavailable candidate bytes, missing or ambiguous mappings,
incomplete Public enumeration, and uncertain design coverage produce an explicit
unknown. A filename, matching digest, or listed design document cannot prove
semantic coverage. New candidate/effect evidence updates the affected C inputs
before dependent effects or promotion rely on the classification.

The model cases must distinguish an unchanged repeat consumer, a helper change
that alters Public serialization, and missing/invalidated design coverage. Roles
consume the same valid C result; a role change alone does not rerun the walk.
Actual subject/basis/evidence changes trigger the affected computation and any
residual judgment. STDO owns this logical requirement; computation remains with
the explicitly selected source/worksite/qualification tool owners.

**M01-C2 — Recorded and reusable classification/materiality judgments.**
Every J result needed by another activation is recorded once with the owning
work in the existing appropriate carrier. Its logical content identifies the
judgment/question, exact subject and governing basis, supporting C/evidence,
responsible actor and authority, conclusion and scope, uncertainty, and the
revising observation that would change it. That observation is concrete enough
for a consumer to decide whether the conclusion still applies.

An unchanged consumer recovers the J result and checks its applicability rather
than re-authoring it. Material new evidence, changed basis, or the stated
revising observation causes bounded reassessment, preserving the prior result
and its successor relation. A new role or conversation alone is not an
invalidation. The carrier lifetime follows the existing run/sprint/durable-work
rule; this introduces no separate judgment registry or ticket per judgment.

Judgment reuse does not turn J into an owner ruling, admit a contract, or satisfy
an independently required assessment by relabeling the author's result. The model
must demonstrate unchanged reuse, triggered revision, and the required-independent
assessment case without discarding valid mechanical facts.

**M01-C3 — A competent-developer reference for the minimal local envelope.**
The minimal local case is calibrated against a competent developer's cost for
the same fix and required quality/evidence under comparable starting context and
tool access. M01 binds the reference case, recorded cost and provenance, comparison
conditions, and a justified derivation of the proposed agent envelope. An absent
or ungrounded reference leaves this condition unmet.

Prior agent runs never set that reference or normalize their churn into the
allowance. They may diagnose overhead or compare subsequent improvements. Map
developer time/effort to agent time, activations, usage, and owner attention
explicitly; a human has no token count to copy. Any additional allowance has a
stated reason and owner acceptance, not an inferred entitlement from historical
agent cost. Other numeric case limits remain explicit qualification inputs under
this anchor rule.

## Evaluation Criteria

1. The model preserves all thirteen logical functions and their material
   alternatives, including applicability/readiness, a useful first action,
   owner interaction, resumption, evolution, and conclusion. Each function has
   one owner, required inputs, completion/refusal conditions, C/J/O steps, and
   a case-bound cost outcome. M01-C1, M01-C2, and M01-C3 are required model
   acceptance conditions, not deferred tooling or calibration notes.
2. Proportionality qualification includes a local realization that completes
   without unnecessary tickets, diagrams, Executive activation, or independent
   review; a material Public/authority delta caught before reliance; a governing
   relation change routed to its owner; and a mistaken route corrected within
   its allowance. A later role consumes the valid computed classification and
   recorded residual judgment; unchanged inputs do not cause another walk.
   The case oracle is established independently before exposure.
3. Direct Writer use conserves the exact grant and boundary. An actual
   multi-context coordination need activates the appropriate coordination;
   necessary independence and reserved owner decisions remain explicit. A frame
   name alone does not require a separate actor, and no role can invent authority.
4. Condition-based closure, semantic evidence sufficiency, and required owner
   judgment remain distinguishable. Review checks applicable evidence. A new
   material counterexample invalidates its affected claim while surviving
   accepted relations are reused.
5. M01-C1's declared authority traversal, Public serialization delta, and
   accepted-design coverage are supplied as a C output. Missing bindings or
   candidate evidence remain unknown. Applicable declared rules settle what
   they can; residual materiality and semantic sufficiency remain J. Prompts
   and status accounts consume the result and preserve uncertainty, including
   original diagnostics and effect status during recovery.
6. An owner can rule from a compact truthful question and a later agent consumes
   the exact ruling once, without reconstructing the history, broadening its
   interpretation, or asking for unchanged authorization again. Classification
   and materiality judgments also satisfy M01-C2: basis-bound recording,
   unchanged reuse, and a concrete revising observation, with required
   independent assessment and owner authority preserved.
7. The Reviewer overview, conditional assessment branch, design-view
   applicability, and closure reconstruction/reuse rules are mutually coherent.
   Obligations, applicability triggers, cost limits, and invalidation conditions
   are colocated at their owner and faithfully consumed by subordinate material.
   A weaker three-view gate satisfies M02's measured-cost and preserved-coverage
   condition; absent that evidence the current gate remains operative.
8. Shared native workflows retain actual positive and refusal behavior for both
   supported hosts, including read-only boundaries, existing-ticket reuse,
   no-ticket bounded work, drafted-contract refusal, and lawful same-invocation
   progression. Correct prose or skill discovery alone does not establish this.
9. T-029 proves the complete selected update and its refusal/recovery cases.
   The manager does not infer arbitrary composition, manufacture semantic map
   acceptance, or report complete readiness from a basis-only update.
10. Each qualifying case states expected and maximum activations, total and
    method-overhead time, available input/cache/output usage, owner attention,
    and recovery allowance before qualification. The minimal local envelope
    derives from M01-C3's competent-developer reference for the same fix;
    historical agent runs cannot supply its anchor. Retain end-to-end cost across
    actors, host/subject conditions, required independence, and external waits.
    Missing usage remains unknown; a valid functional result exceeding its
    envelope fails the efficiency claim. A hard cap is claimed only where an
    available mechanism enforces it.
