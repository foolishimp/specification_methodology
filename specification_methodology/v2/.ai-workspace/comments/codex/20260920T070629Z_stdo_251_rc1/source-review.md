## Discriminators and limits

- Immutable established fact across helpers: reuse permitted; changed basis or
  mutable destination: refresh affected proof before effect.
- Equivalent recomputation with different names: inspect total path; distinct
  scope/freshness/effect obligations: preserve their checks.
- Excluded native-object attack: no retention claim solely from the test;
  supported serialized malformed input: admission/refusal obligation remains.
- Temporary shadow oracle: retention needs purpose/scope/cost/removal trigger;
  enduring independent cold replay: retain only its distinct obligation.
- New conversation/status: preserve applicable C/J/O and required independent
  assessment; changed candidate: revisit only affected support.
- Ordinary actor missing a material domain rule: usability not established;
  candidate rejection under an adequate contract: not automatically a defect.
