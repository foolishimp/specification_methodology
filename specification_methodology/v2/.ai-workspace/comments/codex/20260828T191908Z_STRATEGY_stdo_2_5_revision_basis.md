### Immutable Traversal Occurrence Over Evolving Terrain

The optional occurrence theory distinguishes a reusable immutable traversal
definition from each immutable application occurrence:

```text
v = immutable traversal definition
b = exact authority and subject basis
s_n = state observed at the start of occurrence n

o_n = Occurrence(
  occurrence_id_n,
  application = F_K[v],
  b,
  s_n,
  inputs_n,
  authority_n,
  causal_predecessor_refs_n
)

Apply(o_n, inputs_n, s_n)
  -> result_n + authorized_effect(s_n, s_n_plus_1)
  | stop_n
```

The traversal definition may recur. The occurrence does not. A retry, repair,
re-entry, resume, or later lifecycle pass creates a fresh occurrence with fresh
identity over the then-current state.

ODD specializes `s_n` as the mutable workspace `W_n`:

```text
A_n = immutable binding of current observation/context over W_n
B_n = immutable typed intended outcome

o_n = Bind(F_K[v], workspace_binding, A_n, B_n, authority_n)

effect_realizer(o_n, W_n)
  -> mutate W_n toward B_n
  -> W_n_plus_1

Delta_n = Observe(W_n, W_n_plus_1)
J_n = Evaluate(A_n -> B_n, Delta_n, admitted_evidence_n)

lawful continuation
  -> fresh o_n_plus_1 over W_n_plus_1
```

The workspace is a parameter of every occurrence and the carrier of compounded
construction value. `A_n` is an immutable observation/binding over `W_n`, not a
replacement copy of it. `B_n` is the typed outcome the occurrence is authorized
to pursue. The evaluator judges evidence for the `A_n -> B_n` transition; it
does not own or replay the mutation. An event, ledger row, response payload,
content snapshot, cache, materializer, or projection may describe or
authenticate workspace state; it cannot silently replace the workspace as the
state carrier.

### Causal Occurrence DAG

Program topology may contain recursion, repeated edges, and lifecycle loops.
Occurrence history remains acyclic because every application creates a new
immutable node:

```text
OccurrenceHistory = (Occ, Cause)

Cause is a directed relation over Occ
transitive_closure(Cause) intersect identity(Occ) = empty_set
```

An attempted causal cycle, occurrence-identity reuse, predecessor from the
future, or unresolved predecessor fails closed. A selected ODD or runtime
carrier may use a topological rank or store-assigned admission ordinal as its
mechanical acyclicity witness. That witness is a specialization, not a required
field of the carrier-neutral calculus. Iteration is therefore not a mutable node
looping back to itself. It is a fresh occurrence covering evolved terrain.

An importing evented profile may record occurrence identity, causal edges,
observation, authorized effect, evidence, judgment, disposition, and
continuation in immutable history. Event logging and replay are not mandatory
`a_c` or `Sigma_occurrence` implementation. Where selected, replay reconstructs
the lifecycle; it does not replace the state carrier with response artifacts.

This acyclicity applies to the occurrence-causation relation. It does not claim
that the complete STDO authority graph, Product graph, or GTL program topology
is one DAG.

### Effect And State-Carrier Conservation

If the separate work item is selected, its application-neutral law states only
that the declared carrier, effect realizer, evidence owner, judgment owner, and
continuation owner remain distinct and conserved. An importing ODD profile may
then bind mutable `W_n`. No concrete Product, engine, runtime subsystem, event
schema, or role name enters the constitutional principle:

| Concern | Owner |
|---|---|
| declared work topology and callable contract | the subject Product and its selected declarative profile |
| traversal of the admitted definition | the selected conforming traversal realization |
| mutation of the selected state carrier | the declared effect realization under exact effect authority |
| admission of runtime facts and causal continuation | the separately declared runtime-fact and continuation relation |
| semantic or structural judgment | the separately activated evaluator under its declared authority |
| projection and replay | derived read models over admitted immutable history |

The following substitutions are non-conformant:

- a response payload or candidate snapshot becomes the construction state;
- an adapter or materializer becomes the undeclared effect realizer;
- a local retry cache becomes continuation authority;
- a snapshot is required to regenerate work that the current workspace already
  carries;
- ledger or event truth is mutated to imitate rollback; or
- a worker acquires admission, acceptance, decision, or continuation authority
  because it can write files.

Git or another checkpoint mechanism may snapshot `W_n` for recovery,
comparison, branching, or explicit rollback policy. That is an optional effect
and evidence mechanism. It is not the ordinary state carrier and is not a
prerequisite for iteration.

### Reference Frame Principle, Frame Definition, And Binding Separation

The Reference Frame Method already supplies finite attention, material
sufficiency, exact basis, authority conservation, invalidation, revision, and
missing-frame signals. Those are pure Reference Frame principles. They must not
contain a concrete Product, runtime, role assignment, workspace binding, or
engine identity.

Three surfaces remain separate:

1. `REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD` owns pure frame algebra, lifecycle, sufficiency,
   invalidation, and result law.
2. A separately identified reusable frame definition may declare the material
   question, required authority closure, capabilities, evidence, and stop
   conditions for one concern such as invariant conservation.
3. A separate binding profile maps an activation to roles, capability evidence,
   grants, and a concrete subject. `STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE` may provide
   Executive, Worker, and Reviewer role bindings; a consuming Product binds
   those roles again to its own actors and tools.

Neither the binding profile nor a consuming Product's binding amends the
Reference Frame principles.

The proposed missing lifecycle law is that a material frame definition is
activated and re-evaluated at declared points where its subject can change. It
still requires separate Product selection.

One proposed **Fundamental Invariant Conservation Frame Definition** could be
owned by an optional profile or consuming Product. It would evaluate one named
invariant through a finite, digest-bound closure of every exact owning
authority that materially defines or constrains it. It is not added to the pure
Reference Frame principles by this proposal.

The existing pure relation remains authoritative and complete:

```text
F = <Q, B, M, C, I, A, E, X, R, J, K, D>
```

The tuple preserves evaluation family, workspace/topology basis, material
manifold, coordinate/equality law, invariants, authority, evidence/provenance,
exclusions, result algebra, invalidation/rechart law, actor capability envelope,
and declared dependencies. It is a relation, not a replacement runtime schema.

An activated frame continues to bind one declaration to the exact evaluation,
subject, basis, actor, and evidence-acquisition boundary. The following is only
explanatory notation for a separate Product projection; it neither truncates nor
redefines the RF tuple or activation relation:

```text
beta_P(
  optional_profile,
  complete F,
  exact_evaluation,
  exact_subject,
  exact_basis,
  actor,
  evidence_acquisition_boundary,
  capabilities,
  grants,
  triggers
) -> FrameBasis_P
```

`FrameBasis_P` may carry Product-specific invariant, carrier, realizer,
evidence-owner, judgment-owner, continuation-owner, and prohibited-substitute
references. Those values belong to the Product binding, not the Reference Frame
principles.

For the illustrative workspace invariant, the Product-owned frame would
evaluate whether:

- mutable workspace is the construction reality carrier;
- every traversal application has fresh immutable occurrence identity;
- only the authorized effect realizer mutates the declared write territory;
- immutable events/ledger record admitted facts and causal continuation;
- typed outcome and evidence are not state carriers;
- snapshots are optional evidence/recovery mechanisms; and
- every later attempt observes the current workspace under the same stable
  workspace authority binding unless an admitted rebind occurs.

The result remains the Reference Frame Method algebra:

```text
satisfied | falsified | indeterminate | out_of_frame | invalid_basis
```

An unresolved source ref, missing required binding, stale activation,
insufficient capability, omitted material invariant, or context budget that
trims the invariant fails closed. The frame returns to its separately declared
disposition authority; it does not repair the candidate itself.

### Illustrative Product-Owned Binding, Not Reference Frame Principle

A consuming Product may define a Fundamental Invariant Conservation frame in
its own frame-basis declaration. The following trigger schedule is an
illustrative downstream binding, not STDO constitutional law. Product authority
would decide whether its frame activates:

1. after Product and requirement synthesis establishes the invariant;
2. before acceptance of a design that assigns its carriers or owners;
3. before implementation begins on the affected causal cone;
4. whenever state carrier, effect owner, storage, retry, rollback, workspace
   binding, or continuation semantics change;
5. after a topology or authority-basis change invalidates prior results;
6. when a missing-frame signal or integration failure exposes invisible drift;
   and
7. before Product or release promotion.

Each activation has fresh identity. If the Product imports an occurrence
profile, it may represent those activations as immutable occurrences. A
predecessor verdict is evidence, not a verdict over a changed subject.

The optional STDO baseline may bind this lifecycle to role labels without
making them universal Reference Frame principles:

- **Executive** selects the exact outcome and invariant, assigns capable
  frames, verifies coverage and currentness, and disposes the returned result.
- **Worker** receives the finite construction frame and exact mutation grant,
  mutates the authorized workspace, returns candidate/evidence/residuals, and
  cannot self-accept.
- **Reviewer** independently reacquires the invariant closure and exact
  candidate, evaluates without mutating it, and returns a closed result to the
  Executive.

That mapping belongs to the optional baseline. The concrete actor, grants,
subject, basis, capabilities, and triggers belong to the consuming Product's
frame-basis declaration. Repeated activation is not mutable reuse; each
activation evaluates the current subject under a new exact binding.
