# STDO Logical Product Use

## Owner And Distribution Boundary

STDO means Specification, Ticketing, Design, and Outcome-Driven Development.
The [STDO Product](PRODUCT.md#logical-product-use) owns every logical function
and source qualification condition in this model. Each function consumes its
named method owners; the selected consumer Product retains its concrete
meaning, operation grants, work state, judgment, and decision authority.

This file is the single detailed source Product-use model and model-case owner.
It specifies observable outcomes and their qualification obligations.
It is not a second standards library, an executable workflow, a runtime schema,
or evidence that the functions have been realized. Product/model acceptance,
implementation, native qualification, publication, and consumer adoption are
distinct claims carried by their existing work and release owners.

The released standards member set remains the singular consumer constitutional
authority. A release qualification record binds the exact source model used
for qualification separately from the explicit released member inventory.
Source-model inclusion here does not add a release member. Generic method
changes required to realize an outcome must be admitted at their owning
standards before a consumer relies on them. Until then, the selected existing
law remains operative and a conflicting path is an unresolved realization gap,
not a waiver. Every standard name or clause below resolves through the exact
applicable constitutional basis, never by proximity to mutable source.

STDO owns these logical contracts. The bounded manager owns only its admitted
distribution, integrity, locator, bootstrap, and explicit adoption mechanics.
Selected source/worksite and qualification tools own their declared computed
outputs. Representation owns its admitted compression, navigation, and frame-use
capabilities; Axiom Indexer owns its declared mechanics; ABIogenesis owns generic
execution and runtime truth. No function transfers those responsibilities.

## Access And Lifecycle

A user can enter with an ordinary outcome, question, observed problem, or
request to resume. Entry does not require a method verb, change-class label,
preclassified ticket, diagram, or advance knowledge of frame names. The first
useful response identifies the relevant capability, current readiness, smallest
supported action, and only the missing inputs that can change that action.
It does not adopt a basis or perform an effect merely to answer applicability.

Two related lifecycles remain distinguishable:

| Relationship with STDO | Individual work |
|---|---|
| understand applicability; resolve or decline setup; establish exact usable context | state the outcome; obtain proportionate treatment; bind the lawful action |
| use and observe the selected context; evolve it under its owner | construct; evaluate the declared conditions; conclude or continue |
| suspend, withdraw, or conclude participation with retained obligations | pause, refuse, recover, resume, or re-enter when a condition requires it |

These are logical transitions, not a required stored state machine or sequence
of actors. Owner interaction, observation, interruption, and recovery apply at
any material transition. A status view distinguishes ready, incomplete,
unknown, refused, active, suspended, withdrawn, and completed conditions from
their owning records; a display cannot manufacture their truth. A user can
recover the exact selected basis, permitted next action, pending condition,
recorded decision, and resumption condition without reconstructing a transcript.
Detailed provenance remains reachable from the compact account.

Ordinary access preserves existing meanings and unrelated work. Missing access,
ambiguous Product selection, unavailable authority, uncertain effect state,
and unsupported capability produce a specific bounded refusal or unknown and
its next resolving observation. Absence of a grant is not solved by creating a
ticket. A capability becoming available does not itself authorize its use.

## Computation, Judgment, And Owner Ruling

`C` identifies reproducible computed facts and application of declared rules to
sufficient facts. `J` identifies residual assessment of applicability,
materiality, semantic sufficiency, or unresolved consequences. `O` identifies a
decision reserved to its actual owner. These are model annotations, not new
calculus functor kinds, assumed ABIogenesis regimes, or authority grants.

A rule that settles treatment from sufficient C facts requires no invented J
step. A C output cannot turn missing evidence into certainty. A J result cannot
admit work, waive independence, or manufacture O. An authorized effect remains
an operation of its selected owner even when a tool computes its result.

## Computed Classification Contract

The classification producer is the explicitly selected source/worksite or
qualification capability owner for the work. Its logical output binds the
following sufficient input tuple; a carrier may encode it using existing
records and references rather than introduce a prescribed schema.

| Input | Required identity and content |
|---|---|
| request and work | exact ordinary request, desired outcome, known targets/effects, permitted operations, affected scope, work lifetime, existing carrier and exact admission evidence where applicable |
| governing basis | Product Definition identity and exact bytes; selected immutable method and dependency identities; current Goal/Intent/Product/requirements and accepted frame/configuration authorities relevant to the scope |
| source and candidate | exact baseline and available proposed/actual candidate bytes or applicable projection, changed byte and relation population, environment/configuration and effect targets; absence is explicit |
| declared relations | source addresses, layer/owner bindings, imports, dependencies, composition, Public projection and accepted-design trace relations, their revisions and applicability; declared closure boundary and known unresolved edges |
| Public boundary | owning declaration of the complete relevant serialized member universe, member identity and value interpretation, exact projection/serializer identity and its applicability to this candidate |
| accepted design | exact accepted design and acceptance record, accepted scope/invariants, trace bindings to changed bytes/relations, and dependencies/invalidation conditions |
| evidence and rules | exact evidence subject/population and results; applicable declared rule identities, predicates, precedence or composition, consequences, and relevant earlier C/J/O results |

For each fact, the output exposes its source and sufficient reproduction
relation. It identifies the producing capability, selected implementation or
procedure revision, complete input identity tuple, output identity, scope,
computation status, and invalidation conditions. Byte hashes support exactness;
they do not establish authority, applicability, or semantic equivalence. A
request label, filename, or nearby definition cannot select an owner or route.

### Authority And Owner Walk

C follows the declared source, ownership, authority, dependency, and projection
edges reachable from the known targets and effects inside the affected scope.
Each reached node identifies its exact source address, layer, owner, basis,
relationship to the request, and changed/unchanged/unknown status. Every edge
retains the declaration authorizing its traversal. A proposed root, owner,
grant, or composition change is part of this walk even when no Public member
bytes change.

The result supplies the reached closure and frontier: traversed relations,
authority-bound exclusions, missing or ambiguous declarations, and unresolved
owners. A missing edge is not proof that the far side is irrelevant. C reports
the declared reachability facts; J evaluates residual materiality of undeclared
or semantically uncertain relations. The first changed governing owner supplies
the lawful re-entry. Multiple material owners retain their distinct constraints.

### Public Serialization Delta

C enumerates the complete relevant Public serialized set at baseline and
candidate under the declared Public owner and projection. Each member binds
its stable identity, typed value, and exact serialized bytes. The output gives
added and removed identities and every retained member whose value/bytes
change, with the exact before/after values. A rename is removal plus addition
unless the owner supplies an explicit identity-preserving relation. Membership
equality alone cannot imply equal values.

The enumeration includes declared helper and generated dependencies: a helper
can change a Public value or membership without an edit to a Public-named file.
If candidate bytes, projection applicability, complete enumeration, value
interpretation, or an identity mapping is unavailable, the affected delta is
unknown with that cause. Known partial facts remain useful but cannot project
an unchanged complete boundary. Serialization equality does not prove unchanged
behavior, authority, or semantic sufficiency outside that serialized relation.

### Accepted-Design Coverage And Treatment

For every changed byte range and relation, C joins its explicit trace to the
exact accepted design scope and current acceptance. It reports `covered`,
`missing`, `stale`, `ambiguous`, or `outside_accepted_scope`, with the trace and
acceptance evidence. The last four leave the claimed accepted coverage unknown
or disproved as specified by the evidence; they cannot become a semantic pass.
A listed design file, filename match, or matching content hash without its
scope/trace/acceptance relation establishes no coverage. Even `covered` means
that this declared trace is current; residual semantic sufficiency is J.

C applies each applicable declared rule whose inputs are sufficient and records
the matched predicate, input facts, owning rule, and resulting treatment. The
outcome distinguishes information-only use, authorized local realization,
design re-entry, requirement/Product/Goal/Intent re-entry at the actual changed
owner, operational handling, and unresolved treatment. Conflicting rules or
missing precedence remain unresolved. A partial settled route retains all
residuals needed before dependent reliance.

An observed Public delta and a change to governing meaning are separate facts.
C can establish that a candidate violates an exact declared contract. If that
contract still satisfies the authorized intended outcome, the candidate is
rejected or locally repaired under the existing grant; the violation supplies
no reason to rewrite its requirement. Re-entry is required when the explicitly
selected desired outcome needs a governing change, or an applicable assessment
establishes that the current governing relation is insufficient. C settles that
route only from sufficient declared premises, including the desired outcome;
remaining meaning or necessity is J, and a reserved choice is O. Unknown desired
meaning stays unresolved. A changed candidate cannot decide its own target law.

The treatment names the smallest lawful re-entry, permitted next action,
required evidence/assessment or O, and existing carrier lifetime. It does not
grant the action. A cheapest useful discriminator may resolve an unknown only
inside an existing operation grant and its declared scope. Unknown treatment
blocks the dependent effect or promotion; unrelated actions with sufficient
authority need not inherit that blockage.

An exact existing grant may separately authorize constructing and checking a
source candidate to resolve those unknowns. That provisional action requires
its own sufficient subject, territory, operations, and stopping conditions; it
does not assert that the candidate preserves the contract. New candidate facts
update C before accepted behavior, an operational effect, or promotion relies
on them. Without that construction/evidence grant, candidate absence supplies
no permission to edit. This distinction preserves a first useful action before
candidate bytes exist without admitting unknown behavior.

### Validity And Reuse

A later role, conversation, or resumed run consumes the same valid C output
after checking its exact applicability. Role change alone does not rerun the
walk. Freshness is a relation to the current inputs and invalidators, not a
timestamp or generic cache lifetime.

Changed request, candidate, effect, basis, owner/root relation, projection,
design acceptance/trace, rule, configuration, or material evidence invalidates
the affected output before dependent effects or promotion rely on it. Newly
available candidate bytes replace the corresponding unknown. The successor
retains the earlier result identity, changed inputs and consequences, surviving
facts, and affected J dependencies. Recompute only the affected relation where
its independence from retained results is established; otherwise retain the
larger unresolved frontier. No stale C/J result gains currency through reuse.

## Recorded Judgment And Owner Continuity

A J result needed by another activation is recorded once with its owning work
in the existing run, admitted-sprint entry, or durable ticket, following
`TICKET_METHOD.md`'s Work-Carrier-To-Execution Rule. Its logical content is:

- question and result identity; exact subject, governing basis, and scope;
- supporting C/evidence identities and the facts or uncertainty left unsettled;
- responsible actor, capability and evaluation authority; required independence;
- conclusion, bounded consequences, uncertainty, and assumptions; and
- a concrete revising observation and the affected claims it would reopen.

For example, "revise if the selected Public projection adds a member or changes
its timeout value, or if design acceptance D0 is withdrawn" lets a consumer
check applicability. "Revisit if necessary" does not. A missing basis or
indeterminate revising condition leaves the dependent judgment unavailable.

An unchanged consumer checks applicability and recovers J instead of
re-authoring it. Material new evidence or a stated revising observation creates
a bounded successor judgment preserving the prior result, changed evidence,
and successor relation. Unrelated evidence does not reopen an unaffected claim.
Carrier lifetime follows the existing work boundary; no judgment registry,
ticket per judgment, or obligatory separate artifact is introduced.

An independently required assessment acquires sufficient exact source and
evidence and supplies its own J through its authorized assessor. It may consume
valid C facts and prior J as evidence; it cannot relabel the author's conclusion
as independent. The assessment is a distinct required question/result, not an
excuse to repeat every unchanged classification fact.

O remains separate. The owning work carrier preserves the owner's original
wording, source, actor/authority, exact subject/basis, scope, decision, permitted
consequences, and invalidators once. An agent's interpretation is separately
identified and cannot widen that ruling. A later consumer applies the existing
unchanged ruling without asking for the same authorization again. A materially
changed or ambiguous reserved question returns to its owner with only the new
decision need. General nonclaim/debt policy remains reserved; a local nonclaim
or this model cannot decide or waive that policy.
