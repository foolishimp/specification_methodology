This is the Product-owned frame configuration for work over the mutable source
project. It supersedes revision 1 only for continuing mutable source work. It
is not a member of an already released STDO distribution, does not alter an
immutable release, and does not adopt frames for another consumer. Source
presence alone does not admit the declaration: the Product Definition Overlay
must locate it and the cited Product authority and exact decision must remain
current.

Each activation narrows the governed outcome to one exact Goal, ticket, claim,
subject, checkpoint, and evidence population. The mutable workspace is the
construction subject. Immutable commits, trees, digests, release cuts, and
installed distributions are distinct checkpoints or assurance subjects.

## Coverage Tuple And Exactness

Coverage is evaluated as `Coverage(W, G, B, E, S, T)`:

| Member | Binding |
|---|---|
| `W` | the source project plus only the repositories or installed subjects expressly included by an activation |
| `G` | the Product outcome above narrowed by the exact current Goal and work authority |
| `B` | the complete cut above, current Product authorities, exact source checkpoint, dependencies, configuration, and topology |
| `E` | the evaluation inventory below plus material evaluations discovered from the exact work subject and history |
| `S` | the engagement, invariant, specialist, and testing frames acquired or activated below |
| `T` | one immutable checkpoint and evaluation interval |

Equality requires exact identities, revisions, digests, coordinates, and
declared translations. A branch name, path, matching spelling, rebuilt output,
summary, or equal payload is not exact-subject equality. Missing or ambiguous
basis information returns `invalid_basis`; an evaluation outside the declared
manifold returns `out_of_frame`.

## Engagement Frames, Actors, And Grants

Every acquired frame is reconstructable only when its activation binds its
stable declaration and revision, exact evaluation, result algebra, subject and
basis, material manifold, coordinates and invariants, semantic/evaluation/
operation/decision grants, admissible evidence, actor capability envelope,
exclusions, relations, invalidation, and qualification evidence. A role label
or source access supplies none of those fields.

| Frame | Declaration or acquisition | Actor and capability envelope | Grant and result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive | `urn:stdo:reference-frame:specification-methodology:executive:v1`; acquire `STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#derived-executive-frame` from the exact profile basis | direct Product authority or a named bounded proxy able to resolve method, work authority, frame coverage, actor fit, and current results | exact activation and disposition grant only; returns one Frame Method result and, where authorized, `accept`, `local_repair`, `re_enter`, or `reject` |
| Worker | `urn:stdo:reference-frame:specification-methodology:worker:v1`; acquire `STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#derived-worker-frame` and apply the project binding below | an exact human, agent, deterministic tool, or mixed actor with the required method, domain, implementation, tool, access, and finite-context capability | inherited construction or transformation operation grant and self-review only; returns one closed Worker result to Executive |
| Reviewer | `urn:stdo:reference-frame:specification-methodology:reviewer:v1`; acquire `STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#derived-reviewer-frame` from the exact profile basis | an exact actor capable of live-subject acquisition, applicable method/domain/assurance evaluation, counterexample search, and the independence required by the claim | evaluation only; returns one closed review result to Executive; no repair, mutation, disposition, acceptance, publication, or continuation authority |
| Fundamental Invariant Conservation | `urn:stdo:reference-frame:fundamental-invariant-conservation:v1`; complete declaration below | an author-independent Reviewer capable of acquiring every exact owner in the invariant closure and evaluating the affected relation cone | evaluation only; returns one closed conservation result to Executive |

Frames and actors are separate identities. One actor may occupy different
frames across different activations only when every grant and any claimed
independence remain valid. Reviewer independence binds the exact candidate and
verdict carrier; Executive inspection is not another independent review.

The authority classes remain disjoint:

| Authority | Existing owner and activation rule |
|---|---|
| semantic | the exact Product, requirement, standard, accepted design, identity, release, or other owner of the evaluated meaning |
| evaluation | the exact activation issued under this basis |
| operation | only the owner-authorized work relation; an effectful activation names operation, subject, write territory, exclusions, and stop conditions |
| decision | direct Product/work authority or a named bounded proxy acting inside its exact disposition envelope |
| acceptance and publication | direct Product and release authority where their law requires it; never inferred from a frame result |

### Worker Project Binding

| Frame element | Product binding |
|---|---|
| evaluation and result | construct the exact authorized outcome through the smallest coherent causal cone; return `candidate_ready`, `refused`, `incomplete`, or `re_entry_requested` with its required Frame Method result |
| subject and basis | sufficient `STDO-UP-020` intake, exact mutable workspace/checkpoint, governing authorities, affected cone, selected dependencies, configuration, and topology |
| material manifold | affected entities, lifecycles, operators, owners, effects, dependencies, seams, failures, competing paths, and proof obligations |
| coordinates and invariants | exact activation, source, input, dependency, configuration, write-territory, output, candidate, and evidence identities; every governing relation material to the cone |
| authority | the exact inherited construction or transformation grant; it names permitted operations, mutation subject, write territory, and exclusions |
| evidence | live source and authority, mutations, diffs, tests and test semantics, artifacts, installed or operational observations where claimed, and transition evidence |
| capability | method, domain, implementation, tool, access, and context competence required by the cone |
| exclusions | upstream semantic change, mutation outside the grant, hidden competing paths, self-acceptance, publication, disposition, and advancement after result return |
| invalidation | any material intake, subject, basis, checkpoint, dependency, configuration, capability, authority, or topology change |

The mutable workspace is the Worker's explicit construction subject when the
grant says so. Mutation compounds over that workspace during one bounded
activation. Each later iteration or review binds the then-current exact
checkpoint; immutable history and checkpoints do not replace the workspace.
A lawfully activated Worker that later encounters an owner-valid prohibition
returns the closed `refused` result; it does not retroactively convert that
result into `activation_refusal`.

This frame activates for every material proposal or candidate capable of
affecting a Product invariant, before accepted-design or source admission and
again before release promotion. It reactivates when the change alters an
authority owner, semantic or state carrier, effect target, operation grant,
evidence boundary, storage or retry relation, representation boundary, or
owner-closure topology. The author may supply the candidate and evidence but
cannot qualify that candidate through self-review. The frame evaluates
conservation; it creates no invariant, authority, design, operation grant,
repair, acceptance, or release decision.

For each narrower activated outcome, Executive records one of:

1. an exact instance because the family is material;
2. authority-bound evidence that the family is non-material; or
3. an explicit `uncovered`, `conditionally_covered`, `conflicted`, `unknown`,
   `excluded_by_authority`, or `out_of_frame` residual.

Artifact absence and a shared actor are not non-materiality evidence. One
frame may cover several families only when its declaration preserves every
distinct evaluation, authority, result, and seam relation. A composite result
is `satisfied` only when each mandatory constraint is satisfied and every
material identity, basis, value/evidence, lifecycle, authority, and provenance
seam commutes or has an explicit lawful translation.

| End To End | `urn:stdo:reference-frame:specification-methodology:test:end-to-end:v1`; material when a complete runnable Product path is claimed | exact installed/deployed subject, authoritative entry-to-outcome path, owners, transitions, effects, persistence/recovery, configuration, combinations, and Product-derived oracle | equal output through bypass, obsolete, fallback, rebuilt, source-only, or otherwise forbidden path |
| Integration | `urn:stdo:reference-frame:specification-methodology:test:integration:v1`; material for each claimed composition seam | exact participants and versions, interface/carrier, state, authority, configuration, timing, failure/recovery population, admitted substitutions, and contract oracle | one-sided or mocked boundary presented as the real composition, happy-path-only population, or whole-Product projection |
| Unit | `urn:stdo:reference-frame:specification-methodology:test:unit:v1`; material for every coded module and module-owned law | exact module boundary/revision, owned law, operator/state/input/failure population, independent law-derived oracle, combinations, and residuals | implementation-shaped oracle, incidental branch count, or local evidence presented as integration, installed, user, or Product proof |

The activation owns a finite scenario matrix and forbidden-path inventory for
its claim. Pairwise or reduced combinations require a basis showing why omitted
higher-order interactions cannot alter the claim; otherwise they remain
residual. User-acceptance, end-to-end, integration, and unit results remain
distinct even when one execution contributes evidence to several frames.

If a testing family is non-material to one narrow outcome, the coverage ledger
identifies the capable Product, requirement, design, module, or release owner
making that disposition. It cannot be silently omitted.

This durable basis supplies acquisition and disposition rules. It does not by
itself claim operational full coverage for a moving workspace. Such a claim is
valid only for one exact coverage tuple after every known material evaluation,

and residual is closed or explicitly retained.

## Activation, Results, And Conjunction

```text
existing work authority
  -> Executive activation or activation_refusal
  -> Worker activation
  -> WorkerResult -> Executive
  -> optional independently activated Reviewer -> ReviewResult -> Executive
  -> exact mandatory specialist/testing result conjunction
  -> accept | local_repair | re_enter | reject
  -> exact checkpoint and only the next already-authorized action
```

`activation_refusal` is not a Worker result. Worker never activates Reviewer.
Worker and Reviewer results return to Executive. Reviewer findings are
evaluation evidence, not repair instructions or a second disposition family.
A repair or any material subject/basis change creates a new candidate and new
affected activations.

Every closed result records frame and activation identity, evaluation, exact
subject and basis, actor/capability envelope, semantic/evaluation/operation/
decision authority, evidence, Frame Method result, inference relation,
findings or transition evidence, residual uncertainty, invalidation, and
consuming decision or conjunction.

No conjunction is implicit. Mandatory results compose by conjunction; one
`falsified`, `invalid_basis`, unresolved conflict, missing material result, or
non-commuting seam prevents `satisfied`. Translation is declared only for the
exact coordinates, values/evidence, lifecycle, authority, and provenance it
preserves.

## Lifecycle Triggers, Invalidation, And Revision

The basis is consulted at intake. Executive activates only the frames material
to the exact outcome and retains every omitted family as an authority-bound
non-material disposition or explicit residual.

Mandatory lifecycle triggers are:

- before a Worker performs an authorized effect;
- before independent assessment when required by work, assurance, Product, or
  release authority;
- before accepted-design admission, source acceptance, and release promotion
  for every affected Product invariant;
- when a Product/testing claim selects a runnable, installed, interface,
  module, user, path, population, or oracle subject; and
- after missing-frame pressure, repeated repair, hidden rival authority,
  proof masking, topology change, or a historical counterexample.

Any material change to method/profile identity, Product or work authority,
outcome, checkpoint, subject, candidate, dependency, configuration, actor,
capability, independence, grant, evidence population, oracle, path, topology,
owner closure, or result relation invalidates affected activations, results,
conjunctions, and coverage claims. Re-evaluation uses a new activation over the
new terrain; prior immutable results remain history and do not transfer.

Frame diagnosis follows the Frame Method order: activation, exact basis,
capability, evidence acquisition, missing variable/invariant, conjunction or
translation, evaluation family, then new frame. A material revision changes
the frame identity or revision coordinate and states which prior results and
coverage claims require re-evaluation. Product-wide frame-set changes re-enter
this Product-owned declaration; method/profile changes re-enter their owning
standard and a new exact cut before adoption.

Qualification includes representative decision, reconstruction, sensitivity,
irrelevance, stale-basis, authority, counterexample, capability, result-
consumption, mutation-territory, independent-review, testing-altitude, and
forbidden-path cases. Qualification is bound to the exact frame revisions,
actor envelopes, bases, and evidence populations evaluated.
