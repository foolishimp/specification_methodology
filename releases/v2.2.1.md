## Product Delta

### Capability-Aware Proportional Constraint

STDO intentionally constrains the admissible reasoning and realization space.
Each coherent constraint family is justified by the semantic ambiguity it
removes and remains proportional to the effective reasoning complexity it
introduces. Rival authority, failure classification, and evidence uncertainty
count only where they expose materially distinct admissible semantic,
authority, outcome, or acceptance interpretations. They measure or evidence
the same interpretation space removed; they are not independent proportional
benefits. This does not require a per-clause rationale carrier. STDO does not
prescribe an internal search, decomposition, collaboration, tool, or synthesis
procedure when variation in that procedure cannot affect a governed semantic,
authority, evidence, safety, or release property.

For symbolic design, materially divergent implementation paths, runtime states,
tests, reviews, and reconciliation joins are counterfactual evidence of the
semantic alternatives contracted. They are not an independent numerator.
Expanded agent capability may enlarge the bounded relation an agent can
resolve. It cannot grant authority, weaken an invariant, or waive acceptance.

This clarifies `STDO-UP-014` by operationalizing its existing
ambiguity-contraction numerator. It does not supersede that numerator or
introduce a model-specific reasoning procedure, numeric score, mandatory
context size, or blanket design-first rule.

### Decision-Complete Symbolic Design

Where Design Module Method applies, co-evolution is admissible only when
`decision_complete(B)` holds. The complete material-relation set must have no
unresolved member, form one jointly satisfiable constraint network, and admit
no materially non-equivalent network under the governing Product,
requirements, and accepted design relations. Outside an adopted Design Module
Method boundary, the retained generic no-unresolved-material-design-decision
test applies. Neither co-evolution nor prior design is the global default. The
same proportionality relation selects between them.

Design/implementation bidirectionality carries evidence, counterexamples,
falsification, and reconciliation. It does not reverse semantic authority from
code into design. A material surprise gates the smallest causally closed
affected decision set; serial code repair cannot establish design acceptance.

A design boundary is decision-complete only when its complete material-relation
set—entities, identities, relationships, cardinalities, invariants,
authorities, authority transitions, functions, higher-order composition laws,
topology, lifecycle states and transitions, public contracts, externally
observable Product meaning, declared domains and admission conditions, failure
and refusal routes, effect and closure laws, projections, module mappings, and
material algorithmic obligations—has no unresolved member, forms one jointly
satisfiable constraint network, and admits no materially non-equivalent
network. Ontology, Prime, IACS, views, modules, implementation, and tests
project that network. Their presence or individual plausibility does not
establish completeness.

The constraint-network reconstruction, evidence-only bidirectionality, and
smallest causally closed affected scope clarify the accepted proportional
sequencing relation. They do not invalidate unrelated work, require a separate
design document, or require re-acceptance of an unchanged boundary.

The co-evolution admissibility predicate is a bounded supersession. `v2.2.0`
permitted retained design, implementation, and tests to co-evolve while
material design relations outside its unresolved-architecture test were still
being discovered, provided they were reconciled before promotion. Under
`v2.2.1`, retained co-evolution inside an adopted Design Module Method boundary
requires `decision_complete(B)` across the complete `M(B)`; otherwise only a
disposable spike may cross the unresolved, contradictory, or materially
non-equivalent relation before the smallest causally closed affected design set
is accepted.

The proportional mode choice, design ownership of the structural `HOW`,
provisional implementation as evidence, and unrelated accepted work remain
conserved. This supersession changes no Product boundary, adds no mandatory
design artifact, and does not make draft `2.2.1` law operative before release
and consumer selection.

### Prime Semantic Atoms

Prime is decided at semantic-design altitude. A Prime atom is an irreducible
semantic relation, not a file, source function, class, schema, diagram,
operation, module, or owner category. Whole-family Prime evaluates the complete
candidate semantic-atom family and every proposed realization projection, not
only functions, carriers, or isolated units. Realization units are projections
of accepted atoms and may map many-to-many.

An accepted Prime atom governs every admitted instance in its declared domain.
Later use cases compose or parameterize it rather than creating feature-local
replacements. An in-domain counterexample re-enters at the shared atom's design
boundary.

Prime evidence records every accepted atom's admitted domain, the
whole-family contraction, and the explicit total mapping from every proposed
realization projection to its accepted atom or atoms. An atom without a
declared domain remains a candidate and cannot confer Prime-conformance.

This supersedes wording that defined a realization unit itself as Prime.
Top-level realization boundaries remain subject to Prime-conformance, IACS,
whole-family contraction, recursive Prime, and root-conservation law.

### Semantic Review Reconstruction

Design closure review reconstructs the complete selected function from
governing requirements through accepted Ontology, Prime atoms, IACS, views,
target modules, implementation, and module-owned proof. Exact hashes establish
subject identity. Headings and artifact presence establish location. Green
tests establish only the properties they actually assert. None supplies a
missing semantic relation or substitutes a weaker property for the governed
claim.

An independent-review claim supports promotion or closure only when its exact
subject and verdict remain durably traceable through an existing evidence
carrier. This adds no separate receipt, carrier, review round, or mandatory
review boundary.

The ticket-method compression also restores the accepted source qualifier
`competing or ambiguous authority`, which its `v2.2.0` projection shortened to
`competing authority`.
