## Product Delta

### Outcome Succession, Evidence Role, And Continued-Growth Authority

A Product-progress wave binds one explicitly selected unresolved
Product-defined outcome instance and declares its acceptance interval.
Acceptance ends that instance's authority to select later progress but retains
its witnesses as regression and preservation evidence. Enduring guarantees,
recurring obligations, and broader Product families remain live beyond the
accepted instance or interval. Evidence evaluates the bound outcome; it cannot
select, enlarge, or replace it. An artifact serving as Product output for one
claim and evidence for another declares those roles separately.

Within a Product-outcome-bearing work wave, material realization growth derives
authority from the selected unresolved Product outcome, an admitted named
bounded prerequisite to it, or an admitted named bounded experiment whose
stated observation discriminates a stated decision for it. A prerequisite or
experiment is admitted or renewed only by the authority owning the selected
work wave or its explicitly bounded proxy and remains durably recorded in
existing Goals or ticket authority. Each admitted prerequisite or experiment
declares its provisional bound and terminal condition. It cannot enlarge
Product, authorize downstream work, confer promotion or closure, or waive
applicable law.

Acceptance of the outcome, discharge of the prerequisite, resolution of the
experiment's stated decision, another admitted terminal condition, exhaustion,
rejection, withdrawal, supersession, repricing of the basis away, or
falsification of the basis ends that growth authority. Evidence, active work
state, prior admission, repair, or continuation cannot renew it; further
material work requires a new admission from the owning authority or its
explicitly bounded proxy.

Supported Product behavior, required evidence, regression protection, and
bounded donor material may remain without inheriting further growth authority.
Consumers own deletion, archival, quarantine, and salvage mechanics. Salvage
does not carry the enclosing work's authority, dependencies, completion claim,
or power to select further work.

This relation is orthogonal to Prime. Prime continues to contract candidate
function and carrier families to irreducible compositional building blocks;
Product Outcome Conservation governs whether mutable realization built from
those blocks remains authorized to grow. Existing provisional co-evolution and
bounded-spike law remains conserved.

This supersedes the prior `STDO-UP-013` relation that required Product re-entry
for every outcome change. Selecting the next already-defined outcome is a Goals
and work-sequencing decision; changing Product meaning or introducing an
outcome still re-enters at Product. This adds no frontier algorithm, score,
threshold, scheduling runtime, survival state machine, or prescribed disposal
process.

### Product-Outcome Binding And Implementation-Independent Method Roles

Graph-native construction consumes the selected Product outcome as external
authority. Product models, gaps, assurance, ledgers, projections, and edge
closure may interpret or evidence that outcome but cannot author, select,
enlarge, or accept it. Next-action selection remains inside that outcome or an
admitted bounded prerequisite or experiment. A reprice disposition requests
constitutional re-entry; it does not amend Product or select a successor
outcome.

`ODD`, `GTL`, and `ABG` denote normative capability roles and construction
algebra within the method. They do not select a required repository, package,
vendor, runtime implementation, or downstream Product. Consumers bind concrete
conforming realizations and immutable identities in their own authority
surfaces.

Constitutional examples now use neutral capability, authority, and realization
identities. Concrete downstream products, commands, paths, versions, and
implementation symbols remain valid consumer evidence or provenance but do not
supply reusable method meaning.

This supersedes the predecessor's downstream-labelled examples and its
guide-level identification of the ABG role with one concrete runtime. It does
not weaken or replace the accepted graph-native algebra.

### Migration Strategy And Path-Relative Closure

When bounded evolution and fundamental re-adoption are both lawful and
feasible, bounded evolution is the rebuttable selection presumption when a
working predecessor can reach the admitted Product outcome without competing or
ambiguous authority. It never requires continuing an unsafe or constitutionally
inadmissible predecessor path. Core-interface evolution uses the Inside-Out
Hard-Break strategy; bounded evolution is not a third migration strategy.
Fundamental re-adoption requires explicit human Product selection based on
comparative Product value, feedback latency, authority risk, reversibility,
total cost, retained predecessor semantics, and an abort or re-entry condition.

Product-slice promotion and complete migration closure are distinct claims. A
slice may promote only under one governing authority across the full causal
closure of its outcome, including every admitted entrypoint, producer,
consumer, resolver, fallback, shared state surface, event, projection, and proof
surface capable of affecting it. Ticket or review wording cannot exclude
causally applicable Product, requirement, design, or retained-predecessor
authority. Old execution may remain only outside that closure or under an
explicitly specified, deterministically routed, non-overlapping Product or
compatibility scope. Residual material must be unable to falsify current or
retained claims. Migration, qualification, and release closure retain their own
retirement criteria.

This supersedes the accepted predecessor rule that placed retirement of the
complete superseded-interface family on the same closure bar as feature
delivery. It also supersedes the predecessor's treatment of major intentional
re-derivation on a materially different implementation basis as sufficient
migration-strategy selection guidance when bounded evolution is also lawful and
feasible. Fundamental re-adoption requires the comparative human selection and
abort or re-entry condition above in all cases.

### Review And Design Horizon

A review finding blocks current promotion when it falsifies the exact claim,
contradicts causally applicable active authority, exposes competing or ambiguous
authority, violates safety or retained accepted behavior, or establishes a
durable architectural decision that forecloses an admitted Product outcome.
Optional cleanup, future generality, and cross-boundary observations outside
those conditions are repricing inputs rather than automatic expansion of the
reviewed subject. The retained recurrence rule still requires commonization
review at the second credible recurrence and an explicit consume, extend, or
do-not-commonize disposition before a third local rebuild.

The post-ticket design check remains required for a claimed design boundary,
but it may be part of normal verification and does not require a separate
artifact, review round, reviewer, or automatic follow-up ticket.

This supersedes the accepted predecessor rules that automatically absorbed
feasible cleanup and commonization and required separate work items for every
cross-boundary or reusable-pattern opportunity discovered during post-ticket
design review.

### Release Subject And Exact Publication

The Product release subject is the exact 41-member
`specification/standards/` distribution and aggregate recorded below. This
release note is the release-scoped claim surface. Co-located mutable
source-project state is excluded from both, limited for RC-to-final delta
purposes to milestone-state cells in `specification/GOALS.md` and status,
review, and closure bookkeeping in T-002. Standards, `PRODUCT.md`, this release
note, Goals meaning, ticket target truth, evaluation criteria, and release scope
are not state-only deltas.

One immutable RC tag identifies the final-ready subject and carrier for
independent exact-cut review. Before tap, final-delta evaluation proves that the
Product member set, Product bytes, aggregate, and this release note are
unchanged from the reviewed RC. Direct human acceptance binds that subject,
the final carrier commit, and the final-delta relation before the final branch
and `v2.2.0` tag are published. Publication-caused work-state closure is
recorded afterward on the continuing source branch and does not move the
immutable release tag.

This supersedes the predecessor release sequence that finalized tracked
release-facing content after candidate acceptance. Any change to Product or
release-scoped bytes after RC review requires affected re-evaluation and exact
acceptance.
