## Product Delta

### Outcome Succession, Evidence Role, And Continued-Growth Authority

A Product-progress wave binds one explicitly selected unresolved
Product-defined outcome instance. Acceptance ends that instance's authority to
select later progress but retains its witnesses as regression and preservation
evidence. Enduring guarantees, recurring obligations, and broader Product
families remain live beyond the accepted instance or interval. Evidence
evaluates the bound outcome; it cannot select, enlarge, or replace it. An
artifact serving as Product output for one claim and evidence for another
declares those roles separately.

Within a Product-outcome-bearing work wave, material realization growth derives
authority from the selected unresolved Product outcome, an admitted named
bounded prerequisite to it, or an admitted named bounded experiment whose
stated observation discriminates a stated decision for it. A prerequisite or
experiment authorizes only its declared provisional bound; it cannot enlarge
Product, authorize downstream work, confer promotion or closure, or waive
applicable law. Once the outcome or bounded basis is exhausted or falsified,
the work cannot select further Product-progress work without another admitted
basis.

Supported Product behavior, required evidence, regression protection, and
bounded donor material may remain without inheriting further growth authority.
Consumers own deletion, archival, quarantine, and salvage mechanics. Salvage
does not carry the enclosing work's authority, dependencies, completion claim,
or power to select further work.

This relation is orthogonal to Prime. Prime continues to contract candidate
function and carrier families to irreducible compositional building blocks;
Product Outcome Conservation governs whether mutable realization built from
those blocks remains authorized to grow. Existing provisional co-evolution and
bounded-spike law remains conserved.

This supersedes the prior `STDO-UP-013` relation that required Product re-entry
for every outcome change. Selecting the next already-defined outcome is a Goals
and work-sequencing decision; changing Product meaning or introducing an
outcome still re-enters at Product. This adds no frontier algorithm, score,
threshold, scheduling runtime, survival state machine, or prescribed disposal
process.

### Migration Strategy And Path-Relative Closure

When bounded evolution and fundamental re-adoption are both lawful and
feasible, bounded evolution is the rebuttable selection presumption when a
working predecessor can reach the admitted Product outcome without competing or
ambiguous authority. It never requires continuing an unsafe or constitutionally
inadmissible predecessor path. Core-interface evolution uses the Inside-Out
Hard-Break strategy; bounded evolution is not a third migration strategy.
Fundamental re-adoption requires explicit human Product selection based on
comparative Product value, feedback latency, authority risk, reversibility,
total cost, retained predecessor semantics, and an abort or re-entry condition.

Product-slice promotion and complete migration closure are distinct claims. A
slice may promote only under one governing authority across the full causal
closure of its outcome, including every admitted entrypoint, producer,
consumer, resolver, fallback, shared state surface, event, projection, and proof
surface capable of affecting it. Ticket or review wording cannot exclude
causally applicable Product, requirement, design, or retained-predecessor
authority. Old execution may remain only outside that closure or under an
explicitly specified, deterministically routed, non-overlapping Product or
compatibility scope. Residual material must be unable to falsify current or
retained claims. Migration, qualification, and release closure retain their own
retirement criteria.

This supersedes the accepted predecessor rule that placed retirement of the
complete superseded-interface family on the same closure bar as feature
delivery.

### Review And Design Horizon

A review finding blocks current promotion when it falsifies the exact claim,
contradicts causally applicable active authority, exposes competing or ambiguous
authority, violates safety or retained accepted behavior, or establishes a
durable architectural decision that forecloses an admitted Product outcome.
Optional cleanup, recurrence, future generality, and cross-boundary observations
outside those conditions are repricing inputs rather than automatic expansion
of the reviewed subject.

The post-ticket design check remains required for a claimed design boundary,
but it may be part of normal verification and does not require a separate
artifact, review round, reviewer, or automatic follow-up ticket.

This supersedes the accepted predecessor rules that automatically absorbed
feasible cleanup and commonization and required separate work items for every
cross-boundary or reusable-pattern opportunity discovered during post-ticket
design review.

  salvage process;
- mandatory independent review for local implementation steps;
- global scheduling algorithm;
- consumer runtime or adoption change; or
- automatic ticket-generation rule.
