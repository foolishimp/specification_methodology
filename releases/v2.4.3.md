## Product Delta

### Bounded-Context Semantic Isolation

Equal spelling across bounded contexts establishes no equality of meaning,
concept identity, equivalence, or authority. A material term occurrence is
resolved through one semantic address consisting of:

- the term or exact concept reference;
- the bounded-context identity;
- the owning semantic authority;
- the selected basis; and
- the governed scope.

Unqualified prose remains lawful when those coordinates resolve exactly one
applicable meaning. Zero or multiple applicable meanings fail closed. File
names, headings, directory nesting, proximity, shared actors, and nominal
equality declare no context and create no import.

A context consumes meaning from another context only through an explicit,
owner-authorized semantic import, translation, equivalence, specialization, or
disambiguation. Each relation binds exact source and target concepts and
contexts, direction where material, preserved and changed meaning, loss and
refusal conditions, both semantic owners, selected bases, scope, provenance,
lifecycle, and invalidation. Such a relation never transfers either owner's
authority.

### Locator-Only Glossary

`GLOSSARY_GUIDE.md` is a non-deciding semantic index. Its rows record an
indexed spelling, a source-declared bounded-context identity, an exact owning
concept clause, and an exact relation clause where applicable. It contains no
semantic definitions and provides no context-free shared default.

Every indexed locator resolves inside the selected complete release. Repeated
spellings are intentional collision candidates. A matching glossary row cannot
choose a meaning without the occurrence's context, owner, basis, and scope.

The aggregate compression classifies the glossary through separate
`index_refs` and `index_digests`. It is not one of the deciding sources whose
meaning the compression projects.

`Source Project`, not an unchanged import or alias. The relation names the
exact source and target concept identities, preserved and narrowed meaning,

scope, provenance, and invalidation conditions.

### Product Definition Overlay Revision 2

The Product Definition Overlay schema identity is
`urn:stdo:schema:product-definition:2`. Every non-empty
`local_constitution.disambiguations` entry binds:

- `uri` — the owning resolution carrier;
- `term` — the exact lexical label;
- `context` — the bounded context in which resolution applies;
- `disambiguates` — the complete material candidate set;
- `resolves_to` — the selected concept, which conformance verifies is a
  candidate;
- `authority` — the lawful resolution and relation authorities;
- `basis` — the selected semantic bases; and
- `applies_to` — the exact governed scope.

The overlay locates and relates these facts without restating source-owned
meaning or minting semantic authority. Definitions whose disambiguation array
is explicitly empty retain their shape. A retained non-empty revision-1 entry
adds the new term, context, selected-concept, and basis bindings.

Portable Draft 2020-12 validation continues to prove structural shape only.
URI syntax assertion, resolution, selected-candidate membership, semantic-
relation completeness, authority congruence, and cross-file identity checks
remain separately required conformance operations.

### Collision And Resolution Cases

Semantic conformance explicitly covers the terms `Frame`, `Owner`, `Product`,
`Tenant`, and `User` across their material contexts. Nominal match cannot:

- enroll a Product- or runtime-owned `Frame` as an evaluation frame;
- grant authority to an `Owner` specialist evaluation family;
- collapse an immutable released `Product` into a mutable Product Definition;
- treat STDO build tenancy as hosted, customer, account, runtime, or data
  tenancy; or
- supply a downstream `User` meaning in place of Product-owned person,
  principal, account, actor, operator, or data-subject law.

Positive cases resolve one exact local concept or carry a complete authorized
cross-context relation. Negative cases cover zero candidates, remaining
multiple candidates, a selection outside the candidate set, glossary fallback,
incomplete relation coordinates, authority or basis mismatch, undeclared loss,
and invalidated relations.

### Compression And Bootstrap Projection

bounded-context, cross-context-relation, glossary-index, World Model, and
Product Definition Overlay rules. Each compression digest resolves either to a
deciding source or to the separately classified non-deciding glossary index in
the same selected cut.

Codex and Claude bootstrap templates require agents to discover the applicable
bounded context, resolve material terms under exact authority and basis, and
fail closed instead of guessing among unresolved meanings. Product and overlay
templates project the same separation without becoming semantic owners.
