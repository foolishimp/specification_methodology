**Release Tag**: `v2.0.0`

## Position

`v2.0.0` is an incremental successor to the complete accepted `v1.8.0`
standards Product. It does not reconstruct the method from either rejected 2.0
candidate.

The release preserves STDO as an opinionated normative construction method.
STDO owns construction algebra, authority relations, admissibility and refusal
conditions, necessary causal order, and evidence invariants. Consumers own the
concrete realization of those laws in product types, schemas, files, tools,
runtimes, orchestration, tests, and conformance evidence.

Version 2 adds design-boundary and delivery-governance lessons without adding
an STDO conformance engine, workflow runtime, fixture suite, or consumer
Product.

## Increment

The 2.0 increment has two parts:

1. the separately authorized three-view and Ontology/IACS/Prime design-method
   additions; and
2. bounded incident-derived laws for proof-target identity, authority
   conservation, milestone truth, independent review, truth-layer separation,
   real-path negative evidence, exact-candidate qualification, Product-outcome
   conservation, proportional method and delivery judgment, and semantic
   predecessor conservation.

The second part is expressed as normative outcomes and authority relations. It
does not prescribe a consumer scheduler, workflow engine, progress algorithm,
carrier schema, fixture registry, or executable proof implementation.

## New Method Value

Version 2 adds these singularly owned laws:

- `STDO-SURFACE-001`: one complete versioned constitutional surface;
- `STDO-UP-001`: proof-target identity and nearest weaker excluded property;
- `STDO-UP-002`: native constructability;
- `STDO-UP-003`: recursive Prime and root-authority conservation;
- `STDO-UP-004`: authority identity and direct or bounded proxied human
  authority;
- `STDO-UP-005`: governance cost in Prime review;
- `STDO-UP-006`: milestone-scoped truth;
- `STDO-UP-007`: independent verification and durable ruling disposition;
- `STDO-UP-008`: semantic, evidence, and projection truth separation;
- `STDO-UP-009`: work-state projection drift refusal;
- `STDO-UP-010`: real authority-path negative evidence;
- `STDO-UP-011`: exact candidate and final-delta qualification;
- `STDO-UP-012`: shared-method propagation as an authority relation;
- `STDO-UP-013`: Product-outcome conservation for progress and promotion;
- `STDO-UP-014`: proportional method obligation and delivery priority; and
- `STDO-UP-015`: semantic predecessor and watched-support-line conservation.

Ontology, IACS, Prime, and the three semantic design views apply at material
semantic boundaries. They do not require separate files, repeated approvals,
or a universal design pack when an existing design already answers the domain,
interaction, lifecycle, and authority questions.

## Qualification

Qualification for the exact cut is standards integrity and method-consistency
review over:

- the complete standards tree, not only the final repair delta;
- the full `v1.8.0..v2.0.0` release delta;
- predecessor-family conservation;
- singular law ownership and source/compression digest agreement;
- absence of executable STDO conformance or workflow implementation;
- exact member identity and `git diff --check`; and
- independent exact-cut review followed by direct human acceptance for this
  bootstrap release.

Consumer behavioral conformance is produced by each adopting Product. It is
not implemented or claimed by this standards repository.
