### Universal Reference Frame Method

`REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md` introduces the finite engagement atom above any one
specification methodology or domain realization.

A frame binds one evaluation family to an exact workspace and topology basis,
material-relation manifold, coordinates and equality law, inherited invariants,
semantic/evaluation/decision authorities, admissible evidence, exclusions,
closed result algebra, actor capability envelope, declared dependencies, and
invalidation conditions.

The method separately defines:

- frame-set declaration;
- exact activation;
- evaluation and closed result return;
- independent co-evaluation and result conjunction;
- evidence or coordinate translation;
- material composition where an evaluation requires it;
- tuple-relative operational coverage with explicit residual uncertainty; and
- empirical revision after failures, topology mutation, evidence change, or
  capability change.

Frames need not form a hierarchy, partition, tree, service topology, or runtime
ontology. They may overlap, remain independent, refine, restrict, translate,
compose, or succeed one another. Role, persona, actor, abstraction, component,
and lens remain distinct from a frame; each may help construct or activate one
but does not become one by label.

The method explicitly admits `satisfied`, `falsified`, `indeterminate`,
`out_of_frame`, and `invalid_basis`. Operational full coverage is relative to
one declared workspace, outcome, basis, evaluation inventory, frame set, and
time. It does not assert that no undiscovered material evaluation exists.

Method self-qualification covers every claimed function and failure relation:
declaration, activation, evaluation, conjunction, translation, coverage,
revision, result consumption, and material composition.

### Applied STDO Executive, Worker, And Reviewer Baseline

`STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md` is an optional derived application profile,
not a second execution constitution. It maps three overlapping finite
engagement frames onto existing STDO owners:

```text
Executive activates Worker
  -> Worker returns candidate_ready | refused | incomplete | re_entry_requested
     to Executive
  -> Executive disposes directly when independent review is not required
     or activates Reviewer when it is required
  -> Reviewer independently reacquires and evaluates the exact subject
     and returns one closed result to Executive
  -> Executive applies the existing STDO disposition and continuation relation
```

The Worker cannot accept itself or activate the Reviewer. The Reviewer cannot
implement repair, dispose the result, or select continuation. The Executive
does not gain universal semantic or implementation authority. Frames and actors
are separately bound; the profile requires no fixed actor count.

Projects may adopt the baseline or define another frame configuration through
existing Goals, Product, requirement, or accepted-design authority. A local
axioms carrier projects that authority and does not mint a parallel
constitutional layer.
