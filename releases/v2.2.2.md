## Product Delta

### Entity Recovery Separated From Representation Geometry

`WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md` carried two incompatible accounts of how a published
cut comes to exist. Its Method Flow recovers entities from enacted function,
authority, state, and adjacency, and holds sparse first publication lawful
where the object is bounded, distinguishable, and evidence-backed. Its Markov
Object Construction Law declared the cut *constructed* by a directional
interventional procedure and required an identity direction and effective
coordinate as publication minima. Method Units then offered a recovered entity
only two destinations: converge into a Markov-object cut, or be reclassified
as a treatment surface, covariance edge, or temporal reference artifact.

A recovered institutional entity is none of those, and the geometric procedure
presumes a representation space that heterogeneous symbolic ledger claims do
not supply. The discovery pipeline therefore produced output the publication
law refused to accept.

The Markov object is restored to its stated role: the theoretical account of
why a recovered boundary is a real object rather than an arbitrary cluster. It
is not a claim that the builder invents the object. Institutional entities are
already committed by people, implicitly in code paths, records, and workflows
or explicitly in schemas and policy. The method's practical work is to trawl
existing systems and recover them.

The successor:

1. establishes the **entity cut** as the primary published semantic unit,
   lawful without an identity direction, carrying identity, boundary,
   authority, state, evidence, treatment, covariance, and ambiguity, each
   traced to ledger entries and their assurance basis;
2. establishes the **Markov-object overlay** as an optional geometric overlay,
   admissible only where a representation space is declared, which strengthens
   an entity cut and whose absence does not weaken one;
3. adds **Entity Cut Construction Law** as proportional constitutional law,
   not protocol: an entity cut is admissible when its identity, boundary,
   authority, state, evidence, treatment, covariance, and ambiguity are
   explicit, traceable, and discriminated from plausible alternatives at
   evidence altitude proportional to the claim, where "explicit" admits a
   stated value, stated absence, or stated non-applicability on every
   surface. Sparse first publication remains lawful, and a
   unique, historical, single-context, inaccessible, or regulated entity is
   publishable when that test holds and residual ambiguity is declared;
4. names cross-context invariance, null-peer comparison, adjoint round-trip,
   transplant, and coat stripping as non-mandatory discrimination techniques
   rather than universal mechanics, and records that the adjoint round-trip
   performs at semantic altitude the discrimination the geometric boundary
   test performs in a representation space;
5. scopes **Markov Object Construction Law** as the representation-substrate
   specialization of that law, makes the declared representation space a
   required and versioned overlay member, and makes a re-fitted representation
   space an explicit supersession even when no ledger entry changed; and
6. leads **Representation Law** with the interventional boundary statement,
   treating the low-rank projection as the declared-space form of the same
   law; and
7. states one epistemic truth for the Markov construct: the named
   conditional-independence promotion test ran and did not meet its
   predicate, its multi-layer successor did not either, the gate is not
   relocated, and the construct remains candidate-class. That status
   attaches to the construct and to overlays built on it, and does not
   attach to entity cuts, which are lawful published truth on their own
   admissibility test. The companion
   research surface is bound by commit and document digest and is recorded as
   research evidence, not constitutional authority.

This supersedes the predecessor rule that made the Markov-object cut the sole
publication unit and required a verified identity direction before a recovered
entity could be published.

It weakens no geometric obligation. Where a representation space is declared,
every prior construction, verification, and storage obligation continues to
apply, and the overlay now additionally carries the space it is expressed in.
