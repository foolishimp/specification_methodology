## Product Delta

### Complete Enclosing-Relation Admission (`STDO-UP-021`)

For a material transition, every admission owner independently derives the
causally complete enclosing relation from authoritative input. Admission binds
one exact admission-valid basis through validation and commit; currentness
advancement causes the declared predecessor/currentness check or effect-free
re-entry, and participating owners use an equal or design-declared coherent
composite basis rather than combining independently valid mixed bases.

Every participant and equality join validates before any transition effect.
The complete transition is one semantic commit against the exact basis:
failure or interruption admits all or none, never a participant subset. Direct
and every supported composite or nested admission preserve that law, competing
same-scope authority cannot bypass it, and fresh reconstruction and replay
reproduce the exact relation, basis, and outcome or refusal.

Standing qualification is instantiated for every owner and supported boundary.
It observes direct, supported composite or nested, forged, ambiguous, stale or
advanced-basis, incoherent multi-owner-basis, competing-authority,
fresh-process, exact-replay, and atomic-publication cases. Post-validation
failure is exercised at every materially distinct commit or publication
boundary capable of exposing a subset, unless accepted design declares a
dominance/equivalence proof that named representative observations cover the
complete partial-failure surface. An indivisible unit retains immediate
before/after evidence.

`DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md` requires accepted design to declare the owner and
supported boundary, exact basis and continuity, enclosing relation and joins,
refusal/effect boundary, semantic commit unit, every materially distinct
partial-failure boundary or complete-surface coverage proof, reconstruction,
supported structural forms, and competing paths. `SPEC_METHOD.md` remains the
sole owner of the federal admission and standing-qualification semantics.
