## Product Delta

### Complete Enclosing-Relation Admission (`STDO-UP-021`)

For a material transition, every admission owner independently derives the
causally complete enclosing relation from authoritative input. Admission binds
one exact admission-valid basis through validation and commit; currentness
advancement causes the declared predecessor/currentness check or effect-free
re-entry, and participating owners use an equal or design-declared coherent
composite basis rather than combining independently valid mixed bases.

Every participant and equality join validates before any transition effect.
The complete transition is one semantic commit against the exact basis:
failure or interruption admits all or none, never a participant subset. Direct
and every supported composite or nested admission preserve that law, competing
same-scope authority cannot bypass it, and fresh reconstruction and replay
reproduce the exact relation, basis, and outcome or refusal.

Standing qualification is instantiated for every owner and supported boundary.
It observes direct, supported composite or nested, forged, ambiguous, stale or
advanced-basis, incoherent multi-owner-basis, competing-authority,
fresh-process, exact-replay, and atomic-publication cases. Post-validation
failure is exercised at every materially distinct commit or publication
boundary capable of exposing a subset, unless accepted design declares a
dominance/equivalence proof that named representative observations cover the
complete partial-failure surface. An indivisible unit retains immediate
before/after evidence.

`DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md` requires accepted design to declare the owner and
supported boundary, exact basis and continuity, enclosing relation and joins,
refusal/effect boundary, semantic commit unit, every materially distinct
partial-failure boundary or complete-surface coverage proof, reconstruction,
supported structural forms, and competing paths. `SPEC_METHOD.md` remains the
sole owner of the federal admission and standing-qualification semantics.

### Assurance-Boundary Congruence, Method Qualification, And IACS Revision (`STDO-UP-022`)

The second bounded amendment closes the five selected assurance and revision
relations:

- **F1 — assurance subject:** assurance supporting promotion or closure binds
  the exact claimed subject and authoritative composition relation. Per-file or
  per-carrier evidence cannot close an unevaluated composite claim.
- **F2 — IACS revision:** carrier addition, removal, merge, and split proceed
  through accepted Ontology/design re-entry, complete-set re-derivation,
  Promotion and Boundary Inflation replay over the full revised set, update of
  every existing conformance/checker subject to the composite carrier relation,
  and implementation reconciliation before closure. No checker is required to
  exist.
- **F3 — verdict scope:** quantifier, population, and scope cannot outrun
  evaluated evidence. Generalization declares its inference relation,
  comparable population, counterexample treatment, and governing evidence;
  later counterevidence invalidates dependent verdicts until requalification.
- **F4 — mechanical evidence:** mechanical enforcement names an executable or
  reproducible predicate and a witness reachable through the declared ordinary
  assurance path. Without either, the claim stays open or narrows to the
  planned property; planned or debug-only evidence is not observed ordinary-
  path verification.
- **F5 — generic method qualification:** the Probabilistic Work Boundary,
  `STDO-UP-020`, and Reconstruction Litmus govern a fresh competent constructor
  inside a declared capability, context, and configuration envelope, using only
  the declared ordinary method and authority surfaces, plus independently
  authorized evaluation under a declared basis and comparison predicate.

Where prior exposure would compromise reconstructive independence, material
expected/reference outcomes, source exemplars or incumbents, author memory, and
ad hoc rescue remain withheld until the construction result is frozen. The
frozen result is compared against the mandatory governing semantic basis. Any
separately held expected/reference outcome, if it exists and is applicable, is
optional, non-authoritative evidence; equivalence to it is required only where
the governing basis requires it. A basis-permitted alternative may pass.
Subjective similarity, byte or structural identity, unique derivation,
determinism, and incumbent equality are not generic proof criteria.
Post-exposure revision is a declared method constituent and qualification
boundary or a new qualification subject; it cannot retroactively pass the
original frozen result.
