## Product Delta

### Working Refinements Reconciled To Their Pinned Source

The four working refinements in `WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md` were a faithful
projection of a companion research surface that has since been re-cut. The set
of findings survived, but their order, emphasis, and grounding no longer
matched the source, and none cited the experiment supporting it. Drift in
either direction was undetectable by reading.

The successor:

1. restates the refinements in the order and sense the pinned companion
   carries: effective blanket is geometric rather than set-theoretic;
   attribute schemas sense and fragment rather than isolate; core identity
   rather than core size discriminates objecthood; identity is a DC shift
   rather than a principal-variance axis;
2. cites the grounding experiment for each, so each refinement is checkable
   against the pinned source;
3. states that the pin in *Companion Surfaces* fixes which reading this method
   adopted, so a later re-cut of that program is detectable rather than
   silent; and
4. scopes the refinements explicitly to the Markov construct and overlays
   built on it, and not to entity cuts, which are governed by *Entity Cut
   Construction Law*.

This clarifies the relation between a normative section and its evidence
source. It supersedes no admissibility condition, publication requirement, or
law.

## Conserved Law

The successor retains the complete `v2.2.2` semantic baseline. All four
findings survive in substance, including the exclusion of principal-component
and maximum-variance constructions as identity directions, which now carries
its grounding experiment. Entity-cut admissibility, the geometric construction
law, and the epistemic status of the construct are unchanged.
